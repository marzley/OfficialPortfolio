Services

Services offered by Marzley Tech Solutions

By Kelvin Wanyoike · 28 Sep 2026 · 3 min read

Marzley Tech Solutions is a technology company based in Kenya. We design and build websites and systems, help businesses get paid online, and train people in ICT and digital skills. Here’s everything we offer.

1. Websites

  • Landing pages for campaigns, events and personal brands.
  • Business websites with your services, Google Maps, WhatsApp button and contact forms.
  • School, college and organisation websites with courses, news and downloads.
  • Portfolios for professionals and creatives.

Every site is mobile-first, fast on mobile data, secure (https) and built for Google from day one. Packages start from KSh 15,000. See packages and prices.

2. E-commerce and online payments

  • Online shops with product catalogues, carts and orders.
  • M-Pesa STK push (customers get a prompt on their phone), Paystack cards and PayPal.
  • Invoices and payment links, hotspot Wi-Fi billing and other pay-per-use systems.

Read how M-Pesa payments on a website work.

3. Custom systems

  • Hospital and pharmacy systems: patient records, appointments and billing.
  • School and college systems: students, fees, results and portals.
  • Business systems: sales, stock, staff and reports.
  • Education tools like CBET Planner for TVET trainers.
  • Construction and engineering platforms like BuildPro for architects and engineers.
  • Marketplaces and delivery platforms like Jobs Connect, which matches skilled workers with customers.
  • Welfare, SACCO and chama systems for contributions and members.
  • APIs and integrations between your tools.

4. UI/UX and graphic design

  • Interface and user experience design for websites and apps.
  • Logos, branding and brand kits.
  • Posters, flyers and social media designs.
  • Photo editing and effects.

5. Hosting, domains and care plans

  • Domain registration (.co.ke, .com and more) and hosting setup.
  • Care plans from KSh 1,500 a month: backups, security updates, uptime monitoring and small changes, so your site never breaks or expires. Read why a care plan matters.

6. SEO and getting found online

Search-friendly websites, Google Search Console, Google Business Profile help and regular SEO checks. Read what SEO is and how we do it for you, or check your website free.

7. ICT training and mentorship

  • Web development and programming.
  • Design skills.
  • Digital skills for individuals, schools and organisations.
  • Technical mentorship and ICT consultation.

See our training courses, or learn on your own in the free learning hub.

How we work

  1. Free call to understand what you need.
  2. Written quote with a clear scope and timeline.
  3. Payment in stages, by M-Pesa, bank or card.
  4. Design, build and test, with you seeing progress in your client portal.
  5. Launch, training and support.
