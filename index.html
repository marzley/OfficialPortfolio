02 — Payments integration

We add M-Pesa payments to your systems

Shops, schools, hospitals, Wi-Fi hotspots: we connect your system to M-Pesa so customers pay from their phones and every payment is confirmed automatically. Try it yourself: enter your Safaricom number below and you’ll get the same prompt your customers would see.

  1. Checkout sends an STK push request to Safaricom.
  2. The phone shows the M-Pesa PIN prompt.
  3. Confirmation arrives by SMS and the order updates automatically.

This is a real payment of KSh 1 to Marzley Tech Solutions (Till 6095737). Enter your PIN only in the M-Pesa prompt on your own phone, never on a website.
