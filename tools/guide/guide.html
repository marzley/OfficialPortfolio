Get an instant quote

  1. On Pricing, choose a package in the quote calculator.

Section 06

The client portal

Your private dashboard at marzleytechsolutions.co.ke/portal/: follow your projects, pay, download statements, refer friends and get support. Anyone can create an account.

Create an account or sign in

  1. Open the portal (the round user button at the top of the website, or Client login).
  2. Choose Sign in with Google, or type your email and press Email me a code.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code from your email (and your name the first time).
  4. You're in. You get a client ID (e.g. MT-0005) and a personal referral code.
Kiswahili: press the Kiswahili button at the top of the portal to switch language.

How a lesson works

  1. Pick a subject; the lesson list appears on the left (menu button on phones).
  2. Read the notes and press Run on the Try it code boxes to see results instantly.
  3. Answer the practice questions; they check themselves.
  4. Complete the exercise and press Check my answer.
  5. Use Keep learning for free videos on the topic, then go to the next lesson.
Save your progress: sign in (free) so completed lessons follow you to any device.

▶Useful buttons

  • Examples… loads ready-made programs.
  • ↺ Reset brings back the starter code.
  • Copy copies your code.
  • Your code is saved on your device automatically.
  • The About panel explains the language, gives tips and links to its lessons.

!When code goes wrong

  • The error line is highlighted red; click “Line N” to jump to it.
  • What went wrong and How to fix it in plain English, with an example.
  • HTML/CSS get tips: unclosed tags, missing ; or }, “colour” instead of color, missing units.
  • Code that runs forever is stopped after 10 seconds; the page never freezes.
Safe by design: learners' code runs in a sealed sandbox. It cannot reach the website, cookies, accounts or payments, and it doesn't load our server.

Section 13

Safety, privacy, help and contacts

Safety and privacy

  • The whole site uses https (the padlock).
  • Sign-in uses Google or one-time email codes: no passwords to steal.
  • M-Pesa payments are confirmed by Safaricom; we never ask for your PIN.
  • Your data is handled under Kenya's Data Protection Act (2019). See the Privacy page.
  • Analytics cookies are only used if you accept them.
