How to get paid faster: M-Pesa invoices, reminders and part payments

By Kelvin Wanyoike · 27 Sep 2026 · 3 min read

For many small businesses in Kenya, the hardest part of a job isn’t the work. It’s getting paid for it. Invoices sit in WhatsApp chats, reminders feel awkward, and clients who want to pay can’t easily find the details. A few simple changes make a big difference.

1. Make paying a one-tap job

When an invoice has a “Pay with M-Pesa” button, the client enters their number, gets the prompt on their phone and types their PIN. No copying till numbers, no mistyped references, and the payment is matched to the right invoice automatically. Adding card payments as well helps clients abroad and companies that pay by card.

2. Let reminders do the chasing

Nobody enjoys sending “just following up” messages. Automatic reminders are polite, consistent and never forget. A good pattern is:

  • 3 days before the due date: a friendly heads-up.
  • On the due date: a short reminder with the pay button.
  • 3, 7 and 14 days late: firmer reminders, by email and SMS.

Because the system sends them, your relationship with the client stays about the work, not the money.

3. Accept part payments

Large invoices are easier to pay in instalments, and M-Pesa has a per-transaction limit anyway. When clients can pay part of an invoice and see the balance, money starts coming in sooner instead of waiting for the full amount.

4. Bill in stages

Instead of one big invoice at the end, tie payments to milestones the client approves: a deposit to start, a payment when they approve the design, and the balance at launch. Each approval can create the next invoice automatically, so billing keeps pace with the work.

5. Make “I’ve already paid” easy to report

Some clients will still pay to your till or by bank transfer. Give them a simple way to send the M-Pesa code or a bank slip against the invoice, so you can confirm it in one click instead of searching statements.

6. Keep clean records

Numbered invoices in an unbroken series, receipts sent automatically and a monthly export of payments make life easier for your accountant and for KRA.
