FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about websites, payments, systems, training and support. Can’t find yours? Ask on WhatsApp.

Websites

How long does it take to build a website?

Most landing pages take about a week and business websites a few weeks. Online shops and systems depend on the features. You get a clear timeline with your quote, and having your content (text and photos) ready speeds things up.

Will my website work well on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on phones, because most visitors in Kenya browse on mobile data.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We can build on WordPress or add a simple admin area so you can change text, photos and products, and we show you how to use it.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. The domain, hosting account, files and content are yours, and you get all the logins when the project is done.

Payments

How does M-Pesa payment on a website work?

The customer enters their phone number at checkout and gets an M-Pesa PIN prompt (STK push) on their phone. Once they pay, Safaricom confirms the payment and the website updates the order automatically.

Do I need a paybill or till number?

Yes. Payments go straight to your own till or paybill. We help you set up the Safaricom Daraja connection your website needs.

How do I pay for my project?

A deposit starts the work, and the balance is paid in stages as the project progresses. You can pay by M-Pesa (Till 6095737), bank transfer or card.

Systems & portals

Can you build a system for my hospital, school or business?

Yes. Past work includes hospital management systems, CBET Planner for TVET trainers, a Wi-Fi billing system and online stores with admin tools. Each system is built around how you work.

Is my data safe in the system?

Systems use secure logins, https, careful user permissions and regular backups. Your data belongs to you and is only used for your project.

How do I follow my project’s progress?

Clients get access to the client portal to see progress, updates and files, approve designs, open support requests and pay invoices by M-Pesa.

Training

Do I need experience to join a class?

No. Courses start from the basics and build up with real projects. Tell us your level when you enrol and we’ll place you in the right class.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Students who complete a course get a certificate with a code that employers can verify online.

Support & care

What happens after my website goes live?

We show you how to manage it and stay available for support. For ongoing peace of mind, care plans cover backups, updates, security and small changes every month.

What if my website gets hacked or breaks?

With a care plan, we restore it from a backup and fix the cause. Without one, contact us and we’ll quote the repair quickly.
