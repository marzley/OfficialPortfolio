Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information Marzley Tech Solutions (“we”, “us”) collects through this website, our client portal and our services, why we collect it, and your rights under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who we are

Marzley Tech Solutions is a web development, systems and IT training business in Kenya. For any privacy question or request, contact us at marzleytechsolutionltd@gmail.com or +254 745 789 590.

What we collect

  • When you contact us or book a call: your name, email, phone number and your message.
  • When you pay by M-Pesa: your phone number, the amount, and the M-Pesa receipt number and result that Safaricom sends us.
  • In the client portal: the name and email of the Google account you sign in with, your projects, invoices, files, approvals, support messages and course progress. We keep a log of sign-ins and changes (with the IP address) to keep accounts secure.
  • When you use the website chat: your questions are answered in your browser from our own information. If AI answers are switched on, a question we can’t answer that way is sent, with the last few messages of the chat, to our AI provider to write a reply. We don’t store chat conversations. If you ask for a call back, your name and number are saved like any other enquiry.
  • When you use the free website check: the website address you enter. We keep a short record of your IP address to stop misuse.
  • Website statistics: only if you accept, Google Analytics records how pages are used, with IP addresses shortened. If you decline, it never loads. You can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of each page.
  • On your device: the site remembers your theme, accessibility choices and similar settings in your browser. These stay on your device.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you, prepare quotes and deliver the work you ask for (performing a contract with you).
  • To take payments, send invoices, receipts and reminders, and keep financial records (contract and legal obligations, including tax).
  • To run the client portal securely and prevent fraud (our legitimate interest in protecting you and us).
  • To understand and improve the website, only with your consent.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with the services we need to do the work: Safaricom (M-Pesa payments), Paystack (card payments), Google (portal sign-in and, with consent, Analytics), Formspree (contact form delivery), Africa’s Talking (SMS alerts, if enabled), Anthropic (to answer questions typed into the website chat, if AI answers are switched on; please don’t type personal or payment details there), and our web hosting and email provider. Each handles your data under its own privacy terms. Some of these providers may process data outside Kenya, with appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become a project: up to 12 months.
  • Client, project and payment records: for as long as you are a client, then up to 7 years for tax and accounting.
  • Security logs: up to 12 months. Backups are kept for 14 days.

How we protect it

The site uses https everywhere. Portal passwords are handled by Google, sessions sign out automatically when idle, private files and backups are stored outside the public website, and access is limited to our team.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it (where we do not need to keep it by law), stop using it for a purpose, or give you a copy. Contact us at the details above and we will respond within 7 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at www.odpc.go.ke.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. Students under 18 join our training only with a parent’s or guardian’s agreement.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Important changes will be shown on this page.

See also our terms of service.
