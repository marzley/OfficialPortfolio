Website, app and game testing platforms: UserTesting, Userlytics, Trymata, uTest and more

Testing pays the best of the easy-entry work: you use a website or app, think aloud, and report what confused you or what broke. Clear speaking and good English help a lot.

Check before you rely on it

Countries, payment options and minimum payouts change often. Confirm on each platform's official website, and never pay anyone to join.

38. UserTesting

What it is: The best-known usability testing platform.

TasksRecorded tests where you speak your thoughts while using a site or app; live interviews
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaOpen to many countries; test supply is higher for some countries
Realistic earningsLow to medium per test; live interviews pay more
Best forClear speakers with a quiet place and a mic

How to start: Do the practice test well: it decides your access. Speak continuously and specifically. Pros: Good pay per test. Cons: Tests go fast; screeners filter you out. Watch out: Never share test content: it's confidential.

39. Userlytics

What it is: A usability testing platform with recorded and moderated tests.

TasksRecorded think-aloud tests, surveys, card sorting
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forTesters with good audio

How to start: Complete your profile; respond fast to invites. Pros: Fair pay per test. Cons: Irregular invites. Watch out: Use a quiet room; poor audio gets rejected.

40. Trymata (formerly TryMyUI)

What it is: A usability testing platform for websites and apps.

TasksThink-aloud tests with written follow-up
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forNative-level English speakers

How to start: Pass the qualification test. Pros: Simple tests. Cons: Limited volume. Watch out: Follow the tasks exactly.

41. Testbirds

What it is: A German crowdtesting company for apps, websites and devices.

TasksBug testing, usability tests, device tests
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forPeople with various devices

How to start: Register your devices; join projects. Pros: Bug bounties per valid bug. Cons: Projects are selective. Watch out: Report bugs clearly with steps and screenshots.

42. uTest

What it is: A large community for software testers (Applause).

TasksFunctional testing, bug reporting, usability, payments testing
How you're paidPayPal or Payoneer
KenyaOpen to many countries; projects depend on location and devices
Realistic earningsLow to medium; experienced testers more
Best forPeople who want to grow into software testing (QA)

How to start: Complete the uTest Academy courses; start with small projects. Pros: Path into a QA career. Cons: Competitive at first. Watch out: Duplicate or low-quality bug reports hurt your rating.

43. Test IO

What it is: A crowdtesting platform paying for valid bugs in websites and apps.

TasksExploratory testing, bug reports, reproductions
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forDetail-oriented testers

How to start: Learn bug report writing; join test cycles quickly. Pros: Pays per valid bug. Cons: Competition for bugs. Watch out: Follow scope: testing outside scope isn't paid.

44. Ubertesters

What it is: A crowdtesting company for mobile apps and websites.

TasksFunctional and usability testing
How you're paidPayPal or Payoneer
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forMobile testers

How to start: Register devices and apply to projects. Pros: Varied projects. Cons: Irregular. Watch out: Keep test apps confidential.

45. PlaytestCloud

What it is: A platform where players test mobile games before release.

TasksPlay and record your reaction to new games
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaOpen to several countries; check availability
Realistic earningsLow per test
Best forMobile gamers

How to start: Speak your thoughts as you play. Pros: Fun. Cons: Limited tests. Watch out: Keep games confidential.

46. Respondent

What it is: A research platform paying for interviews with professionals and consumers.

TasksPaid interviews, focus groups, diary studies
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaMany studies target specific countries; some are remote and global
Realistic earningsMedium to high per interview
Best forProfessionals (IT, business, health) and specific consumers

How to start: Complete your profile with your real job details; apply to studies that match. Pros: High pay per session. Cons: Many applications needed. Watch out: Never misrepresent your job: you'll be removed.

47. User Interviews

What it is: A platform connecting researchers with participants for paid studies.

TasksInterviews, usability tests, surveys
How you're paidPaid by researchers, often via PayPal or gift cards
KenyaMany studies are US-focused; some accept global participants
Realistic earningsMedium per study
Best forPeople who fit specific study criteria

How to start: Apply to studies that genuinely fit you. Pros: Good pay per study. Cons: Selective. Watch out: Honesty is required.

Summary

  • Testing pays best among easy-entry work: UserTesting, Userlytics, Trymata, Testbirds, uTest, Test IO, Ubertesters and PlaytestCloud.
  • Respondent and User Interviews pay well for interviews when your profile fits.
  • Clear audio, honest profiles and confidentiality protect your access.
