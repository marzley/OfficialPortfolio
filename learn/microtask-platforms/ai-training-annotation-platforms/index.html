AI training and data annotation platforms: Outlier, DataAnnotation, TELUS Digital, OneForma and more

AI companies pay people to label data, write and rate answers, and check AI output. These platforms pay the best in the microtask world, but you must pass tests, and projects start and stop.

Check before you rely on it

Countries, payment options and minimum payouts change often. Confirm on each platform's official website, and never pay anyone to join.

19. Outlier

What it is: A platform that pays people to train and evaluate AI models, including experts in coding, maths and other subjects.

TasksWriting and rating AI responses, coding and reasoning tasks, fact-checking
How you're paidUsually PayPal or other methods offered in your account
KenyaOpen to some countries and projects; check current eligibility for Kenya
Realistic earningsMedium when projects are available; specialists earn more
Best forStrong writers, coders and graduates with subject expertise

How to start: Apply with your real details, take assessments seriously, and read every guideline. Pros: Better pay than most microtasks. Cons: Work can stop suddenly (“empty queue”); strict quality checks. Watch out: Fake “Outlier recruiters” on WhatsApp asking for fees are scams.

20. DataAnnotation

What it is: A platform paying for AI training work such as writing, rating and coding tasks.

TasksRating chatbot responses, writing prompts, coding evaluation
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaMostly open to a limited list of countries; check whether Kenya is accepted
Realistic earningsMedium where available
Best forExcellent English writers and coders

How to start: Take the starter assessment carefully; there's no second chance on some tests. Pros: Good hourly-style pay. Cons: Limited countries; no feedback on rejected applications. Watch out: Ignore sites copying the name and asking for payments.

21. CrowdGen (Appen)

What it is: Appen's crowd platform for search evaluation, data collection and AI projects.

TasksSearch evaluation, social media evaluation, data collection, transcription, linguistics
How you're paidPayoneer or PayPal depending on project
KenyaProjects in many countries, including some for Kenya and Kiswahili
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forPatient applicants who pass project qualifications

How to start: Complete your profile, apply to projects that match your language and location, prepare for exams. Pros: Long-running company. Cons: Long waits; qualification exams; projects close. Watch out: Never pay for “exam answers”: it gets you banned.

22. TELUS Digital AI Community

What it is: TELUS Digital's (formerly TELUS International AI / Lionbridge AI) crowd of raters and data workers.

TasksSearch engine and ads evaluation, maps checks, data collection
How you're paidUsually Payoneer or bank, monthly invoices
KenyaProjects in many countries; Kenya projects appear from time to time
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forDetail-focused people with steady internet

How to start: Apply, pass the exam (studying the guidelines), and keep to your weekly hours. Pros: Steady part-time work when accepted. Cons: Hard exams; quality audits. Watch out: Real exams are free and online; anyone selling access is a scammer.

23. OneForma

What it is: A crowd platform (by Centific) for language and AI data projects.

TasksTranscription, translation, data collection, rating
How you're paidPayoneer or PayPal
KenyaOpen to many countries with language projects, including Kiswahili at times
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forMultilingual people

How to start: Fill in language skills accurately; apply to open projects. Pros: Strong for languages. Cons: Irregular projects. Watch out: Use only the official site to apply.

24. RWS TrainAI

What it is: RWS's AI data community for language and data projects.

TasksData annotation, search rating, language data, content checks
How you're paidUsually via the payment method in your account
KenyaProjects in many countries
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forLinguists and detail-oriented workers

How to start: Register, complete assessments, and watch for project invites. Pros: Reputable language company. Cons: Projects vary. Watch out: Same scam warnings as above.

25. Welocalize

What it is: A localisation company that hires raters and linguists for AI and search projects.

TasksSearch and ads evaluation, data collection, language QA
How you're paidMonthly invoices, usually bank or Payoneer
KenyaLocation-specific projects; check open roles
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forLinguists and raters

How to start: Apply to open rater and data roles on their careers page. Pros: Structured projects. Cons: Location requirements. Watch out: Applications are free.

26. Mindrift

What it is: An AI training platform where experts write and review data for AI models.

TasksWriting and reviewing AI tasks in many fields
How you're paidPayment method in your account
KenyaOpen to many countries for some projects
Realistic earningsMedium for qualified experts
Best forGraduates and professionals

How to start: Apply in your field of expertise and take tests carefully. Pros: Uses real expertise. Cons: Competitive. Watch out: Ignore middlemen offering “guaranteed approval”.

27. Alignerr (by Labelbox)

What it is: An AI training community for experts in coding, maths, languages and more.

TasksEvaluating and writing AI responses, specialist reviews
How you're paidPayment method in your account
KenyaOpen to many countries; eligibility varies by project
Realistic earningsMedium for experts
Best forSpecialists

How to start: Apply in a field where you're genuinely strong. Pros: Good for experts. Cons: Selective. Watch out: No fees are ever needed.

28. CloudFactory and Sama (Nairobi employers)

What it is: Data annotation companies with operations in Kenya that hire staff and contractors.

TasksImage annotation, data labelling, quality checks
How you're paidSalary or contract pay in Kenya
KenyaKenya-based roles when hiring
Realistic earningsMedium (formal employment)
Best forPeople who want steady annotation work with training

How to start: Watch their official careers pages and LinkedIn; apply directly. Pros: Training and steady income. Cons: Competitive hiring; shift work. Watch out: Real employers don't charge recruitment fees.

About Remotasks

Remotasks, once popular in Kenya, stopped operating in Kenya and several other countries in 2024. Treat any offers of “Remotasks Kenya accounts” as scams or rule-breaking.

Summary

  • AI training platforms (Outlier, DataAnnotation, CrowdGen, TELUS Digital, OneForma, RWS, Welocalize, Mindrift, Alignerr) pay best, but need tests and projects come and go.
  • In Kenya, CloudFactory and Sama offer formal annotation jobs when hiring.
  • Remotasks left Kenya in 2024; never buy accounts or pay for exam answers.
