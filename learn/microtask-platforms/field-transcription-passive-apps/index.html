Field data, transcription and passive apps: Premise, GoTranscript, TranscribeMe, Honeygain and more

Some apps pay you to collect information around your town; transcription platforms pay you to type audio; “passive” apps pay to share your unused internet. Each has its own trade-offs.

Check before you rely on it

Countries, payment options and minimum payouts change often. Confirm on each platform's official website, and never pay anyone to join.

48. Premise

What it is: A data collection app that pays for tasks such as photographing prices or answering surveys about your area.

TasksPhotos of shops and prices, location checks, surveys
How you're paidPayment options offered in the app by country
KenyaActive in Kenya and many other countries
Realistic earningsLow
Best forPeople who move around town anyway

How to start: Follow task instructions exactly; respect private property and people's privacy. Pros: Uses your phone and daily routine. Cons: Tasks depend on location. Watch out: Never photograph people without consent or enter restricted areas.

49. Streetbees

What it is: An app that pays for photo and video stories about your daily life and purchases.

TasksShort surveys with photos or videos
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaAvailable in many countries; check Kenya in-app
Realistic earningsLow
Best forPhone users

How to start: Answer with clear photos and honest stories. Pros: Simple tasks. Cons: Irregular tasks. Watch out: Don't share personal documents in photos.

50. GoTranscript

What it is: A transcription company that accepts transcribers from around the world.

TasksTranscribing short audio files, captions, translations
How you're paidPayPal or Payoneer, weekly
KenyaOpen to Kenyan transcribers
Realistic earningsLow at first; rises with quality and speed
Best forFast, accurate typists with good English

How to start: Pass the entry test; start with short files; build your quality score. Pros: Steady supply; accepts Kenyans. Cons: Low starting rates; strict style guide. Watch out: Use headphones and follow the style guide strictly.

51. TranscribeMe

What it is: A transcription platform that splits audio into short chunks.

TasksShort transcription tasks, some specialist teams (medical, translation)
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsLow
Best forTypists who want short tasks

How to start: Pass the exam; work up to better-paid teams. Pros: Short, flexible tasks. Cons: Low base pay. Watch out: Respect confidentiality of audio.

52. Scribie

What it is: A transcription platform paying per audio minute, with review levels.

TasksShort audio files, reviewing
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsLow
Best forDetail-oriented typists

How to start: Do the test and learn their guidelines. Pros: Clear progression. Cons: Low pay at first. Watch out: Accuracy matters more than speed.

53. Rev

What it is: A well-known transcription and captioning company.

TasksTranscription, captions, foreign subtitles
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaAccepted countries vary; check whether Kenya is open for new freelancers
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Best forFast, accurate typists

How to start: Apply only if your country is accepted; pass the tests. Pros: Big client base. Cons: Country limits; competition. Watch out: Never buy someone else's account.

54. Honeygain

What it is: An app that pays you for sharing unused internet bandwidth.

TasksPassive: runs in the background
How you're paidPayPal or crypto after a minimum
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsVery low; uses your data
Best forPeople with unlimited Wi-Fi only

How to start: Only use on unlimited home Wi-Fi, never mobile data bundles. Pros: Passive. Cons: Very low pay; your connection is used by others. Watch out: Understand that others' traffic goes through your connection: read the terms and privacy policy.

55. Pawns.app

What it is: Another bandwidth-sharing app with small surveys.

TasksPassive bandwidth sharing, surveys
How you're paidPayPal or crypto
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsVery low
Best forPeople with unlimited Wi-Fi

How to start: Same rules as Honeygain. Pros: Passive. Cons: Very low pay. Watch out: Same privacy caution.

Captcha-solving sites

Some sites pay to type captchas. Pay is extremely low, and solving captchas often helps bots get past security systems meant to stop spam and fraud. We don't recommend them.

Summary

  • Premise and Streetbees pay for local data; GoTranscript, TranscribeMe, Scribie and Rev pay for transcription.
  • GoTranscript accepts Kenyan transcribers; quality and speed raise your earnings.
  • Bandwidth-sharing apps (Honeygain, Pawns) pay very little: use only on unlimited Wi-Fi and read the privacy terms.
