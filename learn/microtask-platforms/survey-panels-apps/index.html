Survey panels and apps: Toluna, YouGov, Attapoll, GeoPoll, Prolific and more

Survey platforms pay for your opinions. Supply depends heavily on your age, location and profile, and screen-outs are normal. Academic research platforms usually pay the fairest rates.

Check before you rely on it

Countries, payment options and minimum payouts change often. Confirm on each platform's official website, and never pay anyone to join.

29. Toluna

What it is: A large global survey community with points for surveys and polls.

TasksMarket research surveys, polls, product tests
How you're paidPoints redeemed for gift cards or cash options by country
KenyaOperates in many countries; check if there's a panel for Kenya
Realistic earningsLow
Best forPeople who enjoy giving opinions

How to start: Complete your profile; answer consistently. Pros: Big brand; many surveys in supported countries. Cons: Gift-card heavy; country-limited. Watch out: Inconsistent answers get accounts closed.

30. YouGov

What it is: A polling and research company with online panels.

TasksOpinion polls on news, brands and society
How you're paidPoints redeemed for cash or vouchers in supported countries
KenyaPanels exist in many countries; check whether Kenya has one open
Realistic earningsLow
Best forPeople interested in current affairs

How to start: Join only if your country panel is listed. Pros: Respected research company. Cons: Slow to reach payouts. Watch out: Use the official site only.

31. Attapoll

What it is: A survey app that pools surveys from many providers.

TasksShort surveys
How you're paidPayPal and other options, low minimum
KenyaAvailable in many countries including Kenya
Realistic earningsLow
Best forPhone users with spare minutes

How to start: Turn on notifications for new surveys; answer honestly. Pros: Low minimum payout. Cons: Many screen-outs. Watch out: Survey routers sometimes fail: report it, don't create new accounts.

32. Poll Pay

What it is: A survey and task app with short polls.

TasksSurveys, short tasks
How you're paidPayPal and gift cards
KenyaAvailable in many countries
Realistic earningsLow
Best forPhone users

How to start: Install from the official store; check reviews. Pros: Simple. Cons: Low pay. Watch out: Avoid look-alike apps.

33. Surveytime

What it is: A survey site that pays a fixed small amount per completed survey.

TasksSurveys from partner networks
How you're paidInstant PayPal or gift cards
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsLow
Best forPeople who want instant small payouts

How to start: Profile honestly; don't rush. Pros: Instant payouts. Cons: Few surveys for some countries. Watch out: Rushing gets you flagged.

34. TGM Panel

What it is: A global research panel (TGM Research) covering many countries including in Africa.

TasksMarket research surveys
How you're paidPayPal and other options
KenyaHas panels in many African countries; check Kenya at sign-up
Realistic earningsLow
Best forSurvey takers in emerging markets

How to start: Complete profile surveys to receive invites. Pros: Includes African markets. Cons: Irregular invites. Watch out: One account per person.

35. GeoPoll

What it is: An Africa-focused research company that surveys people by SMS, app and phone.

TasksShort surveys on brands, health, media and society
How you're paidAirtime after completing a survey; GeoPoll also lists M-Pesa as an incentive in Kenya
KenyaActive in Kenya, with direct links to mobile networks
Realistic earningsLow (airtime)
Best forAnyone with a phone in Kenya

How to start: Respond to genuine GeoPoll survey messages; check the sender is official. Pros: Works on basic phones; Kenya-focused. Cons: Small rewards. Watch out: Scammers may impersonate survey firms: GeoPoll never asks for your M-Pesa PIN.

36. LifePoints

What it is: A survey panel run by Kantar, a major research company.

TasksMarket research surveys
How you're paidPoints for PayPal or vouchers by country
KenyaOperates in many countries; check availability
Realistic earningsLow
Best forSurvey takers in supported countries

How to start: Join if your country is listed. Pros: Reputable research company. Cons: Country-limited. Watch out: Use the official app or site.

37. Prolific

What it is: An academic research platform used by universities to run studies.

TasksSurveys, experiments, AI-related studies
How you're paidPayPal
KenyaOnly some countries are accepted; check the current list before joining
Realistic earningsLow to medium per hour (studies must pay a fair minimum)
Best forHonest, attentive participants

How to start: Join the waitlist if your country is supported; answer attention checks carefully. Pros: Fair pay and respectful treatment. Cons: Limited countries; waitlists. Watch out: Never lie about your location or demographics: it's research fraud.

Summary

  • Survey platforms pay little and depend on your profile; screen-outs are normal.
  • Attapoll, TGM Panel and GeoPoll (airtime) are practical in Kenya; Toluna, YouGov, LifePoints and Prolific are country-limited.
  • Answer honestly and consistently; never fake your location or details.
