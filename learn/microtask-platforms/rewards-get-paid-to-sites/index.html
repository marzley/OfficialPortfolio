Rewards and “get paid to” sites: Timebucks, ySense, Freecash and 7 more

Rewards sites (also called GPT, “get paid to”, sites) mix small tasks, surveys, app and game offers and short videos. They're easy to join and pay little, so use them for spare minutes, not as a job.

Check before you rely on it

Countries, payment options and minimum payouts change often. Confirm on each platform's official website, and never pay anyone to join.

1. Timebucks

What it is: A long-running rewards site with many ways to earn small amounts.

TasksSurveys, short tasks, app installs and offers, watching content, social tasks, referrals
How you're paidPayPal, Payeer and crypto (options vary by country); payouts are processed weekly once you reach the minimum balance
KenyaOpen to most countries and widely used in Kenya
Realistic earningsLow: typically a few dollars a month with regular use
Best forBeginners learning how rewards sites work

How to start: Sign up with your real details, complete your profile so surveys match you, and stick to tasks with clear instructions. Pros: Many task types; long track record. Cons: Very low pay per task; survey screen-outs. Watch out: Offers that ask for card details or paid subscriptions: skip them.

2. ySense

What it is: A rewards and survey site (formerly ClixSense) with a clean dashboard.

TasksSurveys from many partners, tasks, offers, daily checklists
How you're paidPayPal, Payoneer, Skrill or gift cards (options vary by country)
KenyaAvailable in many countries including Kenya; survey supply depends on your profile
Realistic earningsLow
Best forPeople who prefer surveys over offers

How to start: Fill in every profile survey; do the daily checklist; withdraw once you reach the minimum. Pros: Reliable payouts reported over many years. Cons: Few surveys for some demographics. Watch out: Never answer randomly: quality checks can close your account.

3. Freecash

What it is: A rewards site focused on game and app offers, with surveys and tasks too.

TasksPlay games to set levels, try apps, surveys
How you're paidCrypto, PayPal or gift cards, with low minimums
KenyaOpen to many countries; check offers available in Kenya
Realistic earningsLow to medium for heavy gamers; offers vary a lot
Best forPeople who already play mobile games

How to start: Pick offers with clear milestones; screenshot your progress in case support needs proof. Pros: Fast, low payouts. Cons: Offers can take weeks; some never credit. Watch out: Don't make in-game purchases to reach a milestone unless you'd buy them anyway.

4. Swagbucks

What it is: One of the best-known rewards sites, mostly for a few wealthy markets.

TasksSurveys, shopping cashback, videos, searches
How you're paidPayPal and gift cards
KenyaMainly the US, UK, Canada, Australia and a few others; usually not open in Kenya
Realistic earningsLow
Best forPeople living in supported countries

How to start: Check the supported country list first. Pros: Big, trusted brand. Cons: Not available in most of Africa. Watch out: Ads claiming Swagbucks “now pays Kenyans via M-Pesa” are not official.

5. PrizeRebel

What it is: A rewards site with surveys and offers and a points system.

TasksSurveys, offers, tasks
How you're paidPayPal, gift cards, some crypto
KenyaOpen to many countries; check Kenya support for your account
Realistic earningsLow
Best forSurvey-focused users

How to start: Complete profile surveys and try a few offers to learn which pay reliably. Pros: Long history. Cons: Low pay; country-dependent supply. Watch out: Same rules: no VPN, one account.

6. JumpTask

What it is: A task and survey app that pays in its own crypto token.

TasksShort tasks, surveys, app tests, social tasks
How you're paidCrypto token, which you can convert on supported exchanges
KenyaOpen in many countries including in Africa
Realistic earningsLow; value changes with the token price
Best forPeople comfortable with crypto wallets

How to start: Learn how to store and convert tokens safely before you start. Pros: Mobile-friendly; many tasks. Cons: Token value can fall; converting has fees. Watch out: Never share your wallet's secret phrase with anyone.

7. Paidwork

What it is: An app combining surveys, small tasks, games and short writing tasks.

TasksSurveys, typing and simple writing, games, offers
How you're paidPayPal and other options by country
KenyaOpen to many countries; check in-app
Realistic earningsLow
Best forPhone-first users

How to start: Install from the official app store; read task rules before starting. Pros: One app for many task types. Cons: Low pay per task. Watch out: Fake look-alike apps exist: check the developer name.

8. Gamehag

What it is: A gaming rewards site paying for game milestones and tasks.

TasksPlay games, complete quests, small tasks
How you're paidGift cards and game items, some cash options
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsLow
Best forGamers

How to start: Use it only for games you'd play anyway. Pros: Fun if you game. Cons: Mostly gift cards and game items. Watch out: Avoid spending real money to complete quests.

9. Cointiply

What it is: A crypto rewards site with offers, surveys, games and a small faucet.

TasksOffers, surveys, PTC (paid-to-click) ads, faucet
How you're paidCrypto (Bitcoin and others)
KenyaOpen to many countries
Realistic earningsVery low
Best forCrypto beginners curious about earning small amounts

How to start: Treat it as learning; withdraw small amounts to a wallet you control. Pros: Long-running. Cons: Very low rates; time-consuming. Watch out: Never deposit money hoping to earn more interest.

10. InboxDollars

What it is: A cash rewards site for surveys, emails and shopping.

TasksSurveys, reading emails, offers
How you're paidCash via PayPal or gift cards
KenyaUS only
Realistic earningsLow
Best forOnly people living in the US

How to start: Not open to Kenya. Pros: Simple. Cons: Country-limited. Watch out: Anyone selling “US accounts” is breaking the rules: you'll lose the earnings.

Summary

  • Rewards sites are easy to join and pay little; Timebucks and ySense are open to Kenyans, while Swagbucks and InboxDollars mostly aren't.
  • Prefer surveys and tasks over offers that need card details.
  • Payouts are mostly PayPal, Payoneer, Skrill, crypto or gift cards: confirm your option before working.
