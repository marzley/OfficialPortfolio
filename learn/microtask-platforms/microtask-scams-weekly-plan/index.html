Task scams to avoid, the best platforms to start with in Kenya, and a realistic weekly plan

You now know 55 real platforms. This lesson shows the scams that copy them, which ones to start with, and how to turn spare hours into steady small income and then into better-paid work.

Task scams that target Kenyans

ScamHow it worksThe giveaway
Deposit-to-unlock “task apps”You earn small amounts liking videos or rating products, then must top up to unlock “premium” tasks or withdrawAny payment to work or to withdraw
Telegram/WhatsApp “like and earn” groupsA “mentor” pays you a few shillings, then moves you to paid “merchant tasks”Recruitment in chats; growing deposits
Account selling and renting“Verified Remotasks/Outlier/MTurk accounts” for sale or rentBreaks platform rules; sellers often vanish
Fake recruiters“Pay KSh 1,500 for training and placement on TELUS/Appen”Real exams and applications are free
Recruitment schemesEarnings depend on signing up friendsIncome from recruiting, not work
Fake look-alike appsCopies of real apps that steal loginsWrong developer name; asks for M-Pesa PIN

If you've been scammed

Stop paying immediately (scammers often ask for a “final fee”). Keep screenshots and M-Pesa messages, report the number to Safaricom by forwarding scam SMS to 333, and report to the DCI. Warn friends. See Avoiding online money scams.

Always confirm current country eligibility on each official site.

A realistic weekly plan (1–2 hours a day)

DayFocus
MondayCheck AI training and testing dashboards for new projects; apply to open ones
TuesdayTranscription or marketplace tasks (Clickworker, GoTranscript)
WednesdayTesting invites and screeners; tidy your tester profiles
ThursdayTranscription or marketplace tasks
FridaySurveys in spare minutes; learn one new skill (Excel, typing speed, testing basics)
WeekendReview earnings, withdraw when you reach minimums, plan next week

Track it: a simple spreadsheet with date, platform, minutes worked and amount earned. After a month, drop the platforms with the lowest pay per hour.

Moving up from microtasks

  1. Typing and English → transcription and captioning.
  2. Testing → QA and software testing careers (see Software testing in the directory).
  3. AI rating → specialist AI training (coding, maths, languages).
  4. Organisation → virtual assistance (see Virtual assistant).
Think about it: You've earned $30 on ySense, $12 on Timebucks and nothing after two weeks on a deposit-based “like videos” app that now asks for KSh 2,000. What should you do?Show answer

Withdraw your ySense and Timebucks earnings once you hit the minimums, and walk away from the deposit app without paying anything: it's a scam pattern. Put the time into one platform where skills raise pay, like transcription or testing.

Summary

  • Deposit-to-unlock apps, “like and earn” groups, account sales and fee-charging recruiters are scams.
  • Start with Timebucks, ySense, Attapoll, Clickworker, GoTranscript, GeoPoll and testing platforms; apply to AI training projects.
  • Work a weekly plan, track pay per hour, and move up to skilled work.
