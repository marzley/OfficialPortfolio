Health support and safety services

Non-clinical services that support hospitals, clinics and safety, adding to health businesses (methods 544–556).

What these methods share

  • Regulation: medical waste and first aid training are regulated.
  • Hygiene: infection control is essential.
  • Reliability: health facilities depend on you.

978. Medical courier

What it is: Transporting lab samples, medicines and documents between clinics, labs and pharmacies. How it works: Scheduled pickups → safe transport → delivery.

SkillsReliability, handling procedures
Cost & equipmentMotorbike or car, cool boxes
Where to find workClinics, labs, pharmacies
PaymentPer trip or contract
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn sample-handling rules; partner with labs. Pros: Steady contracts. Cons & risks: Strict handling rules. Scam warning: Keep patient information confidential. Kenya notes: Labs collect samples from many clinics daily.

979. Hospital laundry services

What it is: Washing, disinfecting and pressing hospital linen. How it works: Collect → wash with infection control → return.

SkillsLaundry, infection control
Cost & equipmentIndustrial machines, transport
Where to find workHospitals, clinics, nursing homes, hotels
PaymentPer kilo or contract
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Meet infection control standards; start with clinics. Pros: Contracts are steady. Cons & risks: High equipment costs. Scam warning: Separate infectious linen properly. Kenya notes: Smaller hospitals often outsource laundry.

980. Medical waste management (licensed)

What it is: Collecting and safely disposing of medical waste. How it works: Licensed collection → transport → treatment or incineration.

SkillsWaste management, safety
Cost & equipmentNEMA licence, vehicles, treatment partner
Where to find workHospitals, clinics, labs, pharmacies
PaymentPer kilo or contract
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Get NEMA licences and a treatment partner. Pros: Essential service. Cons & risks: Heavy regulation. Scam warning: Never dump medical waste. Kenya notes: Medical waste needs licensed handlers in Kenya.

981. First aid trainer

What it is: Training workplaces, schools and drivers in first aid and CPR. How it works: Workshops → practical training → certificates.

SkillsFirst aid qualifications, teaching
Cost & equipmentTraining manikins, kits, approval
Where to find workWorkplaces, schools, matatu saccos, churches
PaymentPer participant
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Get first aid instructor certification and approval to train. Pros: Required for many workplaces. Cons & risks: Must keep certification current. Scam warning: Only issue certificates you're approved to issue. Kenya notes: Workplace safety law requires trained first aiders.

982. Lifeguard

What it is: Supervising swimmers at pools, hotels and beaches. How it works: Shifts → supervise → rescue and first aid when needed.

SkillsStrong swimming, rescue, first aid
Cost & equipmentRecognised lifeguard certification
Where to find workHotels, clubs, schools, public pools
PaymentMonthly or per shift
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Get certified in lifeguarding and first aid. Pros: Clear job path. Cons & risks: Responsibility for lives. Scam warning: Stay alert: never leave the pool unattended. Kenya notes: Hotels and clubs at the coast and in towns employ lifeguards.

Summary

  • Health support services include medical couriers, hospital laundry, licensed medical waste, first aid training and lifeguarding.
  • Regulation and infection control apply.
  • Confidentiality and reliability are essential.
