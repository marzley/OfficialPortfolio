More specialist online and technical niches

These are focused skills that companies hire for remotely or locally, adding to the specialist online work in methods 664–681.

What these methods share

  • Portfolio: show real examples of your work.
  • Tools: learn the industry-standard tools for each niche.
  • Clients: LinkedIn and freelance platforms reach them.

885. App store optimisation (ASO) specialist

What it is: Improving app listings (titles, keywords, screenshots, reviews) so more people find and install them. How it works: Audit listing → keyword research → improve text and images → track installs.

SkillsMarketing, keyword research, design sense
Cost & equipmentLow: laptop, ASO tools (free tiers)
Where to find workApp developers, startups, agencies
PaymentPer project or monthly
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Practise on a friend's app; publish before-and-after results. Pros: Niche with fewer competitors. Cons & risks: Results depend on the app's quality. Scam warning: Never buy fake reviews or installs: stores ban apps for it. Kenya notes: Kenyan fintech, agri and edtech apps need better store visibility.

886. Pinterest marketing manager

What it is: Running Pinterest accounts that drive traffic to blogs and online shops. How it works: Create pins → schedule → optimise boards → track clicks.

SkillsDesign (Canva), keyword research
Cost & equipmentLow: laptop, scheduling tool
Where to find workBloggers, Etsy sellers, e-commerce brands abroad
PaymentMonthly retainer
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Grow your own Pinterest account in one niche as proof. Pros: Underserved skill. Cons & risks: Mostly overseas clients. Scam warning: Avoid clients selling dubious products. Kenya notes: Works fully remotely from Kenya.

887. UX writer

What it is: Writing the words inside apps and websites: buttons, messages, onboarding and errors. How it works: Understand users → write clear microcopy → test and improve.

SkillsClear writing, user empathy
Cost & equipmentLow: laptop, design tools (Figma basics)
Where to find workTech companies, startups, design agencies
PaymentPer project or salary
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Rewrite confusing screens from real apps as portfolio pieces. Pros: Growing tech role. Cons & risks: Needs a portfolio to break in. Scam warning: Ignore “pay to join” UX bootcamps that promise jobs. Kenya notes: Kiswahili UX writing is valuable for apps serving East Africa.

888. GIS mapping and analysis

What it is: Creating maps and analysing location data for planning, farming, health and conservation. How it works: Collect data → analyse in GIS software (e.g. QGIS) → produce maps and reports.

SkillsGIS software, data analysis
Cost & equipmentLaptop; QGIS is free
Where to find workNGOs, counties, researchers, surveyors, utilities
PaymentPer project or contract
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Learn QGIS with free courses; build sample maps of your county. Pros: Valuable, technical skill. Cons & risks: Data access can be hard. Scam warning: Respect data licences and privacy. Kenya notes: Counties, NGOs and conservation groups use GIS heavily.

889. CAD drafting for manufacturing and engineering

What it is: Drawing parts, machines and fabrication plans in CAD software. How it works: Brief → 2D/3D drawings → revisions → final files.

SkillsCAD software (AutoCAD, FreeCAD, Fusion)
Cost & equipmentLaptop, software
Where to find workFabricators, engineers, manufacturers, 3D printing businesses
PaymentPer drawing or project
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Learn a CAD tool; draw real parts from local workshops. Pros: Works locally and remotely. Cons & risks: Needs technical accuracy. Scam warning: Use licensed software for commercial work. Kenya notes: Different from architectural drafting (method 354).

890. Online exam proctor

What it is: Supervising online exams through video to make sure candidates follow the rules. How it works: Join a proctoring company → monitor sessions → report incidents.

SkillsAttention, reliability, good internet
Cost & equipmentLaptop, stable internet, quiet space
Where to find workProctoring companies, universities, certification bodies
PaymentPer hour or session
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Apply to proctoring companies and training institutions. Pros: Remote work. Cons & risks: Shift hours across time zones. Scam warning: Real employers don't charge for “proctor training kits”. Kenya notes: Online exams for certifications and universities need proctors.

Summary

  • Specialist niches include ASO, Pinterest marketing, UX writing, GIS mapping, CAD drafting and online exam proctoring.
  • Build a portfolio with real or practice examples.
  • Never buy fake reviews or pay for job promises.
