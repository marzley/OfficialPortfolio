Personal and niche services

Small but real businesses that meet specific personal needs, completing the directory's first thousand methods.

What these methods share

  • Low start-up: many need little capital.
  • Location and reputation: word of mouth drives most of them.
  • Legality: follow county permits and consumer rules.

991. Shoe shining stand

What it is: Cleaning and polishing shoes for commuters and office workers. How it works: Set up at a busy spot → shine shoes → sell polish and laces.

SkillsShoe care
Cost & equipmentPolishes, brushes, stand, county permit
Where to find workOffice districts, bus stations, malls
PaymentPer pair
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Choose a busy, legal spot near offices. Pros: Daily cash. Cons & risks: Low price per job. Scam warning: Get a county permit for your spot. Kenya notes: City centres have steady demand from office workers.

992. Photo restoration

What it is: Restoring old, damaged family photos digitally and printing them. How it works: Scan → repair digitally → print or share files.

SkillsPhoto editing
Cost & equipmentScanner, editing software, printing access
Where to find workFamilies, funerals, anniversaries, diaspora
PaymentPer photo
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Show before-and-after examples on social media. Pros: Emotional value. Cons & risks: Time-consuming. Scam warning: Return originals carefully. Kenya notes: Funerals and anniversaries often need restored photos.

993. Video and media digitisation

What it is: Converting VHS tapes, DVDs and old audio cassettes to digital files. How it works: Collect media → convert → deliver on flash disk or cloud.

SkillsTechnical setup
Cost & equipmentPlayers, capture devices, computer
Where to find workFamilies, churches, schools
PaymentPer tape or hour
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Collect players secondhand; promote to churches and families. Pros: Few competitors. Cons & risks: Old equipment breaks. Scam warning: Handle originals with care. Kenya notes: Many families have wedding videos on old tapes.

994. Sharpening services

What it is: Sharpening knives, pangas, scissors, lawnmower blades and tools. How it works: Mobile or fixed stall → sharpen → collect fee.

SkillsSharpening technique
Cost & equipmentGrinder or whetstones, bicycle setup for mobile
Where to find workButcheries, salons, households, farms
PaymentPer item
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Go mobile around estates and markets. Pros: Very low cost. Cons & risks: Low price per item. Scam warning: Work safely with grinders. Kenya notes: Butcheries and salons need regular sharpening.

995. Mobile salon (home visits)

What it is: Providing hair, nails and grooming at clients' homes or offices. How it works: Bookings → travel with kit → serve → payment.

SkillsBeauty skills
Cost & equipmentPortable kit, transport
Where to find workBusy professionals, brides, older clients
PaymentPer service plus transport
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Build a booking page; get reviews. Pros: Premium for convenience. Cons & risks: Travel time. Scam warning: Use hygienic, sterilised tools. Kenya notes: Different from owning a salon (method 465).

996. Perfume oils and decants

What it is: Selling perfume oils and small decanted samples of fragrances. How it works: Buy genuine oils or full bottles → decant → sell.

SkillsFragrance knowledge, sales
Cost & equipmentOils, bottles, labels
Where to find workOnline buyers, markets, gift buyers
PaymentPer bottle
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start online with a few popular scents. Pros: Low start-up. Cons & risks: Counterfeits flood the market. Scam warning: Never sell fakes as branded perfumes. Kenya notes: Perfume oils are popular, especially at the coast.

997. Ethical dog breeding (registered)

What it is: Breeding healthy dogs responsibly and selling puppies to good homes. How it works: Health-tested parents → raise puppies → vaccinate → sell with records.

SkillsAnimal care
Cost & equipmentKennels, vet care, registration
Where to find workFamilies, security firms
PaymentPer puppy
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Register with the Kenya Kennel Club; work with a vet. Pros: Good prices for healthy dogs. Cons & risks: Animal welfare responsibility. Scam warning: Avoid overbreeding; never sell sick puppies. Kenya notes: Differs from dog training and kennels (method 596).

998. Film location scout and fixer

What it is: Helping film crews and journalists find locations, permits, transport and local contacts. How it works: Brief → scout → arrange permits and logistics → support on set.

SkillsLocal knowledge, organisation
Cost & equipmentPhone, transport
Where to find workFilm crews, documentary makers, journalists
PaymentPer day
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Network with production companies and the Kenya Film Commission. Pros: Interesting work. Cons & risks: Irregular projects. Scam warning: Ensure filming licences are obtained properly. Kenya notes: Kenya is a popular location for documentaries and adverts.

999. Sports equipment shop and repair

What it is: Selling and repairing balls, rackets, nets, boots and gym gear. How it works: Stock → sell → repair and restring.

SkillsProduct knowledge, repairs
Cost & equipmentStock, repair tools
Where to find workSchools, clubs, gyms, players
PaymentSales and repair fees
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Partner with schools and clubs for bulk orders. Pros: School and club demand. Cons & risks: Seasonal. Scam warning: Avoid counterfeit branded gear. Kenya notes: Schools buy sports equipment every year.

1000. Florist shop

What it is: Arranging and selling flowers for gifts, events, offices and funerals. How it works: Buy from farms or markets → arrange → deliver.

SkillsFloral design
Cost & equipmentStock, buckets, delivery
Where to find workGift buyers, events, offices, funerals
PaymentPer bouquet or contract
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start online with delivery; add office flower contracts. Pros: Peak days (Valentine's, Mother's Day). Cons & risks: Perishable stock. Scam warning: Price clearly for delivery. Kenya notes: Kenya grows world-class flowers; local florists add value.

Summary

  • Niche services include shoe shining, photo restoration, media digitisation, sharpening, mobile salons, perfume oils, ethical dog breeding, film fixing, sports equipment and floristry.
  • Low start-up, location and reputation drive them.
  • Avoid counterfeits and treat animals well.
