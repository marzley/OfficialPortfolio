Creative and performance niches

These creative methods aren't covered in the earlier design, crafts and entertainment categories. They range from calligraphy and props making to choir direction and photo booths, and many find customers through social media and events.

What these methods share

  • Portfolio content on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.
  • Event-driven demand: weddings, parties, corporate functions, churches and schools.
  • Contracts and deposits for bookings.

692. Calligraphy and hand lettering

What it is: Beautiful hand lettering for invitations, signage, certificates and art. How it works: Commissions and workshops.

SkillsCalligraphy practice
Cost & equipmentPens, inks, paper
Where to find workWeddings, events, gift shops, social media
PaymentPer piece or workshop
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Post practice videos; offer name cards for events. Pros: Unique craft. Cons & risks: Niche market. Scam warning: As usual. Kenya notes: Wedding signage and personalised gifts are popular.
