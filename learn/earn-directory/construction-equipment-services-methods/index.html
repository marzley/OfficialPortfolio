Construction equipment and specialist building services

Construction keeps growing in Kenyan towns. Beyond contractors and fundis (methods 347–352), builders need equipment, specialist trades and inspections.

What these methods share

  • Safety: sites need protective gear and safe equipment.
  • Contracts: written terms and deposits protect you.
  • Standards: follow National Construction Authority (NCA) rules where they apply.

876. Scaffolding hire

What it is: Renting and erecting scaffolding for building and maintenance work. How it works: Deliver → erect safely → dismantle → collect.

SkillsScaffolding safety
Cost & equipmentScaffold stock, transport
Where to find workContractors, painters, facility managers
PaymentPer day or week
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Start with a small stock; train crews in safe erection. Pros: Repeat contractor clients. Cons & risks: Theft and damage. Scam warning: Inspect before every job: unsafe scaffolds kill. Kenya notes: Multi-storey building in towns keeps demand high.

877. Formwork and timber hire

What it is: Renting formwork, props and timber for concrete work. How it works: Supply → collect after casting.

SkillsConstruction knowledge
Cost & equipmentFormwork stock, transport
Where to find workContractors, self-builders
PaymentPer day or project
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Partner with contractors; keep stock in good condition. Pros: Reusable assets. Cons & risks: Wear and loss. Scam warning: Record items out and in. Kenya notes: Common on storey buildings.

878. Steel fixing (rebar work)

What it is: Cutting, bending and tying reinforcement steel for concrete structures. How it works: Read drawings → cut and bend → fix in place.

SkillsReading structural drawings, steel fixing
Cost & equipmentBending tools
Where to find workContractors, building sites
PaymentDaily rate or per tonne
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Train on site with experienced steel fixers. Pros: Skilled, in-demand trade. Cons & risks: Physically demanding. Scam warning: Follow engineers' drawings exactly. Kenya notes: Every storey building needs steel fixers.

879. Construction equipment hire

What it is: Renting concrete mixers, compactors, vibrators and excavators. How it works: Book → deliver with or without operator → collect.

SkillsEquipment maintenance, logistics
Cost & equipmentHigh: machines, transport
Where to find workContractors, self-builders, counties
PaymentPer day
Realistic earningsMedium to high
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Start with mixers and compactors; add bigger plant later. Pros: High demand. Cons & risks: Maintenance and breakdowns. Scam warning: Use contracts covering damage. Kenya notes: Different from quarry equipment hire (method 730): this serves building sites.

880. Ready-mix concrete and pumping

What it is: Supplying mixed concrete and pumping it into slabs and columns. How it works: Batch → deliver → pump.

SkillsConcrete technology, logistics
Cost & equipmentVery high: batching plant, trucks, pumps
Where to find workContractors, developers
PaymentPer cubic metre
Realistic earningsHigh
Time to first incomeYears

How to start: Usually starts as a pump-hire service before batching. Pros: Large contracts. Cons & risks: Very capital intensive. Scam warning: Concrete must meet specified strengths: test cubes. Kenya notes: Large projects in towns prefer ready-mix.

881. Building inspection and snag lists

What it is: Inspecting new or renovated buildings and listing defects before handover or purchase. How it works: Inspect → photograph → report defects.

SkillsConstruction knowledge, report writing
Cost & equipmentLow: tools, camera
Where to find workHome buyers, landlords, diaspora
PaymentPer inspection
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Use a checklist; partner with property agents. Pros: Protects buyers. Cons & risks: Liability for missed defects. Scam warning: Disclose any links to the builder. Kenya notes: Buyers of off-plan apartments increasingly hire inspectors.

882. Demolition and salvage

What it is: Safely demolishing structures and selling salvaged materials. How it works: Survey → permits → demolish → salvage and clear.

SkillsDemolition safety
Cost & equipmentTools, transport, permits
Where to find workDevelopers, homeowners, counties
PaymentPer job plus salvage sales
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Learn safe methods; get county permits. Pros: Salvage adds income. Cons & risks: Dangerous; dust and asbestos risks. Scam warning: Old roofs may contain asbestos: use licensed handlers. Kenya notes: Redevelopment in older estates creates demolition work.

883. Prefab and container houses

What it is: Building offices, homes, kiosks and classrooms from containers or prefabricated panels. How it works: Design → fabricate → deliver → install.

SkillsFabrication, design
Cost & equipmentWorkshop, materials
Where to find workSite offices, schools, rental owners, kiosks
PaymentPer unit
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Start with container offices and kiosks. Pros: Fast to build. Cons & risks: Heat control and approvals. Scam warning: Check county approvals for permanent use. Kenya notes: Popular for site offices and quick classrooms.

884. Swimming pool construction

What it is: Building and finishing swimming pools for homes, hotels and schools. How it works: Design → excavate → build → plumbing and finishing.

SkillsConstruction, waterproofing, pool plumbing
Cost & equipmentTools, specialist materials
Where to find workHotels, villas, schools
PaymentPer project
Realistic earningsHigh
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Partner with pool maintenance businesses (method 763). Pros: Big-ticket projects. Cons & risks: Technical risks. Scam warning: Get staged payments in writing. Kenya notes: Hotels and villas at the coast and in towns build pools.

Summary

  • Construction services include scaffolding, formwork, steel fixing, equipment hire, ready-mix concrete, inspections, demolition, prefab units and pools.
  • Safety, NCA standards and written contracts protect you.
  • Asbestos and unsafe scaffolding are serious dangers.
