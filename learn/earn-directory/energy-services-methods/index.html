Energy services beyond solar panels

Power cuts, high bills and off-grid homes create business for energy services. These build on solar sales and installation (methods 381–383) and energy audits (392).

What these methods share

  • Licences: electrical work needs EPRA licensing; mini-grids need EPRA permits.
  • Safety: batteries, gas and generators need careful handling.
  • After-sales: service and spare parts keep customers loyal.

853. Mini-grid operator

What it is: Running a small solar or hybrid power network supplying a village, market or island. How it works: Build or partner on a mini-grid → connect customers → collect payments (often prepaid via M-Pesa).

SkillsElectrical engineering, business management
Cost & equipmentVery high (often with investors or donors); EPRA permit
Where to find workOff-grid villages, islands, trading centres
PaymentMonthly or prepaid power sales
Realistic earningsMedium to high
Time to first incomeYears

How to start: Work for a mini-grid company first; partner with financiers and county governments. Pros: Long-term revenue. Cons & risks: High capital and regulation. Scam warning: Be transparent about tariffs with customers. Kenya notes: Kenya has many mini-grids in off-grid counties and lake islands.

854. E-cooking appliance sales and training

What it is: Selling electric pressure cookers and induction cookers and training households to cook efficiently. How it works: Demonstrate → sell (cash or instalments) → follow-up support.

SkillsSales, cooking demonstrations
Cost & equipmentAppliance stock, demo kit
Where to find workHouseholds, estates, chamas, offices
PaymentPer sale or commission
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Run cooking demos at chamas and churches; offer instalments through partners. Pros: Saves cooking costs. Cons & risks: Depends on reliable electricity. Scam warning: Sell only certified appliances. Kenya notes: Electric pressure cookers are promoted in Kenya as cheaper than charcoal for many dishes.

855. Phone charging kiosks and power-bank rental

What it is: Charging phones or renting power banks in places without reliable power. How it works: Solar or mains charging station → charge per phone or rent power banks.

SkillsBasic electrical skills, customer service
Cost & equipmentCharging station, power banks, solar panel
Where to find workMarkets, rural centres, events, bus stages
PaymentPer charge or rental
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Set up at a busy market or stage; use deposits for power-bank rentals. Pros: Simple and quick to start. Cons & risks: Low price per charge. Scam warning: Use quality chargers to avoid damaging phones. Kenya notes: Rural markets without power still need charging.

856. Generator hire

What it is: Renting generators to events, construction sites and businesses during outages. How it works: Book → deliver → fuel arrangement → collect.

SkillsGenerator operation and maintenance
Cost & equipmentGenerators, transport
Where to find workEvents, construction, shops, funerals
PaymentPer day
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start with one or two well-maintained generators; partner with event planners. Pros: Steady demand. Cons & risks: Maintenance and theft risks. Scam warning: Get deposits and written agreements. Kenya notes: Pairs with generator repair (method 530).

857. LED lighting retrofits

What it is: Replacing old lighting in offices, factories and schools with efficient LEDs to cut bills. How it works: Audit → quote savings → install → measure.

SkillsElectrical installation, energy calculations
Cost & equipmentTools, licensed electrician status
Where to find workFactories, hotels, schools, malls
PaymentPer project
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Partner with energy auditors; show clients the payback period. Pros: Clear savings to sell. Cons & risks: Upfront costs for clients. Scam warning: Use certified LED products; fakes fail fast. Kenya notes: High electricity tariffs make savings attractive.

858. Biogas digester installation

What it is: Building digesters that turn animal manure into cooking gas and fertiliser. How it works: Assess animals and site → build digester → connect stove → train.

SkillsConstruction, biogas technology
Cost & equipmentMaterials, tools, training
Where to find workDairy farmers, schools, institutions
PaymentPer installation
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Train with a biogas programme; target farmers with zero-grazing cows. Pros: Free cooking gas from waste. Cons & risks: Needs enough manure and maintenance. Scam warning: Follow safety design to prevent leaks. Kenya notes: Dairy regions like Central Kenya and the Rift Valley suit biogas.

859. Solar cold rooms for farmers

What it is: Offering cooling storage at markets so farmers and traders keep produce fresh longer. How it works: Install a solar cold room → charge per crate per day.

SkillsBusiness management, refrigeration basics
Cost & equipmentCold room unit (often leased), site
Where to find workFresh produce markets, fishing beaches
PaymentPer crate per day
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Partner with cold room providers and market committees. Pros: Reduces post-harvest losses. Cons & risks: High equipment cost. Scam warning: Agree storage terms and liability in writing. Kenya notes: Fresh produce losses are high in Kenyan markets without cooling.

860. Electric motorbike battery swap station

What it is: Running a station where electric boda riders swap flat batteries for charged ones. How it works: Partner with an e-mobility company → host batteries → earn per swap.

SkillsCustomer service, basic electrical safety
Cost & equipmentSite, power connection, partner equipment
Where to find workBoda boda riders, delivery riders
PaymentCommission per swap
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Partner with an electric motorbike company expanding in your area. Pros: Growing sector. Cons & risks: Depends on partner's growth. Scam warning: Only partner with established companies. Kenya notes: Electric motorbikes are growing fast in Nairobi and other towns.

Summary

  • Energy services include mini-grids, e-cooking, charging kiosks, generator hire, LED retrofits, biogas, solar cold rooms and battery swap stations.
  • EPRA licences apply to electrical work and mini-grids.
  • Safety and after-sales service matter.
