Heritage crafts and art

Kenya's craft traditions sell to tourists, collectors and exporters. These add to crafts and making (methods 265–280).

What these methods share

  • Authenticity: tell the story behind your craft.
  • Sustainable materials: use legal, sustainably sourced wood and stone.
  • Markets: tourism, export, online and corporate gifts.

951. Soapstone carving

What it is: Carving sculptures, bowls and chess sets from soapstone. How it works: Source stone → carve → polish → sell.

SkillsCarving
Cost & equipmentTools, stone
Where to find workTourists, curio shops, exporters, online
PaymentPer piece
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn from carvers in Kisii; sell online and to curio shops. Pros: Distinct Kenyan craft. Cons & risks: Dust; tourist seasonality. Scam warning: Wear masks against dust. Kenya notes: Kisii soapstone is known worldwide.

952. Wood carving

What it is: Carving animals, masks, utensils and décor from wood. How it works: Source legal timber → carve → finish.

SkillsCarving
Cost & equipmentTools, timber
Where to find workTourists, hotels, exporters
PaymentPer piece
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Use sustainable woods; sell to curio markets. Pros: Strong tourist demand. Cons & risks: Timber supply rules. Scam warning: Use legally sourced timber; avoid protected species. Kenya notes: Akamba carving traditions are famous.

953. Musical instrument making and repair

What it is: Making and repairing guitars, drums, nyatiti, keyboards and wind instruments. How it works: Craft or repair → sell or return.

SkillsWoodwork, instrument knowledge
Cost & equipmentTools, materials
Where to find workChurches, schools, musicians
PaymentPer instrument or repair
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Start with repairs for churches and schools. Pros: Steady church and school demand. Cons & risks: Specialist skills. Scam warning: Be honest about instrument quality. Kenya notes: Churches and schools buy and repair instruments regularly.

954. Metal art and sculpture

What it is: Creating sculptures and décor from scrap metal. How it works: Collect scrap → design → weld → finish.

SkillsWelding, design
Cost & equipmentWelding equipment, scrap
Where to find workHotels, homes, galleries, tourists
PaymentPer piece
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Make signature pieces; display at hotels and galleries. Pros: Uses recycled materials. Cons & risks: Heavy items to transport. Scam warning: Buy scrap from licensed dealers only. Kenya notes: Recycled metal art is popular with hotels and tourists.

Summary

  • Heritage crafts include soapstone carving, wood carving, instrument making and metal art.
  • Use legal, sustainable materials and tell the story behind each piece.
  • Sell to tourists, galleries, hotels and online.
