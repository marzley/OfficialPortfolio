Events, hospitality, health and wellness services

Weddings, birthdays, conferences, fitness and wellbeing are all growing markets in Kenya, and social media is the main way these services find customers. These methods mix skills, people and online marketing.

What these methods share

  • Reputation and reviews decide bookings.
  • Contracts and deposits protect you from cancellations.
  • Health services must stay within your qualifications and licences.

313. Event planning

What it is: Planning weddings, parties, corporate events and conferences. How it works: Consultation → budget → vendors → coordination on the day.

SkillsOrganisation, negotiation, calm under pressure
Cost & equipmentPhone, laptop, vendor network
Where to find workCouples, families, companies, social media
PaymentPlanning fee or percentage
Realistic earningsLow to medium–high
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Assist established planners; plan events for friends. Pros: Creative, rewarding. Cons & risks: Stress; vendor failures. Scam warning: Vendors who take deposits and disappear; vet them. Kenya notes: Weddings, ruracio, graduations and corporate events drive demand.

How to start: Record hosting clips; build reviews. Pros: Good pay per event. Cons & risks: Weekend work. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Bilingual MCs (English/Kiswahili/local languages) are in demand.

Summary

  • Use contracts and deposits; stay within qualifications for health services; follow licensing rules.
