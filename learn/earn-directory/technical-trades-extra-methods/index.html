More technical trades and installations

Skilled trades with fewer competitors, adding to repairs (526–543) and installations (782–805).

What these methods share

  • Certification: many of these need inspection approvals or training.
  • Safety: heights, machinery and pressure systems are dangerous.
  • Maintenance contracts: recurring work keeps income steady.

983. Lift (elevator) maintenance technician

What it is: Maintaining and repairing lifts in buildings. How it works: Scheduled maintenance → repairs → safety checks.

SkillsElectrical and mechanical skills
Cost & equipmentTraining, tools; work with an approved company
Where to find workOffice blocks, apartments, hospitals, malls
PaymentMonthly salary or contracts
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Join a lift company as a trainee. Pros: Growing with tall buildings. Cons & risks: Strict safety rules. Scam warning: Lifts must be inspected by approved persons; never skip checks. Kenya notes: Taller buildings in towns need more lift technicians.

984. Boiler operator and maintenance

What it is: Running and maintaining boilers in factories, hospitals and hotels. How it works: Operate shifts → maintain → keep logs → arrange statutory inspections.

SkillsBoiler operation, safety
Cost & equipmentCertification
Where to find workFactories, hospitals, hotels, laundries
PaymentMonthly salary
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Train and certify as a boiler operator. Pros: Steady industrial jobs. Cons & risks: Pressure system dangers. Scam warning: Follow statutory inspection rules. Kenya notes: DOSH oversees inspection of boilers and pressure vessels.

985. Weighing scale calibration and repair

What it is: Repairing and calibrating scales for shops, farms, factories and markets. How it works: Repair → calibrate → certify through proper channels.

SkillsElectronics, metrology basics
Cost & equipmentTest weights, tools
Where to find workShops, butcheries, factories, cereal buyers
PaymentPer service
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Learn calibration; understand Weights and Measures verification rules. Pros: Every trader needs accurate scales. Cons & risks: Regulated work. Scam warning: Never adjust scales to cheat customers. Kenya notes: Scales used in trade must be verified under weights and measures law.

986. Mosquito net and window screen fitting

What it is: Making and fitting mosquito screens for windows and doors, and fitting nets. How it works: Measure → make frames or fittings → install.

SkillsFabrication, measuring
Cost & equipmentTools, mesh, aluminium frames
Where to find workHomes, hotels, schools, hospitals
PaymentPer window
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Start in malaria-prone areas; show samples. Pros: Health benefit. Cons & risks: Competition from DIY. Scam warning: Use durable mesh. Kenya notes: Coast and lake regions have high mosquito pressure.

987. Gabions and soil erosion control

What it is: Building gabion walls, terraces and erosion control structures. How it works: Survey → install gabions or terraces → plant cover.

SkillsConstruction, soil conservation
Cost & equipmentGabion baskets, stones, tools
Where to find workFarms, counties, road contractors, riverside properties
PaymentPer project
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn from county soil conservation officers. Pros: Protects land. Cons & risks: Heavy work. Scam warning: Follow engineering advice for big walls. Kenya notes: Hilly and riverside areas need erosion control.

988. Tree felling and arborist services

What it is: Pruning, felling and removing trees safely near homes and power lines. How it works: Assess → permits → cut safely → clear.

SkillsChainsaw skills, climbing, safety
Cost & equipmentChainsaw, ropes, safety gear
Where to find workHomes, estates, schools, counties
PaymentPer tree or job
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Train in chainsaw safety; get required felling permits. Pros: High-value jobs. Cons & risks: Dangerous work. Scam warning: Get permits: felling certain trees without approval is illegal. Kenya notes: County governments and KFS regulate tree felling.

989. Fish pond construction

What it is: Digging and lining fish ponds and installing water systems. How it works: Site survey → dig → line → fill → stock advice.

SkillsConstruction, aquaculture basics
Cost & equipmentTools, liners, labour
Where to find workFish farmers, schools, institutions
PaymentPer pond
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn pond design from fisheries officers. Pros: Supports fish farming. Cons & risks: Seasonal. Scam warning: Use quality liners; leaks kill businesses. Kenya notes: Fish farming programmes create pond demand.

990. Water bottling plant

What it is: Treating and bottling drinking water for sale. How it works: Treat water → bottle → label → distribute.

SkillsWater treatment, quality control
Cost & equipmentHigh: treatment equipment, bottling, KEBS certification
Where to find workShops, offices, events, schools
PaymentPer bottle
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Get KEBS certification and county approvals before selling. Pros: Daily consumption. Cons & risks: Strict quality standards. Scam warning: Never sell uncertified water. Kenya notes: Differs from a refill station (method 455): you bottle and distribute.

Summary

  • Technical trades include lift maintenance, boilers, scale calibration, mosquito screens, gabions, arborist work, fish ponds and water bottling.
  • Certification, permits and statutory inspections apply.
  • Safety and honesty come first.
