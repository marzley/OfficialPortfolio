Remote professional jobs and specialist freelancing

Professionals with qualifications or experience (HR, law, accounting, project management, research, health admin) can increasingly work remotely for local and international employers or as freelance consultants. These roles usually pay more than entry-level online work, but require real expertise.

What these methods share

  • Credentials matter: degrees, professional certifications and experience.
  • Professional rules: lawyers, accountants and health professionals must follow their regulatory bodies' rules, even when freelancing.
  • Remote job safety: verify employers; legitimate employers don't charge application fees (remote jobs).

395. Remote HR support and recruitment

What it is: Supporting hiring, onboarding, HR policies and employee records remotely. How it works: Freelance or remote roles with SMEs and agencies.

SkillsHR training (e.g. CHRP), employment law knowledge
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workSMEs, recruitment agencies, international remote roles
PaymentHourly/project/salary
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks to months

How to start: Offer HR policy templates and recruitment support to SMEs. Pros: Steady demand. Cons & risks: Legal compliance responsibility. Scam warning: Never charge job seekers for "placement". Kenya notes: Follow Kenyan employment law and data protection rules.

396. Freelance recruiter / headhunter

What it is: Finding candidates for companies and earning placement fees (paid by employers). How it works: Job briefs → sourcing → screening → placement.

SkillsNetworking, interviewing, LinkedIn
Cost & equipmentLaptop, LinkedIn tools
Where to find workCompanies, recruitment agencies
PaymentPlacement fees from employers
Realistic earningsMedium to high (per placement)
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Specialise in an industry you know. Pros: Big fees per placement. Cons & risks: Irregular. Scam warning: Ethical recruiters never charge candidates. Kenya notes: Recruitment for jobs abroad requires proper licensing.
