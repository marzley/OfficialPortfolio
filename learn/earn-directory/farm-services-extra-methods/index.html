More farm and livestock services

Farmers need services as much as inputs. These businesses support livestock and crop farmers, adding to methods 281–295 and 592–595.

What these methods share

  • Animal welfare: treat animals humanely and follow veterinary rules.
  • Licences: county and veterinary permits apply to livestock movement and slaughter.
  • Trust: honest weights and prices keep farmers coming back.

903. Livestock trading

What it is: Buying cattle, goats and sheep from farmers and selling at markets or to butcheries. How it works: Buy at fair prices → move with permits → sell.

SkillsAnimal assessment, negotiation
Cost & equipmentCapital, movement permits
Where to find workLivestock markets, butcheries, abattoirs
PaymentMargin per animal
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn from experienced traders; build buyer relationships. Pros: Fast turnover. Cons & risks: Price risk; disease. Scam warning: Get movement permits; stolen livestock is a serious crime. Kenya notes: Livestock markets in pastoral counties supply towns.

904. Milk collection and cooling centre

What it is: Collecting milk from smallholders, cooling it and selling to processors. How it works: Collect → test → cool → sell in bulk.

SkillsMilk testing, management
Cost & equipmentCooler, testing kit, Kenya Dairy Board licence
Where to find workDairy farmers, processors
PaymentMargin per litre
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Often run by cooperatives; partner with farmer groups. Pros: Steady daily volumes. Cons & risks: Equipment and power costs. Scam warning: Test for adulteration and pay honestly. Kenya notes: Dairy cooperatives play a big role in Kenya's milk supply.

905. Farm labour contractor

What it is: Organising groups of workers for planting, weeding, harvesting and packing. How it works: Farms request → you supply teams → manage and pay workers.

SkillsOrganisation, fairness
Cost & equipmentLow: phone, transport
Where to find workLarge farms, flower farms, greenhouses
PaymentPer worker per day (margin)
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Build a reliable team; agree clear terms with farms. Pros: Steady seasonal demand. Cons & risks: Disputes over pay. Scam warning: Pay workers fully and on time; follow labour laws and minimum wages. Kenya notes: Harvest seasons create big demand for labour teams.

906. Chick hatchery

What it is: Hatching eggs in incubators and selling day-old chicks. How it works: Source fertile eggs → incubate → sell chicks.

SkillsIncubation, biosecurity
Cost & equipmentIncubators, power backup, egg supply
Where to find workPoultry farmers
PaymentPer chick
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Start with a small incubator and reliable power backup. Pros: Constant demand. Cons & risks: Power outages ruin batches. Scam warning: Sell vaccinated, healthy chicks; follow veterinary guidance. Kenya notes: Improved kienyeji chicks are especially popular.

907. Bee pollination services

What it is: Renting beehives to farmers so bees pollinate their crops. How it works: Place hives during flowering → collect after.

SkillsBeekeeping, logistics
Cost & equipmentHives, transport, protective gear
Where to find workAvocado, macadamia, horticulture farms
PaymentPer hive per season
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Partner with farms growing pollination-dependent crops. Pros: Extra income from hives. Cons & risks: Pesticide spraying can kill bees. Scam warning: Agree no-spray periods in writing. Kenya notes: Avocado and macadamia farmers benefit from pollination.

908. Livestock transport

What it is: Moving animals between farms, markets and abattoirs. How it works: Book → load humanely → deliver with permits.

SkillsDriving, animal handling
Cost & equipmentSuitable truck, permits
Where to find workTraders, farmers, abattoirs
PaymentPer trip
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Fit the truck properly for animal welfare. Pros: Regular demand. Cons & risks: Permits and welfare rules. Scam warning: Carry valid movement permits. Kenya notes: Livestock moves from pastoral counties to town markets.

909. Poultry slaughter and dressing unit

What it is: Slaughtering and cleaning chickens hygienically for farmers, hotels and shops. How it works: Receive birds → slaughter and dress → package.

SkillsHygiene, processing
Cost & equipmentPremises, equipment, public health and veterinary licences
Where to find workPoultry farmers, hotels, supermarkets
PaymentPer bird
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Get public health and veterinary approvals; start serving local farmers. Pros: Adds value for farmers. Cons & risks: Strict hygiene rules. Scam warning: Never sell meat from sick birds. Kenya notes: Hotels and supermarkets prefer inspected, dressed chicken.

Summary

  • Farm services include livestock trading, milk cooling, labour teams, hatcheries, pollination, livestock transport and poultry processing.
  • Follow permits, veterinary rules and labour laws.
  • Animal welfare and honest dealing build trust.
