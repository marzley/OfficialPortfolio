Social media and digital marketing services

Most Kenyan businesses know they "should be online" but don't have the time or skills. Digital marketing services help them get found, attract customers and sell. These methods suit people who understand social media, writing, data and customer psychology.

What these methods share

  • Results matter: clients want enquiries and sales, not just likes. Learn to measure (measuring results).
  • Honesty: never promise guaranteed followers, viral posts or #1 rankings; never buy fake followers or reviews for clients.
  • Ad budgets: client ad spend is separate from your fee; agree who pays platforms.
  • See the Digital marketing & SEO subject and marketing services overview.

136. Social media management

What it is: Planning, creating, posting and responding on a business's social media accounts. How it works: Monthly content calendar → designs and captions → scheduling → replying to comments/messages → monthly report.

SkillsContent planning, basic design, writing, customer communication
Cost & equipmentPhone or laptop, Canva, scheduling tools (free tiers)
Where to find workLocal SMEs, restaurants, salons, schools, churches, freelance platforms
PaymentMonthly retainer, M-Pesa or bank
Realistic earningsLow to medium (several retainers)
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Manage one business's pages for a month at a discount; show results in a report (social media management). Pros: Recurring income. Cons & risks: Clients expect quick growth; set realistic goals. Scam warning: Never buy followers or engagement for clients; it damages accounts. Kenya notes: Kenyan SMEs often prefer WhatsApp updates and simple monthly reports.

137. Community management and moderation

What it is: Running online communities: Facebook groups, WhatsApp communities, Discord servers, comment sections; welcoming members and enforcing rules. How it works: Daily moderation, engagement prompts, answering questions, reporting.

SkillsCommunication, diplomacy, consistency
Cost & equipmentPhone or laptop
Where to find workBrands, creators, churches, NGOs, online course creators
PaymentMonthly or hourly
Realistic earningsLow to low–medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Moderate a group you belong to; document growth and activity. Pros: Flexible. Cons & risks: Conflicts and difficult members. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Large Kenyan Facebook groups and WhatsApp communities need moderators.

138. Facebook and Instagram ads management

What it is: Setting up and optimising paid ads on Meta platforms to get leads, messages or sales. How it works: Define audience and goal → create ads → set budget → monitor and optimise → report cost per result.

SkillsAds Manager, copywriting, data analysis (ads and analytics)
Cost & equipmentLaptop; client pays ad spend
Where to find workSMEs, e-commerce sellers, real estate, schools, agencies
PaymentMonthly management fee, sometimes % of spend
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium–high with proven results
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Run small test campaigns for your own offer or a friend's business; document cost per lead. Pros: High-value skill. Cons & risks: Poor results lose clients; account restrictions if rules broken. Scam warning: Never run misleading ads; avoid clients promoting scams. Kenya notes: Ads paid by card; some clients need help with payment methods. Click-to-WhatsApp ads are popular locally.

139. Google Ads management

What it is: Running search, display, shopping and YouTube ads on Google. How it works: Keyword research → campaigns → bids → landing pages → conversion tracking → optimisation.

SkillsGoogle Ads, analytics, keyword research
Cost & equipmentLaptop; client pays ad spend
Where to find workBusinesses with clear search demand (clinics, schools, contractors), agencies
PaymentMonthly fee
Realistic earningsMedium to medium–high
Time to first incomeWeeks to months

How to start: Take Google's free Skillshop training; run small campaigns. Pros: Measurable results. Cons & risks: Wasted spend if poorly managed. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: People searching "plumber near me" or "school in Kiambu" are ready to act; local search ads can work well.

140. TikTok marketing and ads

What it is: Creating TikTok content strategies and running TikTok ads for brands. How it works: Trend research → content production → posting → ads where suitable → analytics.

SkillsShort video, trends, creativity
Cost & equipmentSmartphone, editing apps
Where to find workConsumer brands, restaurants, fashion, entertainment
PaymentMonthly retainer or per campaign
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Grow a niche account to show you understand the platform. Pros: Fast-growing platform. Cons & risks: Trends change fast. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: TikTok is very popular with young Kenyan audiences.

141. SEO services for local businesses

What it is: Improving businesses' visibility on Google search and Maps. How it works: Audit → on-page fixes → Google Business Profile → reviews strategy → content → local citations → monthly reports.

SkillsSEO basics, Google Business, writing (SEO basics)
Cost & equipmentLaptop; free tools (Search Console)
Where to find workLocal businesses, web designers, agencies
PaymentMonthly retainer
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Improve a local business's Google presence and show the before/after. Pros: Retainers; steady demand. Cons & risks: Results take months; never guarantee rankings. Scam warning: Fake reviews and link schemes harm clients. Kenya notes: Many Kenyan businesses lack basic Google Business Profiles.

142. Google Business Profile setup and management

What it is: Creating, verifying and optimising businesses' Google Maps listings, posting updates and managing reviews. How it works: Setup → photos, hours, services → posts → review responses → monthly checks.

SkillsGoogle Business knowledge (Google Business)
Cost & equipmentPhone or laptop
Where to find workShops, clinics, salons, hotels, restaurants, churches
PaymentSetup fee plus optional monthly management
Realistic earningsLow (high volume possible)
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Set up profiles for 3 local businesses; collect testimonials. Pros: Quick, valuable service. Cons & risks: Verification sometimes takes time. Scam warning: Never post fake reviews. Kenya notes: Being on Google Maps matters for "near me" searches across Kenyan towns.

143. Email marketing management

What it is: Building email lists, setting up automation and sending campaigns for businesses. How it works: Choose platform → sign-up forms → welcome sequences → regular campaigns → reports.

SkillsEmail platforms, copywriting, segmentation, data protection compliance
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workE-commerce, coaches, NGOs, SaaS companies
PaymentMonthly or per campaign
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Build a sample automated sequence; learn one platform deeply. Pros: Measurable sales impact. Cons & risks: Must respect consent and unsubscribe rules. Scam warning: Never buy email lists. Kenya notes: Follow the Data Protection Act on consent for marketing messages.

144. WhatsApp Business marketing setup

What it is: Setting up WhatsApp Business for companies: catalogue, quick replies, labels, greeting messages, broadcast lists, and click-to-chat links. How it works: Setup session → templates → staff training → optional monthly broadcast content.

SkillsWhatsApp Business features (WhatsApp Business), customer service
Cost & equipmentSmartphone
Where to find workShops, salons, restaurants, online sellers
PaymentSetup fee, optional monthly content
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Offer a "WhatsApp shop setup" package to local businesses. Pros: Huge local relevance. Cons & risks: Low price point; upsell other services. Scam warning: Don't spam people who haven't opted in. Kenya notes: WhatsApp is central to Kenyan small-business sales.

145. Influencer marketing (as an influencer)

What it is: Building an audience and earning from brand partnerships, sponsored posts and ambassador deals. How it works: Consistent content in a niche → audience growth → media kit → brand deals.

SkillsContent creation, consistency, audience understanding, negotiation
Cost & equipmentSmartphone, good lighting, editing apps
Where to find workBrands, PR agencies, influencer platforms
PaymentPer post/campaign or product exchange
Realistic earningsVery low for most; medium to high for a few with engaged audiences
Time to first incomeMonths to years

How to start: Pick a niche; post consistently; track engagement; create a simple media kit. Pros: Personal brand. Cons & risks: Slow growth; online criticism; disclosure rules for ads. Scam warning: "Pay to join influencer agencies" and fake brand deals asking for deposits. Kenya notes: Disclose paid partnerships clearly; follow advertising standards and laws (especially on alcohol, betting, health and finance).

146. Influencer marketing management (for brands)

What it is: Finding influencers, negotiating, managing campaigns and measuring results for brands. How it works: Campaign brief → influencer selection → contracts → content approval → reporting.

SkillsNetworking, negotiation, project management, analytics
Cost & equipmentLaptop, phone
Where to find workBrands, PR and marketing agencies
PaymentCampaign fees or retainers
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks to months

How to start: Build a database of local micro-influencers by niche; pitch small brands. Pros: Growing industry. Cons & risks: Influencer reliability. Scam warning: Check for fake followers before hiring influencers. Kenya notes: Micro-influencers often deliver better engagement for local brands.

147. Affiliate marketing

What it is: Promoting other companies' products and earning a commission on sales through your links. How it works: Join programmes → create helpful content (reviews, comparisons) → share links → earn per sale.

SkillsContent creation, SEO or social media, trust-building
Cost & equipmentWebsite, social account or YouTube channel
Where to find workAffiliate programmes (local and international; check eligibility and payouts)
PaymentCommissions
Realistic earningsVery low for most; can grow with traffic and trust
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Pick products you genuinely use; create honest reviews (affiliate marketing). Pros: Scalable. Cons & risks: Requires traffic; disclosure required. Scam warning: Pyramid schemes disguised as "affiliate marketing" where you mainly earn by recruiting. Kenya notes: Promoting betting, forex "investment" or get-rich schemes harms your audience.

148. Content strategy and calendars

What it is: Planning what a business should post or publish, when and why, aligned with goals. How it works: Audit → audience research → content pillars → calendar → guidelines.

SkillsStrategy, marketing knowledge (marketing plan and branding)
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workBusinesses, NGOs, agencies
PaymentProject fee
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Create a sample 3-month strategy for a local business type. Pros: Higher-level work. Cons & risks: Needs proof of results. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Include local holidays and seasons (back-to-school, December, Ramadan, Easter).

149. Marketing analytics and reporting

What it is: Setting up tracking (Google Analytics, pixels), building dashboards and explaining what's working. How it works: Tracking setup → dashboards (Looker Studio) → monthly insight reports.

SkillsAnalytics tools, Excel, data storytelling (data analyst roadmap)
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workAgencies, e-commerce businesses, marketing teams
PaymentProject or retainer
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks to months

How to start: Build a sample dashboard with demo data. Pros: Less crowded, valued skill. Cons & risks: Technical setup issues. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Many local businesses run ads without tracking; you fill a real gap.

150. Online reputation and review management

What it is: Monitoring and responding to reviews and mentions, encouraging genuine reviews from happy customers. How it works: Monitoring tools → response templates → review request systems → reports.

SkillsDiplomacy, writing, customer service
Cost & equipmentPhone or laptop
Where to find workHotels, restaurants, clinics, schools
PaymentMonthly
Realistic earningsLow to low–medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Offer review response management to hospitality businesses. Pros: Recurring. Cons & risks: Handling angry customers publicly. Scam warning: Never post fake reviews or attack competitors. Kenya notes: Tourism businesses depend on online reviews.

151. Lead generation for businesses

What it is: Finding potential customers for businesses through ads, outreach, content or databases (legally). How it works: Define ideal customer → campaigns or research → qualified leads delivered.

SkillsResearch, outreach, ads, CRM (funnels, leads and CRM)
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workB2B companies, real estate, insurance, schools
PaymentPer lead or retainer
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Generate leads for one business and track conversions. Pros: Directly tied to revenue. Cons & risks: Lead quality disputes; privacy law compliance. Scam warning: Never sell personal data or spam. Kenya notes: Follow the Data Protection Act; consent matters.

152. Sales funnel and landing page building

What it is: Building landing pages and funnels that turn visitors into leads or buyers. How it works: Offer → landing page → lead magnet → follow-up emails/WhatsApp → sale.

SkillsCopywriting, page builders, marketing
Cost & equipmentLaptop; page builder tools
Where to find workCoaches, course creators, SMEs, real estate
PaymentProject fee
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Build a sample funnel for a training course or event. Pros: Valuable. Cons & risks: Depends on the client's offer quality. Scam warning: Avoid building funnels for scams or pyramid schemes. Kenya notes: Event organisers and trainers increasingly sell through landing pages with M-Pesa.

153. Marketing consulting for small businesses

What it is: Advising businesses on their marketing strategy, channels and budgets. How it works: Discovery → audit → recommendations → optional implementation.

SkillsMarketing experience, business understanding
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workSMEs, start-ups, NGOs
PaymentHourly or project
Realistic earningsMedium (with experience)
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Build experience implementing first; publish case studies. Pros: High value per hour. Cons & risks: Requires track record. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: SMEs need practical, budget-friendly advice.

SkillsYouTube SEO, analytics, organisation
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workCreators, churches, companies

155. Brand ambassador and promoter work (digital)

What it is: Promoting brands online and at events (activations), sometimes combined with social posts. How it works: Agencies hire ambassadors for campaigns; you promote, collect data and report.

SkillsCommunication, energy, reliability
Cost & equipmentSmartphone
Where to find workMarketing and activation agencies
PaymentDaily rates or per campaign
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Register with activation agencies; build a professional social presence. Pros: Networking. Cons & risks: Irregular. Scam warning: Agencies charging for "registration" or "uniform". Kenya notes: Brand activations happen across towns, campuses and malls.

SkillsMarketing knowledge, teaching

Where to find workSME groups, chambers of commerce, SACCOs, women and youth groups
PaymentPer session or participant
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Run a free workshop for a local group; then paid sessions. Pros: Scalable through groups. Cons & risks: Needs teaching skills. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Business groups and county programmes run training for entrepreneurs.

157. Digital marketing for churches and NGOs

What it is: Specialised marketing (social media, livestreams, donations pages, newsletters) for faith organisations and nonprofits. How it works: Monthly support package tailored to events and campaigns.

SkillsSocial media, design, sensitivity to values
Cost & equipmentPhone/laptop
Where to find workChurches, mosques, NGOs, CBOs
PaymentMonthly
Realistic earningsLow to low–medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start with your own church or community organisation. Pros: Loyal clients. Cons & risks: Limited budgets. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Large faith community; many organisations want stronger online presence.

158. Political and campaign digital communication

What it is: Social media, content and messaging for political candidates and civic campaigns. How it works: Strategy, content production, monitoring, especially in election periods.

SkillsCommunication, speed, ethics, legal awareness
Cost & equipmentPhone/laptop
Where to find workCandidates, campaign teams, civic organisations
PaymentCampaign contracts
Realistic earningsLow to medium (seasonal)
Time to first incomeSeasonal

How to start: Build general digital skills; follow election laws and ethics. Pros: Intense seasonal demand. Cons & risks: Misinformation, hate speech and legal risks; payment disputes. Scam warning: Never create disinformation, hate speech or fake accounts; it's illegal and harmful. Kenya notes: Kenyan election laws and cyber laws apply; get written contracts and deposits.

159. Digital PR and media outreach

What it is: Getting businesses mentioned in online media, podcasts and blogs. How it works: Story angles → media lists → pitches → follow-ups → coverage reports.

SkillsWriting, networking, storytelling
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workStart-ups, SMEs, NGOs, PR agencies
PaymentRetainer or campaign fee
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks to months

How to start: Pitch a story for a client and secure one mention as proof. Pros: Builds credibility for clients. Cons & risks: Coverage isn't guaranteed. Scam warning: "Pay-for-guaranteed-coverage" schemes are often low-quality. Kenya notes: Kenyan online media and podcasts welcome good local stories.

Summary

  • Digital marketing methods include social media and community management, Meta/Google/TikTok ads, local SEO and Google Business, email and WhatsApp marketing, influencer work, affiliate marketing, strategy, analytics, reputation, lead generation, funnels, consulting, channel management, activations, training, and specialist niches.
  • Results and honest reporting build long-term clients; never buy fake engagement or reviews.
  • Respect data protection, advertising and election laws.
