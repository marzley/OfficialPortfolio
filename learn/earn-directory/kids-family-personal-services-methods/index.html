Kids, family and personal services

Busy parents, growing families, older relatives and diaspora Kenyans need trusted help: after-school care, lessons, parties, baby gear, ceremonies and errands. These services run on trust, safety and reliability.

What these methods share

  • Child safety first: background checks, safe spaces, first aid and clear rules. Kenya's Children Act (2022) protects children.
  • Licences: child care centres and some activities need county licences and inspections.
  • Trust and reviews: parents choose services recommended by other parents.

745. After-school club and homework centre

What it is: Supervised homework, reading and activities after school. How it works: Monthly fees → pick-up from school → homework → activities → parents collect.

SkillsTeaching, child care
Cost & equipmentSafe space, materials, staff
Where to find workParents near schools and estates
PaymentMonthly fees
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start in a safe, licensed space near a school; keep groups small. Pros: Recurring fees. Cons & risks: Child safety responsibility. Scam warning: Never take children without parents' written permission. Kenya notes: Working parents in towns pay for safe after-school care.

746. Swimming instructor

What it is: Teaching children and adults to swim. How it works: Lessons at hotel, school or private pools.

SkillsSwimming, lifesaving and first aid certificates
Cost & equipmentCertification, kit
Where to find workSchools, hotels, families
PaymentPer lesson or term
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Get lifesaving and first aid training; partner with a pool. Pros: High demand in holidays. Cons & risks: Safety responsibility. Scam warning: Insist on supervision rules; never teach without lifesaving training. Kenya notes: Many schools now include swimming; holiday programmes sell well.

747. Chess coaching

What it is: Teaching chess to children and adults. How it works: School clubs, private lessons, online classes, tournaments.

PaymentPer lesson or term
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start a school club; join the chess federation's events. Pros: Builds thinking skills; growing popularity. Cons & risks: Small market. Scam warning: As usual. Kenya notes: School chess has grown with national competitions.

749. Kids' party planning

What it is: Planning children's birthday parties: themes, cake, decor, entertainment, food. How it works: Packages → planning → setup → party → cleanup.

SkillsOrganisation, creativity
Cost & equipmentDecor stock, supplier network
Where to find workParents, schools, estates
PaymentPer party package
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start with friends' parties; post photos. Pros: Fun and visible. Cons & risks: Weekend work. Scam warning: Get deposits and confirm guest numbers. Kenya notes: Partner with party entertainers (method 493).

750. Children's soft play and indoor play centre

What it is: A safe indoor play space with soft play equipment. How it works: Entry fees, birthday party bookings, café sales.

SkillsBusiness management, child safety
Cost & equipmentSpace, safe equipment, licences, staff
Where to find workFamilies, schools, malls
PaymentEntry fees
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Start small in a mall or estate centre; prioritise safety and cleanliness. Pros: Steady weekend income. Cons & risks: High setup costs. Scam warning: Inspect equipment daily; injuries damage reputations. Kenya notes: Malls and estates lack safe play spaces for younger children.

751. Baby gear rental

What it is: Renting prams, car seats, cots and play pens to visitors and parents. How it works: Rent by day or week; clean and deliver.

SkillsCustomer service, hygiene
Cost & equipmentQuality baby gear, cleaning supplies
Where to find workVisiting diaspora families, tourists, parents
PaymentRental fees
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start with car seats and cots; partner with hotels and Airbnbs. Pros: Low competition. Cons & risks: Damage and hygiene. Scam warning: Use safe, undamaged car seats only; check expiry dates. Kenya notes: Diaspora families visiting in December need baby gear.

752. Back-to-school services (book covering, labels, supplies)

What it is: Covering books, labelling items and supplying school lists. How it works: Parents send lists → you buy, cover and label → deliver.

SkillsOrganisation
Cost & equipmentCovering materials, label printer
Where to find workParents, schools
PaymentPer list or service
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Advertise before term starts in estate groups. Pros: Seasonal peaks. Cons & risks: Seasonal only. Scam warning: As usual. Kenya notes: January, May and September are peak seasons.

753. Senior day care and companionship

What it is: Day programmes and companionship for older people. How it works: Daily or weekly visits, activities, meals, outings.

SkillsCare skills, patience
Cost & equipmentSafe space, transport
Where to find workFamilies, especially diaspora
PaymentWeekly or monthly fees
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Get caregiving and first aid training; start with home visits. Pros: Meaningful. Cons & risks: Responsibility for vulnerable people. Scam warning: Never take control of elders' money or documents. Kenya notes: Diaspora children pay for regular visits and updates for parents in Kenya.

754. Birth companion (doula) support

What it is: Non-medical emotional and practical support before, during and after birth. How it works: Prenatal sessions → labour support → postnatal visits.

SkillsDoula training, empathy
Cost & equipmentTraining
Where to find workExpectant parents, clinics
PaymentPer package
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Train as a doula; work alongside midwives and doctors, not instead of them. Pros: Rewarding. Cons & risks: On-call at odd hours. Scam warning: Never give medical advice or replace a midwife or doctor. Kenya notes: Medical care is for licensed midwives, nurses and doctors; doulas provide support only.

755. Traditional ceremony planning (dowry, ruracio, ayie)

What it is: Coordinating traditional ceremonies: venue, tents, catering, decor, programme. How it works: Meet families → plan → coordinate suppliers → run the day.

SkillsEvent planning, cultural knowledge
Cost & equipmentSupplier network
Where to find workFamilies planning ceremonies
PaymentPlanning fees
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start with your community's ceremonies; respect elders' roles. Pros: Growing demand. Cons & risks: Family disagreements. Scam warning: Agree scope and fees with the family in writing. Kenya notes: Ceremonies such as ruracio (Kikuyu) and ayie (Luo) increasingly use planners.

756. Personal shopper and gift sourcing

What it is: Shopping on behalf of busy clients and diaspora families. How it works: Client list → shop → deliver with receipts.

SkillsOrganisation, honesty
Cost & equipmentPhone, transport
Where to find workBusy professionals, diaspora, corporates
PaymentService fee or percentage
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Start with friends and family; always share receipts. Pros: Low capital. Cons & risks: Trust is everything. Scam warning: Never mark up receipts; share photos and receipts. Kenya notes: Diaspora clients send money for gifts, groceries and items for relatives.

757. House sitting

What it is: Living in or checking on homes while owners travel. How it works: Agreed visits or stays; security checks; plants and pets.

SkillsReliability
Cost & equipmentReferences
Where to find workTravellers, diaspora homeowners
PaymentPer day or month
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Build references; use written agreements. Pros: Low cost. Cons & risks: Liability. Scam warning: Never let others into the house without permission. Kenya notes: Popular with homeowners who travel abroad.

758. Diaspora family concierge

What it is: A trusted local helper for Kenyans abroad: errands, visits, payments, paperwork. How it works: Monthly retainer or per task; reports with photos.

SkillsOrganisation, honesty
Cost & equipmentPhone, transport
Where to find workKenyans abroad
PaymentRetainer or per task
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start with one or two families; send clear reports. Pros: Recurring. Cons & risks: Trust and accountability. Scam warning: Keep receipts and photo evidence for every task. Kenya notes: Pairs with construction monitoring (method 363) and procurement (method 519).

Summary

  • Kids, family and personal services include after-school clubs, swimming, chess, music lessons, parties, play centres, baby gear rental, school prep, senior care, doula support, ceremony planning, personal shopping, house sitting and diaspora concierge.
  • Child safety and trust come first; follow the Children Act and licence rules.
  • Clear agreements and receipts build long-term clients.
