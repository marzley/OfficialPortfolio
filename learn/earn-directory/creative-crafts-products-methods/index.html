Creative products, crafts and making

Making things with your hands, then using the internet to find buyers, is one of the oldest ways to earn, now with a much bigger market. These methods combine a craft skill with online marketing.

What these methods share

  • Quality and consistency build repeat customers.
  • Good photos and stories sell handmade products online.
  • Pricing must cover materials, time, packaging and delivery (pricing).
  • Safety and regulation for products like food, cosmetics and children's items.

265. Beadwork and jewellery making

What it is: Making beaded jewellery, accessories and decor. How it works: Design → make → photograph → sell locally, online and to tourists.

SkillsCraft skill, design, colour sense
Cost & equipmentBeads, wire, tools
Where to find workMarkets, social media, tourist shops, international buyers
PaymentM-Pesa, cash, platform payouts
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Develop a signature style; sell to friends, then markets and online. Pros: Low start-up cost. Cons & risks: Competition; tourist seasonality. Scam warning: Bulk "export orders" that require you to pay fees first. Kenya notes: Respect cultural designs and work fairly with community artisans.

266. Candle and soap making

What it is: Making scented candles, natural soaps and gift sets. How it works: Recipes → production → packaging → sales.

SkillsRecipes, safety, packaging
Cost & equipmentWax/oils, moulds, fragrances, safety gear
Where to find workGift shops, social media, events
PaymentM-Pesa
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn safe processes (soap uses caustic soda: handle carefully). Pros: Gifting demand. Cons & risks: Product standards and labelling. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Check KEBS standards requirements for soaps and cosmetics if selling widely.

Where to find workLocal clients, social media, schools, weddings
PaymentPer garment, deposits
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeDays to weeks

How to start: Build a portfolio on Instagram/TikTok. Pros: Constant demand. Cons & risks: Deadlines for events. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Kitenge/Ankara outfits for weddings and events sell well.
