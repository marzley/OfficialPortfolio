Repair and home-service trades

Fridges break, generators stop, locks jam, carpets get dirty. Repair and home-service trades earn steady money in every town, and WhatsApp, Google Maps and TikTok "fix it" videos now bring customers to skilled people.

What these methods share

  • Training: TVET courses and apprenticeships; certification where electrical or gas safety is involved.
  • Honest diagnosis and quotes: explain the fault, quote before work, give a short warranty.
  • Online presence: Google Business Profile, before/after videos, reviews.
  • Safety: electricity, gas and refrigerants are dangerous; follow safety rules and licensing.

526. Fridge and freezer repair (refrigeration technician)

What it is: Repairing domestic and commercial refrigerators and freezers. How it works: Diagnosis → parts replacement or regassing → testing.

SkillsRefrigeration training, electrical basics
Cost & equipmentGauges, vacuum pump, tools
Where to find workHomes, shops, butcheries, restaurants
PaymentPer job
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks (after training)

How to start: Train at a TVET; apprentice. Pros: Constant demand. Cons & risks: Refrigerant handling rules. Scam warning: Don't charge for parts you didn't replace. Kenya notes: Commercial fridges in dukas and butcheries need fast service.
