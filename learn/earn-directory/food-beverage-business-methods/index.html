Food and drink businesses with online ordering

Everyone eats every day. Food businesses in Kenya range from roadside smokie vendors to cloud kitchens delivering through apps. Online ordering, WhatsApp menus and delivery riders have opened new models with lower start-up costs.

What these methods share

  • Food safety and licences: county public health licences, medical certificates for food handlers, and KEBS standards for packaged products.
  • Consistency: same taste, same portion, every time.
  • Costing: know your cost per plate or unit, including packaging and delivery.
  • See food and baked goods online and catering.

446. Cloud kitchen (delivery-only restaurant)

What it is: A kitchen that sells only through delivery apps and WhatsApp, without a dining area. How it works: Menu → listings on delivery apps → orders → riders deliver.

SkillsCooking, menu costing, operations
Cost & equipmentKitchen, equipment, licences
Where to find workDelivery apps, social media, offices
PaymentApp payouts (minus commission), M-Pesa
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Test a small menu; price for app commissions. Pros: Lower rent than restaurants. Cons & risks: App commissions; competition. Scam warning: As usual: fake suppliers and fake "app listing agents". Kenya notes: County food licences apply even without a dining area.

447. Food truck or mobile food stall

What it is: Selling food from a mobile unit at events, offices or busy areas. How it works: Fixed schedules and event bookings.

SkillsCooking, customer service
Cost & equipmentTruck/stall, equipment, permits
Where to find workEvents, campuses, business districts
PaymentCash/M-Pesa
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Check county permits for mobile food vending. Pros: Go where customers are. Cons & risks: Permits and location rules. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Event bookings via social media help fill slow days.

448. Street food vending (with M-Pesa and WhatsApp orders)

Cost & equipmentStand, cooking equipment, permits
Where to find workBusy streets, stages, schools
PaymentCash/M-Pesa

Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Pick a location with steady foot traffic; get permits. Pros: Low start-up cost. Cons & risks: Permit enforcement; weather. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: A Lipa na M-Pesa till speeds up sales.
