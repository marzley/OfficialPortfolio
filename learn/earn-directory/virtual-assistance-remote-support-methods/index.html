Virtual assistance, admin and remote support

Busy entrepreneurs, professionals and small companies everywhere need help with emails, calendars, customers, bookkeeping and admin. Remote support work suits organised, reliable people with good communication skills. Many of these methods can grow into steady part-time or full-time remote roles.

What these methods share

  • Reliability and communication are the main skills; clients trust you with their inbox, customers or money.
  • Tools: Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, project tools (Trello, Asana, Notion), communication apps, password managers (tools and workday).
  • Confidentiality: sign NDAs when asked; never share client data.
  • See virtual assistant work and customer support jobs.

184. General virtual assistant

What it is: Handling a mix of admin tasks remotely: email, scheduling, documents, research, bookings. How it works: Hourly or monthly retainer; tasks via a shared board; regular check-ins.

SkillsOrganisation, written communication, Google Workspace/Microsoft 365
Cost & equipmentLaptop, reliable internet, headset
Where to find workFreelance platforms, VA agencies, LinkedIn, local professionals
PaymentHourly or monthly; PayPal, Payoneer, bank
Realistic earningsLow to medium (long-term clients)
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Create a services list and sample work (inbox system, travel plan, SOP); apply to VA roles. Pros: Steady long-term clients. Cons & risks: Time zone demands; scope creep. Scam warning: "VA jobs" requiring you to buy equipment, receive packages or forward money. Kenya notes: East Africa Time overlaps with European business hours.

185. Executive assistant (remote)

What it is: High-level support to executives: complex calendars, travel, meeting preparation, stakeholder communication. How it works: Long-term part-time or full-time engagement.

SkillsProfessionalism, discretion, anticipation, advanced tools
Cost & equipmentLaptop, quiet workspace
Where to find workExecutive assistant agencies, LinkedIn, referrals
PaymentMonthly
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Build experience as a general VA or office administrator first. Pros: Better pay. Cons & risks: High expectations; long hours. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Experienced Kenyan administrators can move into international EA roles.

186. Email and inbox management

What it is: Organising inboxes, filtering, drafting replies and flagging priorities. How it works: Rules and labels, daily triage, templates, summary reports.

SkillsWriting, judgement, Gmail/Outlook (email skills)
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workBusy professionals, small business owners
PaymentHourly/monthly
Realistic earningsLow to low–medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Document an "inbox zero" system you'd set up. Pros: Clear deliverable. Cons & risks: Sensitive information access. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Pairs well with calendar management.

187. Calendar and appointment management

What it is: Scheduling meetings, appointments and reminders for clients or their customers. How it works: Booking tools, coordination with time zones, confirmations.

SkillsOrganisation, time zones, communication (calendar and Meet)
Cost & equipmentLaptop or phone
Where to find workCoaches, consultants, clinics, salons
PaymentHourly/monthly
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Offer appointment setting to local clinics or salons. Pros: Simple to start. Cons & risks: Double bookings hurt trust. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Local clinics and salons benefit from reminder systems.

188. Customer support (chat and email)

What it is: Answering customer questions by chat and email for online businesses. How it works: Help desk tools, templates, escalation procedures, shift schedules.

SkillsWritten English, patience, problem-solving, typing
Cost & equipmentLaptop, reliable internet with backup
Where to find workE-commerce companies, SaaS start-ups, BPO companies, remote job boards
PaymentHourly or monthly salary
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Practise writing calm, clear replies; apply to remote support roles. Pros: Steady employment possible. Cons & risks: Shifts at night for other time zones. Scam warning: Fake job offers with "training fees". Kenya notes: Nairobi BPOs and remote employers hire customer support agents.

189. Phone customer service and call centre work

What it is: Handling inbound and outbound calls for businesses. How it works: Office-based call centres or remote with approved setups.

SkillsClear speaking, patience, product knowledge
Cost & equipmentOffice-provided or headset and quiet room
Where to find workBPOs, banks, telcos, insurers
PaymentSalary plus incentives
Realistic earningsLow to low–medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Apply to BPO companies; practise call scenarios. Pros: Entry-level jobs. Cons & risks: Stressful targets. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Kenya's BPO sector hires many graduates.

190. Appointment setting and cold calling

What it is: Calling or messaging prospects to book sales meetings for businesses. How it works: Scripts, CRM, targets; often commission-based.

SkillsPersuasion, resilience, organisation
Cost & equipmentPhone/laptop, headset
Where to find workB2B companies, agencies, real estate, insurance
PaymentHourly, per appointment or commission
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Practise scripts; offer appointment setting to local B2B businesses. Pros: Sales skills pay well over time. Cons & risks: Rejection; commission-only risks. Scam warning: Avoid roles selling scams or pressuring vulnerable people. Kenya notes: Respect data-protection rules on calling and messaging.

191. Bookkeeping

What it is: Recording transactions, reconciling accounts, preparing financial summaries. How it works: Accounting software or spreadsheets; monthly closes; coordination with accountants.

SkillsAccounting basics, accuracy, software (QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho, others)
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workSMEs, NGOs, churches, freelance platforms
PaymentMonthly
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks to months

How to start: Accounting training (e.g. CPA studies) helps; learn one software. Pros: Recurring work. Cons & risks: Errors have consequences; confidentiality. Scam warning: Never help falsify records. Kenya notes: SMEs need KRA-ready books; eTIMS (electronic tax invoices) awareness helps.

192. Payroll support

What it is: Preparing payroll calculations, payslips and statutory deductions for small employers. How it works: Monthly payroll cycles using software or spreadsheets.

SkillsPayroll rules, Excel, accuracy
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workSMEs, schools, NGOs
PaymentMonthly per employee or flat fee
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn current statutory deductions (PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy) and keep updated. Pros: Recurring. Cons & risks: Rules change; mistakes cause penalties. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Statutory rates change; always verify current figures with official sources.

193. KRA iTax filing assistance

What it is: Helping individuals and small businesses file returns, register PINs and use iTax/eTIMS. How it works: Collect documents → file on iTax with the client → explain obligations.

SkillsiTax knowledge (KRA iTax), accuracy, honesty
Cost & equipmentLaptop/phone
Where to find workEmployees, small businesses, cyber cafés
PaymentPer filing
Realistic earningsLow (seasonal peaks before 30 June)
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Learn iTax thoroughly; complex cases go to qualified tax practitioners. Pros: Strong seasonal demand. Cons & risks: Errors affect clients; know your limits. Scam warning: Never ask for clients' passwords permanently; never file false returns. Kenya notes: June filing season is busy across Kenya.

194. eCitizen and government services assistance

What it is: Helping people apply for services online: passports, good conduct certificates, business registration, driving licences, HELB, KUCCPS. How it works: Guide clients through applications; they pay official fees directly; you charge for assistance.

SkillsKnowledge of portals (eCitizen), patience
Cost & equipmentLaptop/phone, printer (optional)
Where to find workWalk-ins, cyber cafés, communities
PaymentService fee
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Master common applications; display official fees transparently. Pros: Constant demand. Cons & risks: Handling personal data. Scam warning: Never promise "faster approval" for extra money or impersonate officials. Kenya notes: Be transparent that government fees are separate from your service fee.

195. Data entry and document typing services

What it is: Typing handwritten documents, forms, records and reports; digitising paper records. How it works: Receive documents → type to format → proofread → deliver.

SkillsFast, accurate typing; formatting
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workSchools, churches, researchers, small businesses, cyber cafés
PaymentPer page
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Offer typing services locally with clear per-page prices. Pros: Simple. Cons & risks: Low pay; confidentiality. Scam warning: "Online data entry jobs" with registration fees are scams. Kenya notes: Typing and printing services are needed in every town.

196. Online research assistant

What it is: Conducting desk research: market research, competitor analysis, literature searches, supplier lists. How it works: Brief → research → organised report.

SkillsSearch skills, source evaluation, summarising
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workConsultants, entrepreneurs, academics, NGOs
PaymentHourly/project
Realistic earningsLow to low–medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Create a sample research report on a Kenyan market. Pros: Interesting work. Cons & risks: Must cite sources accurately. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Investors and NGOs need local market research.

197. Project coordination (remote)

What it is: Coordinating tasks, deadlines and teams for small agencies or projects. How it works: Project tools, status updates, meeting notes.

SkillsOrganisation, communication, project tools
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workAgencies, start-ups, NGOs
PaymentHourly/monthly
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks to months

How to start: Coordinate a volunteer or school project and document your process. Pros: Leads to project management careers. Cons & risks: Responsibility for others' delays. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: NGOs and agencies hire project assistants.

198. E-commerce store assistant

What it is: Managing products, orders, customer messages and inventory for online stores. How it works: Daily store operations on Shopify, WooCommerce or marketplaces.

SkillsE-commerce platforms, customer service, organisation
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workOnline store owners (local and international)
PaymentHourly/monthly
Realistic earningsLow to low–medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn a platform's admin; manage a friend's online shop. Pros: Steady work. Cons & risks: Repetitive. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Local Instagram and WhatsApp shops need order management help.

199. Real estate virtual assistant

What it is: Supporting real estate agents: listings, lead follow-up, scheduling viewings, CRM updates. How it works: Ongoing support with agents or agencies.

SkillsCRM, communication, organisation
Cost & equipmentLaptop/phone
Where to find workAgents and agencies (local and international)
PaymentHourly/monthly
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn property listing platforms and CRMs. Pros: Niche specialisation pays better. Cons & risks: Fast-paced. Scam warning: Watch for rental scams using fake listings; never help publish them. Kenya notes: Kenyan agents increasingly market on social media and listing sites.

200. Medical and dental clinic virtual receptionist

What it is: Handling appointment bookings, reminders and patient enquiries remotely for clinics. How it works: Phone/WhatsApp/booking systems with confidentiality rules.

SkillsCommunication, confidentiality, scheduling
Cost & equipmentPhone/laptop, headset
Where to find workClinics, dental practices, physiotherapists
PaymentMonthly
Realistic earningsLow to low–medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Offer reminder and booking management to local clinics. Pros: Steady contracts. Cons & risks: Health data is sensitive. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Follow data protection rules for health information.

201. Online tutoring coordinator and scheduler

What it is: Managing schedules, payments and communication for tutoring businesses or teachers. How it works: Calendars, reminders, payment tracking.

SkillsOrganisation, communication
Cost & equipmentPhone/laptop
Where to find workTutors, tuition centres, online academies
PaymentMonthly
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Offer admin help to busy tutors. Pros: Simple. Cons & risks: Small budgets. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Tuition centres and holiday programmes need coordination.

202. Transcription and meeting notes (minute-taking)

What it is: Taking minutes for meetings (in person or online) and producing action-item summaries. How it works: Attend or record (with consent) → write minutes in an agreed format → circulate.

SkillsListening, summarising, formal writing
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workBoards, SACCOs, chamas, NGOs, churches, companies
PaymentPer meeting
Realistic earningsLow to low–medium
Time to first incomeDays to weeks

How to start: Create a minutes template; offer to chamas and associations. Pros: Regular meetings = regular work. Cons & risks: Confidential content. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: SACCO and chama meetings need accurate minutes.

203. Chama and group administration

What it is: Managing records for chamas and welfare groups: contributions, loans, minutes, reports and reminders. How it works: Spreadsheet or app-based record keeping; monthly statements.

SkillsExcel, honesty, organisation (Excel)
Cost & equipmentLaptop/phone
Where to find workChamas, welfare groups, investment clubs
PaymentMonthly fee
Realistic earningsLow (scalable with many groups)
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Build a contributions and loans template; manage your own group's records. Pros: Trusted local service. Cons & risks: Money disputes; keep transparent records. Scam warning: Never hold group money in your personal account; groups should use official group accounts. Kenya notes: Kenya has a huge chama culture needing reliable record keeping.

Summary

  • Admin and support methods include general and executive VA work, inbox and calendar management, customer support, call centres, appointment setting, bookkeeping, payroll, KRA and eCitizen help, typing, research, project coordination, e-commerce, real estate and clinic support, scheduling, minutes and chama administration.
  • Reliability, communication and confidentiality are the core skills.
  • Never forward money, receive packages or pay fees for "VA jobs"; never falsify records or promise "fast approvals".
