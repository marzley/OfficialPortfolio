Tourism niches

Adding to tourism businesses (methods 495–509), these niche tours attract specific travellers.

What these methods share

  • Licences: tour guides and operators need the right licences.
  • Reviews: online reviews bring most tourists.
  • Safety: insurance and safety briefings.

969. Birding guide

What it is: Guiding birdwatchers to see and identify birds. How it works: Plan routes → guide → identify species → share lists.

SkillsBird identification, guiding
Cost & equipmentBinoculars, field guide, licences
Where to find workInternational birders, lodges, tour operators
PaymentPer day
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Learn birds through clubs (e.g. Nature Kenya bird walks); get guide licences. Pros: Kenya has over 1,000 bird species. Cons & risks: Specialist knowledge needed. Scam warning: Respect wildlife and protected areas. Kenya notes: Kenya is a top birding destination.

970. Cycling tours

What it is: Leading bike tours in towns, villages, parks and countryside. How it works: Plan routes → provide bikes and helmets → guide.

SkillsCycling, guiding, safety
Cost & equipmentBikes, helmets, repair kit, insurance
Where to find workTourists, expats, corporate groups
PaymentPer person
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start with a scenic route; list on travel platforms. Pros: Healthy, eco-friendly. Cons & risks: Traffic and weather. Scam warning: Always provide helmets and safety briefings. Kenya notes: Places like Hell's Gate and Lake Naivasha suit cycling.

971. Food tours

What it is: Guiding visitors through local food: markets, street food and traditional dishes. How it works: Plan stops → guide tastings → share stories.

SkillsFood knowledge, storytelling
Cost & equipmentLow: partnerships with vendors
Where to find workTourists, expats, business visitors
PaymentPer person
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Partner with hygienic vendors; list on tour platforms. Pros: Unique experience. Cons & risks: Food safety for visitors. Scam warning: Choose vendors with good hygiene. Kenya notes: Nairobi and Mombasa food scenes attract visitors.

Summary

  • Tourism niches include birding guides, cycling tours and food tours.
  • Licences, reviews and safety matter.
  • Respect wildlife and choose hygienic food vendors.
