Agriculture and agribusiness with online tools

Agriculture employs a large share of Kenyans, and digital tools are changing how farmers learn, sell and get paid. These methods combine farming or agribusiness with online marketing, information and M-Pesa.

What these methods share

  • Start small and learn: test before investing heavily; consult agricultural extension officers.
  • Market first: find buyers before producing large volumes.
  • Records and quality: good records and consistent quality bring better prices.
  • Scams: fake seed, fake buyers and "too-good" livestock deals are common.

281. Selling farm produce directly online

What it is: Selling vegetables, fruits, eggs and dairy directly to urban customers via WhatsApp and social media. How it works: Weekly orders → harvest → delivery.

SkillsFarming, logistics, customer service
Cost & equipmentFarm, transport
Where to find workUrban households, offices, restaurants
PaymentM-Pesa
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Build a customer list in one estate; deliver on fixed days. Pros: Better prices than middlemen. Cons & risks: Perishability. Scam warning: Buyers who never pay after delivery; take payment first or on delivery. Kenya notes: Urban demand for fresh, organic produce is growing.

282. Poultry farming with online sales

What it is: Rearing chickens for eggs or meat and selling via social media. How it works: Production → online marketing → delivery.

SkillsPoultry management, biosecurity
Cost & equipmentHousing, chicks, feed
Where to find workHouseholds, hotels, schools
PaymentM-Pesa
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Train with extension services; start small. Pros: Steady demand. Cons & risks: Disease outbreaks; feed costs. Scam warning: Fake "improved kienyeji chicks" sellers; buy from trusted hatcheries. Kenya notes: Improved kienyeji chicken and eggs sell well.

Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Get technical advice from fisheries officers. Pros: Growing demand. Cons & risks: Water management. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: County fisheries departments offer guidance.

SkillsProcessing, hygiene, branding

Where to find workShops, supermarkets, online
PaymentPer product

How to start: Start small; get required permits and standards certification. Pros: Higher margins. Cons & risks: Regulation compliance. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: KEBS and county public health approvals are usually required for food products.

287. Agri-input reselling online (seeds, feeds, fertiliser)

What it is: Selling farm inputs to farmers via WhatsApp groups and online channels. How it works: Partner with suppliers → sell → deliver.

SkillsProduct knowledge, trust
Cost & equipmentStock or dropship arrangements
Where to find workFarmer groups, cooperatives
PaymentM-Pesa
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Sell only certified genuine inputs. Pros: Steady demand. Cons & risks: Counterfeits damage trust. Scam warning: Never sell uncertified seed; it destroys farmers' harvests. Kenya notes: Seed is regulated (KEPHIS); buy from certified sources.

Where to find workFarmers, cooperatives, agribusinesses
PaymentPer visit or retainer
Realistic earningsLow–medium to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Share free advice content to build a following. Pros: Valuable expertise. Cons & risks: Requires real qualifications. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Online agronomy content in Kiswahili reaches many farmers.

295. Farm management for absentee owners

Cost & equipmentPhone, transport

Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Offer transparent weekly reports with photos. Pros: Trust-based long-term contracts. Cons & risks: Disputes over results. Scam warning: Diaspora farmers are often defrauded; transparency builds your reputation. Kenya notes: High demand among diaspora Kenyans.

SkillsAnimal handling
Cost & equipmentSupplies
Where to find workUrban pet owners
PaymentPer service
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeDays

How to start: Offer reliable, insured-like service with references. Pros: Growing urban demand. Cons & risks: Responsibility for animals. Scam warning: Fake puppy-sale scams online. Kenya notes: Pet ownership is growing in Nairobi and other cities.

Summary

  • Agriculture methods include direct produce sales, poultry, fish, mushrooms, honey, value addition, input reselling, consulting, content, aggregation, greenhouses, dairy, machinery rental, nurseries, farm management and pet services.
  • Find buyers first, use certified inputs, keep records, and beware of "guaranteed returns" farming schemes.
