Public-sector careers and official recruitment

Government jobs remain attractive for stability. Many Kenyans lose money to fake recruitment schemes promising government jobs for a fee. This lesson explains the main official routes, so you can apply safely and recognise fraud.

What these methods share

  • Official recruitment is free. Legitimate public bodies don't charge application or "facilitation" fees.
  • Apply only through official channels: official websites, gazette notices, official social media accounts and newspapers.
  • Meet the stated requirements: academic certificates, clearances (good conduct, KRA compliance, HELB, EACC and others as specified).
  • Report fraud to the relevant body and the police.

682. National civil service jobs (Public Service Commission)

What it is: Jobs in national government ministries and departments. How it works: Vacancies advertised by the PSC → online applications → shortlisting → interviews.

SkillsAs per the job advert
Cost & equipmentDocuments, clearances
Where to find workPSC official website and job portal
PaymentGovernment salary scales
Realistic earningsLow to high depending on grade
Time to first incomeMonths (recruitment process)

How to start: Create a profile on the official PSC portal; prepare clearances in advance. Pros: Stability, benefits. Cons & risks: Competitive; slow processes. Scam warning: Anyone selling PSC jobs is a fraudster. Kenya notes: Requirements under Chapter Six of the Constitution (integrity clearances) are common for senior jobs.

683. Teaching jobs through the Teachers Service Commission (TSC)

What it is: Employment as a public school teacher. How it works: TSC registration → advertised recruitment → county-level selection.

SkillsTeaching qualifications, TSC registration
Cost & equipmentDocuments
Where to find workTSC official adverts
PaymentTSC salary scales
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Register with the TSC; watch for official recruitment and internship announcements. Pros: Stability. Cons & risks: Competitive. Scam warning: Fake "TSC agents" promising placements for money. Kenya notes: Teacher internship programmes are announced officially.

684. County government jobs

What it is: Jobs in county departments (health, agriculture, administration). How it works: County Public Service Boards advertise and recruit.

SkillsAs per adverts
Cost & equipmentDocuments
Where to find workCounty Public Service Board websites and notices
PaymentCounty salary scales
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Follow your county's official channels. Pros: Local employment. Cons & risks: Delays. Scam warning: Report anyone demanding bribes. Kenya notes: Counties also recruit health workers and ECDE teachers.

685. Disciplined services recruitment (police, prisons, defence forces, wildlife and forest services)

What it is: Joining uniformed services. How it works: Official recruitment drives with physical and medical tests.

SkillsFitness, academic requirements
Cost & equipmentDocuments
Where to find workOfficial recruitment announcements
PaymentService salary scales
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths (training)

How to start: Prepare physically; attend official recruitment centres. Pros: Career and training. Cons & risks: Demanding. Scam warning: Recruitment is free; paying "officers" for slots is a crime. Kenya notes: Report recruitment corruption to the authorities.

What it is: Jobs in state corporations (energy, water, ports, regulators). How it works: Advertised vacancies; competitive recruitment.

SkillsProfessional qualifications
Cost & equipmentDocuments
Where to find workOfficial corporation websites
PaymentCorporation scales
Realistic earningsMedium to high
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Follow official career pages of state corporations. Pros: Good benefits. Cons & risks: Competitive. Scam warning: As above. Kenya notes: Many positions require professional registration.

What it is: Careers in the courts and Parliament. How it works: Judicial Service Commission and Parliamentary Service Commission recruitment.

SkillsAs per adverts (legal, administrative, ICT)

689. Public procurement supplier (AGPO and open tenders)

What it is: Supplying goods and services to government entities, including set-asides for youth, women and persons with disabilities. How it works: Registration → tenders → supply → payment.

SkillsBusiness compliance, tender preparation
Cost & equipmentRegistered business, compliance documents
Where to find workGovernment e-procurement platforms
PaymentContract payments
Realistic earningsMedium to high
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Register your business; get AGPO certification if eligible. Pros: Large contracts. Cons & risks: Payment delays; competition. Scam warning: Never pay bribes; never "rent" your AGPO certificate to others. Kenya notes: AGPO reserves a share of procurement for youth, women and PWDs.

690. Government internships and attachment programmes

What it is: Official internship programmes for graduates. How it works: Applications through official portals; stipends.

SkillsRecent graduate status
Cost & equipmentDocuments
Where to find workPSC and ministry portals
PaymentStipends
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Watch official announcements. Pros: Experience. Cons & risks: Temporary. Scam warning: Internships are free to apply. Kenya notes: Public service internship programmes run regularly.

691. Jobs abroad through official labour mobility programmes

What it is: Working abroad through government-to-government or licensed agency programmes. How it works: Registration → vetting → placement through licensed agencies or official schemes.

SkillsAs per job
Cost & equipmentDocuments, passport
Where to find workOfficial labour ministry announcements, licensed agencies
PaymentForeign salaries
Realistic earningsVaries widely
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Verify agencies on official lists of licensed recruitment agencies. Pros: Higher wages abroad. Cons & risks: Exploitation risks. Scam warning: Fake overseas jobs with huge "processing fees" are a major scam. Kenya notes: The National Employment Authority maintains information on licensed agencies.

Summary

  • Public-sector routes include PSC civil service, TSC teaching, county jobs, disciplined services, state corporations, NYS, judiciary and Parliament, public procurement (AGPO), internships and official labour mobility.
  • Official recruitment is free; apply only through official channels; report fraud.
