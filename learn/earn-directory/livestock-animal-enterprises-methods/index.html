Livestock and animal enterprises

Building on the agriculture category (poultry, fish, dairy, bees), these are further livestock and animal-related enterprises. Many Kenyan farmers market animals and animal products through WhatsApp groups, Facebook and local networks.

What these methods share

  • Animal health: work with veterinary officers; vaccinations and biosecurity.
  • Markets first: know who will buy before scaling.
  • Records: breeding, feeding, costs and sales records.
  • Scams: "high-return" livestock investment schemes and fake breed sellers.

585. Pig farming

What it is: Raising pigs for pork. How it works: Housing → breeding/fattening → slaughterhouse or butcher sales.

SkillsPig husbandry, biosecurity
Cost & equipmentHousing, piglets, feed
Where to find workButcheries, processors, hotels
PaymentPer animal/kg
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Train with extension officers; start small. Pros: Fast growth cycles. Cons & risks: Disease (e.g. African swine fever). Scam warning: Fake "contract farming" schemes with guaranteed buy-back. Kenya notes: Biosecurity is vital.

Summary

  • Livestock and animal methods include pigs, rabbits, dairy goats, meat goats and sheep, beef fattening, quails, AI services, vet services, agrovets, feed milling, fodder, dog training, pet shops and aquariums.
  • Work with vets, confirm markets, and beware of hype-driven livestock schemes.
