Sports, outdoor and recreation businesses

Kenyans play, run, hike and cycle more every year, and corporates pay for team building. These methods turn sport and the outdoors into income, from pitch rental to mountain guiding.

What these methods share

  • Safety and insurance: first aid, safe equipment and public liability cover.
  • Licences: guides need licences from the Tourism Regulatory Authority (TRA) and parks rules from KWS; physiotherapists register with their council.
  • Bookings and reviews: online booking and good reviews fill slots.

759. Football pitch (astroturf) rental

What it is: Renting a five-a-side or full pitch by the hour. How it works: Hourly bookings, leagues and tournaments.

SkillsBusiness management
Cost & equipmentLand, artificial turf, lights, fencing (high capital)
Where to find workCorporate teams, youth, schools
PaymentPer hour
Realistic earningsMedium to high
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Start with a five-a-side pitch; run weekly leagues. Pros: Steady evening and weekend income. Cons & risks: High setup and maintenance. Scam warning: Get proper lease and county approvals. Kenya notes: Evening five-a-side games are popular in towns.

760. Bicycle rental and repair

What it is: Repairing bicycles and renting bikes for commuting and leisure. How it works: Repairs, servicing, spares, daily or hourly rentals.

SkillsBike mechanics
Cost & equipmentTools, spares, bikes for rent
Where to find workCommuters, tourists, cyclists
PaymentPer job or rental
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn repairs; start a roadside workshop. Pros: Low capital. Cons & risks: Theft of rental bikes. Scam warning: Use deposits and ID for rentals. Kenya notes: Cycling clubs and tourist areas (Naivasha, Hell's Gate) need rentals.

761. Mountain and hiking guide (licensed)

What it is: Guiding hikers on mountains, forests and trails. How it works: Bookings → guided hike → safety briefing.

SkillsNavigation, first aid, fitness
Cost & equipmentLicences, gear
Where to find workHikers, tourists, hiking clubs
PaymentPer hike or day
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Train with experienced guides; get licensed; learn first aid. Pros: Outdoor work. Cons & risks: Weather risks. Scam warning: Use registered guides for parks; fake guides endanger lives. Kenya notes: Mt Kenya, the Aberdares and Ngong Hills attract hikers; parks follow KWS rules.

762. Team building and outdoor adventure activities

What it is: Running team-building days with games, obstacle courses and adventure activities. How it works: Corporate bookings → activities → facilitation.

SkillsFacilitation, safety
Cost & equipmentEquipment, venue partners, insurance
Where to find workCorporates, schools, churches
PaymentPer participant
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Partner with a venue; design safe activities. Pros: Corporate budgets. Cons & risks: Seasonal; safety responsibility. Scam warning: Insurance and safety briefings are essential. Kenya notes: End-year and retreat seasons are busy.

763. Swimming pool maintenance

What it is: Cleaning and maintaining pools for homes, hotels and schools. How it works: Weekly visits: cleaning, water testing, chemicals.

SkillsPool chemistry
Cost & equipmentTesting kits, tools
Where to find workHomes, hotels, schools, apartments
PaymentMonthly contracts
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Learn water testing; start with apartments and homes. Pros: Recurring contracts. Cons & risks: Chemical safety. Scam warning: Store and handle chemicals safely. Kenya notes: Apartments with pools need regular maintenance.

764. Sports jersey and kit customisation

What it is: Printing names, numbers and logos on team kits. How it works: Orders → design → print → deliver.

SkillsDesign, printing
Cost & equipmentHeat press or sublimation, supplier
Where to find workTeams, schools, companies, clubs
PaymentPer kit
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start with heat-press printing; show samples to teams. Pros: Bulk orders. Cons & risks: Deadlines. Scam warning: Don't print copyrighted club logos without permission. Kenya notes: Inter-company tournaments and school games create demand.

SkillsMartial arts grading, teaching

Where to find workSchools, gyms, women's groups, corporates
PaymentMonthly fees
Realistic earningsLow to medium

How to start: Get graded by a recognised federation; start a school club. Pros: Recurring. Cons & risks: Injury risk. Scam warning: As usual. Kenya notes: Self-defence workshops for women and students are popular.

766. Sports event organising (runs, tournaments)

What it is: Organising fun runs, marathons, tournaments and sports days. How it works: Registration fees, sponsorships, partnerships.

SkillsEvent management
Cost & equipmentPermits, medals, timing, medical support
Where to find workRunners, corporates, schools
PaymentRegistration and sponsorship
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Start with a small community fun run. Pros: Sponsorship potential. Cons & risks: Permits and logistics. Scam warning: Plan medical support and road safety with authorities. Kenya notes: Charity runs are popular with corporates and churches.

767. Sports physiotherapy (licensed)

What it is: Treating and preventing sports injuries. How it works: Clinic, team contracts, events.

SkillsPhysiotherapy degree and registration
Cost & equipmentClinic equipment
Where to find workTeams, gyms, athletes, schools
PaymentPer session or contract
Realistic earningsMedium to high
Time to first incomeYears (training)

How to start: Study physiotherapy and register with the Physiotherapy Council of Kenya. Pros: Respected profession. Cons & risks: Long training. Scam warning: Only licensed professionals should treat injuries. Kenya notes: Athletics regions like Iten and Eldoret have many athletes.

Cost & equipmentPhone, basic kit
Where to find workRunners, corporates, estates

Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start a weekend running group; add training plans. Pros: Community building. Cons & risks: Weather. Scam warning: As usual. Kenya notes: Many new runners want structured training for marathons.

769. Water sports instructor (kite surfing, kayaking, snorkelling)

What it is: Teaching water sports at the coast and lakes. How it works: Lessons and tours.

SkillsCertification, swimming, safety
Cost & equipmentEquipment, certification, insurance
Where to find workTourists, hotels
PaymentPer lesson or tour
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Get certified; work with a hotel or water sports centre. Pros: Tourist income. Cons & risks: Seasonal; safety risk. Scam warning: Always use safety equipment and check conditions. Kenya notes: Diani and Watamu are popular for kite surfing and snorkelling.

770. Golf caddie

What it is: Carrying bags and advising golfers on courses. How it works: Work through golf clubs; tips and fees.

SkillsGolf knowledge
Cost & equipmentNone
Where to find workGolf clubs
PaymentPer round plus tips
Realistic earningsLow
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Register with a golf club's caddie programme. Pros: Entry into the golf world. Cons & risks: Irregular income. Scam warning: As usual. Kenya notes: Some caddies grow into coaches and tournament staff.

Summary

  • Sports and outdoor businesses include pitch rental, bikes, guiding, team building, pool maintenance, kit printing, martial arts, events, physiotherapy, running coaching, water sports and caddying.
  • Safety, insurance and licences (TRA, KWS rules, professional councils) come first.
  • Online bookings and reviews fill slots.
