More event services

Adding to event planning, sound and tents (methods 313–330), these services complete the event package.

What these methods share

  • Bookings: take deposits and confirm in writing.
  • Reliability: events can't be repeated, so turn up on time.
  • Safety: electrical and structural safety at venues.

960. Stage and lighting hire

What it is: Supplying stages, trusses and lighting for concerts, weddings and corporate events. How it works: Book → set up → operate → dismantle.

SkillsLighting design, rigging safety
Cost & equipmentLights, trusses, stage platforms, transport
Where to find workEvents, churches, concerts, corporate
PaymentPer event
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Partner with sound companies; start with basic lighting packages. Pros: Pairs with sound hire. Cons & risks: Equipment costs. Scam warning: Rig safely: falling equipment injures people. Kenya notes: Lighting is increasingly expected at weddings and concerts.

961. Event ushering and hostess agency

What it is: Providing trained ushers, hostesses and brand ambassadors for events. How it works: Recruit and train staff → supply for events → manage on the day.

SkillsRecruitment, coordination
Cost & equipmentLow: uniforms, training
Where to find workCorporate events, conferences, weddings, product launches
PaymentPer staff per day (margin)
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Build a reliable team; partner with event planners. Pros: Low start-up. Cons & risks: Staff reliability. Scam warning: Pay staff fairly and on time. Kenya notes: Corporate launches often hire ushers.

962. Bouncy castles and kids' rides hire

What it is: Renting bouncy castles, trampolines and play equipment for parties and fun days. How it works: Deliver → set up safely → supervise → collect.

SkillsSafety supervision
Cost & equipmentEquipment, blower, transport
Where to find workKids' parties, schools, churches, malls
PaymentPer event
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Start with one bouncy castle; always provide a supervisor. Pros: Popular at events. Cons & risks: Weather; wear and tear. Scam warning: Anchor equipment properly and supervise children. Kenya notes: School fun days and kids' parties create demand.

963. LED screen hire

What it is: Renting LED screens for events, churches, rallies and sports viewing. How it works: Book → set up → operate → collect.

SkillsAV setup
Cost & equipmentLED panels, processors, transport
Where to find workEvents, churches, corporates, campaigns
PaymentPer day
Realistic earningsMedium to high
Time to first incomeMonths

How to start: Partner with event planners; offer operators with screens. Pros: High rates per event. Cons & risks: Expensive equipment. Scam warning: Insure equipment. Kenya notes: Large events and churches increasingly use LED screens.

964. Wedding car hire and decoration

What it is: Renting decorated cars for weddings, with drivers. How it works: Book → decorate → drive the couple → return.

SkillsDriving, customer service
Cost & equipmentVehicles (owned or partnered), décor, insurance
Where to find workCouples, wedding planners
PaymentPer event
Realistic earningsMedium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Partner with owners of clean, smart vehicles. Pros: Steady wedding season demand. Cons & risks: Vehicle wear. Scam warning: Confirm bookings with deposits. Kenya notes: Weddings in Kenya often feature decorated car convoys.

Summary

  • More event services include stage lighting, ushering agencies, kids' rides, LED screens and wedding cars.
  • Take deposits, confirm in writing and be reliable.
  • Safety for rigging and children's equipment matters.
