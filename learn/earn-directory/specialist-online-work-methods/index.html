Specialist online work

These are more specialised online roles that aren't covered in earlier categories: niche skills that international and local clients pay for remotely. Most build on general digital, writing, design or tech skills.

What these methods share

  • Specialisation pays more than generic tasks.
  • Portfolio and proof: samples, certifications and case studies.
  • Platforms and direct clients: freelance marketplaces, LinkedIn and referrals (freelancing subject).

664. UGC (user-generated content) creator

What it is: Creating authentic-style videos and photos for brands to use in ads and social media (you don't need a big following). How it works: Brand brief → film product demos, testimonials, unboxings → deliver files.

SkillsOn-camera presence, filming and editing on a phone
Cost & equipmentSmartphone, lighting, mic
Where to find workBrands, UGC platforms, agencies, direct outreach
PaymentPer video or package
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Create a UGC portfolio with products you own. Pros: No large following needed. Cons & risks: Must disclose if content is presented as reviews. Scam warning: Fake brands asking you to pay for "product samples". Kenya notes: Kenyan and African brands want relatable local creators.

665. Fact-checker

What it is: Verifying claims in articles, videos, reports and social media. How it works: Research → verify sources → report findings.

SkillsResearch, critical thinking, source verification
Cost & equipmentLaptop
Where to find workMedia houses, fact-checking organisations, publishers, platforms
PaymentPer article or contract
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks to months

How to start: Learn verification techniques (reverse image search, source tracing). Pros: Meaningful work. Cons & risks: Online harassment risk. Scam warning: As usual. Kenya notes: Elections increase demand for fact-checking.
