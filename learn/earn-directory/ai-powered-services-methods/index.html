AI-powered services

AI tools make many tasks faster: drafting, design, research, video, coding and automation. People earn by using AI to deliver services better and faster, by teaching others to use AI, and by building AI solutions for businesses. The rules: be honest about AI use where clients care, check accuracy, and respect privacy and copyright.

What these methods share

  • You remain responsible for the quality and accuracy of AI-assisted work (hallucinations and fact-checking).
  • Disclose AI use where clients or platforms require it.
  • Data privacy: don't paste confidential client data into tools without permission.
  • No get-rich-quick claims: "AI makes passive income automatically" is marketing, not reality.
  • See AI tools for work and study and earning with AI.

364. AI-assisted content production service

What it is: Producing blogs, social posts and newsletters faster with AI drafting plus human editing and fact-checking. How it works: Brief → AI-assisted draft → editing, voice, facts → delivery.

SkillsWriting, editing, prompting (prompting)
Cost & equipmentLaptop, AI tool subscriptions
Where to find workSMEs, agencies
PaymentPer piece or monthly
Realistic earningsLow to medium
Time to first incomeWeeks

How to start: Show samples with clear quality standards. Pros: Faster output. Cons & risks: Clients may ban AI; generic content has low value. Scam warning: Don't sell unedited AI text as "human-written". Kenya notes: Local knowledge and accuracy are your advantage over raw AI output.
