Level 3: Earning with AI skills

AI has created new kinds of paid work, and it makes existing freelance work faster. Here are realistic ways to earn with AI skills from Kenya, from beginner to advanced.

1. AI training and data annotation (beginner to expert)

Companies pay people to rate, correct and write examples for AI models, and to label data. Specialists (coders, maths graduates, doctors, lawyers, speakers of African languages) earn more. Full details and platforms: AI training & data annotation.

2. AI-assisted freelance services

Use AI to deliver normal services faster and better, not to deliver AI junk:

Many clients now ask whether you use AI. Be honest: "I use AI tools to work faster, and I check and edit everything myself."

3. AI services for local businesses

Most Kenyan small businesses know AI exists but not how to use it. You can sell:

  • AI setup and training: teach a shop owner and staff to use ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini for marketing and customer replies (half-day workshop).
  • Content packages: monthly social media posts produced with AI and edited by you.
  • Prompt and template libraries: a business-specific set of prompts (for example, for a real-estate agency or a school).
  • Simple automations: forms → spreadsheet → AI summary → email (see the next lesson).
  • WhatsApp catalogue and auto-replies, upgraded later to an AI assistant.

4. Prompt and automation specialist (intermediate)

Businesses need people who can design reliable AI workflows: writing system prompts, testing them, connecting tools with Zapier/Make/n8n, and documenting how they work. Learn the automation lesson, then build 2 to 3 example workflows for your portfolio.

5. Building AI-powered apps (advanced)

Developers who can connect AI models to websites, apps and business systems are in high demand: chatbots trained on a company's documents, AI document processing, AI features in school or hospital systems. Levels 4 and 5 of this course start you on that path; combine with the Python, JavaScript, PHP and Flutter subjects.

6. Teaching AI

Run AI literacy classes for teachers, SACCO members, church groups and youth programmes (such as Jitume and Ajira centres). Prepare simple, practical sessions using Levels 1 and 2 of this course.

Prices depend on your skill and the client's size: start modestly, collect results and testimonials, then raise prices.

Ethics that protect your income

  • Don't sell AI output as purely human work when a client asked for that.
  • Check facts before delivering: one hallucinated statistic can lose a client.
  • Respect copyright: don't feed clients' confidential documents into tools without permission; don't copy other creators' work.
  • Avoid contract cheating (writing students' assignments with AI). It's unethical and platforms ban it.

Your 30-day AI earning plan

  1. Week 1: Finish Levels 1 and 2. Pick one service from this lesson.
  2. Week 2: Create 3 portfolio samples using AI + your own editing.
  3. Week 3: Offer the service to 10 local businesses and create one marketplace profile.
  4. Week 4: Deliver your first job, ask for a testimonial, refine your prompts and process.
