G. Marketing services (ways 68–78): social media management, ads, SEO, affiliate and WhatsApp marketing

Most small businesses know they should be online, but the owner is busy running the shop, clinic, school or farm. They'll pay someone who can bring them customers: posting consistently, running adverts, getting them onto Google Maps, managing WhatsApp enquiries. Marketing services are some of the most practical ways to earn in Kenya because the clients are all around you, and results are easy to explain.

Core skills for all marketing services

  • Understanding customers: who they are, what they want, where they spend time online
  • Clear writing and basic design (Canva)
  • Using analytics to show results (reach, clicks, messages, sales)
  • Reliability and communication: clients want consistency more than genius
  • Learn on this hub: the Digital marketing subject, AI tools, Graphic design. Free certifications: Google Skillshop, Meta Blueprint, HubSpot Academy.

How to get your first local clients

  1. Practise on 1–2 businesses free or cheap for one month (a relative's shop, your church, a friend's salon) in exchange for a testimonial and results you can show.
  2. Create a simple offer with a clear price: "12 posts a month + replies to messages: KSh X".
  3. Walk in and talk to businesses near you: show a sample of what their page could look like.
  4. Report results monthly: followers, reach, messages received, bookings. Results keep clients paying.

68. Social media management

What it is. Running a business's social media: planning content, designing posts, writing captions, posting on schedule, replying to comments and DMs, and reporting results.

How to start, step by step:

  1. Learn each platform's basics: Facebook Pages and Meta Business Suite (schedule posts for Facebook and Instagram free), TikTok business accounts, LinkedIn pages.
  2. Create a content plan template: monthly calendar with themes (product posts, tips, testimonials, behind-the-scenes, offers).
  3. Get 1–2 practice clients; document before/after numbers.
  4. Offer packages:
  5. Basic: 8–12 posts/month on 2 platforms
  6. Standard: + stories/Reels + replying to messages
  7. Premium: + ad management (method 69) + monthly report
  8. Use tools: Meta Business Suite, Canva (with Content Planner), Google Sheets for calendars, CapCut for Reels.

Who pays: salons, restaurants, schools, clinics, real estate agents, churches, hardware shops, hotels, boutiques, car yards, NGOs.

69. Facebook and Instagram ads manager

What it is. Creating and managing paid adverts on Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp-click ads) to reach specific audiences: location, age, interests.

How to start:

  1. Learn Meta Ads Manager: campaign objectives (awareness, traffic, engagement/messages, leads, sales), audiences, budgets, placements, the Meta Pixel.
  2. Take Meta Blueprint free courses.
  3. Run a small test campaign with your own money (e.g. KSh 500–1,000) for a practice business to learn.
  4. Understand key numbers: reach, CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions), CTR (click-through rate), cost per message/lead, return on ad spend.
  5. Charge a management fee separate from the client's ad budget (the client pays Meta directly with their card).

Click-to-WhatsApp ads

"Click-to-WhatsApp" ads are very effective for Kenyan businesses: customers tap the ad and land in a WhatsApp chat with the business, where most local sales actually happen.

70. Google Ads

What it is. Paid adverts on Google Search, YouTube and websites. Search ads catch people at the moment they're searching ("emergency plumber Nairobi").

How to start: take Google Skillshop courses and the Google Ads certifications (free); learn keyword match types, ad copy, landing pages and conversion tracking; start with small local search campaigns for clients with clear services (clinics, schools, lawyers, service businesses).

71. SEO for local businesses

What it is. Helping businesses rank higher on Google organically: website improvements, content, local listings, reviews and links.

How to start:

  1. Learn SEO fundamentals (this hub's Marketing and HTML SEO lessons, Google's SEO Starter Guide).
  2. Do an SEO audit for a business: page titles, descriptions, speed, mobile-friendliness, Google Business Profile, reviews, content gaps.
  3. Offer an audit + fix package, then a monthly plan (content, profile updates, review requests, reports).
  4. Show results with Google Search Console and Google Business Profile insights.

Honesty rule. Never promise "number one on Google guaranteed". Promise work and reports; results come over months.

72. Google Business Profile setup

What it is. Creating, verifying and optimising a business's free Google Business Profile so it appears on Google Maps and in "near me" searches with photos, hours, phone number and reviews.

How to start, step by step:

  1. Learn the process on Google's help pages: create the profile, choose the right category, add accurate address/service area, hours, phone, website, photos, products/services.
  2. Complete verification (Google chooses the method: video, phone/SMS, email, or postcard where applicable).
  3. Optimise: 10+ quality photos, services with descriptions, regular posts, Q&A, and a system for getting genuine reviews from real customers (never fake reviews, which break Google's rules).
  4. Offer this as a fixed-price service to every business on your street without one. It's quick to deliver and easy to understand.

Pair it with: a simple website (method 79) and WhatsApp Business setup (method 75).

73. Email marketing

What it is. Building email lists and sending newsletters, promotions and automated sequences (welcome series, abandoned cart reminders) for businesses.

How to start: learn Mailchimp, MailerLite or Brevo (free tiers); understand consent (only email people who signed up; Kenya's Data Protection Act applies), list building with sign-up forms, segmentation and automation. Offer to online shops, coaches, schools and event organisers.

74. Affiliate marketing

What it is. Earning a commission when people buy through your unique link.

Programmes to know (check current terms and whether they accept Kenyan affiliates):

  • Jumia's affiliate (KOL) programme: earn commission on Jumia sales from your links.
  • Amazon Associates: international products; check eligibility and payment options for Kenya.
  • Software and hosting affiliates: many web hosting companies, website builders and online tools pay per sign-up.
  • Course platforms and digital products (Udemy, Selar products, others).
  • Local brands and fintechs running referral/affiliate programmes.

How to start:

  1. Choose a niche where you can genuinely recommend products (e.g. "best budget phones", "tools for freelancers").
  2. Create helpful content: reviews, comparisons, tutorials (blog, YouTube, TikTok, WhatsApp channel).
  3. Place links naturally and disclose they're affiliate links.
  4. Track which content earns; do more of it.

Watch out. Promoting forex "signal groups", unlicensed investment schemes or betting can harm your followers and may be illegal. Only promote products you'd recommend to family.

75. WhatsApp Business setup and marketing

What it is. Setting up WhatsApp Business for clients: business profile, catalogue with prices and photos, quick replies, greeting and away messages, labels for orders, broadcast lists, WhatsApp Channels, and click-to-chat links/QR codes.

How to start: master the free WhatsApp Business app features; create a setup checklist; offer setup + training as a fixed package to small businesses, plus monthly management (status updates, broadcasts, catalogue updates). For bigger businesses, learn about the WhatsApp Business Platform (API) via official providers for automated messages and chatbots.

Rules. Only message customers who agreed; spam gets numbers banned.

76. Community management

What it is. Managing online communities: Facebook groups, Telegram and WhatsApp communities, Discord servers, alumni and church groups, brand communities.

Tasks: welcoming members, moderating, starting discussions, organising events/lives, enforcing rules, reporting.

How to start: run your own community in a niche to learn, then offer services to brands, creators, crypto/tech projects (be careful with unregulated ones), NGOs and associations.

77. Lead generation and appointment setting

What it is. Finding potential customers for businesses and booking meetings or calls with them, by email, LinkedIn, phone or social media. Common for agencies, B2B companies, real estate and coaches abroad.

How to start: learn LinkedIn prospecting, writing short outreach messages, CRM tools (HubSpot free CRM, Google Sheets), and phone etiquette. Offer on Upwork ("appointment setter", "lead generation specialist"). Pay can include commission per booked meeting or sale; prefer a base fee plus commission.

78. Influencer marketing management

What it is. Connecting brands with creators: finding suitable influencers, negotiating, managing campaigns, ensuring disclosure, and reporting results. Or managing creators (talent management) by finding them brand deals for a percentage.

How to start: build relationships with micro-influencers in a niche, learn campaign planning and contracts, start with small local brand campaigns, and track results (reach, engagement, sales codes).

Building a small marketing agency

Once you have several clients:

  1. Standardise packages and an onboarding form (logins, brand assets, goals).
  2. Use contracts with scope, payment terms (monthly in advance) and notice periods.
  3. Hire or partner: a designer, a video editor, a writer.
  4. Monthly reports with results and next month's plan.
  5. Register your business (business name on eCitizen/BRS), get a KRA PIN for the business and keep records.

Practice tasks

  1. Choose a local business and write a one-month content calendar with 12 post ideas.
  2. Create a free Meta Business Suite account and schedule 3 posts for a practice page.
  3. Audit a local business's Google presence (Maps listing, photos, reviews, website) and list 5 improvements.
  4. Set up WhatsApp Business with a catalogue of 5 items for a practice business.
  5. Complete one free certification (Google Skillshop, Meta Blueprint or HubSpot).

Summary

  • Marketing services: social media management, Meta ads, Google Ads, local SEO, Google Business Profile, email marketing, affiliate marketing, WhatsApp Business, community management, lead generation and influencer management.
  • Local businesses everywhere need these services; start with practice clients and show results.
  • Charge clear packages and management fees (ad budgets are separate).
  • Be honest: no guaranteed rankings, fake reviews, spam or harmful promotions.
