Vetted talent networks: Toptal, Andela, Turing, Arc, Braintrust and Proxify

Vetted networks test your skills before you join, then match you with companies. They pay well and you don't bid against hundreds of people, but entry is hard. Most focus on developers, with some design, data and product roles.

Check before you rely on it

Fees, accepted countries and payout options change often. Confirm on each platform's official website.

9. Toptal

What it is: A selective network that says it accepts only a small percentage of applicants.

WorkSoftware development, design, finance, product and project management
Fees for youFree for freelancers; clients pay Toptal
Getting paidPayoneer, bank or other methods in your account
KenyaAccepts applicants worldwide, including Kenya
CompetitionVery selective
Best forSenior developers and specialists

How to start: Prepare for language, timed coding and live interview stages; apply when you're truly senior. Pros: High rates; quality clients. Cons: Very hard screening. Watch out: Applications are free; ignore anyone selling “Toptal referrals”.

10. Andela

What it is: A talent network that grew from training and placing African software engineers with global companies.

WorkSoftware engineering, data, cloud, QA
Fees for youFree for engineers; companies pay Andela
Getting paidPaid per the contract (methods vary)
KenyaStrong focus on African talent, including Kenya
CompetitionSelective
Best forMid-level and senior engineers in Africa

How to start: Create your profile, take the assessments and keep your skills profile current. Pros: Built with African engineers in mind. Cons: Competitive; matching can take time. Watch out: Use the official Andela site only.

11. Turing

What it is: A platform that vets developers and matches them with remote roles at US companies.

WorkSoftware development, AI and data roles
Fees for youFree for developers
Getting paidPaid per the contract
KenyaOpen to developers worldwide
CompetitionSelective
Best forDevelopers wanting long-term remote roles

How to start: Complete the profile and coding tests; keep your availability updated. Pros: Long-term roles. Cons: Tests are demanding; waiting for matches. Watch out: Never pay for “guaranteed placement”.

12. Arc

What it is: A remote job and freelance network for developers, with a vetting process.

WorkSoftware development, data, design
Fees for youFree for developers
Getting paidPaid per contract
KenyaOpen to developers worldwide
CompetitionSelective
Best forDevelopers wanting remote freelance or full-time roles

How to start: Complete vetting, write a clear profile, and apply to matched roles. Pros: Good remote opportunities. Cons: Competition from many countries. Watch out: Beware of fake recruiters using the Arc name.

13. Braintrust

What it is: A talent network where freelancers keep 100% of their rate and clients pay the network fee.

WorkDevelopment, design, product, marketing
Fees for youFree for freelancers
Getting paidPaid per contract
KenyaOpen to many countries
CompetitionSelective
Best forExperienced professionals

How to start: Apply, get vetted, and apply to roles that match. Pros: You keep your full rate. Cons: Fewer roles than big marketplaces. Watch out: Same advice: official site only.

14. Proxify

What it is: A network matching vetted developers with companies, mostly in Europe.

WorkSoftware development
Fees for youFree for developers
Getting paidPaid per contract
KenyaAccepted countries vary: check whether Kenya is eligible
CompetitionSelective
Best forExperienced developers

How to start: Check eligibility, then complete the screening. Pros: Long-term contracts. Cons: Country limits. Watch out: Don't misstate your location.

Summary

  • Vetted networks (Toptal, Andela, Turing, Arc, Braintrust, Proxify) are free for freelancers and pay well, but screening is hard.
  • Andela focuses on African engineers; Toptal is the most selective.
  • Never pay for referrals or “guaranteed placement”.
