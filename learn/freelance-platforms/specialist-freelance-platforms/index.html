Specialist platforms by skill: 99designs, Dribbble, Behance, Reedsy, ProZ, Codeable, Voices and more

If you have a clear skill, a specialist platform puts you in front of clients who already want exactly that. Profiles and portfolios matter more than bids.

Check before you rely on it

Fees, accepted countries and payout options change often. Confirm on each platform's official website.

15. 99designs (by Vista)

What it is: A design marketplace with contests and direct one-to-one projects.

WorkLogos, brand identity, packaging, web and app design
Fees for youA platform fee per project
Getting paidPayPal or Payoneer (check current options)
KenyaOpen to designers worldwide
CompetitionHigh in contests
Best forGraphic designers building a portfolio

How to start: Enter a few contests to learn, then aim for direct projects once you have wins. Pros: Lots of design briefs. Cons: Contests can mean unpaid work if you don't win. Watch out: Never copy others' designs or use unlicensed fonts and images.

16. Dribbble

What it is: A design community with a jobs board and freelance project listings.

WorkUI/UX, product design, illustration, branding
Fees for youFree to use; paid Pro features
Getting paidPaid by clients directly or through the platform (depending on the feature)
KenyaOpen worldwide
CompetitionHigh
Best forUI/UX designers and illustrators

How to start: Post polished shots regularly; complete your profile; apply to freelance listings. Pros: Great for showing design work. Cons: Competitive. Watch out: Verify clients before starting work off-platform.

17. Behance (Adobe)

What it is: Adobe's portfolio network, with a jobs and freelance hiring section.

WorkDesign, illustration, photography, motion graphics
Fees for youFree; freelance payments through Adobe may carry fees
Getting paidPayment methods vary by country
KenyaOpen worldwide
CompetitionHigh
Best forCreatives who want a portfolio that clients discover

How to start: Publish case-study style projects showing your process, not just final images. Pros: Strong portfolio exposure. Cons: Discovery takes time. Watch out: Watermark previews when sending work before payment.

18. Reedsy

What it is: A curated marketplace of editors, designers and marketers for authors and publishers.

WorkBook editing, proofreading, cover design, ghostwriting, book marketing
Fees for youA fee on projects
Getting paidPayment methods in your account
KenyaVetted; open to professionals worldwide
CompetitionSelective
Best forExperienced editors and book designers

How to start: Apply with publishing credits or strong samples. Pros: Serious clients. Cons: Hard to get accepted. Watch out: Respect authors' confidentiality.

19. ProBlogger Jobs

What it is: A long-running job board for blog and content writers.

WorkBlog writing, content marketing, copywriting
Fees for youFree for writers
Getting paidPaid by the client directly
KenyaOpen worldwide; some jobs want specific locations
CompetitionHigh
Best forWriters with samples

How to start: Keep a small portfolio of published posts; apply quickly to new listings. Pros: Real writing jobs. Cons: Many applicants. Watch out: Agree payment terms before writing; no unpaid “test articles” longer than a short sample.

20. ProZ.com

What it is: The largest community and job marketplace for translators and interpreters.

WorkTranslation, localisation, interpreting, subtitling
Fees for youFree basic membership; paid membership for more jobs
Getting paidPaid by agencies or clients directly
KenyaOpen worldwide; Kiswahili and other African languages are in demand
CompetitionMedium to high
Best forTranslators (including Kiswahili–English)

How to start: Complete your profile, list language pairs, and check agencies' reputations on the platform's “Blue Board”. Pros: Big translation network. Cons: Some low rates. Watch out: Check the agency's rating before accepting work.

21. TranslatorsCafé

What it is: A translator community with job listings and an agency directory.

WorkTranslation and localisation
Fees for youFree and paid memberships
Getting paidPaid by clients directly
KenyaOpen worldwide
CompetitionMedium
Best forTranslators

How to start: Build a profile and respond to listings. Pros: More translation leads. Cons: Smaller than ProZ. Watch out: Beware fake translation jobs that send overpayment cheques.

22. Codeable

What it is: A vetted marketplace for WordPress and WooCommerce experts.

WorkWordPress development, WooCommerce, site fixes
Fees for youClients pay the platform fee
Getting paidPayment methods in your account
KenyaVetted; open to developers worldwide
CompetitionSelective
Best forExperienced WordPress developers

How to start: Pass the technical screening with a strong WordPress portfolio. Pros: High-quality WordPress clients. Cons: Strict vetting. Watch out: Follow coding and security standards.

23. Kolabtree

What it is: A platform connecting scientists, researchers and data experts with businesses.

WorkResearch, data analysis, statistics, medical writing
Fees for youA fee on projects
Getting paidPayment methods in your account
KenyaOpen to qualified experts worldwide
CompetitionSelective
Best forPhDs, statisticians, scientists

How to start: Apply with your credentials and publications. Pros: Uses deep expertise. Cons: Narrow audience. Watch out: Don't do students' graded assignments: it's academic cheating.

24. Voices

What it is: A marketplace for voice-over talent.

WorkAds, explainers, e-learning, audiobooks, IVR
Fees for youPaid membership options and fees on jobs
Getting paidPayment methods in your account
KenyaOpen worldwide; African accents are requested for some projects
CompetitionHigh
Best forVoice actors with good home recording setups

How to start: Record clean demos; audition quickly and professionally. Pros: Big voice-over client base. Cons: Membership costs; many auditions. Watch out: Never pay “agents” for guaranteed jobs.

Summary

  • Specialist platforms reward clear skills: 99designs, Dribbble and Behance for design; Reedsy and ProBlogger for writing and editing; ProZ and TranslatorsCafé for translation; Codeable for WordPress; Kolabtree for research; Voices for voice-over.
  • A strong portfolio matters more than bidding.
  • Respect copyright and confidentiality, and avoid academic cheating.
