Remote job boards: LinkedIn, We Work Remotely, Remote OK, Remotive, Wellfound and more

Remote job boards list contracts and full-time jobs with companies. There are no platform fees for you, but also no payment protection, so check every company carefully. Filter for “worldwide” or roles open to Africa.

Check before you rely on it

Fees, accepted countries and payout options change often. Confirm on each platform's official website.

25. LinkedIn

What it is: The world's professional network, with jobs, freelance services and direct client contact.

WorkEvery professional field
Fees for youFree; Premium optional
Getting paidPaid by employers or clients directly
KenyaFully available in Kenya
CompetitionHigh
Best forEveryone: it's also your public CV

How to start: Complete your profile, post about your work weekly, and use “Open to work” and service pages. Pros: Direct access to decision-makers. Cons: Takes consistent effort. Watch out: Fake recruiters ask for fees or move you to Telegram: real ones don't.

26. We Work Remotely

What it is: One of the largest remote job boards.

WorkProgramming, design, support, marketing, sales
Fees for youFree for job seekers
Getting paidPaid by the employer
KenyaFilter for roles open worldwide
CompetitionHigh
Best forExperienced remote workers

How to start: Apply to roles marked anywhere in the world; tailor each application. Pros: Quality companies. Cons: Many roles limited to certain countries. Watch out: Check the company's website and LinkedIn before sharing documents.

27. Remote OK

What it is: A large remote job board with location filters.

WorkTech, marketing, support, design
Fees for youFree for job seekers
Getting paidPaid by the employer
KenyaUse the worldwide filter
CompetitionHigh
Best forTech and marketing workers

How to start: Set alerts for worldwide roles in your field. Pros: Lots of listings. Cons: Mixed quality. Watch out: Avoid roles that ask you to buy equipment upfront.

28. Remotive

What it is: A curated remote job board and community.

WorkSoftware, customer support, marketing, product
Fees for youFree for job seekers
Getting paidPaid by the employer
KenyaSome roles open worldwide
CompetitionHigh
Best forRemote job seekers

How to start: Use filters and apply early. Pros: Curated listings. Cons: Location limits on many roles. Watch out: Same company checks.

29. Working Nomads

What it is: A remote job board collecting listings from many sources.

WorkDevelopment, design, marketing, admin
Fees for youFree
Getting paidPaid by the employer
KenyaSome worldwide roles
CompetitionHigh
Best forRemote job seekers

How to start: Subscribe to category emails. Pros: Wide coverage. Cons: Duplicate listings. Watch out: Verify listings on the company's own site.

30. Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent)

What it is: A job platform for startups.

WorkEngineering, product, design, growth roles
Fees for youFree
Getting paidPaid by the startup
KenyaSome startups hire remotely worldwide
CompetitionHigh
Best forPeople wanting to work with startups

How to start: Build a profile; filter for remote roles open to your region. Pros: Startup roles with equity sometimes. Cons: Many US-only roles. Watch out: Check startup legitimacy and funding.

31. Himalayas

What it is: A remote job board with company profiles and timezone filters.

WorkTech, marketing, operations, support
Fees for youFree
Getting paidPaid by the employer
KenyaFilter for roles open to Africa
CompetitionMedium to high
Best forRemote job seekers

How to start: Set your location and timezone so matching roles show. Pros: Clear company info. Cons: Competitive. Watch out: Same checks before sharing ID documents.

32. JustRemote

What it is: A remote job board with job and contract listings.

WorkDevelopment, marketing, design, writing
Fees for youFree basic access
Getting paidPaid by the employer
KenyaSome roles open worldwide
CompetitionMedium
Best forRemote job seekers

How to start: Filter by “anywhere” roles. Pros: Extra listings. Cons: Some features paid. Watch out: Never pay to apply.

Summary

  • LinkedIn is essential for everyone; We Work Remotely, Remote OK, Remotive, Working Nomads, Wellfound, Himalayas and JustRemote list remote roles.
  • Filter for roles open worldwide or to Africa.
  • No payment protection: verify companies, and never pay to apply or buy equipment upfront.
