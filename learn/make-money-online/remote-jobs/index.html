Remote jobs

A remote job is a normal job (salary, fixed hours, a team) that you do from home, often for a company abroad or a Kenyan company with remote staff.

Common remote roles

Customer support, virtual assistant, software developer, QA tester, data analyst, content writer, social media manager, sales/business development, tech support, project manager.

Where to find them

  • LinkedIn Jobs (filter "Remote").
  • We Work Remotely, Remote OK, Remotive, Working Nomads.
  • BrighterMonday, Fuzu, MyJobMag Kenya (local, some remote).
  • Company career pages; many global tech companies hire in Africa.
  • Programmes like ALX, Andela and similar have hiring partners (check current offerings).

Getting hired

  • CV: one page, results with numbers ("Answered 60+ tickets a day with 95% satisfaction").
  • Portfolio or GitHub for technical roles.
  • Cover letter tailored to each job: why this company, how you solve their problem.
  • Equipment: laptop, stable internet plus a backup (mobile hotspot), headset, quiet place, backup power (power bank for router or a UPS).
  • Interview: test your camera and mic, dress properly, have examples ready (use the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result).

Red flags

  • Asked to pay for training, equipment, "processing" or a work permit.
  • Interview only on Telegram/WhatsApp text, no company website or LinkedIn presence.
  • A cheque to buy equipment "from our vendor". A classic scam.
