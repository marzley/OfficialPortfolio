How to choose

Ask yourself:

  1. What do I enjoy enough to practise every day?
  2. What am I already a bit good at? (Designing church posters? Explaining maths? Always on Instagram?)
  3. Does it pay well enough? Is there demand (search the skill on Upwork and Fiverr and count the jobs)?

Learn by doing

For every skill: learn → copy a professional example → make your own version → show it to people → improve. Keep all your work: it becomes your portfolio.

The combination that pays best for many Kenyans: web development + M-Pesa integration. Every business wants a website that takes M-Pesa. The HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP tracks in this hub are the path.
