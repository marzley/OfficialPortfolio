Making money online: the honest truth

Thousands of Kenyans earn a living online, as freelancers, content creators, online sellers and remote workers. Kenya is one of Africa's biggest freelancing countries. But the people who succeed have one thing in common: they sell a skill or a product that someone is willing to pay for. There is no app that pays you for doing nothing.

What actually works

  1. Selling a skill (freelancing, remote jobs): web development, design, writing, video editing, virtual assistance, data work, social media management, tutoring.
  2. Selling products: physical goods (online shop, WhatsApp/Instagram selling) or digital products (e-books, templates, courses).
  3. Building an audience (YouTube, TikTok, blogging) and earning from ads, sponsors and your own products. Slower, but it compounds.

What doesn't work (and costs people money)

  • "Invest KSh 1,000 and earn KSh 300 daily" apps, forex "account managers", "task" apps that ask for deposits, pyramid schemes. See the lesson on scams.

What you need to start

  • A smartphone; a laptop is strongly recommended for most skills (a good second-hand one from KSh 20,000–35,000 is enough).
  • Reliable internet (home fibre or bundles) and a quiet place to work.
  • A professional email address (firstname.lastname@gmail.com) and a LinkedIn profile.
  • M-Pesa, and later a bank account, PayPal and/or Payoneer to receive payments.
  • Time: plan for 3–6 months of learning and building before regular income. Treat it like starting a business.

A realistic timeline

MonthFocus
1–2Learn one skill properly (this learning hub, YouTube, free courses)
2–3Build 3–5 portfolio pieces; offer 1–2 jobs cheaply to local businesses or friends for reviews
3–4Create profiles, send proposals daily, pitch local businesses
4–6First paying clients; raise prices as reviews grow
6–12Repeat clients, referrals, higher rates, maybe a small agency

Tip: Your first client is usually someone you know: a church, a friend's shop, your former school. Real work for a real client beats any certificate.
