Sell digital products

Make it once, sell it many times. Digital products have no stock and no delivery costs.

Ideas that sell

  • E-books and guides: "Complete guide to poultry farming in Kenya", "KCSE Chemistry revision notes".
  • Templates: CV templates, business plan templates, budget spreadsheets, Canva social media templates, website templates.
  • Courses: recorded video lessons (like the videos in this hub), cohort classes on WhatsApp or Zoom.
  • Printables: school timetables, wedding planners, colouring pages.
  • Stock assets: photos of Kenyan life, illustrations, sound effects, presets.
  • Code: WordPress themes/plugins, scripts, website templates.

Where to sell

  • Selar (selar.co): popular with African creators, supports local payments.
  • Gumroad, Payhip: international, easy setup.
  • Your own website with Paystack or M-Pesa checkout (full control, no marketplace fee).
  • Etsy (templates, printables): check payout options for Kenya.
  • Envato Market / Creative Market: for designers and developers (strict quality review).

Steps

  1. Find a problem people already pay to solve (look at questions in Facebook groups, comments, what people ask you).
  2. Make a small first version fast (a 20-page guide, 5 templates).
  3. Get feedback from 5–10 people, improve it.
  4. Set a price (KSh 300–2,000 for small guides and templates; more for courses).
  5. Market it through your content, WhatsApp status and groups, email list, and affiliates who earn a commission.

Protect your work

  • Add your name and website to every page.
  • Accept that some copying will happen; focus on updates and support buyers can't get from a pirated copy.
