Social media management

Businesses know they should be on social media but have no time. You can run their pages for a monthly fee.

What you do for a client

  1. Strategy: who are the customers, which platforms (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn), what the business wants (bookings, calls, shop visits).
  2. Content plan: a monthly calendar (e.g. 12 posts + 8 stories + 4 reels).
  3. Create content: designs in Canva, short videos, captions, hashtags.
  4. Post and schedule with Meta Business Suite (free) or other tools.
  5. Engage: reply to comments and messages quickly (or agree who does).
  6. Report monthly: reach, followers, messages, what worked.
  7. Optional: run ads (Meta Ads) with the client's budget.

Getting clients

  • Start with businesses whose pages look abandoned: restaurants, salons, gyms, schools, real estate agents.
  • Offer a free audit: 3 quick improvements for their page.
  • Show before/after results from your first clients.

Skills to learn

Canva, basic photography and video (CapCut), copywriting, Meta Ads Manager, analytics. Free training: Meta Blueprint, Google Digital Skills for Africa.
