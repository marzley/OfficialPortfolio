Getting paid in Kenya

Local clients

  • M-Pesa (Buy Goods till or Paybill for a business; personal number for small amounts). Always send a receipt or invoice.
  • Bank transfer for bigger amounts.
  • Ask for a deposit before starting (commonly 50%).

International clients and platforms

Protect yourself

  • Use the same names as on your national ID on every payment account; mismatches get accounts frozen.
  • Turn on 2-step verification for email, PayPal, Payoneer and your freelancing accounts.
  • Never share OTP codes. Real PayPal or Safaricom staff won't ask.
  • Watch exchange rates and fees: compare what arrives in your M-Pesa, not just the fee shown.
  • Overpayment scam: a "client" sends too much and asks you to refund the difference. The original payment later reverses. Refuse.

Keep records

Keep a simple spreadsheet: date, client, amount, method, fee, amount received in KSh. You'll need it for KRA (see the taxes lesson) and to know your real income.
