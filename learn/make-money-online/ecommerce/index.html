Sell physical products online

Where Kenyans sell

  • WhatsApp Business: catalogue, quick replies, labels for orders. Many shops run entirely on WhatsApp.
  • Instagram and Facebook: product photos, reels, Facebook Marketplace, groups.
  • TikTok: short demos drive huge demand (use TikTok Shop where available).
  • Jumia and other marketplaces: become a seller; they bring buyers and handle some delivery.
  • Your own online shop with M-Pesa checkout: builds a brand and repeat customers (e.g. WooCommerce + M-Pesa, or a custom shop).

Running it well

  1. Product: something with demand and margin (after buying price, packaging and delivery, you should still keep 30%+).
  2. Photos: clean background, daylight, several angles, a short video.
  3. Payments: M-Pesa till number (looks professional and keeps business money separate), cash on delivery only if you trust the area and customer.
  4. Delivery: boda riders in town, courier/matatu parcel services for other towns, pick-up points.
  5. Customer service: reply fast, confirm orders, send tracking or rider numbers, follow up after delivery.
  6. Reviews: post customer photos and screenshots (with permission).

Dropshipping and pre-orders

  • Dropshipping: you sell, a supplier delivers. Works best with local suppliers so delivery is quick. Very long delivery times from abroad cause complaints.
  • Pre-orders: collect orders and a deposit before you import stock. Lowers risk.
