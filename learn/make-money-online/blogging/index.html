Blogging and ad income

A blog earns from display ads (e.g. Google AdSense), affiliate links, sponsored posts, and your own products or services. It is slow (6–12 months to meaningful traffic) but a good article can bring visitors for years.

Step by step

  1. Pick a niche people search for and you can write 50+ articles about: e.g. "study tips for university students in Kenya", "poultry farming", "tech how-tos", "budget travel in East Africa".
  2. Get a domain and hosting: a .co.ke or .com domain and cheap cPanel hosting, then install WordPress (or build your own with HTML/CSS from this hub).
  3. Research keywords: what do people type into Google? Use Google autocomplete, "People also ask", and free tools like Google Search Console once you're live.
  4. Write genuinely helpful articles: answer the question fully, with your own experience, photos and steps. 1,000–2,000 words for main topics.
  5. On-page SEO: clear title with the keyword, headings, short paragraphs, internal links, fast mobile pages.
  6. Promote on Facebook groups, WhatsApp, Pinterest, LinkedIn.
  7. Apply for AdSense when you have enough original content and some traffic.

What ads pay

Ad income is measured in RPM (revenue per 1,000 page views). Traffic from Kenya earns less per view than traffic from the US or UK, so:

  • Combine ads with affiliate links and your own services.
  • A blog about "how to build a website" can sell your web design service. That's usually worth far more than ads.

Rules

  • Never click your own ads or ask others to: AdSense will ban you.
  • Don't copy other people's articles; Google penalises it and it's illegal.
  • AI tools can help you outline and edit, but add your own experience or the content won't rank.
