Affiliate marketing

You recommend a product with a special tracking link; when someone buys through it, you earn a commission. No stock, no customer service.

How it works

  1. Join an affiliate programme and get your unique link.
  2. Share it in helpful content: a review, a comparison, a tutorial, a video description.
  3. A visitor clicks (a cookie remembers you for some days) and buys.
  4. You receive a percentage (often 2–10% for physical goods, 20–50% for digital products and software).

Programmes to look at

  • Jumia's affiliate programme (Kenya and other African countries): commission on products sold through your links.
  • Hosting and software companies (web hosting, VPNs, website builders, email tools) often pay high commissions per sign-up.
  • Selar and similar platforms: promote other creators' e-books and courses.
  • Amazon Associates: availability and payouts depend on the country; check whether you can join and get paid from Kenya.
  • Local businesses: offer to promote them for a commission per sale (write the deal down).

What makes it work

  • Trust: only recommend what you've used or researched well. One bad recommendation loses followers.
  • Intent: "best laptop for university students under KSh 50,000" converts far better than a random link.
  • Disclosure: tell people it's an affiliate link. It's honest, and platforms and laws often require it.
  • Traffic: affiliate income needs an audience: a blog, YouTube channel, WhatsApp channel or TikTok.

Example calculation

A video comparing budget phones gets 20,000 views. 2% click the link (400), 3% of those buy (12), average price KSh 18,000, commission 4% → 12 × 18,000 × 0.04 = KSh 8,640. Useful, and it keeps earning while the video gets views.
