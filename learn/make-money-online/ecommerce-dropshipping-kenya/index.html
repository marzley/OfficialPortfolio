E-commerce and dropshipping in Kenya

Selling online in Kenya has never been easier: customers already pay with M-Pesa, order on WhatsApp and Instagram, and couriers deliver countrywide. This lesson lists the platforms, explains dropshipping honestly, and walks you through delivery, payment and the numbers.

Start where your customers already are (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok), then add a website when you have steady sales and want to look bigger and take payments automatically.

Dropshipping, explained honestly

Dropshipping means you sell products you don't keep in stock: when a customer orders, your supplier sends it directly to them. You earn the difference between your price and the supplier's price.

TypeHow it works in KenyaWatch out for
International (AliExpress, Alibaba, CJ Dropshipping)Supplier in China ships to the customerLong delivery (weeks), customs duties and delays, returns are hard. Kenyan customers rarely accept waiting.
Local dropshippingPartner with a wholesaler in Nairobi (Luthuli Avenue, Eastleigh, Gikomba, Industrial Area) who packs and sends orders you bringYou need a reliable supplier and clear agreement on stock, prices and who pays for returns.
Import and hold small stockBuy small quantities (from local wholesalers or import via consolidators) and deliver yourselfNeeds capital, but delivery is fast and you control quality.

Most successful Kenyan "dropshippers" actually do local dropshipping or small-stock selling: they market on TikTok/Instagram, collect orders and payment, and a nearby supplier or rider delivers the same or next day.

If ads cost more than KSh 450 per sale, you lose money even though "sales are coming in". Track cost per sale every week. (Practise these sums in the Excel subject.)

Legal and trust basics

  • Register a business name on eCitizen (Business Registration Service) when you grow; get a KRA PIN and understand Turnover Tax.
  • Clear return policy (for example, 7 days for faulty items) builds trust.
  • Real photos and videos of your actual products beat stock images.
  • Don't sell counterfeits or restricted goods: marketplaces ban sellers, and it's illegal.
  • Respond fast: WhatsApp quick replies, opening hours, and delivery updates.

Your first 30 days

  1. Pick one product category you understand and can source reliably.
  2. Set up WhatsApp Business (catalogue, greeting message, labels) and one social account.
  3. Post daily: product videos, customer reviews, how-to-use clips.
  4. Get your first 10 sales from friends, family and local groups; ask each for a review.
  5. Track every sale, cost and profit in a spreadsheet. Only then try paid ads.
