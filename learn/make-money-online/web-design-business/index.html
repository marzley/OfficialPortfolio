Start a web design business

Every business in Kenya increasingly needs a website, a Google listing and M-Pesa payments. If you learn HTML, CSS, JavaScript and a bit of PHP or WordPress, you can build a real business.

Services to offer (with typical local price ranges)

Your first 5 clients

  1. Build 3 demo sites for imaginary businesses (a restaurant, a school, a salon). Make them excellent.
  2. Walk into businesses near you with your phone, show the demos, and offer a special price for the first few.
  3. Search Google Maps for businesses without websites and send a short, polite WhatsApp message with a demo link.
  4. Offer to redo your church's or school's website in exchange for a testimonial.
  5. Ask every happy client for two referrals (and consider paying a referral reward).

Deliver like a professional

  • Written quote with what's included, timeline and payment terms.
  • 50% deposit, balance before going live.
  • Use a checklist: mobile test, speed test, contact forms work, SSL (https), Google Business Profile, analytics.
  • Hand over all logins and a short training video.
  • Offer a care plan afterwards: recurring income is what makes a business stable.

Tools

  • Code editor: VS Code (free). Hosting: any cPanel host. Design: Figma or Canva.
  • Payments: M-Pesa Daraja API, Paystack.
  • Invoicing: a simple template, or a client portal like the one on this website.
