Freelancing platforms

A freelancing platform connects you with clients around the world and handles payments, taking a fee.

The main ones

Upwork (upwork.com)

  • Clients post jobs; you send proposals. You buy Connects (credits) to apply.
  • Hourly and fixed-price contracts, with payment protection.
  • Service fee is taken from your earnings (check Upwork's current fee page).
  • Best for: developers, designers, writers, virtual assistants, anyone ready to write good proposals.
  • Withdraw to M-Pesa, bank, Payoneer or PayPal (options and fees change; check "Get paid" settings).

Fiverr (fiverr.com)

  • You create gigs ("I will design a modern logo") with packages; buyers come to you.
  • Fiverr keeps 20% of each order.
  • Best for: well-defined services (logos, video editing, voice-overs, WordPress fixes).
  • Needs strong gig images and fast responses to rank.

Freelancer.com, PeoplePerHour, Guru: similar to Upwork; smaller in Kenya but worth a profile.

LinkedIn: not a marketplace, but many clients hire directly after seeing your posts and profile. Posting your work weekly works.

Specialist platforms:

  • Toptal, Andela, Turing: vetted developers, high pay, hard tests.
  • 99designs, Dribbble, Behance: designers.
  • Contra: commission-free freelancing.

Local work (often easier to start with)

  • Businesses in your town: shops, schools, churches, hotels, clinics. They need websites, logos, social media and M-Pesa payments.
  • Kenyan groups on Facebook, WhatsApp and LinkedIn where people post jobs.
  • Friends and family: ask for referrals, not charity.

Tips that work on every platform

  • Complete profile: professional photo, clear headline ("WordPress developer | M-Pesa & Paystack integration"), specific skills.
  • Portfolio: 3–6 real examples. Screenshots with a short "problem → what I did → result".
  • Respond fast: within an hour when possible.
  • Deliver early and communicate often; ask happy clients for a review.
  • Never take payment outside the platform for platform clients; it breaks the rules and removes your protection.
  • Watch for fake clients asking you to pay for "training" or "software", or to chat on Telegram first.
