Transcription and writing work

If you type fast and accurately and have good English (or Kiswahili), transcription and writing are two of the quickest ways to start earning online with only a laptop and headphones.

Transcription: turning audio into text

Clients include podcasters, researchers, lawyers, journalists, YouTubers and companies recording meetings. You listen and type exactly what's said, following a style guide (for example "clean verbatim": remove "umm" and repeated words; or "full verbatim": keep everything).

Kiswahili and local languages are an advantage: researchers and NGOs regularly need interviews in Kiswahili, Sheng, Kikuyu, Dholuo or Somali transcribed and translated.

Tools and setup

  • Good headphones (wired over-ear headphones beat cheap earphones).
  • Express Scribe (free) or oTranscribe (free, in the browser) to slow down audio and use keyboard shortcuts to pause and rewind.
  • A foot pedal is optional later.
  • Practise typing to 60+ words per minute with near-perfect accuracy (try monkeytype.com or keybr.com daily).

Using AI tools honestly

AI writing tools are now normal, but clients pay for accuracy, local knowledge, a human voice and responsibility. Use AI to brainstorm and outline; write and fact-check yourself. Many clients forbid AI-generated text: follow the brief. Never hand in AI text as fully your own where it's banned.

A warning about "academic writing"

Many Kenyans have been drawn into writing essays and exams for foreign students ("academic writing", "account renting"). It's contract cheating: it breaks universities' rules, platforms ban it, and renting accounts in someone else's name means you can lose all your money with no protection. Build a writing career you can put your real name to.

Your first 2 weeks

  1. Pick a niche: transcription in English and Kiswahili, or writing about one area (tech, agriculture, travel in Kenya, personal finance).
  2. Make 3 samples: a 1-page transcript (record yourself or a friend), a 600-word article, 5 product descriptions. Put them in a Google Drive folder with view links.
  3. Apply to 2 transcription platforms and set up an Upwork or Fiverr profile.
  4. Send 5 proposals a day for 14 days, each one written for that job (see A winning profile and proposals).
