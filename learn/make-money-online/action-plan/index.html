Your 30-day action plan

Reading is not enough. Here is a plan you can start today.

Week 1: choose and learn

  • Day 1: Pick one skill from the "Skills that pay" lesson. Write down why.
  • Days 2–7: Study 1–2 hours daily (lessons in this hub, free courses, YouTube). Take notes. Practise every day.

Week 2: build proof

  • Make 3 portfolio pieces (3 websites, 5 logos, 3 articles, 3 edited videos…).
  • Create a simple portfolio: a free site (GitHub Pages, Behance, Google Drive folder) with your best work.
  • Set up a professional email, LinkedIn, and a profile on one freelancing platform.

Week 3: find clients

  • Send 5 personalised messages a day to local businesses or proposals on platforms.
  • Post your work on LinkedIn, Facebook, WhatsApp status and TikTok, explaining how you made it.
  • Offer 1–2 jobs at a starter price in exchange for a review.

Week 4: deliver and improve

  • Deliver your first job(s) early and excellently.
  • Ask for a review and a referral.
  • Review: what worked? Double down. What didn't? Change it.
  • Set a goal for next month (e.g. 3 paying clients or KSh 20,000).

Daily checklist

  • [ ] 1 hour learning
  • [ ] 1 hour building or practising
  • [ ] 5 outreach messages or proposals
  • [ ] Reply to every message within an hour
