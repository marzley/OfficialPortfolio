Online tutoring and small online tasks

Online tutoring

If you're strong in a subject, you can teach online:

  • Local: Maths, sciences, English, Kiswahili, CBC support, KCSE revision, coding for kids. Market to parents on WhatsApp and Facebook; teach on Google Meet or Zoom. Typical: KSh 500–2,000 per hour.
  • International: English conversation, maths, programming. Platforms like Preply and italki let you set your rate (they take a commission; check each platform's rules and whether they accept tutors from Kenya).
  • Group classes pay better per hour than one-to-one.

Tips: a good headset, a whiteboard app (Google Jamboard alternatives, Microsoft Whiteboard), prepared lesson plans, and sending parents a short progress report.

Small online tasks ("microwork")

  • Data annotation and AI training (labelling images, rating AI answers), transcription, online surveys, website testing (e.g. UserTesting).
  • Pay is usually low and work availability changes a lot; platforms open and close countries without warning. Treat it as side income while you build a real skill.
  • Legit platforms never charge you to join. "Pay KSh 1,000 to activate your account" is a scam.

A better long-term plan

Microtasks → learn a skill in the evenings (this learning hub) → freelancing or a remote job. Each step pays more.
