Freelance marketplaces directory (with links)

A freelance marketplace connects you with clients who need work done, holds the client's money safely and pays you when the work is approved. This is a directory of the main ones: what each is for, how it charges, and who it suits. Use it with Freelancing platforms compared and A winning profile and proposals.

Fees and rules change: check each site's current fee page before you join.

Choosing your first marketplace

If you...Start on
Have a clear service you can packageFiverr
Want long-term clients and are willing to write proposalsUpwork
Are an experienced developerToptal, Arc, Turing, Andela (alongside Upwork)
Teach a language or subjectPreply or italki
Already have clients from your networkContra or LinkedIn Services

Focus on one or two at first. A complete profile, 3 portfolio samples and daily proposals on one platform beat a half-empty profile on ten.

Staying safe on marketplaces

  • Keep communication and payment on the platform until you trust the client. Moving off-platform early loses your payment protection (and breaks most platforms' rules).
  • Never pay a "client" to receive work, buy "training materials" from them, or deposit cheques for them.
  • Don't rent or buy accounts. Use your own identity: verified accounts in your name protect your earnings.
  • Watch for fake job posts asking you to install apps, share passwords or contact them on Telegram immediately.
