Comparing all 10 options and a safety checklist

You've now seen ten ways people trade and invest from Kenya. This final lesson puts them side by side, gives a simple way to choose, and a checklist to protect yourself before sending money anywhere.

A simple decision flow

  1. Do you have an emergency fund (3–6 months) and no expensive debt?
  2. No → focus on income, budgeting and an MMF first. Stop here for now.
  3. Is the money for a goal within 1–3 years?
  4. Yes → MMFs, T-bills, fixed deposits, short bonds.
  5. Is it long-term money (5+ years)?
  6. Yes → diversified investing: NSE shares or equity funds, global ETFs, bonds, retirement savings.
  7. Do you want to learn active trading?
  8. Only with risk capital you can lose completely → study the forex course properly, demo for months, then tiny sizes.
  9. Binary options, synthetic indices, leveraged crypto?
  10. Understand the maths and the risks; for most people, avoid. If you participate, treat it as entertainment money with strict limits.

The safety checklist (before sending money anywhere)

  1. Is the company licensed? Check the exact name on the official website of the CMA, CBK, SASRA or the relevant foreign regulator, not a link they send you.
  2. Is the money going into an account in my own name at that licensed company (not to an individual, an agent's personal number, or a crypto wallet someone gives me)?
  3. Are returns promised, fixed or "guaranteed"? If yes, stop.
  4. Is there pressure to act fast, keep it secret or recruit others? If yes, stop.
  5. Do I understand exactly how I make and lose money, and the costs?
  6. Can I afford to lose this money completely without affecting rent, fees, food or debts?
  7. Have I tested a small deposit and withdrawal first?
  8. Is my account secure: unique password, 2FA, no shared logins?
  9. Would I be comfortable explaining this to a sensible family member or a licensed adviser?

If any answer is wrong, don't proceed.

Where to learn more and get help

  • Regulators: CMA (capital markets, forex brokers, fund managers, investor education and alerts), CBK (banks, government securities), SASRA (SACCOs).
  • Scams: report to the police/DCI, your bank or Safaricom, and the regulator.
  • Gambling concerns: see the warning signs and help steps in trading psychology.
  • Skills and income: freelancing, ways to earn, career roadmaps.

Summary

  • The ten options range from low-risk foundations (T-bills, MMFs) to very high-risk speculation (binary options, synthetic indices, leveraged crypto).
  • Build the foundation first: income, emergency fund, long-term investing; speculate only with small risk capital.
  • Use the safety checklist every time: licence, account in your name, no guarantees, no pressure, small test withdrawal, money you can lose.
