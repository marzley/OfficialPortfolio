AI for images, design and content creation

AI tools can now create images, remove backgrounds, write captions, generate voice-overs and edit videos in seconds. For freelancers, small businesses and content creators, that's a big productivity boost, as long as you use them with skill, honesty and respect for copyright.

Writing good image prompts

A strong prompt describes subject, setting, style, lighting, composition and format:

"A smiling Kenyan woman selling fresh mangoes at a busy open-air market in Mombasa, natural morning light, colourful fabrics, shallow depth of field, realistic photo style, square format, space at the top for text."

PartExamples
Subjecta boda boda rider, a laptop on a wooden desk, a cup of chai
SettingNairobi skyline at dusk, a school computer lab, a farm in Kericho
Stylerealistic photo, flat illustration, 3D render, watercolour, minimalist logo
Lighting & moodwarm sunset light, bright and clean, dramatic
Compositionclose-up, wide shot, top-down, empty space on the left for text
Formatsquare for Instagram, 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails, 9:16 for Status

Iterate: change one thing at a time ("same image, but at night", "make the background plain white").

Watch out for

  • Hands, text and logos: AI often gets fingers and written words wrong. Add text yourself in Canva.
  • Stereotypes: AI may default to stereotypes. Be specific and check for respectful, accurate representation.
  • Uncanny results: for real products and real people, real photos build more trust.

Copyright and honesty

  • Don't ask for images "in the style of" a living artist or copy brand logos and characters.
  • Don't use AI to create fake photos of real people, fake reviews or misleading adverts. It damages trust and may be illegal.
  • Check each tool's terms for commercial use before using images for a client.
  • Label AI-generated content where appropriate (many platforms now require this for realistic images).

AI for writing marketing content

A workflow that keeps quality high:

  1. Give context: "I run a hardware shop in Kitengela. Customers are builders and homeowners. Tone: friendly, simple English, some Sheng is OK."
  2. Ask for options: "Give me 5 Facebook post ideas for this week's cement offer."
  3. Pick one and ask for a draft with a clear call to action (WhatsApp number, location).
  4. Edit it yourself: check prices, dates and claims, and make it sound like you.
  5. Pair it with a real photo or a Canva design.

Earning with these skills

Clients pay for results, not for pressing "generate". Combine AI with real skills: design sense (see the graphic design tutorial), marketing knowledge, and good communication. Services you can offer: social media content packs, product photo clean-ups, simple ad creatives, presentation design, and short videos with captions.
