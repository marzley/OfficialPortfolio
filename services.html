Payments integration

We add M-Pesa payments to your systems

Shops, schools, hospitals, Wi-Fi hotspots: I connect your system to M-Pesa so customers pay from their phones and every payment is confirmed automatically. Try it yourself: enter your Safaricom number below and you’ll get the same prompt your customers would see.

  1. Checkout sends an STK push request to Safaricom.
  2. The phone shows the M-Pesa PIN prompt.
  3. Confirmation arrives by SMS and the order updates automatically.

FAQ

Questions clients ask

Can’t find your answer? Ask me on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start at KSh 15,000 for a landing page, KSh 25,000 for a small business site, KSh 40,000 for e-commerce and KSh 60,000 for a corporate site. Every project is quoted to its scope. Use the quote builder above for an estimate.

Can you add M-Pesa or card payments?

Yes. I integrate M-Pesa, Paystack and PayPal. The Marzley E-Commerce shop and the Wi-Fi hotspot billing system in my work both take payments this way.

Do you set up the domain and hosting?

Yes. Domain and hosting setup is available from KSh 3,000 per year, and I deploy the site for you so it’s live on your own address.

Do you build systems as well as websites?

Yes. Past work includes CBET Planner (a documentation platform for TVET trainers), hospital management systems, a Wi-Fi billing system and online stores with admin tools, alongside business and institutional websites.

Do you offer training?

Yes. I run practical, project-based training in web development, programming and design for students and professionals, and I’ve trained over 200 students.

When can I reach you?

Anytime. We offer our services 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Contact us online through WhatsApp, email or the contact form and we’ll respond quickly.
