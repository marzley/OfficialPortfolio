Kelvin Wanyoike, known as Marzley, is a Kenyan web and app developer and the founder of Marzley Tech Solutions. This page collects the main projects he and the team have built, what each one does, and the problem it solves. For his story, read Meet Kelvin Wanyoike.

Platforms (software as a service)

  • CBET Planner: helps TVET trainers in Kenya turn curricula into learning plans, session plans and information sheets in minutes instead of weekends.
  • BuildPro: for architects and building engineers, from site survey to drawings, 3D model, structural design, bill of quantities and documentation.
  • Jobs Connect: connects skilled professionals (fundis, technicians, freelancers) with customers, with M-Pesa escrow and a delivery module for riders.

Healthcare systems

  • MediFlow HMS, MediCare Hospital and Supreme HMS: patient records, appointments, billing and pharmacy for health facilities. Read our guide to hospital management systems in Kenya.

Education

  • Gatanga Technical and Vocational College website: a fast, mobile-first college website with courses, news and admissions information.
  • Umoja Welfare: a staff welfare platform for the same community, with contributions and member records.
  • The Marzley Tech learning hub: a free learning platform with hundreds of lessons in coding, app development, design, Excel, networking and earning online, a live code editor, quizzes, notes and videos.

Business and payments

  • E-commerce shops with M-Pesa, Paystack and PayPal checkout.
  • A Wi-Fi hotspot billing system where customers buy internet with M-Pesa (see how hotspot billing works).
  • Business websites for companies, schools, churches and NGOs across Kenya.
  • The Marzley client portal: clients follow project progress, approve designs, pay invoices by M-Pesa and get support.

What ties the projects together

  • Real problems first: every project started with someone losing time or money.
  • Built for Kenya: M-Pesa payments, mobile-first design and pages that load on slow data.
  • Secure and maintained: logins, roles, backups and long-term support.
