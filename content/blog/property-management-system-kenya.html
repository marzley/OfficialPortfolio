Landlords and property agents in Kenya spend hours every month matching M-Pesa messages to tenants, chasing arrears, calculating water bills and writing receipts. A rental property management system does that work for you and gives tenants a clear record.

Key features

  • Properties and units: buildings, floors, units, rent amounts and deposits.
  • Tenants: contacts, ID, lease dates and documents.
  • Rent by M-Pesa: tenants pay to your Paybill with their unit number as the account; payments are matched automatically.
  • Automatic invoices and receipts by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Arrears tracking and polite automatic reminders.
  • Utility billing: water meter readings turned into bills; garbage and service charges.
  • Maintenance requests: tenants report issues; you assign a fundi and track the cost.
  • Owner statements for agents managing on behalf of landlords: collections, expenses and commission.
  • Reports: occupancy, collections per property and expected versus received rent.

Why M-Pesa matching matters

When tenants pay to a Paybill with the right account number, the system knows exactly who paid, how much, and when, and issues the receipt instantly. No more “I sent it last week, check your messages” disputes.

For agents and caretakers

Caretakers can record meter readings and repairs from a phone. Agents get one dashboard for every landlord they manage, and landlords get read-only access to their own buildings.

Frequently asked questions

Can tenants pay rent by M-Pesa automatically?

Yes. Tenants pay to your Paybill using their unit number, and the system matches the payment and sends a receipt.

Can it calculate water bills?

Yes. Enter meter readings and the system calculates each unit’s bill and adds it to the invoice.

Is it suitable for property agents?

Yes. Agents manage many landlords’ properties and produce owner statements with collections, expenses and commission.
