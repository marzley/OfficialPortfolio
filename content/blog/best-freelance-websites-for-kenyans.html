Thousands of Kenyans earn from clients abroad through freelance websites. Each site works differently. Here’s a plain comparison so you choose the right one to start, plus how to get paid in Kenya.

The main freelance websites

  • Upwork: clients post jobs; you send proposals using “Connects”. Hourly and fixed-price contracts with payment protection. Best for long-term clients.
  • Fiverr: you create “gigs” (fixed packages), and clients buy them. Best for clear, repeatable services like logos, video edits and website fixes.
  • Freelancer.com: bid on projects and contests. Lots of jobs, lots of price competition.
  • PeoplePerHour: UK-focused clients; proposals and fixed “offers”.
  • Guru: profiles, quotes and workrooms for many skills.
  • Contra: commission-free profile and payments for clients you bring yourself.
  • Toptal: elite network for experienced developers, designers and finance experts, after tough screening.
  • Andela, Turing and Arc: vetted networks for software engineers.
  • 99designs: design contests and 1-to-1 projects.
  • Preply and italki: online tutoring and language teaching.

Fees change, so check each site’s current fee page before you join.

Which should you start with?

  • Packaged service (logo, CV, edits): Fiverr.
  • Long-term work and willing to write proposals: Upwork.
  • Experienced developer: Upwork plus Toptal, Andela, Turing or Arc.
  • Teacher or language speaker: Preply or italki.
  • Clients from your network: Contra or LinkedIn.

Getting paid in Kenya

  • PayPal → M-Pesa: link your PayPal account to M-Pesa and withdraw to your phone.
  • Payoneer: receive from many marketplaces and withdraw to a Kenyan bank.
  • Direct bank transfer: supported by many platforms.

Winning your first client

  1. Complete your profile 100%, with a clear photo and a specific title (“Kiswahili and English transcriber”, not “Freelancer”).
  2. Add 3 strong portfolio samples.
  3. Write every proposal for that job: show you read it, explain how you’ll do it, and keep it short.
  4. Start with smaller jobs to earn reviews, then raise your prices.
  5. Keep communication and payment on the platform for protection.

Frequently asked questions

Which freelance website is best for beginners in Kenya?

Fiverr suits clear, packaged services; Upwork suits people willing to write proposals for longer-term work. Start with one and complete your profile fully.

Can I withdraw Upwork or Fiverr earnings to M-Pesa?

Many Kenyans withdraw to PayPal and then to M-Pesa, or to Payoneer and then a Kenyan bank. Check each platform’s current withdrawal options.

Is it safe to rent a freelance account?

No. Renting or buying accounts breaks platform rules and you can lose all your earnings. Use your own verified account.
