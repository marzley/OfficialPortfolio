If you train at a TVET institution in Kenya, you know the paperwork that comes with Competency-Based Education and Training (CBET). Every unit needs proper documentation, and preparing it by hand can take evenings and weekends. That’s why we built CBET Planner.

What CBET documentation involves

For each unit they teach, a trainer typically prepares:

  • A learning plan that maps the unit’s content across the term.
  • Session plans for individual lessons, with objectives, activities and resources.
  • Information sheets that give learners the content for each session.
  • Assessment tools, such as assessments and marking schemes.

Each has to follow the curriculum closely and use the right format, and each has to be redone when curricula change.

How CBET Planner helps

CBET Planner is an online platform for TVET trainers. You upload your curriculum or an existing learning plan, and it drafts the documents for you:

  1. Start from the curriculum. Upload the curriculum for your unit.
  2. Generate a learning plan. The platform organises the content across your sessions.
  3. Create session plans and information sheets from that learning plan.
  4. Review and adjust. You stay in control: edit anything before you use it.

The result is documentation in the right structure in a fraction of the time, so trainers can spend more energy on actual teaching.

Who it’s for

  • Heads of department who want consistent documentation across their teams.
  • New trainers learning the CBET format for the first time.
