Jitume is a Government of Kenya programme that gives young people access to computers, internet and training for digital and online work, through Jitume digital labs in TVETs, universities, constituencies and other public institutions. Here’s how to join and how to get real results from it, from a Jitume facilitator.

Who can join?

Jitume targets young Kenyans who want to learn digital skills and earn online, including students and out-of-school youth. Requirements and schedules differ by lab, so ask at the lab near you. You don’t need to be a computer expert: labs start from the basics.

How to find a lab

  1. Ask at your nearest TVET college, university or constituency office.
  2. Follow Jitume’s official social media pages and the ICT Ministry for announcements.
  3. Ask your county or constituency youth office.

Be careful of anyone asking you to pay to “register for Jitume”. Confirm with the lab or official channels.

What you’ll learn

  • Computer and internet basics, email and online safety.
  • The ten online earning categories.
  • Setting up profiles on freelance and work platforms.
  • Getting paid safely in Kenya and avoiding scams.

A plan for your first 6 weeks

  1. Week 1: pick one category; set up a professional email, CV and payment method.
  2. Week 2: learn the core skill using the lab and our free making money online lessons.
  3. Week 3: create 3 portfolio samples.
  4. Week 4: build profiles on 1 or 2 platforms; ask your facilitator to review them.
  5. Week 5: send 5 tailored applications a day and approach 10 local businesses.
  6. Week 6: deliver your first job brilliantly and ask for a review.

Tips from the lab

  • Use lab time to produce: profiles, samples and applications. Learn theory on your phone at home.
  • Keep a notebook of logins (safely), applications sent and replies.
  • Work with others: study groups keep you consistent.
  • Never share your account or let anyone “manage” it for you.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Jitume digital lab?

A computer lab with internet, set up under the Government of Kenya’s Jitume programme in public institutions, where young people learn digital skills and do online work.

Is Jitume free?

Jitume labs are a government programme. Confirm any requirements with the lab and official channels, and don’t pay anyone claiming to sell Jitume registration.

Who is a Jitume facilitator?

A trainer who guides learners in a Jitume lab, from basic computer skills to finding and doing online work. Kelvin Wanyoike (Marzley) is a Jitume facilitator.
