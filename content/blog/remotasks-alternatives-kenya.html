Remotasks, owned by Scale AI, was a major source of online income for Kenyan workers until it stopped offering work in Kenya in March 2024. Many workers lost access overnight. AI training work hasn't disappeared, though; it has moved to other platforms and employers, and it increasingly rewards skills like accuracy, coding, subject expertise and languages.

Types of AI work to look for

  • Image and video annotation (bounding boxes, segmentation)
  • Text labelling and translation checking
  • AI response rating (RLHF) and prompt writing
  • Coding tasks for AI training (Python, JavaScript and others)
  • Expert AI training for graduates in maths, sciences, health, law and finance
  • Search and ads rating
  • Kiswahili and local-language data projects

Where to look

Check the current country eligibility on each platform's official site: Appen, TELUS Digital (formerly TELUS International AI), OneForma, Toloka, Clickworker and Outlier, plus employers with a Nairobi presence such as Sama and CloudFactory, and "AI trainer" roles on LinkedIn and Upwork.

Lessons from Remotasks

  1. Never depend on one platform.
  2. Never buy or rent accounts to get around country restrictions; they get banned and you lose earnings.
  3. Build transferable skills that pay more: coding, a subject speciality, languages.
