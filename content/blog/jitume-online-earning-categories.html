In Jitume digital labs, learners are introduced to ten categories of online work. As a Jitume facilitator, Kelvin Wanyoike explains each one below: what the work is, who it suits, and where to learn it free. Choose one category to start; trying all ten at once is the most common reason beginners earn nothing.

1. AI training and data annotation

Labelling images, rating and correcting AI chatbot answers, and recording voice data. Suits careful readers with good English or a specialty (maths, coding, a local language). Learn more and see the platforms.

2. Transcription and writing

Typing out audio recordings, and writing articles, product descriptions and captions. Suits fast, accurate typists with strong grammar. Transcription and writing lesson.

3. Social media management

Running Facebook, Instagram and TikTok pages for businesses: planning, designing posts, captions and replying to customers. Great for people who love social media and can stay consistent. Social media management lesson.

4. Content creation

Making videos, podcasts and posts, and earning from platform payouts, brand deals and your own products. Slowest to pay, but can grow big. How each platform pays.

5. Graphic design

Logos, posters, flyers, social media graphics and templates, for local clients and on global marketplaces. Learn Canva free and see where designers earn.

6. Programming and web development

Building websites, apps and systems. Takes the longest to learn but pays the best. Start with HTML, then Flutter for apps. Where developers earn.

7. E-commerce and dropshipping

Selling products on WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Jumia or your own website, paid with M-Pesa. E-commerce and dropshipping in Kenya.

8. Micro-tasks and rewards

Surveys, small online tasks and website testing. Easy to start but pays little; good for learning and a stepping stone to testing work. Beware of “deposit to unlock” task scams. Micro-tasks lesson.

9. Virtual assistance and support

Email, calendars, admin, customer chat and calls for businesses abroad and in Kenya. Where to find VA and support jobs.

10. Freelance marketplaces

Upwork, Fiverr and others, where you sell any of the skills above to clients worldwide. Freelance marketplaces directory.

How to choose

  • Need income soon? Transcription, annotation, VA work or local social media clients.
  • Building for the long term? Programming, design, content creation or e-commerce.
  • Only a smartphone? Social media management, content creation and some micro-tasks.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Jitume online earning categories?

AI training and data annotation, transcription and writing, social media management, content creation, graphic design, programming and web development, e-commerce and dropshipping, micro-tasks and rewards, virtual assistance and support, and freelance marketplaces.

Which online work category is best for beginners?

Choose based on skills you already have. Fast typists do well in transcription; careful readers in data annotation; social media lovers in social media management.

Do I have to pay to get online work?

No. Real platforms never charge you to get work. Anyone asking for a fee to “unlock” jobs is likely a scammer.
