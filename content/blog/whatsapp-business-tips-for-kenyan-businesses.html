Most Kenyan customers would rather send a WhatsApp message than fill in a form. The free WhatsApp Business app has tools that make you look professional and save you time, but many businesses only use it like normal WhatsApp. Here are ten ways to get more from it.

Set it up properly

  1. Use WhatsApp Business, not the normal app. It's free from the Play Store or App Store, and you can move your existing number to it.
  2. Complete your business profile: business name, category, a clear description, address, opening hours, email and website. Customers check it before they buy.
  3. Use a clear logo as the profile photo, not a group photo or a random picture.

Sell and reply faster

  1. Add a catalogue with photos, prices and short descriptions of your products or services, so customers see what you sell without asking for photos every time.
  2. Turn on the greeting and away messages, so nobody waits in silence, for example “Thanks for contacting us! We reply within 30 minutes, 8am to 8pm.”
  3. Create quick replies for questions you answer every day: prices, location, delivery, how to pay with M-Pesa.
  4. Use labels (New customer, Paid, Pending delivery, Follow up) so no order or enquiry is forgotten.

Bring customers to you

  1. Share your WhatsApp link (wa.me/2547XXXXXXXX) on your posters, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google Business Profile, so people can message you in one tap.
  2. Post on Status regularly: new stock, offers, happy customers and behind the scenes. Your contacts see it for free.
  3. Use broadcast lists carefully. A broadcast only reaches people who have saved your number. Send useful offers, not spam, or people will block you.

WhatsApp works even better with a website

A website with a WhatsApp button lets new customers find you on Google, see your work and prices, then message you ready to buy. You can also add an online shop with M-Pesa checkout for orders that don't need a chat. Read do I need a website or just social media?
