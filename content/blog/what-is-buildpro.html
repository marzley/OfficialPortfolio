BuildPro is an online platform built by Marzley Tech Solutions for architects and building engineers. It takes a building project from concept to complete project: site survey, architectural drawings, a 3D model, structural and civil engineering, quantity takeoff, the bill of quantities (BQ), materials, costing and project documentation, all in one coordinated place.

Open it at buildprov1.cbetplanner.co.ke.

Why BuildPro?

On most projects the drawings are in one program, the BQ is in a spreadsheet, material lists are somewhere else and the reports are typed in Word. When the client asks for a change, every file has to be updated by hand, and mistakes creep in: wrong quantities, outdated costs, documents that no longer match the drawings.

BuildPro keeps everything connected to one project model, so a change in the design flows through to the quantities, the costs and the documents.

What you can do with BuildPro

  1. Site survey. Start from the site information.
  2. Design and drawings. Develop the architectural design and drawings.
  3. 3D model. See the building in 3D as you design.
  4. Structural and civil engineering. Work on the structure alongside the architecture.
  5. Quantity takeoff and BQ. Quantities come from the model and the BQ is prepared automatically.
  6. Materials and costing. Get material schedules and cost estimates from the quantities.
  7. Documentation. Produce project documents, with revisions tracked.

There’s also an AI design assistant: describe what you want to change, review the suggestion, then apply it.

Who it’s for

  • Architects who want drawings, models and documents in step.
  • Structural and civil engineers working on the same project as the architect.
  • Quantity surveyors who need reliable quantities for BQs and estimates.
  • Contractors and developers planning materials and costs.
  • Students in architecture, building and civil engineering learning the full design-to-build process.

How to start

  1. Go to buildprov1.cbetplanner.co.ke.
  2. Click Create Account.
  3. Choose Start a Project and add your site details.
  4. Work through design, engineering, BQ and documents.
