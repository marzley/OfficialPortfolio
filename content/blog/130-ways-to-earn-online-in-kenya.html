The rules that keep you safe

  • Never pay a "registration" or "activation" fee to get an online job.
  • Never buy or rent someone else's account on Upwork, an AI platform or a transcription site.
  • Anyone guaranteeing weekly profits from forex, crypto or an "investment" is running a scam.
  • Check brokers and fund managers on the Capital Markets Authority (CMA) website.
  • Declare your income and file your KRA return by 30 June.
