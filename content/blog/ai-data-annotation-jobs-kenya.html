AI systems learn from examples that humans label and check. That has created a huge amount of AI training and data annotation work, and Kenya is one of the world’s busiest hubs for it. Here’s what the work involves and how to start safely.

What the work looks like

  • Image and video labelling: drawing boxes around objects, tagging scenes.
  • Rating AI answers: comparing two chatbot replies and explaining which is better and why.
  • Writing prompts and ideal answers: especially valuable if you know coding, maths, finance, medicine or law.
  • Audio: recording or transcribing speech, including Kiswahili and other Kenyan languages.
  • Search and ad evaluation: judging whether results match what someone searched for.

Platforms and companies

  • Outlier (by Scale AI)
  • DataAnnotation
  • TELUS Digital AI community
  • Appen
  • OneForma
  • Toloka and Mindrift
  • CloudFactory and Sama, which have teams in Nairobi

Availability changes by country and project. Remotasks, once popular in Kenya, stopped operating here in 2024. Always check the official site.

Skills that get you accepted

  1. Excellent written English and careful reading of long guidelines.
  2. Attention to detail: quality scores decide whether you get more work.
  3. A specialty (coding, maths, a profession or a local language) for better-paid projects.
  4. A reliable laptop, internet and basic Google Docs/Sheets skills.

Passing the assessment

  • Read the instructions twice; many tests are one attempt only.
  • Follow the guideline exactly, even when you disagree.
  • Take your time: accuracy beats speed.
  • Practise with the sample questions in our AI training and data annotation lesson.

Avoid these traps

  • Buying or renting accounts registered to someone else: it breaks the rules and you can lose everything.
  • Paying “agents” to get you onboarded.
  • Telegram groups promising guaranteed daily pay for “AI jobs”.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI data annotation work real?

Yes. Companies pay people to label data and rate AI answers. Apply only through official platform websites and never pay to join.

Do I need a degree for AI training jobs?

Not always. Basic tasks need careful reading and good English; specialist projects ask for degrees or professional skills.

Can I do AI annotation work on a phone?

Some simple tasks work on phones, but most platforms expect a laptop or desktop with reliable internet.
