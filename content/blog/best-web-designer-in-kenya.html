7 signs of great website design

  1. Mobile first. Most visitors in Kenya come from phones. Text is readable without zooming and buttons are easy to tap.
  2. One clear action per page. “Call now”, “WhatsApp us” or “Get a quote” is obvious within seconds.
  3. Fast. Compressed images, few heavy scripts, and pages that load on slow mobile data. Beautiful but slow loses customers.
  4. Consistent branding. Your logo, colours and fonts are used the same way everywhere.
  5. Readable. Good contrast, short paragraphs, clear headings. Everyone, including people with poor eyesight, can use it.
  6. Real photos and proof. Your team, your work, reviews from real clients. Stock photos don’t build trust.
  7. Easy to update. You can change prices, photos and posts yourself.

Web designer vs web developer

A designer plans the look, layout and user experience (UI/UX). A developer builds it so it works: forms, payments, databases and speed.
