Many TVET trainers already use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini to help with CBET documents. Others use CBET Planner, a platform built specifically for Kenyan TVET documentation. Which is better? It depends on the job. Here’s an honest comparison from the team that built CBET Planner.

Where general AI chatbots shine

  • Explaining a topic you’re about to teach, with examples.
  • Brainstorming activities, demonstrations and real-life examples.
  • Writing questions and simplifying information sheets for trainees.
  • One-off tasks: a letter, a report, a presentation outline.

Where general chatbots struggle with CBET documents

  • Drifting from the curriculum: without careful prompting, they invent content or mix up elements and performance criteria.
  • Format: tables come out differently every time and need reformatting for your template.
  • Consistency: session plans don’t always match the learning plan they came from.
  • Long prompts: you must paste the curriculum and repeat instructions for every unit.

Where CBET Planner fits

  • Starts from your curriculum (upload the unit’s curriculum or an existing learning plan).
  • Drafts the learning plan in the expected structure, then session plans and information sheets from it, so documents stay consistent.
  • Assessment help: assessment tools and marking schemes.
  • You stay in control: review and edit before you download.
  • Built for Kenyan TVET trainers, by people who work with TVET institutions.

Side by side

  • Learning plans from a curriculum: CBET Planner is purpose-built; a chatbot can do it with careful prompts and checking.
  • Session plans consistent with the learning plan: CBET Planner generates them from the plan; with a chatbot you must keep them aligned yourself.
  • Explaining topics and brainstorming: a chatbot is excellent.
  • Formatting for your documents: CBET Planner produces structured documents; chatbot output usually needs reformatting.

The best approach: use both

  1. Generate your learning plan, session plans and information sheets in CBET Planner.
  2. Use a chatbot to enrich sessions: real-world examples, demonstrations, discussion questions.
  3. Review everything against the curriculum and your workshop’s reality.

Frequently asked questions

Is CBET Planner better than ChatGPT for learning plans?

For turning a curriculum into consistent CBET learning plans, session plans and information sheets, a purpose-built tool like CBET Planner saves formatting and alignment work. Chatbots are great for explanations, examples and questions.

Can I use ChatGPT and CBET Planner together?

Yes. Many trainers generate documents in CBET Planner and use a chatbot to add examples, activities and questions.
