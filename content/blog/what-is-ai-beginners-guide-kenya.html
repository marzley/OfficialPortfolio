Artificial intelligence (AI) is technology that lets computers do things that normally need human intelligence: understanding language, recognising faces and voices, translating, spotting patterns and creating text, images and video. You already use it every day, often without noticing.

AI you already use in Kenya

  • M-Pesa and banks: fraud detection blocks unusual transactions.
  • Google Maps: predicts traffic and the fastest route.
  • Gmail: filters spam.
  • YouTube and TikTok: recommend what to watch next.
  • Google Translate and voice typing: English ↔ Kiswahili, speech to text.
  • Chat assistants: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Meta AI in WhatsApp.

How AI is different from normal software

Normal software follows rules a programmer wrote. AI learns patterns from examples (data): show it millions of past transactions labelled “fraud” or “OK” and it learns what fraud looks like. This is called machine learning. Chat assistants are built on large language models, trained on huge amounts of text to predict and write language.

What AI is good at, and what it isn’t

  • Good at: first drafts, explaining topics simply, summarising, translating, brainstorming, finding patterns in data.
  • Risky for: exact facts, numbers, laws and recent news (it can confidently make things up, called “hallucinating”), and health, legal or money decisions without a professional.

The golden rule: AI is a very fast, well-read assistant that sometimes makes mistakes. You stay responsible for checking what it gives you.

How AI can help you this week

  • Students: explain topics and quiz you before exams (learn, don’t copy).
  • Job seekers: improve your CV honestly and practise interviews.
  • Small businesses: product descriptions, social media posts, customer replies, simple budgets.
  • Office workers: emails, Excel formulas, meeting summaries, reports.
  • Farmers: questions about crops and markets (confirm with an extension officer).

Frequently asked questions

What is AI in simple words?

AI is technology that lets computers learn from examples and do tasks that normally need human intelligence, like understanding language, recognising images and making predictions.

Can I use AI for free in Kenya?

Yes. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Meta AI all have free versions that work on a smartphone.

Will AI take my job?

AI changes jobs more than it removes them. People who learn to use AI well often become more productive and valuable than those who don’t.
