A pharmacy loses money in quiet ways: expired stock nobody noticed, items sold below cost, cash that doesn’t match sales, and fast-moving drugs that run out on a busy Saturday. A good pharmacy POS (point of sale) and stock system closes those gaps.

Must-have features

  • Fast selling screen: search by name, generic name or barcode, with the price and stock shown instantly.
  • M-Pesa payments: STK push or till confirmation recorded against each sale, plus cash and card.
  • Batch and expiry tracking: the system sells the oldest batch first and warns you months before expiry.
  • Reorder levels: an alert (or a ready purchase order) when stock falls below the level you set.
  • Buying price and margins: see profit per item and stop selling below cost.
  • Prescriptions and controlled drugs: record who prescribed, keep a register for controlled medicines.
  • Suppliers and purchases: deliveries, invoices and balances owed to each supplier.
  • Staff accounts and shifts: each cashier logs in; end-of-shift cash-up shows any difference.
  • Receipts: printed (thermal printer) or sent by SMS/WhatsApp.
  • Reports: daily sales, best sellers, slow movers, expiring stock and profit.

Single shop or several branches?

With a cloud (web-based) system, the owner sees every branch’s sales and stock from a phone, moves stock between branches, and sets prices centrally. A local network setup can keep a single shop selling even when the internet is down, then sync later.

Hardware you may need

  • A computer or tablet (an existing laptop is often enough).
  • A barcode scanner (optional but much faster).
  • An 80 mm thermal receipt printer.
  • A cash drawer (optional).

Related systems

Pharmacies attached to clinics often need the pharmacy to receive prescriptions straight from the doctor. That’s part of a hospital management system. Shops selling other goods use the same ideas in a POS and inventory system.

Frequently asked questions

Can a pharmacy POS track expiry dates?

Yes. A good system records the batch and expiry of each delivery, sells the oldest first and alerts you before stock expires.

Does it work with M-Pesa?

Yes. Sales can be paid by M-Pesa STK push or till, with the payment confirmed and recorded automatically.

Can I see my pharmacy’s sales from my phone?

Yes, with a web-based system you can check sales, stock and profit for every branch from your phone.
