Almost every job in Kenya now needs some digital skills: sending professional emails, working in Excel, staying safe from scams, using AI tools, or earning online. A digital skills facilitator helps groups learn these skills by doing, not by memorising notes. Kelvin Wanyoike (Marzley) is a digital skills facilitator, ICT trainer and Jitume facilitator, and runs sessions through Marzley Tech Solutions.

Who we train

  • Schools and TVET colleges: learners and trainers, including CBET documentation with CBET Planner.
  • Youth groups and community programmes: digital literacy and online work.
  • Businesses and staff teams: productivity, online safety and customer communication.
  • Individuals: one-on-one mentorship in coding, design and freelancing.

Topics we cover

  • Computer and digital literacy: files, email, Word and Google Docs.
  • Excel and spreadsheets: formulas, tables, charts and simple reports.
  • Online safety: passwords, two-step login, scams, M-Pesa fraud and fake job offers.
  • AI tools: using AI assistants responsibly for writing, research and work.
  • Earning online: freelancing profiles, finding genuine work and getting paid safely.
  • Web design and coding: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python, with practice on our free learning hub.
  • Digital marketing: Google Business Profile, social media and a simple website.

How our sessions work

  1. Needs check: we ask what your group already knows and what they need.
  2. Hands-on sessions: short explanations, then real tasks on computers or phones.
  3. Practice at home: free lessons, quizzes and a code editor on our learning hub.
  4. Follow-up: support and, for courses, certificates of completion.
