What is Jitume?

Jitume is a Government of Kenya digital skills initiative. It sets up digital labs in places like TVET colleges, universities and community centres, and trains young people to use computers and the internet for learning, business and online work. The name comes from the Kiswahili jitume: “put yourself to work” or “apply yourself”.

What a Jitume facilitator does

  • Teaches digital skills in the labs: computer basics, internet use, online safety and productivity tools.
  • Prepares learners for online work: finding genuine opportunities, building a profile, communicating with clients and getting paid safely.
  • Guides hands-on practice: learners do real tasks, not just listen.
  • Mentors: helps learners choose a path, such as web development, design, data or digital marketing.

What learners gain

  • Confidence using computers, email, documents and spreadsheets.
  • Safe internet habits: strong passwords, spotting scams and fake job offers.
  • A starting point for online work and freelancing.
  • For those who want more: a path into coding and web design, practised on our free learning hub.
