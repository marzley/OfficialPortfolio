Almost every job, business and government service in Kenya now involves a phone or computer. The good news: the most useful digital skills can be learned free, in your own time, even on a phone. Here are ten worth learning this year, with free lessons for each.

1. Online safety

Strong passwords, two-step verification on WhatsApp and Gmail, and spotting M-Pesa and job scams. This protects your money before anything else. Learn online safety.

2. Excel and Google Sheets

The number one computer skill employers ask for: formulas, IF, VLOOKUP, charts and pivot tables. Useful for shops, chamas, schools and offices. Learn Excel.

3. Word processing and professional documents

CVs, letters, reports with tables of contents and mail merge. Learn Word and Google Docs.

4. Email, cloud storage and online meetings

Professional email, Google Drive sharing and working together on documents. Learn email and Google Drive.

5. Government services online

eCitizen, KRA iTax (including nil returns), KUCCPS and HELB. Useful for yourself and a real income for cyber café work. Learn online government services.

6. Graphic design with Canva

Posters, social media posts, logos and business cards. A popular side hustle. Learn graphic design.

7. Digital marketing and SEO

Getting a business found on Google and social media. Learn digital marketing, and read what SEO is.

8. Using AI tools well

Writing good prompts, using AI for work and study, and checking its answers. Learn AI tools.

9. Coding

Start with HTML and CSS, then JavaScript or Python. The code runs in your browser as you learn. Start coding, or read what programming is.

10. Making money online, safely

Freelancing, remote work, getting paid to M-Pesa and avoiding scams. Learn to earn online.

How to learn without getting overwhelmed

  • Pick one skill to start with, based on your goal (job, business or side income).
  • Study 30–60 minutes a day and practise with real tasks.
  • Build something you can show: a budget sheet, a poster, a web page.
  • Add each skill to your CV with a link to your work.
