AI isn’t only for PhDs. It has created a ladder of paid work, from beginner-friendly roles to highly paid engineering jobs. Here’s the roadmap, with what to learn at each step.

The AI skills ladder

  1. AI user: use assistants well in any job. Everyone should be here.
  2. Power user: prompting, fact-checking, AI in Office and Google Workspace. Roles: virtual assistant, social media manager, content creator, team AI trainer.
  3. Automation builder: custom assistants and no-code workflows (Zapier, Make, n8n). Roles: AI automation freelancer, operations specialist.
  4. Technical practitioner: Python, SQL, data analysis and machine learning basics. Roles: data analyst, junior data scientist, AI data specialist.
  5. AI engineer: build AI apps with APIs, retrieval (RAG), tools, agents and evaluation. Roles: AI engineer, full-stack developer with AI, ML engineer.

Entry points for beginners

  • AI training and data annotation: rating and correcting AI answers, labelling data. See AI data annotation jobs in Kenya.
  • AI-assisted freelancing: writing, design, video editing and virtual assistance delivered faster with AI (and checked by you).
  • AI training for local businesses: many shops, schools and SACCOs want someone to show them how to use AI.

Skills that matter most

  • Clear thinking and writing: the foundation of good prompting.
  • Python and SQL: the languages of data and AI.
  • Statistics basics: averages, spread, probability.
  • Building and deploying: web backends, databases, APIs.
  • Ethics and data protection: Kenya’s Data Protection Act and responsible AI.

Projects that get you hired

Build things that solve Kenyan problems: a Kiswahili/English FAQ assistant for a school, a crop disease classifier, a sales forecasting dashboard, or a customer service bot that checks order status. Frequently asked questions

Can I get an AI job in Kenya without a degree?

Yes, for many roles. AI-assisted freelancing, data annotation and automation work depend on skills and a portfolio. Engineering and research roles often value degrees but also strong projects.

What should I learn first for a career in AI?

Learn to use AI well, then Python and SQL, then machine learning basics and how to build apps with AI APIs.

How long does it take to become an AI engineer?

With consistent study, many people go from beginner programmer to building real AI apps in about a year.
