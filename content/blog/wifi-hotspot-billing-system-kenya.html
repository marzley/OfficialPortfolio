Small internet providers, apartments, hostels, cyber cafés, restaurants and estates across Kenya sell Wi-Fi by the hour, day or month. Doing it with paper vouchers and manual M-Pesa checks is slow and leaks money. A Wi-Fi hotspot billing system lets customers pay with M-Pesa and get connected automatically, any time of day.

Marzley Tech Solutions built a hotspot billing system where customers choose a package, pay by M-Pesa STK push and are online within seconds.

How it works

  1. The customer connects to your Wi-Fi and sees your captive portal (a branded login page).
  2. They pick a package, for example 1 hour, 24 hours, a week or a month.
  3. They enter their phone number and confirm the M-Pesa prompt with their PIN.
  4. The system confirms the payment and tells the router to let that device online for the time or data bought.
  5. When the package ends, access stops automatically; they can buy again in seconds.

Features to expect

  • Time-based and data-based packages, with speed limits per package.
  • Automatic M-Pesa confirmation (no manual checking).
  • Vouchers for customers who pay cash.
  • Monthly PPPoE customers for home connections, with automatic disconnection when unpaid.
  • Dashboard: active users, sales per day, top packages.
  • SMS notifications when a package is about to expire.
  • Support for MikroTik routers (the most common choice for hotspots).

What you need

  • An internet connection to resell (fibre is best). Check your provider’s terms allow resale and any licensing requirements with the Communications Authority of Kenya.
  • A MikroTik router and access points to cover your area.
  • A Paybill or Till number for M-Pesa.
  • Hosting for the billing system.

Frequently asked questions

Can customers buy Wi-Fi with M-Pesa automatically?

Yes. They choose a package on the login page, approve the M-Pesa prompt and get connected within seconds, without your involvement.

Which routers work with hotspot billing?

MikroTik routers are the most common, because they support hotspots, user limits and remote control by the billing system.

Can I sell monthly home internet too?

Yes. Monthly PPPoE accounts can be billed and disconnected automatically when unpaid.
