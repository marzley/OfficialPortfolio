The same AI gives a weak answer to a vague question and an excellent answer to a clear one. Writing clear instructions is called prompting, and it’s the most useful AI skill there is.

The formula: role, task, context, format, examples

  • Role: who should the AI act as? “Act as an experienced KCSE chemistry teacher.”
  • Task: what exactly to do. “Explain moles and write 5 practice questions.”
  • Context: what it needs to know. “My student is in Form 3 and struggles with calculations.”
  • Format: how the answer should look. “Short steps, then numbered questions, answers at the end.”
  • Examples: what good looks like. Show one or two.

Weak vs strong

  • Weak: “Write a business plan.” Strong: “I’m starting a mobile car wash in Ruiru with KSh 80,000. Write a one-page business plan for a youth fund application: services and prices, customers, startup and monthly costs, expected income and risks. Use a table for the numbers.”
  • Weak: “Write a CV.” Strong: “Write a one-page CV for a fresh IT diploma graduate applying for junior IT support. Skills: networking, Windows, Excel, customer service. Three months’ attachment at a county office.”
  • Weak: “Help with Excel.” Strong: “Column B has sales and column C says M-Pesa or Cash. Give me a formula for total M-Pesa sales and explain it.”

Techniques that work

  1. Ask it to ask you questions first: “Before you write, ask me 5 questions.”
  2. Give it your material: notes, the job advert, your draft.
  3. Iterate: “shorter”, “more formal”, “add prices in KSh”.
  4. Break big jobs into steps: outline, then each section.
  5. Ask it to check itself: “List any mistakes or assumptions in your answer.”

A template to save

Act as [role]. I need you to [task]. Background: [situation and facts]. Requirements: [length, tone, must-include]. Format: [table, bullets, email, language]. First, ask me any questions you need.

Frequently asked questions

What is a prompt in AI?

A prompt is the instruction or question you give an AI tool. Clearer prompts with context and a requested format give better answers.

Do prompts work the same in ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini?

Yes, the same principles work in all of them: a clear task, context, the format you want and examples.

Can I write prompts in Kiswahili?

Yes. Most assistants understand Kiswahili, and you can ask for answers in Kiswahili, though checking accuracy is important.
