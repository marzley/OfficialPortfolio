A shop owner with a smartphone and an AI assistant can now produce marketing, customer replies and plans that used to need a team. Here’s how Kenyan businesses use AI today, step by step, and where to be careful.

1. A month of social media content in an hour

Ask: “Create a 4-week content calendar for my bakery in Nakuru on Instagram and TikTok: 3 posts per week, with captions under 40 words, hashtags and a short video idea for each.” Then design the images in Canva and adjust the captions to your voice, including Kiswahili or Sheng.

2. Product descriptions that sell

Give the AI the product, the customer and the key benefits, and ask for 80 words, benefits first, specs as bullets and a WhatsApp call to action. For many products at once, use a spreadsheet and an automation.

3. Faster, consistent customer replies

Write a document of approved facts (prices, delivery areas, hours, return policy, Till or Paybill). Then ask: “Using only these facts, reply politely to this customer.” “Using only these facts” stops the AI inventing promises.

4. Pricing and planning

AI helps you think through costs, prices and business plans, but always check the maths. Note that “30% profit” can mean margin or markup, which give different prices.

5. Automation

Tools like Zapier, Make and n8n connect your forms, Gmail and Google Sheets with AI steps: summarise enquiries, flag complaints, file receipts. Keep a human review step for anything sent to customers or involving money.

6. AI on your website

A website assistant that answers customers from your real documents (called RAG) can work day and night. The AI key must stay on your server, and answers should be grounded in your facts. Talk to us about adding AI to your website or system.

The rules that protect your business

  • Review everything before you post or send it.
  • Never let AI invent prices, policies or testimonials; never post fake reviews.
  • Don’t paste customers’ personal data into public AI tools (the Data Protection Act applies).
  • Tell customers when they’re chatting with an AI assistant, and offer a human.
