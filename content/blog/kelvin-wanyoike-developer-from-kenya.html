Kelvin G. Wanyoike, known in the tech space as Marzley, is a web and app developer, designer and IT professional from Kenya. He is the founder of Marzley Tech Solutions, an ICT trainer, a digital skills facilitator and a Jitume facilitator, and the developer behind systems used by schools, hospitals, businesses and trainers.

A developer from Kenya, building for Kenya

Kelvin’s work starts from a simple belief: technology should solve real, everyday problems. A trainer shouldn’t lose every weekend to paperwork. A small shop should be able to take M-Pesa payments at midnight. A hospital shouldn’t lose patient records in a pile of files.

That belief grew into Marzley Tech Solutions, which builds:

  • CBET Planner, an online platform that helps TVET trainers turn curricula into learning plans, session plans and information sheets.
  • BuildPro, a platform for architects and building engineers that goes from site survey to drawings, 3D model, structural design, BQ, materials and documentation.
  • Jobs Connect, a marketplace that connects skilled professionals to customers who need work done, with M-Pesa escrow and a delivery module where riders enrol and offer deliveries.
  • Hospital and healthcare systems for patient records, appointments and billing.
  • Institutional websites such as Gatanga Technical and Vocational College, and a staff welfare platform for the same community.
  • E-commerce sites with M-Pesa, Paystack and PayPal payments, Wi-Fi hotspot billing and many business websites.

The skills behind the work

  • Front-end and UI/UX: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and responsive, mobile-first design.
  • Back-end and APIs: PHP, MySQL, and payment integrations with M-Pesa (Daraja), Paystack and PayPal.
  • Platforms: WordPress, hosting and deployment.
  • Systems: security, backups and data management.
  • Design: branding, logos, posters and marketing materials.

A trainer, digital skills and Jitume facilitator

Alongside client work, Kelvin trains and mentors students and professionals in IT, programming and digital skills, with a focus on real, project-based learning: learners build actual websites and projects, not just theory.

As a Jitume facilitator, Kelvin supports the Government of Kenya’s Jitume digital skills initiative, helping young people build the skills for digital and online work. Read more about Jitume.

That same passion is behind the free Marzley Tech learning hub: 46 subjects and 530+ lessons in coding, app development, AI, design, Excel, online safety, networking and making money online, free for anyone with a phone and an internet connection. The goal is simple: make it easier for young Kenyans to gain the skills that lead to jobs, freelancing and their own businesses.
