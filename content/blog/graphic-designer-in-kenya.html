Your logo and brand are often the first thing a customer sees: on your shop sign, your WhatsApp profile, your receipts and your website. Searching for the best designer in Kenya is really a search for someone who understands your business and turns it into a look people remember. Here’s what good design includes and how we work.

What good design includes

  • A logo that works small and large. It should be clear as a WhatsApp profile picture and on a big banner, in colour and in black and white.
  • A simple colour palette. Two or three main colours, with enough contrast for text to be readable.
  • Fonts. One or two fonts used consistently across print and web.
  • Files you own. PNG and SVG versions for print, web and social media.
  • Brand guidelines. A short page showing how to use the logo and colours so everyone stays consistent.
