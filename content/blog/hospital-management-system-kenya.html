A hospital management system (HMS) replaces paper files, duplicate registers and lost receipts with one secure system for patients, doctors, billing, the pharmacy and the lab. For clinics, nursing homes, dental practices and hospitals in Kenya, the right system means shorter queues, fewer revenue leaks and reports at the click of a button.

Marzley Tech Solutions has built hospital and healthcare systems including MediFlow HMS, MediCare Hospital and Supreme HMS. Here’s what we’ve learned about what a Kenyan facility really needs.

Core modules of a good HMS

  • Patient registration: one record per patient with ID or phone number, next of kin, insurance details and full visit history.
  • Queue and triage: patients move from reception to triage to the doctor without paper cards going missing.
  • Consultation (clinical notes): symptoms, diagnosis (ICD-10 codes), prescriptions and lab requests in one screen.
  • Laboratory: requests from doctors, results entry, printable and shareable results.
  • Pharmacy and stock: dispensing against prescriptions, batch numbers, expiry alerts and reorder levels.
  • Billing and payments: automatic bills from services and drugs, M-Pesa STK push, cash, card and insurance.
  • Insurance and SHA: track schemes, pre-authorisations and claims so nothing is forgotten.
  • Appointments and SMS reminders: fewer missed visits.
  • Reports: daily collections, top diagnoses, drug usage, staff activity and MOH reporting summaries.
  • User roles and audit trail: a receptionist sees different screens from a doctor, and every change is logged.

Things Kenyan facilities often forget to ask for

  • Working through internet outages: a local server or offline mode for the busiest desks.
  • Backups off-site, every day, tested.
  • Data protection: patient data is sensitive personal data under the Kenya Data Protection Act. Access must be controlled and logged.
  • Training for every shift, including night staff.
  • Who owns the data if you change providers. It should always be you.

Off-the-shelf or custom?

Ready-made systems are quicker to start but you pay every month and adapt your work to the software. A custom system fits how your facility already works, can grow module by module and has no per-user licence fees. Many facilities start with the core (registration, consultation, billing, pharmacy) and add lab, insurance and inpatient modules later.
