Churches in Kenya run large communities: members, ministries, youth and women’s groups, small groups, events, building projects and giving. A church management system and website helps leaders stay organised and keeps members connected during the week, not only on Sunday.

The website: your church online

  • Service times, location on Google Maps, and how to get there.
  • Sermons (YouTube videos, audio and notes) and live stream links.
  • Events calendar and announcements.
  • Ministries and groups with contacts.
  • Online giving by M-Pesa: tithes, offerings and projects with clear labels.
  • Prayer request and contact forms.

The management system: behind the scenes

  • Member register: families, contacts, ministries, baptism and membership dates.
  • Groups and attendance for cell groups, choirs, youth and Sunday school.
  • Giving records: M-Pesa contributions recorded automatically per member and per project, with statements.
  • Bulk SMS for reminders and announcements.
  • Events and registrations: conferences, camps and weddings.
  • Reports for the board and finance committee.
  • Roles: pastors, group leaders and the finance team see what they need.

Transparency in giving

When giving goes to the church’s own Paybill with a clear account (for example “TITHE” or “BUILDING”), every shilling is recorded and reports match the bank. Members can get statements of their giving, which builds trust.

Frequently asked questions

Can members give tithes by M-Pesa on the website?

Yes. Members choose tithe, offering or a project and pay by M-Pesa; each gift is recorded automatically.

Can the system send SMS to members?

Yes. Send announcements and reminders to all members or specific groups.
