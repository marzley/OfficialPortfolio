Chamas, SACCOs and welfare groups move serious money, often on WhatsApp messages, notebooks and one treasurer’s Excel sheet. That works until a member disputes a balance, the treasurer changes, or the book gets lost. A SACCO or chama management system gives every member a clear, trusted record.

We built Umoja Welfare, a staff welfare platform for the Gatanga community, so we know how much trust and transparency matter.

What members and officials get

  • Member accounts: each member sees their contributions, shares, loans, fines and dividends on their phone.
  • M-Pesa contributions: pay by STK push or Paybill with the member number as the account; balances update automatically. No more screenshots.
  • Loans: applications, guarantors, approval steps, repayment schedules and automatic interest and penalty calculations.
  • Fines and levies: late payments, missed meetings and special contributions (for example harambees or bereavement).
  • Statements: downloadable member statements and group reports.
  • SMS and email notices: payment confirmations, meeting reminders and loan due dates.
  • Officials’ dashboard: totals, arrears, loan book and cash position.
  • Audit trail: every change is logged with who made it, which protects officials as much as members.

Security is not optional

  • Two-step approval for loans and withdrawals.
  • Role-based access (members, treasurer, chair, auditor).
  • Daily off-site backups.
  • Payments go directly to the group’s own Paybill or bank, never through the developer.

Chama, welfare or registered SACCO?

A small chama needs contributions, loans and statements. A welfare group adds benefits and claims. A registered SACCO has regulatory reporting, larger loan products and more controls. We size the system to your group so you don’t pay for what you don’t need.

Frequently asked questions

Can members pay contributions by M-Pesa?

Yes. Members pay through STK push or your Paybill using their member number, and their balance updates automatically.

Can members see their own statements?

Yes. Each member logs in on their phone to see contributions, loans, fines and download statements.

Is it suitable for a small chama?

Yes. Start with contributions, loans and statements, then add features as the group grows.
