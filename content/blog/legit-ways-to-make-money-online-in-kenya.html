Search “make money online in Kenya” and you’ll find a mix of real opportunities and scams that cost people their savings. This guide lists only legitimate ways to earn, with the platforms people actually use, what they pay for, and what you need to start. 1. Freelancing (sell a skill)

Sell writing, design, coding, video editing, virtual assistance or marketing to clients worldwide.

  • Upwork: long-term clients; you send proposals.
  • Fiverr: sell packaged “gigs”.
  • Freelancer.com and Contra: more options.

Compare the best freelance websites for Kenyans.

2. AI training and data annotation

Rate and correct AI answers, label images, record voice. Platforms include Outlier, TELUS Digital, Appen, OneForma and Toloka; availability depends on country and skills. Read our guide to AI data annotation jobs in Kenya.

3. Transcription and writing

Type out audio or write articles and product descriptions. Try GoTranscript, TranscribeMe or Scribie, and find writing clients on Upwork and LinkedIn. Kiswahili transcription is in demand.

4. Graphic design

Design for local businesses, sell on Fiverr and 99designs, and sell templates on Canva Creators or Creative Market. Learn Canva free on our graphic design course.

5. Programming, websites and apps

The best-paid online skill. Build websites and systems for Kenyan businesses or find clients on Upwork, Toptal and Andela. Learn free: HTML, JavaScript, Flutter.

6. Selling online

Sell through WhatsApp Business, Instagram, TikTok, Jumia, Kilimall, Jiji or your own website with M-Pesa. See e-commerce with M-Pesa.

7. Content creation

YouTube (Partner Program after 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours), Facebook, TikTok and Instagram brand deals, podcasts and fan memberships on Patreon. Slow to start, powerful long term.

8. Online teaching

Teach Kiswahili, English or school subjects on Preply or italki, or sell recorded courses on Udemy.

9. Virtual assistance and customer support

Admin, email and customer support for businesses abroad: find roles on Upwork, LinkedIn, We Work Remotely and Kenyan BPOs.

10. Testing websites and micro-tasks

Small but real: UserTesting, uTest and Clickworker. Good for learning; don’t expect a salary.

How to spot a scam

  • You must pay to get a job, “activate” an account or “unlock withdrawals”.
  • Promises of big daily pay for liking videos, watching ads or simple tasks.
  • Someone selling or renting platform accounts.
  • Requests for your M-Pesa PIN, OTP codes or ID photos over WhatsApp/Telegram.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest legit way to make money online in Kenya?

Use a skill you already have: fast typists start with transcription, careful readers with AI data annotation, and social media lovers with managing pages for local businesses.

How do I receive money from online work in Kenya?

Common options are PayPal linked to M-Pesa, Payoneer to a Kenyan bank, direct bank transfers, and M-Pesa for local clients.

Is online income taxable in Kenya?

Yes. Get a KRA PIN, keep records and speak to an accountant about your obligations as your income grows.
