Registering your business name makes it official: you can open a business bank account, apply for an M-Pesa Paybill or Till in the business name, bid for tenders and look more trustworthy to customers. In Kenya it is done online through eCitizen and the Business Registration Service (BRS). Here are the steps.

Business name or limited company?

  • Business name (sole proprietorship or partnership): quick and cheap to register. You and the business are legally the same, so you are personally responsible for its debts. Good for most small businesses starting out.
  • Limited company: a separate legal entity with directors and shareholders. It costs more, needs more paperwork and has yearly filing duties, but protects your personal assets.

This guide covers a business name.

Before you start, have these ready

  • An eCitizen account (it uses your ID number).
  • Your ID and KRA PIN (and those of any partners).
  • Two or three name ideas, in case your first choice is taken.
  • Your business address and location, and what the business does.
  • M-Pesa or a card for the government fee.

Step by step

  1. Sign in to eCitizen and open the Business Registration Service.
  2. Search for and reserve the name. BRS checks that it isn't already used or too similar to another business. If it's rejected, try one of your other ideas.
  3. Choose “Register a business name” and fill in the details: the reserved name, the nature of the business, the address, and the owners or partners.
  4. Pay the fee. The amount is shown before you pay. Fees are set by the government and change from time to time, so always go by the amount eCitizen shows you.
  5. Wait for approval, then download your certificate from your eCitizen account.

Only use the official eCitizen website. Be careful with agents who ask for extra money to “speed up” registration.

After you register

  • Get paid properly: apply for an M-Pesa Till or Paybill in the business name (Till vs Paybill).
  • Get a domain and email: a .co.ke domain and an email like info@yourbusiness.co.ke look professional (how to get a .co.ke domain).
  • Get found online: create a free Google Business Profile and a simple website (website prices).
  • Check your other duties: your county business permit and KRA tax obligations.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

Often a few days once the name is approved, but it can take longer at busy times. Check your eCitizen account for updates.

Do I need a lawyer?

Not for a business name. You can do it yourself on eCitizen with the details above.
