Selling on WhatsApp and Instagram works, but it’s hard to keep track of orders, stock and payments. An e-commerce website lets customers browse, order and pay by M-Pesa any time, while you get orders in one place. Here’s what a good online shop in Kenya includes and how we build them.

What a Kenyan online shop needs

  • M-Pesa checkout: STK Push to the customer’s phone, with automatic confirmation. Cards via Paystack or PayPal if you sell abroad.
  • Product pages that sell: good photos, clear prices, sizes and colours, and stock levels.
  • Delivery options: pickup, riders within town and couriers countrywide, with fees by area.
  • Order management: see new orders, mark them paid, packed and delivered, and message the customer.
  • WhatsApp ordering: a button for customers who prefer to chat first.
  • Found on Google: product titles, descriptions and structured data so products show in search.
  • Fast on phones: most shoppers are on mobile data.
