You don’t need expensive bootcamps to become an app developer. With a laptop (or even a phone to start), discipline and free resources, you can build and publish real Android and iPhone apps. Here’s a roadmap, using the free Marzley Tech learning hub.

Step 1: programming basics (2 to 4 weeks)

Learn variables, conditions, loops, functions and lists. Our Python or JavaScript lessons run in your browser, so you can practise on any device.

Step 2: Dart (2 weeks)

Flutter apps are written in Dart. Work through Dart basics, functions, collections, classes and async.

Step 3: Flutter (6 to 10 weeks)

Our Flutter app development course goes step by step: setup and your first app, widgets, layouts, lists, themes, navigation, forms, state management (Provider and Riverpod), loading data from APIs, local storage with SQLite, Firebase, phone features, animations, a complete shop stock app project, and testing and publishing.

Step 4: backends and APIs (3 to 4 weeks)

Most apps talk to a server. Learn SQL and PHP & MySQL to build your own API, or use Firebase. Understand how M-Pesa payments work: secret keys always stay on the server, never in the app.

Step 5: build 3 portfolio apps

  • A shop stock and sales app (the course project).
  • An app that loads data from an API with login.
  • An app that solves a real problem for someone you know: a chama, a school, a church or a small business.

Step 6: publish and get paid

Publish to Google Play (a one-time US$25 developer account), put your code on GitHub, and record short demo videos. Then find clients locally and on freelance platforms. See Flutter vs React Native and what clients pay for apps in Kenya.

Tips that make the difference

  • Code every day, even 30 minutes.
  • Build, don’t just watch tutorials.
  • Test on a cheap Android phone: that’s what most users have.
  • Join a community or a class for accountability. Marzley Tech also runs project-based training.

Frequently asked questions

Can I learn app development for free in Kenya?

Yes. The Marzley Tech learning hub has free Dart and Flutter lessons with examples, quizzes and printable notes, plus free tools like DartPad and Android Studio.

How long does it take to become an app developer?

With consistent daily practice, many learners build their first real app in 3 to 6 months.

Do I need a powerful computer for Flutter?

8 GB of RAM is a practical minimum for Android Studio. You can start learning on any device using DartPad in the browser.
