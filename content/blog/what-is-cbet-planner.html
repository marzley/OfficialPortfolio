CBET Planner is an online platform, built by Marzley Tech Solutions, that helps trainers in Kenya’s TVET institutions prepare their Competency-Based Education and Training (CBET) documents quickly. Instead of typing every learning plan and session plan from scratch, a trainer starts from the curriculum and the platform drafts the documents in the right structure.

You can open it at cbetplanner.co.ke.

Why it was built

CBET is the approach used across TVET colleges, vocational training centres and national polytechnics in Kenya. It focuses on what learners can actually do at the end of a unit. That is great for learners, but it comes with a lot of paperwork for trainers. Every unit needs a learning plan, session plans, information sheets and assessment tools, and they all have to match the curriculum.

Trainers told us they were spending evenings and weekends formatting documents instead of preparing lessons. CBET Planner was built to give that time back.

What CBET Planner does

  • Starts from your curriculum. Upload the curriculum for your unit, or an existing learning plan.
  • Drafts a learning plan that spreads the content across your sessions.
  • Creates session plans and information sheets from that learning plan, so everything stays consistent.
  • Helps with assessments such as assessment tools and marking schemes.
  • Keeps you in control. You review and edit everything before you download and use it.
  • Works on any device: phone, tablet or computer, in the browser.

Who it’s for

  • TVET trainers who want to spend less time on paperwork.
  • New trainers learning the CBET format for the first time.
  • Heads of department who want consistent documents across a team.
  • Institutions preparing for quality audits, where complete documentation matters.

How to get started

  1. Go to cbetplanner.co.ke and create an account.
  2. Follow the guides on the site to upload your curriculum.
  3. Generate your learning plan, then your session plans and information sheets.
  4. Review, edit and download.
