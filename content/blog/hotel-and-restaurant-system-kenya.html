Hospitality runs on speed and trust: orders must reach the kitchen correctly, bills must be right, rooms must never be double-booked, and the owner must know what was sold tonight. A hotel and restaurant system handles all of that, whether you run a café in Thika, a guest house in Naivasha or a hotel in Mombasa.

Restaurant and bar features

  • Table and waiter orders from a tablet or phone, sent straight to the kitchen or bar.
  • Kitchen display or printer: orders print where they’re prepared.
  • Menu with variations: portions, extras and combos.
  • Bills and split bills, paid by M-Pesa, cash or card.
  • Stock and recipes: selling a plate of pilau reduces rice, meat and spices automatically.
  • Waiter and shift reports: sales per waiter, voids and cash-up.
  • QR menus and online ordering for delivery and take-away.

Hotel and guest house features

  • Room calendar: availability at a glance, no double bookings.
  • Online booking on your website with M-Pesa or card deposits, so you keep the commission you’d pay booking sites.
  • Check-in and check-out with guest records.
  • Charges to the room from the restaurant, bar and laundry.
  • Housekeeping status for each room.
  • Occupancy and revenue reports.

Frequently asked questions

Can guests book rooms on my website?

Yes. Guests choose dates, see available rooms and pay a deposit by M-Pesa or card, and the room calendar updates automatically.

Can waiters take orders on phones?

Yes. Orders go from the waiter’s phone or tablet to the kitchen or bar printer instantly.

Does the system track kitchen stock?

Yes. Recipes link menu items to ingredients, so stock reduces with every plate sold.
