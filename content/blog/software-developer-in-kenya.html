A custom system built by a software developer in Kenya who understands local needs (M-Pesa payments, TVET requirements, slow connections) can fix that.

How a custom system project works

  1. Discovery: we map how you work today and what slows you down.
  2. A written scope: features, users, reports, timeline and price.
  3. Build in stages: you test the core features early, then we add the rest.
  4. Training and handover: your team learns the system, and you get the logins and documentation.
  5. Support: backups, updates and fixes on a care plan.

Technology we use

PHP and MySQL on reliable Kenyan and international hosting, modern JavaScript on the front end, and integrations with M-Pesa (Daraja), Paystack, PayPal, email and SMS. Systems are secured with https, role-based logins, CSRF protection and regular backups, and payment keys stay on the server.
