A website isn’t finished on launch day. Like a car, it needs regular care to keep running. Most business owners only find out something is wrong when a customer calls to say the site is down, or worse, when customers quietly stop calling.

What goes wrong without care

  • The domain expires. Domains renew every year. Miss the date and your website and business email both stop working. Getting the name back can take days, and sometimes it’s gone for good.
  • The site goes down and nobody notices. Hosting problems, full storage or an expired SSL certificate can take a site offline for days before anyone checks.
  • Old software gets hacked. Out-of-date plugins and themes are the most common way small business sites are broken into. The first sign is often Google warning visitors that your site is unsafe.
  • No backup when it matters. After a hack or a mistake, a recent backup means you’re back online in an hour. Without one, you may be paying to rebuild.
  • Small changes pile up. New prices, a new branch, a staff photo. When updates are hard to get done, the site slowly goes out of date.
