“How much is an app?” is like asking “how much is a house?” A one-room house and a mansion are both houses. This guide explains what drives the cost of an app in Kenya, typical ranges, and the hidden costs people forget.

What makes an app cheaper or more expensive

  • Type: a web app or PWA is usually cheaper than native Android and iPhone apps. Cross-platform tools like Flutter build Android and iPhone from one codebase.
  • Number of screens and features: login, profiles, payments, chat, maps, notifications, offline mode and admin dashboards each add work.
  • Payments: M-Pesa STK push, card payments and escrow need a secure backend.
  • The backend and admin panel: most apps need a server, database and a web dashboard for the owner. This is often half the work.
  • Design: custom design and branding versus a clean standard design.
  • Integrations: SMS, WhatsApp, Google Maps, KRA eTIMS or other systems.

Typical ranges in Kenya

  • Simple web app or portal (forms, records, reports): from about KSh 40,000 to 60,000 and up.
  • Business app with payments and an admin dashboard: commonly KSh 100,000 to 300,000.
  • Marketplace or on-demand app (customers, providers, payments, maps, ratings): KSh 300,000 to 1,000,000+.

These are typical market ranges, not fixed prices. Get a written quote based on a written list of features.

Costs after the build

  • Google Play developer account: US$25 once. Apple’s developer programme is US$99 per year.
  • Hosting and domain for the backend: monthly or yearly.
  • SMS and other services you use.
  • Maintenance: Android and iOS update every year; apps need updates, fixes and security patches. Budget a monthly support plan.

How to save money (without a bad app)

  1. Start with an MVP: the smallest version that solves the main problem. Add features after real users try it.
  2. Consider a PWA first: one app for all phones, no store approval, cheaper to update.
  3. Use cross-platform (Flutter) if you need Android and iPhone.
  4. Write down your requirements before asking for quotes, so quotes are comparable.
  5. Don’t choose the cheapest quote blindly: ask to use apps the developer has built.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a simple app cost in Kenya?

A simple web app or portal often starts from about KSh 40,000 to 60,000. Apps with payments, admin dashboards and native features cost more.

How much is a Google Play developer account?

A one-time fee of US$25. Apple charges US$99 per year for iPhone apps.

Is a PWA cheaper than an Android app?

Usually, yes. One PWA works on all phones and computers and updates instantly without store approval.
