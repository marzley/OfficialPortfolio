Whether you run a minimart, a hardware store, an agrovet, a boutique or a supermarket, the same questions keep owners awake: How much did we really sell today? What’s running out? Is anyone stealing? A POS (point of sale) and inventory system answers them automatically.

What the system does

  • Sell fast: barcode scanning or quick search, discounts, and split payments (part cash, part M-Pesa).
  • M-Pesa built in: STK push to the customer’s phone or till confirmation, recorded with the sale.
  • Live stock: every sale reduces stock; every delivery adds it. Units like pieces, kilos, metres and packets.
  • Low-stock alerts and suggested orders.
  • Suppliers and purchases: track deliveries and what you owe.
  • Credit customers: record debts and send reminders (very common for hardware and agrovet shops).
  • Staff accounts: who sold what, voids and discounts logged.
  • Reports: daily, weekly and monthly sales, profit, best and slow sellers, stock value.
  • Records for tax: clean sales records make KRA returns and eTIMS compliance easier (ask your accountant about your obligations).

Why custom instead of a generic app?

Every type of shop is different. A hardware store sells cement by the bag and wire by the metre. An agrovet tracks seed batches. A boutique tracks sizes and colours. A system built around your shop avoids the workarounds that make staff stop using generic software.

Signs you need a POS now

  • You can’t tell your profit without counting stock by hand.
  • Cash and M-Pesa totals rarely match the sales book.
  • Items run out before you notice.
  • You want to open a second branch but can’t be in two places.

Frequently asked questions

Does a POS system work without internet?

It can. A local setup keeps selling offline and syncs to the cloud when the connection returns.

Can customers pay with M-Pesa?

Yes. The system sends an STK push or confirms till payments and records them against the sale.

Can I manage several shops?

Yes. A web-based POS shows every branch’s sales and stock in one dashboard, and you can move stock between branches.
