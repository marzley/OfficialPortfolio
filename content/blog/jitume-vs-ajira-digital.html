Two government programme names come up whenever young Kenyans talk about online work: Jitume and Ajira Digital. They’re related but not the same. Here’s a simple explanation.

Ajira Digital

  • What: a government initiative to help young Kenyans find digital and online work (“ajira” means employment).
  • Offers: training in online work, mentorship, awareness about real platforms and scams, Ajira clubs in universities and TVETs, and Ajira centres.
  • Website: ajiradigital.go.ke

Jitume

  • What: a government programme that sets up Jitume digital labs (computers and internet) in public institutions and trains young people for digital work.
  • Offers: physical access to equipment and internet, facilitators, training in the ten online earning categories, and links to work opportunities.
  • Run by: the Konza Technopolis Development Authority with the ICT Authority and partners.

The key difference

Think of Ajira as the training and awareness movement for online work, and Jitume as the network of labs that gives you a place, a computer, internet and a facilitator to do it. Many learners benefit from both: Ajira’s training and community, and a Jitume lab for daily practice and work.

Which should you join?

  • No computer or internet at home? Find a Jitume lab first.
  • At a university or TVET with an Ajira club? Join it for training and peers.
  • Both available? Use both.

Frequently asked questions

Are Jitume and Ajira Digital the same?

No. Both are government programmes for digital work: Ajira Digital focuses on training, awareness and mentorship, while Jitume provides digital labs with computers, internet and facilitators.

Can I join both Jitume and Ajira?

Yes. Many learners use Ajira training and a Jitume lab together.

Does Ajira Digital give jobs directly?

Ajira trains and connects you to opportunities and platforms; you still apply for and win the work yourself.
