When someone in Nakuru searches “plumber near me” or “website designer Kenya”, Google shows a list of results. Most people click one of the first few. SEO (search engine optimisation) is the work of making your website appear high in those results, without paying for ads.

How search engines work

  1. Crawling. Google’s robots visit web pages and follow links to find new ones.
  2. Indexing. Google stores what each page is about in a huge index.
  3. Ranking. When someone searches, Google picks the most useful, trustworthy pages from its index and orders them.

SEO makes sure Google can find your pages, understands them, and trusts them enough to show them.

The three parts of SEO

1. Technical SEO

  • A secure website (https).
  • Fast loading, especially on phones and mobile data.
  • A mobile-friendly design.
  • A sitemap and clean links so Google can find every page.

2. On-page SEO (your content)

  • A clear page title and search description for every page. These are what people see in Google’s results.
  • Content that answers real questions your customers ask, using the words they actually search for.
  • Proper headings, image descriptions (alt text) and internal links between your pages.

3. Off-page SEO (trust)

  • A complete Google Business Profile with photos, hours and reviews. See our guide to getting your business on Google Maps.
  • Links from other trusted websites: directories, partners, news mentions.
  • Consistent business name, phone and location everywhere online.

Simple SEO steps you can take today

  1. Run a free website check to see your security, speed, mobile and Google basics.
  2. Create or claim your Google Business Profile and ask happy customers for reviews.
  3. Add your site to Google Search Console (free) and submit your sitemap.
  4. Write one helpful page or blog post a month answering a customer question.
  5. Share your pages on WhatsApp, Facebook and LinkedIn.

How long does SEO take?

SEO is not instant. New pages can take days to weeks to appear, and good rankings for competitive searches usually take a few months of steady work. Be careful with anyone who “guarantees number one on Google”: nobody can promise that.
