Payroll in Kenya has many moving parts: PAYE, SHIF (which replaced NHIF), NSSF tiers and the Affordable Housing Levy, plus allowances, overtime, advances and loans. Rates change with new laws. An HR and payroll system calculates it correctly every month and keeps records ready for audits.

Payroll features

  • Gross pay with allowances, overtime, commissions and bonuses.
  • Statutory deductions: PAYE (with personal relief and other reliefs), SHIF, NSSF and Housing Levy, using rates you can update when the law changes.
  • Other deductions: SACCO contributions, loans, advances and welfare.
  • Payslips sent by email or downloadable by staff.
  • Statutory reports for filing (for example P10 data for KRA iTax and contribution schedules).
  • Bank and M-Pesa bulk payment files.

HR features

  • Employee records: contracts, IDs, KRA PIN, NSSF and SHIF numbers, next of kin.
  • Leave applications and approvals with balances.
  • Attendance (manual, biometric or phone check-in).
  • Staff self-service: payslips, leave and personal details.
  • Documents: offer letters, warnings and appraisals.

Accuracy and privacy

Payroll data is confidential. Access should be limited to HR and finance, every change logged, and backups kept. Always have your accountant confirm the current statutory rates; a good system lets you update them without a developer.

Frequently asked questions

Does the payroll system calculate PAYE, SHIF, NSSF and Housing Levy?

Yes. It calculates statutory deductions from rates you can update when the law changes, and produces reports for filing.

Can staff download their payslips?

Yes. Staff log in to see payslips, apply for leave and update their details.

Can salaries be paid by M-Pesa?

Yes. The system can produce bulk payment files for M-Pesa B2C or your bank.
