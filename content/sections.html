How we keep you safe

Clear promises on ownership, security and your data, in plain language.

You own everything

Your domain, hosting account, website files and content are yours. We hand over all logins when the project is done.

Secure by default

Every site runs on https, with up-to-date software, strong passwords and protection for forms and logins.

Backups

We set up backups so a mistake or hack never costs you your website. Care plans keep them running.

Customer information is only used for your project, kept to what’s needed, and handled with Kenya’s Data Protection Act in mind.
