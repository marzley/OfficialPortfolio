FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about websites, payments, systems, training and support. Can’t find yours? Ask on WhatsApp.

Websites

How long does it take to build a website?

Most landing pages take about a week and business websites a few weeks. Online shops and systems depend on the features. You get a clear timeline with your quote, and having your content (text and photos) ready speeds things up.

Will my website work well on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on phones, because most visitors in Kenya browse on mobile data.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We can build on WordPress or add a simple admin area so you can change text, photos and products, and we show you how to use it.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. The domain, hosting account, files and content are yours, and you get all the logins when the project is done.

Payments

How does M-Pesa payment on a website work?

The customer enters their phone number at checkout and gets an M-Pesa PIN prompt (STK push) on their phone. Once they pay, Safaricom confirms the payment and the website updates the order automatically.

Do I need a paybill or till number?

Yes. Payments go straight to your own till or paybill. We help you set up the Safaricom Daraja connection your website needs.

How do I pay for my project?

A deposit starts the work, and the balance is paid in stages as the project progresses. You can pay by M-Pesa (Till 6095737), bank transfer or card.

Systems & portals

Can you build a system for my hospital, school or business?

Yes. Past work includes hospital management systems, CBET Planner for TVET trainers, a Wi-Fi billing system and online stores with admin tools. Each system is built around how you work.

Is my data safe in the system?

Systems use secure logins, https, careful user permissions and regular backups. Your data belongs to you and is only used for your project.

How do I follow my project’s progress?

Clients get access to the client portal to see progress, updates and files, approve designs, open support requests and pay invoices by M-Pesa.

Training

Do I need experience to join a class?

No. Courses start from the basics and build up with real projects. Tell us your level when you enrol and we’ll place you in the right class.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Students who complete a course get a certificate with a code that employers can verify online.

Support & care

What happens after my website goes live?

We show you how to manage it and stay available for support. For ongoing peace of mind, care plans cover backups, updates, security and small changes every month.

What if my website gets hacked or breaks?

With a care plan, we restore it from a backup and fix the cause. Without one, contact us and we’ll quote the repair quickly.

    Privacy policy

    Last updated 27 September 2026

    This policy explains what personal information Marzley Tech Solutions (“we”, “us”) collects through this website, our client portal and our services, why we collect it, and your rights under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.

    Who we are

    Marzley Tech Solutions is a web development, systems and IT training business in Kenya. For any privacy question or request, contact us at marzleytechsolutionltd@gmail.com or +254 745 789 590.

    What we collect

    • When you contact us or book a call: your name, email, phone number and your message.
    • When you pay by M-Pesa: your phone number, the amount, and the M-Pesa receipt number and result that Safaricom sends us.
    • In the client portal: the name and email of the Google account you sign in with, your projects, invoices, files, approvals, support messages and course progress. We keep a log of sign-ins and changes (with the IP address) to keep accounts secure.
    • If you join our mailing list: your email (and name if you give it), kept only after you confirm by email. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and we never share the list.
    • If you join the referral programme: your name and M-Pesa number, so we can send your reward when someone you referred becomes a client.
    • If you rate your project: your rating and comment. We only show your comment and name on our website if you tick the box allowing it.
    • When you use the website chat: your questions are answered in your browser from our own information. If AI answers are switched on, a question we can’t answer that way is sent, with the last few messages of the chat, to our AI provider to write a reply. We don’t store chat conversations. If you ask for a call back, your name and number are saved like any other enquiry.
    • In the learning hub: if you create an account, your name and email (or your Google account’s name and email), the lessons you complete, the videos you unlock with the M-Pesa details above, and your likes and comments. Other learners who unlocked a video see your comments with your first name and initial, never your email. While you watch a paid video, your email and M-Pesa number are shown faintly over it on your own screen, to discourage sharing recordings. Code you write in the practice editor runs only in your browser and is saved only on your device.
    • When you use the free website check: the website address you enter. We keep a short record of your IP address to stop misuse.
    • Website statistics: only if you accept, Google Analytics records how pages are used, with IP addresses shortened. If you decline, it never loads. You can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of each page.
    • On your device: the site remembers your theme, accessibility choices and similar settings in your browser. These stay on your device.

    Why we use it

    • To reply to you, prepare quotes and deliver the work you ask for (performing a contract with you).
    • To take payments, send invoices, receipts and reminders, and keep financial records (contract and legal obligations, including tax).
    • To run the client portal securely and prevent fraud (our legitimate interest in protecting you and us).
    • To understand and improve the website, only with your consent.

    We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

    Who we share it with

    Only with the services we need to do the work: Safaricom (M-Pesa payments), Paystack (card payments), Google (portal sign-in and, with consent, Analytics), Formspree (contact form delivery), Africa’s Talking (SMS alerts, if enabled), Anthropic (to answer questions typed into the website chat, if AI answers are switched on; please don’t type personal or payment details there), and our web hosting and email provider. Each handles your data under its own privacy terms. Some of these providers may process data outside Kenya, with appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

    How long we keep it

    • Enquiries that do not become a project: up to 12 months.
    • Client, project and payment records: for as long as you are a client, then up to 7 years for tax and accounting.
    • Security logs: up to 12 months. Backups are kept for 14 days.

    How we protect it

    The site uses https everywhere. Portal passwords are handled by Google, sessions sign out automatically when idle, private files and backups are stored outside the public website, and access is limited to our team.

    Your rights

    You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it (where we do not need to keep it by law), stop using it for a purpose, or give you a copy. Contact us at the details above and we will respond within 7 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at www.odpc.go.ke.
