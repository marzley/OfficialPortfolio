The problem

Prospective students and parents want quick answers: which courses are offered, what they need to join and how to apply. A college website has to answer those questions clearly, look trustworthy and work well on the phones most visitors use.

The goal

Build an institutional website for Gatanga Technical and Vocational College that presents the college professionally, makes its courses easy to find and keeps students and the public up to date with news.

What we built

  • Course information organised so visitors can quickly find the right programme.
  • Student information in one place, from admissions to what to expect.
  • News and updates so the college can share announcements and events.
  • Mobile-first design that works well on any phone, tablet or computer.
  • Contact details and location that are easy to reach from every page.

How it’s built

  • A responsive website on the college’s own .ac.ke domain.
  • Clean structure and headings, so the site is easy for search engines to understand.
  • Content that the college can keep up to date as courses and news change.

The result

The website is live at gatangatvc.ac.ke, giving the college a professional online presence where students and parents can find courses and news.
