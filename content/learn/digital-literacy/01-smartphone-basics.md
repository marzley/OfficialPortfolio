# Getting the most from your smartphone

For most Kenyans the phone **is** the computer: M-Pesa, WhatsApp, school portals, eCitizen, job applications and online classes all happen on it. A few skills make it faster, safer and cheaper to use.

## Set it up properly

1. **Screen lock**: a PIN (6 digits or more), pattern or fingerprint. Never "None".
2. **Google account** (Android) or Apple ID: needed for the Play Store, backups and finding a lost phone. Use a strong password and turn on 2-step verification.
3. **Find My Device** (Android: Settings → Security → Find My Device; iPhone: Find My): locate, lock or erase a lost phone.
4. **Automatic updates**: Settings → System → Software update, and Play Store → Settings → Auto-update apps.
5. **Backups**: Google One / iCloud for contacts and photos; WhatsApp's own chat backup (Settings → Chats → Chat backup).

## Save data and money

| Tip | Where |
|---|---|
| Turn on **Data Saver** | Settings → Network & internet → Data Saver |
| See which apps use the most data | Settings → Network → Data usage |
| Stop apps using data in the background | App info → Mobile data → Background data off |
| WhatsApp: don't auto-download media | WhatsApp → Settings → Storage and data |
| YouTube: lower quality or download on Wi-Fi | ⚙ → Quality |
| Update apps on Wi-Fi only | Play Store settings |
| Use "Lite" versions | Facebook Lite, Messenger Lite, Google Go |

## Free up storage

- WhatsApp → Settings → Storage and data → **Manage storage**: delete big videos and forwarded files.
- Google Photos → **Free up space** (after backing up).
- Clear the cache of heavy apps: Settings → Apps → App → Storage → Clear cache.
- Uninstall apps you haven't opened in months.

## Useful skills

| Skill | How (Android, similar on iPhone) |
|---|---|
| **Screenshot** | Power + Volume down |
| **Share your internet (hotspot)** | Settings → Network → Hotspot & tethering |
| **Scan a QR code** | Open the camera and point it at the code |
| **Scan documents to PDF** | Google Drive → + → Scan (great for applications) |
| **Copy text from a photo** | Google Lens (in the camera or Photos) |
| **Voice typing** | Tap the microphone on the keyboard |
| **Translate** | Google Translate app, including the camera mode |
| **Split screen** | Recent apps → app icon → Split screen |

## App permissions: who can see what

Settings → Privacy → **Permission manager** shows which apps can use your camera, microphone, location, contacts and SMS. Remove permissions that don't make sense (why does a torch app need your contacts?). Be very careful with apps that request **SMS** or **accessibility** access: fraud apps use them to read M-Pesa messages and one-time codes.

## Battery care

- Lower screen brightness or use auto-brightness and **dark mode**.
- Turn off Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and location when not needed.
- Use the original or a good-quality charger. Cheap chargers can damage batteries.
- Avoid leaving the phone in hot places (a car dashboard).

## Accessibility features

Bigger text (Settings → Display → Font size), **TalkBack** (a screen reader for blind users), magnification, and captions for videos help family members with poor eyesight or hearing.

## Why smartphone skills matter

For many people in Kenya, the smartphone is their main (or only) computer. It's used for M-Pesa and banking, eCitizen and KRA services, job applications, online classes, business on WhatsApp and Instagram, maps, health information and staying in touch with family. Knowing how to use it well saves money on data and airtime, protects your money and personal information, and opens up learning and earning opportunities.

## Security set-up checklist

| Setting | Where (Android, may vary by brand) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Screen lock (PIN of 6+ digits, pattern, or fingerprint) | Settings → Security | Stops anyone who picks up your phone |
| Find My Device | Settings → Google → Find My Device | Locate, ring, lock or erase a lost phone |
| SIM PIN | Settings → Security → SIM card lock | Stops a thief using your SIM in another phone |
| Google account 2-Step Verification | myaccount.google.com → Security | Protects your email, photos and backups |
| Automatic updates | Settings → System → Software update | Fixes security holes |
| Backup | Settings → Google → Backup | Restore contacts, photos and settings on a new phone |
| Lock-screen notifications | Settings → Notifications | Hide message content (and M-Pesa SMS) on the lock screen |

Write down your phone's **IMEI** number (dial `*#06#`) and keep it somewhere safe: the police and your network may need it if the phone is stolen.

## Organising your phone

- Group similar apps in folders (Money, Work, Social, Learning) and keep the most-used on the home screen.
- Remove apps you haven't used in months: they use storage and may run in the background.
- Use **widgets** for things you check often (calendar, weather, notes).
- Turn on **Do Not Disturb** schedules for sleep and study time, with exceptions for family.
- Use **Digital Wellbeing** (Settings → Digital Wellbeing) to see screen time and set app timers.

## Connecting your phone to other devices

- **Hotspot**: share mobile data with a laptop (Settings → Network → Hotspot); set a strong password and watch your data use.
- **Bluetooth**: earphones, speakers, car systems; turn it off when not in use.
- **USB transfer**: connect to a computer and choose "File transfer" to copy photos and documents.
- **Nearby Share / Quick Share**: send files to nearby Android phones without data.
- **Cast/Screen mirroring**: show your screen on a smart TV for presentations or videos.

## Mobile data management

```
Settings → Network & internet → Data usage
   • Set a data warning and limit
   • See which apps used the most data this month
   • Turn on Data Saver
   • Restrict background data for heavy apps
```

Big data savers: turn off auto-play videos in Facebook, Instagram and TikTok; set WhatsApp media auto-download to "Wi-Fi only"; download YouTube videos and Spotify music on Wi-Fi; update apps on Wi-Fi only (Play Store settings).

## Troubleshooting common problems

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Phone slow or freezing | Restart; free up storage (keep at least 10% free); remove unused apps; update |
| Battery drains fast | Check Battery usage for a misbehaving app; lower brightness; turn off unused location, Bluetooth, hotspot |
| App keeps crashing | Clear cache (Settings → Apps → app → Storage), update, or reinstall |
| No mobile data | Check bundle balance, toggle Airplane mode, check APN settings, restart |
| Phone overheating | Remove the case, stop charging, close heavy apps, keep it out of the sun |
| Forgot the screen lock | Use Find My Device or a factory reset (erases data: this is why backups matter) |

## Buying a phone wisely

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| RAM (4 GB minimum, 6 to 8 GB better) | Smooth multitasking |
| Storage (64 GB minimum, 128 GB better) | Space for apps, photos, WhatsApp media |
| Years of software updates promised | Security and app compatibility |
| Battery capacity (mAh) and charging speed | All-day use |
| Genuine device from an authorised dealer | Counterfeit phones may be blocked from networks, and have no warranty |
| Second-hand: IMEI not blacklisted, Google account removed, receipt | Avoid buying stolen phones |

## Accessibility features in more detail

- **TalkBack** (Android) / **VoiceOver** (iPhone): reads the screen aloud for blind users.
- **Font size and display size**: larger text for older users.
- **Magnification**: triple-tap to zoom.
- **Live Caption**: captions for videos and calls.
- **Voice Access**: control the phone by speaking.
- **Colour correction** for colour blindness.

Set these up for parents and grandparents to help them use mobile money and messaging confidently.

## Practice

1. Turn on Find My Device and test it from a computer (ring your phone).
2. Dial `*#06#` and record your IMEI safely.
3. Check this month's data usage and restrict background data for the top app.
4. Scan a document to PDF with Google Drive and email it to yourself.
5. Set up Digital Wellbeing timers for the two apps you use most.

:::think Your phone is stolen. In what order would you act to protect your money and data?
First, call your network (from another phone) to block the SIM and prevent SIM-based M-Pesa or bank access, and alert your bank. Then use Find My Device to lock or erase the phone, change your Google and important passwords, and report to the police with the IMEI. Backups mean you can restore your data on a new phone.
:::
