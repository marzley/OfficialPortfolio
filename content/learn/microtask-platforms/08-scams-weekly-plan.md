# Task scams to avoid, the best platforms to start with in Kenya, and a realistic weekly plan

You now know 55 real platforms. This lesson shows the scams that copy them, which ones to start with, and how to turn spare hours into steady small income and then into better-paid work.

## Task scams that target Kenyans

| Scam | How it works | The giveaway |
|---|---|---|
| **Deposit-to-unlock “task apps”** | You earn small amounts liking videos or rating products, then must top up to unlock “premium” tasks or withdraw | Any payment to work or to withdraw |
| **Telegram/WhatsApp “like and earn” groups** | A “mentor” pays you a few shillings, then moves you to paid “merchant tasks” | Recruitment in chats; growing deposits |
| **Account selling and renting** | “Verified Remotasks/Outlier/MTurk accounts” for sale or rent | Breaks platform rules; sellers often vanish |
| **Fake recruiters** | “Pay KSh 1,500 for training and placement on TELUS/Appen” | Real exams and applications are free |
| **Recruitment schemes** | Earnings depend on signing up friends | Income from recruiting, not work |
| **Fake look-alike apps** | Copies of real apps that steal logins | Wrong developer name; asks for M-Pesa PIN |

:::warning If you've been scammed
Stop paying immediately (scammers often ask for a “final fee”). Keep screenshots and M-Pesa messages, report the number to Safaricom by forwarding scam SMS to 333, and report to the DCI. Warn friends. See [Avoiding online money scams](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=avoid-scams).
:::

## Best platforms to start with in Kenya

| Goal | Start with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Learn how online earning works | Timebucks, ySense, Attapoll | Open to Kenyans, low minimums |
| Steady small tasks | Clickworker (and UHRS through it), Microworkers | Real task marketplaces |
| Better pay with skills | GoTranscript (typing), uTest or UserTesting (testing) | Quality raises your income |
| AI training | CrowdGen (Appen), TELUS Digital, OneForma; Outlier if eligible | Project-based but better paid |
| Local and phone-only | GeoPoll (airtime), Premise | Built for African markets |
| Formal annotation jobs | CloudFactory, Sama (when hiring) | Employment with training |

Always confirm current country eligibility on each official site.

## A realistic weekly plan (1–2 hours a day)

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Monday | Check AI training and testing dashboards for new projects; apply to open ones |
| Tuesday | Transcription or marketplace tasks (Clickworker, GoTranscript) |
| Wednesday | Testing invites and screeners; tidy your tester profiles |
| Thursday | Transcription or marketplace tasks |
| Friday | Surveys in spare minutes; learn one new skill (Excel, typing speed, testing basics) |
| Weekend | Review earnings, withdraw when you reach minimums, plan next week |

**Track it:** a simple spreadsheet with date, platform, minutes worked and amount earned. After a month, drop the platforms with the lowest pay per hour.

## Moving up from microtasks

1. **Typing and English** → transcription and captioning.
2. **Testing** → QA and software testing careers (see [Software testing in the directory](./?track=earn-directory&lesson=web-tech-services-methods)).
3. **AI rating** → specialist AI training (coding, maths, languages).
4. **Organisation** → virtual assistance (see [Virtual assistant](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=virtual-assistant-work)).

:::think You've earned $30 on ySense, $12 on Timebucks and nothing after two weeks on a deposit-based “like videos” app that now asks for KSh 2,000. What should you do?
Withdraw your ySense and Timebucks earnings once you hit the minimums, and walk away from the deposit app without paying anything: it's a scam pattern. Put the time into one platform where skills raise pay, like transcription or testing.
:::

## Summary

- Deposit-to-unlock apps, “like and earn” groups, account sales and fee-charging recruiters are scams.
- Start with Timebucks, ySense, Attapoll, Clickworker, GoTranscript, GeoPoll and testing platforms; apply to AI training projects.
- Work a weekly plan, track pay per hour, and move up to skilled work.
