# Survey panels and apps: Toluna, YouGov, Attapoll, GeoPoll, Prolific and more

Survey platforms pay for your opinions. Supply depends heavily on your age, location and profile, and screen-outs are normal. Academic research platforms usually pay the fairest rates.

:::warning Check before you rely on it
Countries, payment options and minimum payouts change often. Confirm on each platform's official website, and never pay anyone to join.
:::

## 29. Toluna

**What it is:** A large global survey community with points for surveys and polls.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Market research surveys, polls, product tests |
| How you're paid | Points redeemed for gift cards or cash options by country |
| Kenya | Operates in many countries; check if there's a panel for Kenya |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | People who enjoy giving opinions |

**How to start:** Complete your profile; answer consistently.
**Pros:** Big brand; many surveys in supported countries. **Cons:** Gift-card heavy; country-limited.
**Watch out:** Inconsistent answers get accounts closed.

## 30. YouGov

**What it is:** A polling and research company with online panels.

| | |
|---|---|

| How you're paid | Points redeemed for cash or vouchers in supported countries |
| Kenya | Panels exist in many countries; check whether Kenya has one open |
| Realistic earnings | Low |

**How to start:** Join only if your country panel is listed.
**Pros:** Respected research company. **Cons:** Slow to reach payouts.
**Watch out:** Use the official site only.

## 31. Attapoll

**What it is:** A survey app that pools surveys from many providers.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Short surveys |
| How you're paid | PayPal and other options, low minimum |
| Kenya | Available in many countries including Kenya |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | Phone users with spare minutes |

**How to start:** Turn on notifications for new surveys; answer honestly.
**Pros:** Low minimum payout. **Cons:** Many screen-outs.
**Watch out:** Survey routers sometimes fail: report it, don't create new accounts.

## 35. GeoPoll

**What it is:** An Africa-focused research company that surveys people by SMS, app and phone.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Short surveys on brands, health, media and society |
| How you're paid | Airtime after completing a survey; GeoPoll also lists M-Pesa as an incentive in Kenya |
| Kenya | Active in Kenya, with direct links to mobile networks |
| Realistic earnings | Low (airtime) |
| Best for | Anyone with a phone in Kenya |

**How to start:** Respond to genuine GeoPoll survey messages; check the sender is official.
**Pros:** Works on basic phones; Kenya-focused. **Cons:** Small rewards.
**Watch out:** Scammers may impersonate survey firms: GeoPoll never asks for your M-Pesa PIN.

## 37. Prolific

**What it is:** An academic research platform used by universities to run studies.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Surveys, experiments, AI-related studies |
| How you're paid | PayPal |
| Kenya | Only some countries are accepted; check the current list before joining |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium per hour (studies must pay a fair minimum) |
| Best for | Honest, attentive participants |

**How to start:** Join the waitlist if your country is supported; answer attention checks carefully.
**Pros:** Fair pay and respectful treatment. **Cons:** Limited countries; waitlists.
**Watch out:** Never lie about your location or demographics: it's research fraud.

## Summary

- Survey platforms pay little and depend on your profile; screen-outs are normal.
- Attapoll, TGM Panel and GeoPoll (airtime) are practical in Kenya; Toluna, YouGov, LifePoints and Prolific are country-limited.
- Answer honestly and consistently; never fake your location or details.
