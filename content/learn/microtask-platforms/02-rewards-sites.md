# Rewards and “get paid to” sites: Timebucks, ySense, Freecash and 7 more

Rewards sites (also called GPT, “get paid to”, sites) mix small tasks, surveys, app and game offers and short videos. They're easy to join and pay little, so use them for spare minutes, not as a job.

:::warning Check before you rely on it
Countries, payment options and minimum payouts change often. Confirm on each platform's official website, and never pay anyone to join.
:::

## 1. Timebucks

**What it is:** A long-running rewards site with many ways to earn small amounts.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Surveys, short tasks, app installs and offers, watching content, social tasks, referrals |
| How you're paid | PayPal and other options such as bank transfer or wallets; options vary by country; payout after a minimum balance |
| Kenya | Open to most countries and widely used in Kenya |
| Realistic earnings | Low: typically a few dollars a month with regular use |
| Best for | Beginners learning how rewards sites work |

**How to start:** Sign up with your real details, complete your profile so surveys match you, and stick to tasks with clear instructions.
**Pros:** Many task types; long track record. **Cons:** Very low pay per task; survey screen-outs.
**Watch out:** Offers that ask for card details or paid subscriptions: skip them.

## 2. ySense

**What it is:** A rewards and survey site (formerly ClixSense) with a clean dashboard.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Surveys from many partners, tasks, offers, daily checklists |
| How you're paid | PayPal, Payoneer, Skrill or gift cards (options vary by country) |
| Kenya | Available in many countries including Kenya; survey supply depends on your profile |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | People who prefer surveys over offers |

**How to start:** Fill in every profile survey; do the daily checklist; withdraw once you reach the minimum.
**Pros:** Reliable payouts reported over many years. **Cons:** Few surveys for some demographics.
**Watch out:** Never answer randomly: quality checks can close your account.

## 3. Freecash

**What it is:** A rewards site focused on game and app offers, with surveys and tasks too.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Play games to set levels, try apps, surveys |
| How you're paid | Crypto, PayPal or gift cards, with low minimums |
| Kenya | Open to many countries; check offers available in Kenya |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium for heavy gamers; offers vary a lot |
| Best for | People who already play mobile games |

**How to start:** Pick offers with clear milestones; screenshot your progress in case support needs proof.
**Pros:** Fast, low payouts. **Cons:** Offers can take weeks; some never credit.
**Watch out:** Don't make in-game purchases to reach a milestone unless you'd buy them anyway.

## 4. Swagbucks

**What it is:** One of the best-known rewards sites, mostly for a few wealthy markets.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Surveys, shopping cashback, videos, searches |
| How you're paid | PayPal and gift cards |
| Kenya | Mainly the US, UK, Canada, Australia and a few others; usually not open in Kenya |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | People living in supported countries |

**How to start:** Check the supported country list first.
**Pros:** Big, trusted brand. **Cons:** Not available in most of Africa.
**Watch out:** Ads claiming Swagbucks “now pays Kenyans via M-Pesa” are not official.

## 5. PrizeRebel

**What it is:** A rewards site with surveys and offers and a points system.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Surveys, offers, tasks |
| How you're paid | PayPal, gift cards, some crypto |
| Kenya | Open to many countries; check Kenya support for your account |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | Survey-focused users |

**How to start:** Complete profile surveys and try a few offers to learn which pay reliably.
**Pros:** Long history. **Cons:** Low pay; country-dependent supply.
**Watch out:** Same rules: no VPN, one account.

## 6. JumpTask

**What it is:** A task and survey app that pays in its own crypto token.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Short tasks, surveys, app tests, social tasks |
| How you're paid | Crypto token, which you can convert on supported exchanges |
| Kenya | Open in many countries including in Africa |
| Realistic earnings | Low; value changes with the token price |
| Best for | People comfortable with crypto wallets |

**How to start:** Learn how to store and convert tokens safely before you start.
**Pros:** Mobile-friendly; many tasks. **Cons:** Token value can fall; converting has fees.
**Watch out:** Never share your wallet's secret phrase with anyone.

## 7. Paidwork

**What it is:** An app combining surveys, small tasks, games and short writing tasks.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Surveys, typing and simple writing, games, offers |
| How you're paid | PayPal and other options by country |
| Kenya | Open to many countries; check in-app |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | Phone-first users |

**How to start:** Install from the official app store; read task rules before starting.
**Pros:** One app for many task types. **Cons:** Low pay per task.
**Watch out:** Fake look-alike apps exist: check the developer name.

## 8. Gamehag

**What it is:** A gaming rewards site paying for game milestones and tasks.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Play games, complete quests, small tasks |
| How you're paid | Gift cards and game items, some cash options |
| Kenya | Open to many countries |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | Gamers |

**How to start:** Use it only for games you'd play anyway.
**Pros:** Fun if you game. **Cons:** Mostly gift cards and game items.
**Watch out:** Avoid spending real money to complete quests.

## 9. Cointiply

**What it is:** A crypto rewards site with offers, surveys, games and a small faucet.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Offers, surveys, PTC (paid-to-click) ads, faucet |
| How you're paid | Crypto (Bitcoin and others) |
| Kenya | Open to many countries |
| Realistic earnings | Very low |
| Best for | Crypto beginners curious about earning small amounts |

**How to start:** Treat it as learning; withdraw small amounts to a wallet you control.
**Pros:** Long-running. **Cons:** Very low rates; time-consuming.
**Watch out:** Never deposit money hoping to earn more interest.

## 10. InboxDollars

**What it is:** A cash rewards site for surveys, emails and shopping.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Surveys, reading emails, offers |
| How you're paid | Cash via PayPal or gift cards |
| Kenya | US only |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | Only people living in the US |

**How to start:** Not open to Kenya.
**Pros:** Simple. **Cons:** Country-limited.
**Watch out:** Anyone selling “US accounts” is breaking the rules: you'll lose the earnings.

## Summary

- Rewards sites are easy to join and pay little; Timebucks and ySense are open to Kenyans, while Swagbucks and InboxDollars mostly aren't.
- Prefer surveys and tasks over offers that need card details.
- Payouts are mostly PayPal, Payoneer, Skrill, crypto or gift cards: confirm your option before working.
