# Field data, transcription and passive apps: Premise, GoTranscript, TranscribeMe, Honeygain and more

Some apps pay you to collect information around your town; transcription platforms pay you to type audio; “passive” apps pay to share your unused internet. Each has its own trade-offs.

:::warning Check before you rely on it
Countries, payment options and minimum payouts change often. Confirm on each platform's official website, and never pay anyone to join.
:::

## 48. Premise

**What it is:** A data collection app that pays for tasks such as photographing prices or answering surveys about your area.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Photos of shops and prices, location checks, surveys |
| How you're paid | Payment options offered in the app by country |
| Kenya | Active in Kenya and many other countries |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | People who move around town anyway |

**How to start:** Follow task instructions exactly; respect private property and people's privacy.
**Pros:** Uses your phone and daily routine. **Cons:** Tasks depend on location.
**Watch out:** Never photograph people without consent or enter restricted areas.

## 49. Streetbees

**What it is:** An app that pays for photo and video stories about your daily life and purchases.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Short surveys with photos or videos |
| How you're paid | PayPal |
| Kenya | Available in many countries; check Kenya in-app |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | Phone users |

**How to start:** Answer with clear photos and honest stories.
**Pros:** Simple tasks. **Cons:** Irregular tasks.
**Watch out:** Don't share personal documents in photos.

## 50. GoTranscript

**What it is:** A transcription company that accepts transcribers from around the world.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Transcribing short audio files, captions, translations |
| How you're paid | PayPal or Payoneer, weekly |
| Kenya | Open to Kenyan transcribers |
| Realistic earnings | Low at first; rises with quality and speed |
| Best for | Fast, accurate typists with good English |

**How to start:** Pass the entry test; start with short files; build your quality score.
**Pros:** Steady supply; accepts Kenyans. **Cons:** Low starting rates; strict style guide.
**Watch out:** Use headphones and follow the style guide strictly.

## 51. TranscribeMe

**What it is:** A transcription platform that splits audio into short chunks.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Short transcription tasks, some specialist teams (medical, translation) |
| How you're paid | PayPal |
| Kenya | Open to many countries |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | Typists who want short tasks |

**How to start:** Pass the exam; work up to better-paid teams.
**Pros:** Short, flexible tasks. **Cons:** Low base pay.
**Watch out:** Respect confidentiality of audio.

## 52. Scribie

**What it is:** A transcription platform paying per audio minute, with review levels.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Short audio files, reviewing |
| How you're paid | PayPal |
| Kenya | Open to many countries |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Best for | Detail-oriented typists |

**How to start:** Do the test and learn their guidelines.
**Pros:** Clear progression. **Cons:** Low pay at first.
**Watch out:** Accuracy matters more than speed.

## 53. Rev

**What it is:** A well-known transcription and captioning company.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Transcription, captions, foreign subtitles |
| How you're paid | PayPal |
| Kenya | Accepted countries vary; check whether Kenya is open for new freelancers |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Best for | Fast, accurate typists |

**How to start:** Apply only if your country is accepted; pass the tests.
**Pros:** Big client base. **Cons:** Country limits; competition.
**Watch out:** Never buy someone else's account.

## 54. Honeygain

**What it is:** An app that pays you for sharing unused internet bandwidth.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Passive: runs in the background |
| How you're paid | PayPal or crypto after a minimum |
| Kenya | Open to many countries |
| Realistic earnings | Very low; uses your data |
| Best for | People with unlimited Wi-Fi only |

**How to start:** Only use on unlimited home Wi-Fi, never mobile data bundles.
**Pros:** Passive. **Cons:** Very low pay; your connection is used by others.
**Watch out:** Understand that others' traffic goes through your connection: read the terms and privacy policy.

## 55. Pawns.app

**What it is:** Another bandwidth-sharing app with small surveys.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Passive bandwidth sharing, surveys |
| How you're paid | PayPal or crypto |
| Kenya | Open to many countries |
| Realistic earnings | Very low |
| Best for | People with unlimited Wi-Fi |

**How to start:** Same rules as Honeygain.
**Pros:** Passive. **Cons:** Very low pay.
**Watch out:** Same privacy caution.

:::warning Captcha-solving sites
Some sites pay to type captchas. Pay is extremely low, and solving captchas often helps bots get past security systems meant to stop spam and fraud. We don't recommend them.
:::

## Summary

- Premise and Streetbees pay for local data; GoTranscript, TranscribeMe, Scribie and Rev pay for transcription.
- GoTranscript accepts Kenyan transcribers; quality and speed raise your earnings.
- Bandwidth-sharing apps (Honeygain, Pawns) pay very little: use only on unlimited Wi-Fi and read the privacy terms.
