# More event services

Adding to event planning, sound and tents (methods 313–330), these services complete the event package.

## What these methods share

- **Bookings:** take deposits and confirm in writing.
- **Reliability:** events can't be repeated, so turn up on time.
- **Safety:** electrical and structural safety at venues.

## 960. Stage and lighting hire

**What it is:** Supplying stages, trusses and lighting for concerts, weddings and corporate events.
**How it works:** Book → set up → operate → dismantle.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Lighting design, rigging safety |
| Cost & equipment | Lights, trusses, stage platforms, transport |
| Where to find work | Events, churches, concerts, corporate |
| Payment | Per event |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Partner with sound companies; start with basic lighting packages.
**Pros:** Pairs with sound hire. **Cons & risks:** Equipment costs.
**Scam warning:** Rig safely: falling equipment injures people.
**Kenya notes:** Lighting is increasingly expected at weddings and concerts.

## 961. Event ushering and hostess agency

**What it is:** Providing trained ushers, hostesses and brand ambassadors for events.
**How it works:** Recruit and train staff → supply for events → manage on the day.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Recruitment, coordination |
| Cost & equipment | Low: uniforms, training |
| Where to find work | Corporate events, conferences, weddings, product launches |
| Payment | Per staff per day (margin) |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Build a reliable team; partner with event planners.
**Pros:** Low start-up. **Cons & risks:** Staff reliability.
**Scam warning:** Pay staff fairly and on time.
**Kenya notes:** Corporate launches often hire ushers.

## 962. Bouncy castles and kids' rides hire

**What it is:** Renting bouncy castles, trampolines and play equipment for parties and fun days.
**How it works:** Deliver → set up safely → supervise → collect.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Safety supervision |
| Cost & equipment | Equipment, blower, transport |
| Where to find work | Kids' parties, schools, churches, malls |
| Payment | Per event |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Start with one bouncy castle; always provide a supervisor.
**Pros:** Popular at events. **Cons & risks:** Weather; wear and tear.
**Scam warning:** Anchor equipment properly and supervise children.
**Kenya notes:** School fun days and kids' parties create demand.

## 964. Wedding car hire and decoration

**What it is:** Renting decorated cars for weddings, with drivers.
**How it works:** Book → decorate → drive the couple → return.

| | |
|---|---|

**How to start:** Partner with owners of clean, smart vehicles.

## Summary

- More event services include stage lighting, ushering agencies, kids' rides, LED screens and wedding cars.
- Take deposits, confirm in writing and be reliable.
- Safety for rigging and children's equipment matters.
