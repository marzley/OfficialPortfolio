# Teaching, tutoring and training

If you know something well, someone wants to learn it: a Form 3 student struggling with chemistry, a parent wanting their child to code, a business owner learning Excel, a foreigner learning Kiswahili. Teaching can be done face to face, online, or through courses you record once and sell many times.

## What these methods share

- **Subject knowledge plus teaching skill:** explaining clearly, patience, checking understanding.
- **Safeguarding:** when teaching children, follow child-protection good practice (parents present or informed, no private messaging without parents' knowledge, appropriate conduct online).
- **Honesty about outcomes:** never promise grades, admission or jobs.
- **Licensing:** running a formal school or college requires registration with the relevant authorities; private tutoring and short courses are simpler but still need professionalism.
- See [teaching and expertise earning](./?track=earn-online&lesson=teaching-expertise-earning) and [online teaching platforms](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=online-teaching-platforms).

## 204. Home tutoring (primary and secondary)

**What it is:** One-on-one or small-group tutoring at students' homes or a shared venue.
**How it works:** Assess the learner → plan sessions → regular lessons → progress updates to parents.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Strong subject knowledge, patience, curriculum familiarity (CBC, 8-4-4 or international) |
| Cost & equipment | Books, transport |
| Where to find work | Parents in your area, schools' referrals, WhatsApp groups, churches |
| Payment | Per hour or per month, M-Pesa |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium (more students, holiday tuition) |
| Time to first income | Days to weeks |

**How to start:** Start with one subject you're excellent at; ask satisfied parents for referrals.
**Pros:** Strong local demand. **Cons & risks:** Travel time; holiday peaks and quiet terms.
**Scam warning:** Agencies charging tutors "registration fees" without delivering clients.
**Kenya notes:** Follow current Ministry of Education guidance on holiday tuition; keep sessions safe and parent-approved.

## 205. Online tutoring (local and international)

**How it works:** Use Zoom/Google Meet with a digital whiteboard; schedule regular sessions.

| | |
|---|---|

**Scam warning:** "Tutoring jobs" asking for upfront payment for "verification".
**Kenya notes:** Diaspora families want tutors who know Kenyan curricula and Kiswahili.
