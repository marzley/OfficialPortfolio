# Events, hospitality, health and wellness services

Weddings, birthdays, conferences, fitness and wellbeing are all growing markets in Kenya, and social media is the main way these services find customers. These methods mix skills, people and online marketing.

## What these methods share

- **Reputation and reviews** decide bookings.
- **Contracts and deposits** protect you from cancellations.
- **Health services** must stay within your qualifications and licences.

## 313. Event planning

**What it is:** Planning weddings, parties, corporate events and conferences.
**How it works:** Consultation → budget → vendors → coordination on the day.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Organisation, negotiation, calm under pressure |
| Cost & equipment | Phone, laptop, vendor network |
| Where to find work | Couples, families, companies, social media |
| Payment | Planning fee or percentage |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium–high |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Assist established planners; plan events for friends.
**Pros:** Creative, rewarding. **Cons & risks:** Stress; vendor failures.
**Scam warning:** Vendors who take deposits and disappear; vet them.
**Kenya notes:** Weddings, ruracio, graduations and corporate events drive demand.
