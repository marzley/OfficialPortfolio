# More farm and livestock services

Farmers need services as much as inputs. These businesses support livestock and crop farmers, adding to methods 281–295 and 592–595.

## What these methods share

- **Animal welfare:** treat animals humanely and follow veterinary rules.
- **Licences:** county and veterinary permits apply to livestock movement and slaughter.
- **Trust:** honest weights and prices keep farmers coming back.

## 903. Livestock trading

**What it is:** Buying cattle, goats and sheep from farmers and selling at markets or to butcheries.
**How it works:** Buy at fair prices → move with permits → sell.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Animal assessment, negotiation |
| Cost & equipment | Capital, movement permits |
| Where to find work | Livestock markets, butcheries, abattoirs |
| Payment | Margin per animal |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Learn from experienced traders; build buyer relationships.
**Pros:** Fast turnover. **Cons & risks:** Price risk; disease.
**Scam warning:** Get movement permits; stolen livestock is a serious crime.
**Kenya notes:** Livestock markets in pastoral counties supply towns.

## 904. Milk collection and cooling centre

**What it is:** Collecting milk from smallholders, cooling it and selling to processors.
**How it works:** Collect → test → cool → sell in bulk.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Milk testing, management |
| Cost & equipment | Cooler, testing kit, Kenya Dairy Board licence |
| Where to find work | Dairy farmers, processors |
| Payment | Margin per litre |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Often run by cooperatives; partner with farmer groups.
**Pros:** Steady daily volumes. **Cons & risks:** Equipment and power costs.
**Scam warning:** Test for adulteration and pay honestly.
**Kenya notes:** Dairy cooperatives play a big role in Kenya's milk supply.

## 905. Farm labour contractor

**What it is:** Organising groups of workers for planting, weeding, harvesting and packing.
**How it works:** Farms request → you supply teams → manage and pay workers.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Organisation, fairness |
| Cost & equipment | Low: phone, transport |
| Where to find work | Large farms, flower farms, greenhouses |
| Payment | Per worker per day (margin) |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Build a reliable team; agree clear terms with farms.
**Pros:** Steady seasonal demand. **Cons & risks:** Disputes over pay.
**Scam warning:** Pay workers fully and on time; follow labour laws and minimum wages.
**Kenya notes:** Harvest seasons create big demand for labour teams.

## 906. Chick hatchery

**What it is:** Hatching eggs in incubators and selling day-old chicks.
**How it works:** Source fertile eggs → incubate → sell chicks.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Incubation, biosecurity |
| Cost & equipment | Incubators, power backup, egg supply |
| Where to find work | Poultry farmers |
| Payment | Per chick |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Start with a small incubator and reliable power backup.
**Pros:** Constant demand. **Cons & risks:** Power outages ruin batches.
**Scam warning:** Sell vaccinated, healthy chicks; follow veterinary guidance.
**Kenya notes:** Improved kienyeji chicks are especially popular.

## 907. Bee pollination services

**What it is:** Renting beehives to farmers so bees pollinate their crops.
**How it works:** Place hives during flowering → collect after.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Beekeeping, logistics |
| Cost & equipment | Hives, transport, protective gear |
| Where to find work | Avocado, macadamia, horticulture farms |
| Payment | Per hive per season |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Partner with farms growing pollination-dependent crops.
**Pros:** Extra income from hives. **Cons & risks:** Pesticide spraying can kill bees.
**Scam warning:** Agree no-spray periods in writing.
**Kenya notes:** Avocado and macadamia farmers benefit from pollination.

## 908. Livestock transport

**What it is:** Moving animals between farms, markets and abattoirs.
**How it works:** Book → load humanely → deliver with permits.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Driving, animal handling |
| Cost & equipment | Suitable truck, permits |
| Where to find work | Traders, farmers, abattoirs |
| Payment | Per trip |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Fit the truck properly for animal welfare.
**Pros:** Regular demand. **Cons & risks:** Permits and welfare rules.
**Scam warning:** Carry valid movement permits.
**Kenya notes:** Livestock moves from pastoral counties to town markets.

## 909. Poultry slaughter and dressing unit

**What it is:** Slaughtering and cleaning chickens hygienically for farmers, hotels and shops.
**How it works:** Receive birds → slaughter and dress → package.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Hygiene, processing |
| Cost & equipment | Premises, equipment, public health and veterinary licences |
| Where to find work | Poultry farmers, hotels, supermarkets |
| Payment | Per bird |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Get public health and veterinary approvals; start serving local farmers.
**Pros:** Adds value for farmers. **Cons & risks:** Strict hygiene rules.
**Scam warning:** Never sell meat from sick birds.
**Kenya notes:** Hotels and supermarkets prefer inspected, dressed chicken.

## Summary

- Farm services include livestock trading, milk cooling, labour teams, hatcheries, pollination, livestock transport and poultry processing.
- Follow permits, veterinary rules and labour laws.
- Animal welfare and honest dealing build trust.
