# Urban transport niches

Beyond matatus, boda bodas and ride-hailing (methods 297–298 and 312), towns need many other ways to move people, water and goods.

## What these methods share

- **Licences:** passenger transport needs NTSA and county licensing.
- **Insurance:** protect vehicles, riders and cargo.
- **Maintenance:** downtime is lost income.

## 861. Tuk-tuk (three-wheeler) passenger business

**What it is:** Running tuk-tuks for short trips in towns.
**How it works:** Own or lease tuk-tuks → hire drivers or drive → daily earnings.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Driving, fleet management |
| Cost & equipment | Tuk-tuk, insurance, NTSA and county licences |
| Where to find work | Towns, coast, estates |
| Payment | Per trip or daily driver remittance |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Start with one vehicle; agree clear terms with drivers.
**Pros:** Cheaper than cars. **Cons & risks:** Licensing and route rules.
**Scam warning:** Use written agreements for hired drivers.
**Kenya notes:** Tuk-tuks are common in Mombasa, Malindi and many towns.

## 862. Water bowser delivery

**What it is:** Delivering water by truck to homes, construction sites and estates.
**How it works:** Source from licensed points → deliver → charge per litre or trip.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Driving, logistics |
| Cost & equipment | Water bowser, licences, source agreements |
| Where to find work | Estates, construction sites, hotels |
| Payment | Per trip or volume |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Use licensed water sources and keep tanks clean.
**Pros:** High demand during shortages. **Cons & risks:** Fuel costs; water quality responsibility.
**Scam warning:** Never sell untreated water as drinking water.
**Kenya notes:** Water shortages in Nairobi and other towns keep bowsers busy.

## 863. Handcart (mkokoteni) goods transport

**What it is:** Moving goods by handcart in markets and town centres.
**How it works:** Hire or own a cart → move goods per trip.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Strength, reliability |
| Cost & equipment | Handcart, county permit where required |
| Where to find work | Markets, wholesalers, shops |
| Payment | Per trip |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Time to first income | Days |

**How to start:** Get a strong cart and build relationships with traders.
**Pros:** Very low start-up. **Cons & risks:** Physically demanding.
**Scam warning:** Agree prices before loading.
**Kenya notes:** Busy markets like Gikomba and Marikiti rely on handcarts.

## 864. Motorbike work-to-own leasing

**What it is:** Buying motorbikes and leasing them to riders who pay daily until they own the bike.
**How it works:** Buy bikes → sign lease agreements → collect daily payments → transfer ownership.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Finance management, contracts |
| Cost & equipment | Capital for bikes, tracking devices, insurance |
| Where to find work | Boda boda riders |
| Payment | Daily repayments |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Use clear written contracts, trackers and insurance; start with a few trusted riders.
**Pros:** Helps riders own assets. **Cons & risks:** Default and theft risks.
**Scam warning:** Be fair: hidden charges ruin trust and may break consumer laws.
**Kenya notes:** Many Kenyan boda riders work towards owning their bikes.

## 865. Bicycle delivery service

**What it is:** Delivering parcels, food and documents by bicycle in busy towns.
**How it works:** Take orders from shops and apps → deliver by bike.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Fitness, navigation |
| Cost & equipment | Bicycle, phone, delivery bag |
| Where to find work | Restaurants, shops, offices |
| Payment | Per delivery |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Time to first income | Days |

**How to start:** Partner with nearby restaurants and pharmacies.
**Pros:** Cheap to run; beats traffic. **Cons & risks:** Weather and safety.
**Scam warning:** Wear a helmet and reflective gear.
**Kenya notes:** Short-distance deliveries in town centres suit bicycles.

## Summary

- Urban transport niches include tuk-tuks, water bowsers, handcarts, motorbike leasing and bicycle delivery.
- Get NTSA and county licences, insurance and written agreements.
- Treat riders and drivers fairly.
