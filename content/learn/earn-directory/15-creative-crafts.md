# Creative products, crafts and making

Making things with your hands, then using the internet to find buyers, is one of the oldest ways to earn, now with a much bigger market. These methods combine a craft skill with online marketing.

## What these methods share

- **Quality and consistency** build repeat customers.
- **Good photos and stories** sell handmade products online.
- **Pricing** must cover materials, time, packaging and delivery ([pricing](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=pricing)).
- **Safety and regulation** for products like food, cosmetics and children's items.

## 265. Beadwork and jewellery making

**What it is:** Making beaded jewellery, accessories and decor.
**How it works:** Design → make → photograph → sell locally, online and to tourists.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Craft skill, design, colour sense |
| Cost & equipment | Beads, wire, tools |
| Where to find work | Markets, social media, tourist shops, international buyers |
| Payment | M-Pesa, cash, platform payouts |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Develop a signature style; sell to friends, then markets and online.
**Pros:** Low start-up cost. **Cons & risks:** Competition; tourist seasonality.
**Scam warning:** Bulk "export orders" that require you to pay fees first.
**Kenya notes:** Respect cultural designs and work fairly with community artisans.
