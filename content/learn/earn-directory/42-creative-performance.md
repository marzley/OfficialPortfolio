# Creative and performance niches

These creative methods aren't covered in the earlier design, crafts and entertainment categories. They range from calligraphy and props making to choir direction and photo booths, and many find customers through social media and events.

## What these methods share

- **Portfolio content** on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.
- **Event-driven demand:** weddings, parties, corporate functions, churches and schools.
- **Contracts and deposits** for bookings.

**How it works:** Commissions and workshops.
