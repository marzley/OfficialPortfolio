# Virtual assistance, admin and remote support

Busy entrepreneurs, professionals and small companies everywhere need help with emails, calendars, customers, bookkeeping and admin. Remote support work suits organised, reliable people with good communication skills. Many of these methods can grow into steady part-time or full-time remote roles.

## What these methods share

- **Reliability and communication** are the main skills; clients trust you with their inbox, customers or money.
- **Tools:** Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, project tools (Trello, Asana, Notion), communication apps, password managers ([tools and workday](./?track=freelancing&lesson=freelance-tools-workday)).
- **Confidentiality:** sign NDAs when asked; never share client data.
- See [virtual assistant work](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=virtual-assistant-work) and [customer support jobs](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=customer-support-remote-jobs).

## 184. General virtual assistant

**What it is:** Handling a mix of admin tasks remotely: email, scheduling, documents, research, bookings.
**How it works:** Hourly or monthly retainer; tasks via a shared board; regular check-ins.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Organisation, written communication, Google Workspace/Microsoft 365 |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop, reliable internet, headset |
| Where to find work | Freelance platforms, VA agencies, LinkedIn, local professionals |
| Payment | Hourly or monthly; PayPal, Payoneer, bank |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium (long-term clients) |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Create a services list and sample work (inbox system, travel plan, SOP); apply to VA roles.
**Pros:** Steady long-term clients. **Cons & risks:** Time zone demands; scope creep.
**Scam warning:** "VA jobs" requiring you to buy equipment, receive packages or forward money.
**Kenya notes:** East Africa Time overlaps with European business hours.

## Summary

- Admin and support methods include general and executive VA work, inbox and calendar management, customer support, call centres, appointment setting, bookkeeping, payroll, KRA and eCitizen help, typing, research, project coordination, e-commerce, real estate and clinic support, scheduling, minutes and chama administration.
- Reliability, communication and confidentiality are the core skills.
- Never forward money, receive packages or pay fees for "VA jobs"; never falsify records or promise "fast approvals".
