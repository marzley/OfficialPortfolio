# Health support and safety services

Non-clinical services that support hospitals, clinics and safety, adding to health businesses (methods 544–556).

## What these methods share

- **Regulation:** medical waste and first aid training are regulated.
- **Hygiene:** infection control is essential.
- **Reliability:** health facilities depend on you.

## 978. Medical courier

**What it is:** Transporting lab samples, medicines and documents between clinics, labs and pharmacies.
**How it works:** Scheduled pickups → safe transport → delivery.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Reliability, handling procedures |
| Cost & equipment | Motorbike or car, cool boxes |
| Where to find work | Clinics, labs, pharmacies |
| Payment | Per trip or contract |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Learn sample-handling rules; partner with labs.
**Pros:** Steady contracts. **Cons & risks:** Strict handling rules.
**Scam warning:** Keep patient information confidential.
**Kenya notes:** Labs collect samples from many clinics daily.

## 979. Hospital laundry services

**What it is:** Washing, disinfecting and pressing hospital linen.
**How it works:** Collect → wash with infection control → return.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Laundry, infection control |
| Cost & equipment | Industrial machines, transport |
| Where to find work | Hospitals, clinics, nursing homes, hotels |
| Payment | Per kilo or contract |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Meet infection control standards; start with clinics.
**Pros:** Contracts are steady. **Cons & risks:** High equipment costs.
**Scam warning:** Separate infectious linen properly.
**Kenya notes:** Smaller hospitals often outsource laundry.

## 980. Medical waste management (licensed)

**What it is:** Collecting and safely disposing of medical waste.
**How it works:** Licensed collection → transport → treatment or incineration.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Waste management, safety |
| Cost & equipment | NEMA licence, vehicles, treatment partner |
| Where to find work | Hospitals, clinics, labs, pharmacies |
| Payment | Per kilo or contract |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Get NEMA licences and a treatment partner.
**Pros:** Essential service. **Cons & risks:** Heavy regulation.
**Scam warning:** Never dump medical waste.
**Kenya notes:** Medical waste needs licensed handlers in Kenya.

## 981. First aid trainer

**What it is:** Training workplaces, schools and drivers in first aid and CPR.
**How it works:** Workshops → practical training → certificates.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | First aid qualifications, teaching |
| Cost & equipment | Training manikins, kits, approval |
| Where to find work | Workplaces, schools, matatu saccos, churches |
| Payment | Per participant |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Get first aid instructor certification and approval to train.
**Pros:** Required for many workplaces. **Cons & risks:** Must keep certification current.
**Scam warning:** Only issue certificates you're approved to issue.
**Kenya notes:** Workplace safety law requires trained first aiders.

## 982. Lifeguard

**What it is:** Supervising swimmers at pools, hotels and beaches.
**How it works:** Shifts → supervise → rescue and first aid when needed.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Strong swimming, rescue, first aid |
| Cost & equipment | Recognised lifeguard certification |
| Where to find work | Hotels, clubs, schools, public pools |
| Payment | Monthly or per shift |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Get certified in lifeguarding and first aid.
**Pros:** Clear job path. **Cons & risks:** Responsibility for lives.
**Scam warning:** Stay alert: never leave the pool unattended.
**Kenya notes:** Hotels and clubs at the coast and in towns employ lifeguards.

## Summary

- Health support services include medical couriers, hospital laundry, licensed medical waste, first aid training and lifeguarding.
- Regulation and infection control apply.
- Confidentiality and reliability are essential.
