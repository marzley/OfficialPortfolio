# Logistics, import, export and trade

Moving goods between farms, factories, towns and countries creates many earning opportunities. Kenya is a regional trade hub (Mombasa port, cross-border trade with Uganda, Tanzania and beyond). Digital tools (WhatsApp, tracking, online marketplaces) make these businesses easier to start and manage.

## What these methods share

- **Licences and compliance:** customs, KRA, standards (KEBS), and sector-specific permits.
- **Trust and verification:** trade fraud is common; verify suppliers and buyers.
- **Cash flow:** goods tie up money; plan carefully.

## 510. Clearing and forwarding agent (licensed)

**What it is:** Handling customs clearance and shipping logistics for importers and exporters.
**How it works:** Licensed agents process documents and coordinate shipments.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Customs procedures, documentation |
| Cost & equipment | Licences, office |
| Where to find work | Importers, exporters |
| Payment | Fees |
| Realistic earnings | Medium to high |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Train in customs procedures; work in a licensed firm first.
**Pros:** Essential service. **Cons & risks:** Regulatory responsibility.
**Scam warning:** Fake agents who disappear with duty payments.
**Kenya notes:** KRA licenses customs agents.

## Summary

- Logistics and trade methods include clearing agents, importing, exporting, cross-border trade, wholesale, warehousing, delivery fleets, trucking, cold chains, procurement agents, courier outlets, packaging supply, logistics software, trade facilitation, commodity trading and consulting.
