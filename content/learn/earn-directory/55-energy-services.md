# Energy services beyond solar panels

Power cuts, high bills and off-grid homes create business for energy services. These build on solar sales and installation (methods 381–383) and energy audits (392).

## What these methods share

- **Licences:** electrical work needs EPRA licensing; mini-grids need EPRA permits.
- **Safety:** batteries, gas and generators need careful handling.
- **After-sales:** service and spare parts keep customers loyal.

## 853. Mini-grid operator

**What it is:** Running a small solar or hybrid power network supplying a village, market or island.
**How it works:** Build or partner on a mini-grid → connect customers → collect payments (often prepaid via M-Pesa).

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Electrical engineering, business management |
| Cost & equipment | Very high (often with investors or donors); EPRA permit |
| Where to find work | Off-grid villages, islands, trading centres |
| Payment | Monthly or prepaid power sales |
| Realistic earnings | Medium to high |
| Time to first income | Years |

**How to start:** Work for a mini-grid company first; partner with financiers and county governments.
**Pros:** Long-term revenue. **Cons & risks:** High capital and regulation.
**Scam warning:** Be transparent about tariffs with customers.
**Kenya notes:** Kenya has many mini-grids in off-grid counties and lake islands.

## 854. E-cooking appliance sales and training

**What it is:** Selling electric pressure cookers and induction cookers and training households to cook efficiently.
**How it works:** Demonstrate → sell (cash or instalments) → follow-up support.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Sales, cooking demonstrations |
| Cost & equipment | Appliance stock, demo kit |
| Where to find work | Households, estates, chamas, offices |
| Payment | Per sale or commission |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Run cooking demos at chamas and churches; offer instalments through partners.
**Pros:** Saves cooking costs. **Cons & risks:** Depends on reliable electricity.
**Scam warning:** Sell only certified appliances.
**Kenya notes:** Electric pressure cookers are promoted in Kenya as cheaper than charcoal for many dishes.

## 855. Phone charging kiosks and power-bank rental

**What it is:** Charging phones or renting power banks in places without reliable power.
**How it works:** Solar or mains charging station → charge per phone or rent power banks.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Basic electrical skills, customer service |
| Cost & equipment | Charging station, power banks, solar panel |
| Where to find work | Markets, rural centres, events, bus stages |
| Payment | Per charge or rental |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Time to first income | Days |

**How to start:** Set up at a busy market or stage; use deposits for power-bank rentals.
**Pros:** Simple and quick to start. **Cons & risks:** Low price per charge.
**Scam warning:** Use quality chargers to avoid damaging phones.
**Kenya notes:** Rural markets without power still need charging.

## 856. Generator hire

**What it is:** Renting generators to events, construction sites and businesses during outages.
**How it works:** Book → deliver → fuel arrangement → collect.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Generator operation and maintenance |
| Cost & equipment | Generators, transport |
| Where to find work | Events, construction, shops, funerals |
| Payment | Per day |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Start with one or two well-maintained generators; partner with event planners.
**Pros:** Steady demand. **Cons & risks:** Maintenance and theft risks.
**Scam warning:** Get deposits and written agreements.
**Kenya notes:** Pairs with generator repair (method 530).

## 857. LED lighting retrofits

**What it is:** Replacing old lighting in offices, factories and schools with efficient LEDs to cut bills.
**How it works:** Audit → quote savings → install → measure.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Electrical installation, energy calculations |
| Cost & equipment | Tools, licensed electrician status |
| Where to find work | Factories, hotels, schools, malls |
| Payment | Per project |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Partner with energy auditors; show clients the payback period.
**Pros:** Clear savings to sell. **Cons & risks:** Upfront costs for clients.
**Scam warning:** Use certified LED products; fakes fail fast.
**Kenya notes:** High electricity tariffs make savings attractive.

## 858. Biogas digester installation

**What it is:** Building digesters that turn animal manure into cooking gas and fertiliser.
**How it works:** Assess animals and site → build digester → connect stove → train.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Construction, biogas technology |
| Cost & equipment | Materials, tools, training |
| Where to find work | Dairy farmers, schools, institutions |
| Payment | Per installation |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Train with a biogas programme; target farmers with zero-grazing cows.
**Pros:** Free cooking gas from waste. **Cons & risks:** Needs enough manure and maintenance.
**Scam warning:** Follow safety design to prevent leaks.
**Kenya notes:** Dairy regions like Central Kenya and the Rift Valley suit biogas.

## 859. Solar cold rooms for farmers

**What it is:** Offering cooling storage at markets so farmers and traders keep produce fresh longer.
**How it works:** Install a solar cold room → charge per crate per day.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Business management, refrigeration basics |
| Cost & equipment | Cold room unit (often leased), site |
| Where to find work | Fresh produce markets, fishing beaches |
| Payment | Per crate per day |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Partner with cold room providers and market committees.
**Pros:** Reduces post-harvest losses. **Cons & risks:** High equipment cost.
**Scam warning:** Agree storage terms and liability in writing.
**Kenya notes:** Fresh produce losses are high in Kenyan markets without cooling.

## 860. Electric motorbike battery swap station

**What it is:** Running a station where electric boda riders swap flat batteries for charged ones.
**How it works:** Partner with an e-mobility company → host batteries → earn per swap.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Customer service, basic electrical safety |
| Cost & equipment | Site, power connection, partner equipment |
| Where to find work | Boda boda riders, delivery riders |
| Payment | Commission per swap |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Partner with an electric motorbike company expanding in your area.
**Pros:** Growing sector. **Cons & risks:** Depends on partner's growth.
**Scam warning:** Only partner with established companies.
**Kenya notes:** Electric motorbikes are growing fast in Nairobi and other towns.

## Summary

- Energy services include mini-grids, e-cooking, charging kiosks, generator hire, LED retrofits, biogas, solar cold rooms and battery swap stations.
- EPRA licences apply to electrical work and mini-grids.
- Safety and after-sales service matter.
