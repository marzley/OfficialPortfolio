# Specialist online work

These are more specialised online roles that aren't covered in earlier categories: niche skills that international and local clients pay for remotely. Most build on general digital, writing, design or tech skills.

## What these methods share

- **Specialisation pays more** than generic tasks.
- **Portfolio and proof:** samples, certifications and case studies.
- **Platforms and direct clients:** freelance marketplaces, LinkedIn and referrals ([freelancing subject](./?track=freelancing&lesson=freelancing-roadmap)).

## 664. UGC (user-generated content) creator

**What it is:** Creating authentic-style videos and photos for brands to use in ads and social media (you don't need a big following).
**How it works:** Brand brief → film product demos, testimonials, unboxings → deliver files.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | On-camera presence, filming and editing on a phone |
| Cost & equipment | Smartphone, lighting, mic |
| Where to find work | Brands, UGC platforms, agencies, direct outreach |
| Payment | Per video or package |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Create a UGC portfolio with products you own.
**Pros:** No large following needed. **Cons & risks:** Must disclose if content is presented as reviews.
**Scam warning:** Fake brands asking you to pay for "product samples".
**Kenya notes:** Kenyan and African brands want relatable local creators.
