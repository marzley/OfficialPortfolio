# Health and medical businesses (licensed professionals)

These methods are for **qualified, licensed health professionals** and investors working with them. Health businesses meet essential needs, but they're strictly regulated to protect patients. If you aren't qualified, the roles for you are in administration, marketing, technology and supply (see methods 404–405 and others).

## What these methods share

- **Professional licensing:** clinicians are registered with their councils/boards (e.g. KMPDC, Nursing Council, Clinical Officers Council, Pharmacy and Poisons Board, Physiotherapy Council); facilities are licensed and inspected.
- **Patient safety and privacy:** health data is sensitive personal data under the Data Protection Act.
- **Insurance integration:** SHA and private insurers affect cash flow.
- **Ethics:** no misleading advertising or miracle claims.

## 544. Private clinic or medical centre

**What it is:** A licensed outpatient facility run by qualified clinicians.
**How it works:** Registration → premises → staff → services → insurance empanelment.

**How to start:** Meet KMPDC and county facility licensing requirements.
**Pros:** Essential service. **Cons & risks:** Regulation, insurance payment delays.

## Summary

- Health businesses include clinics, labs, physiotherapy, dental, opticians, pharmacies, home nursing, medical equipment, ambulances, corporate screening, mental health practice, health software and medical tourism facilitation.
- They require qualified, licensed professionals and strict patient safety and privacy practices.
