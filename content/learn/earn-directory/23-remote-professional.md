# Remote professional jobs and specialist freelancing

Professionals with qualifications or experience (HR, law, accounting, project management, research, health admin) can increasingly work remotely for local and international employers or as freelance consultants. These roles usually pay more than entry-level online work, but require real expertise.

## What these methods share

- **Credentials matter:** degrees, professional certifications and experience.
- **Professional rules:** lawyers, accountants and health professionals must follow their regulatory bodies' rules, even when freelancing.
- **Remote job safety:** verify employers; legitimate employers don't charge application fees ([remote jobs](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=remote-jobs)).

## 395. Remote HR support and recruitment

**What it is:** Supporting hiring, onboarding, HR policies and employee records remotely.
**How it works:** Freelance or remote roles with SMEs and agencies.

|---|---|
