# More food and hospitality businesses

Adding to the food businesses in methods 446–462, these serve events, offices and other food businesses.

## What these methods share

- **Food safety:** food handler medical certificates and county public health licences.
- **Consistency:** the same quality every time builds loyalty.
- **Costing:** price for ingredients, labour, transport and waste.

## 917. Mobile bar service

**What it is:** Providing bartenders, drinks setup and cocktails at weddings, parties and corporate events.
**How it works:** Book → set up bar → serve → clear.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Bartending, customer service |
| Cost & equipment | Bar equipment, licences where needed |
| Where to find work | Events, weddings, corporate parties |
| Payment | Per event |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Train in bartending; partner with event planners.
**Pros:** Good event margins. **Cons & risks:** Alcohol licensing rules.
**Scam warning:** Never serve minors; follow licensing for selling alcohol.
**Kenya notes:** Weddings and corporate events often hire mobile bars.

## 918. Popcorn and candy floss vending

**What it is:** Selling popcorn and candy floss at events, schools and malls.
**How it works:** Machine → set up at events → sell per pack.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Food hygiene, selling |
| Cost & equipment | Machines, ingredients, packaging |
| Where to find work | Events, schools, malls, parks |
| Payment | Per pack |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Time to first income | Days |

**How to start:** Start with one machine at kids' parties and events.
**Pros:** Cheap to start. **Cons & risks:** Seasonal and event-dependent.
**Scam warning:** Keep machines clean.
**Kenya notes:** Kids' parties and fun days create demand.

## 919. Vending machines

**What it is:** Placing snack and drink vending machines in offices, campuses and hospitals.
**How it works:** Buy machines → place with host → restock → collect cash or M-Pesa.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Logistics, maintenance |
| Cost & equipment | Machines, stock |
| Where to find work | Offices, hospitals, campuses, gyms |
| Payment | Per sale |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Negotiate placement deals; use cashless payments.
**Pros:** Passive-ish income. **Cons & risks:** Machine costs; vandalism.
**Scam warning:** Buy from suppliers who provide maintenance.
**Kenya notes:** Cashless M-Pesa payments make vending practical.

## 920. Staff canteen operator

**What it is:** Running canteens for factories, offices and schools.
**How it works:** Win a contract → cook and serve daily meals.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Catering, management |
| Cost & equipment | Kitchen equipment (often provided), staff |
| Where to find work | Factories, offices, colleges, hospitals |
| Payment | Per meal or contract |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Start with a small office canteen; tender for bigger contracts.
**Pros:** Steady daily volumes. **Cons & risks:** Thin margins; strict hygiene.
**Scam warning:** Get contracts in writing with payment terms.
**Kenya notes:** Many factories and offices outsource canteens.

## 921. Food safety and hygiene trainer

**What it is:** Training food handlers and businesses in hygiene, safe storage and food safety systems.
**How it works:** Workshops → audits → certificates of attendance.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Food science, training |
| Cost & equipment | Low: training materials |
| Where to find work | Restaurants, hotels, schools, food processors |
| Payment | Per session or audit |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Get recognised food safety qualifications; partner with public health officers.
**Pros:** Helps businesses pass inspections. **Cons & risks:** Must stay current with standards.
**Scam warning:** Don't issue government certificates you aren't authorised to.
**Kenya notes:** County inspections push businesses to train staff.

## 922. Shared commercial kitchen

**What it is:** Renting a licensed kitchen by the hour or month to small food businesses.
**How it works:** Set up a licensed kitchen → rent time slots → provide storage.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Management, hygiene |
| Cost & equipment | High: kitchen equipment, licences |
| Where to find work | Home bakers, caterers, meal prep businesses |
| Payment | Hourly or monthly rent |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Fit out a kitchen that meets public health standards; market to home-based food sellers.
**Pros:** Helps many small businesses. **Cons & risks:** High setup costs.
**Scam warning:** Clear rules on cleaning and storage.
**Kenya notes:** Differs from a cloud kitchen (method 446): you rent the kitchen to others.

## 923. Chapati and mandazi wholesale production

**What it is:** Making chapatis, mandazis and samosas in bulk for kiosks, schools and offices.
**How it works:** Early-morning production → deliver to buyers.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Cooking, hygiene, logistics |
| Cost & equipment | Kitchen, fryers, packaging |
| Where to find work | Kiosks, offices, schools, canteens |
| Payment | Per piece or dozen |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Days |

**How to start:** Start with a few kiosks; deliver on time every day.
**Pros:** Daily orders. **Cons & risks:** Early hours; cooking gas costs.
**Scam warning:** Use fresh oil and good hygiene.
**Kenya notes:** Mandazi and chapati are breakfast staples.

## Summary

- More food businesses include mobile bars, popcorn and candy floss, vending machines, staff canteens, food safety training, shared kitchens and wholesale chapati and mandazi.
- Public health licences and food handler certificates apply.
- Cost carefully and stay consistent.
