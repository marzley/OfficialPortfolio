# Construction, property and real estate work

Kenya's building and property sector is huge: homes, rentals, schools, roads. Skilled trades and property services increasingly find clients through WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok and Google. These methods combine practical or professional skills with online marketing.

## What these methods share

- **Qualifications and registration:** architects, engineers, quantity surveyors, valuers and estate agents are registered professionals; contractors register with the National Construction Authority (NCA). Trades benefit from certification.
- **Contracts and deposits:** written agreements protect both sides.
- **Online portfolio:** before/after photos and videos on social media and Google Business.
- **Scams:** land fraud and fake property listings are common; always verify.

## 347. Building contractor (registered)

**What it is:** Constructing houses and buildings for clients.
**How it works:** Tender or quotation → contract → construction → handover.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Construction management, budgeting, supervision |
| Cost & equipment | NCA registration, tools, team |
| Where to find work | Homeowners, diaspora clients, developers, referrals, social media |
| Payment | Milestone payments |
| Realistic earnings | Medium to high |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Register with the NCA in the right category; start with smaller projects.
**Pros:** Large contracts. **Cons & risks:** Cost overruns; disputes.
**Scam warning:** Diaspora clients are often defrauded by contractors; transparent reporting (photos, receipts) builds your reputation.
**Kenya notes:** County building approvals are required before construction.

## Summary

- Construction and property methods include contracting, trades, electrical and plumbing, painting, welding, interior design, drafting, QS, surveying, estate agency, property management, house hunting, property photos, landscaping, fumigation and diaspora monitoring.
