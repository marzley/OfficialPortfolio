# Graphic design (part 2): social media, digital products, illustration, UI and photo editing

Digital design work is done for screens: social media, YouTube, presentations, apps and websites, plus designs you can sell repeatedly as **digital products**. Much of it can be done for international clients and paid in dollars.

See [part 1](./?track=earn-directory&lesson=graphic-design-branding-print) for branding and print. The shared skills, tools and safety rules listed there apply here too.


## 53. Social media post design

**What it is:** Designing posts, carousels, stories and covers for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and WhatsApp status.
**How it works:** Brand templates + regular content (often monthly packages: e.g. 12–20 posts), sized correctly for each platform.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Layout, brand consistency, short copywriting, platform sizes |
| Cost & equipment | Phone or laptop, Canva |
| Where to find work | Local businesses, social media managers, agencies, freelance marketplaces |
| Payment | Per post or monthly package |
| Realistic earnings | Low to low–medium; monthly retainers give stability |
| Time to first income | Days to weeks |

**How to start:** Create a 9-post grid for a realistic business ([social media design](./?track=graphic-design&lesson=social-media-design)); offer monthly packages.
**Pros:** Recurring demand. **Cons & risks:** Volume work; clients want fast turnaround.
**Scam warning:** "Social media design job" groups requiring payment to join.
**Kenya notes:** Combine with social media management for higher value ([social media management](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=social-media-management)).

## 54. YouTube thumbnail design

**What it is:** Eye-catching thumbnails that make people click on videos: bold text, expressive faces, contrast.
**How it works:** Creator sends the video topic and photos; you deliver 1–3 options at 1280×720; some creators order weekly.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Photo cut-outs, bold typography, understanding of click-worthy design |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop; Photoshop/Photopea/Canva |
| Where to find work | YouTubers (Kenyan and international), freelance marketplaces, creator communities |
| Payment | Per thumbnail or monthly package |
| Realistic earnings | Low to low–medium; strong designers working with big channels earn more |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Redesign thumbnails for 10 existing videos as practice; study top creators' thumbnails.
**Pros:** Steady weekly work with creators. **Cons & risks:** Competitive; results-driven.
**Scam warning:** Don't use copyrighted images without rights.
**Kenya notes:** Kenya's YouTube creator community is growing; many creators need consistent thumbnails.

## 55. Selling Canva templates

**What it is:** Creating editable Canva templates (social posts, CVs, planners, menus, business kits) and selling them as digital products.
**How it works:** Design template sets → share via Canva template links → sell on marketplaces (e.g. Etsy, Creative Market, your own site) or locally.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Template design, product thinking, marketing |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop, Canva (Pro helpful), marketplace fees |
| Where to find work | Online marketplaces, your own shop, social media |
| Payment | Per sale via the marketplace's payout methods |
| Realistic earnings | Very low at first; can grow to low–medium with many products and marketing |
| Time to first income | Weeks to months |

**How to start:** Research best-sellers; create a niche set (e.g. templates for Kenyan salons or churches); check Canva's licensing rules for selling templates.
**Pros:** Sell the same product many times. **Cons & risks:** Crowded market; marketing takes effort; marketplace availability for Kenyan sellers varies.
**Scam warning:** "Buy my template course and make $10,000/month" claims.
**Kenya notes:** Local templates (KRA invoice formats, Kenyan CVs, church programmes) can be a niche; see [digital products](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=digital-products).

## 56. Presentation and pitch deck design

**What it is:** Turning content into clear, professional slides for businesses, start-ups, students, conferences and NGOs.
**How it works:** Restructure content, design a master template, create charts and visuals, deliver PowerPoint/Google Slides/Keynote files.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Slide design, storytelling, charts ([PowerPoint](./?track=powerpoint&lesson=slide-design)) |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop, PowerPoint or Google Slides |
| Where to find work | Start-ups, corporates, consultants, students (theses), freelance marketplaces |
| Payment | Per slide or project |
| Realistic earnings | Low–medium to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Redesign 3 messy decks as before/after samples ([pitch decks](./?track=powerpoint&lesson=pitch-decks-reports)).
**Pros:** Good international demand. **Cons & risks:** Tight deadlines before meetings.
**Scam warning:** As above.
**Kenya notes:** Start-ups pitching investors and NGOs presenting to donors need strong decks.

## 59. Digital planners and printables

**What it is:** Downloadable planners, budget sheets, study timetables, colouring pages and worksheets sold as files.
**How it works:** Design in Canva/InDesign/Affinity → export PDFs (interactive for tablets where relevant) → sell on marketplaces or your own store.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Product design, layout, marketplace SEO |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop, design software, store fees |
| Where to find work | Online marketplaces, your own website, social media |
| Payment | Per sale |
| Realistic earnings | Very low at first; low–medium for some with many products |
| Time to first income | Weeks to months |

**How to start:** Research niches; create a small product line; test with friends and social media.
**Pros:** Passive-ish income once listed. **Cons & risks:** Crowded; copying is common.
**Scam warning:** Courses promising guaranteed Etsy income.
**Kenya notes:** Local versions (CBC worksheets, KCSE revision timetables, chama record books) may sell via WhatsApp and local shops; respect copyright of curriculum materials.

## 60. Print-on-demand designs

**What it is:** Uploading designs to print-on-demand services that print and ship T-shirts, mugs, posters and phone cases when customers order, paying you a margin or royalty.
**How it works:** You design and upload; the platform handles printing, shipping and customer service.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Design for products, niche research, marketing |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop, design software; some platforms need upfront account setup |
| Where to find work | Print-on-demand marketplaces (check availability for Kenyan sellers and payout methods) |
| Payment | Royalties per sale |
| Realistic earnings | Very low for most; some succeed with many designs and strong niches |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Upload original designs in focused niches; avoid trademarks.
**Pros:** No stock to hold. **Cons & risks:** Low margins; trademark violations get accounts closed.
**Scam warning:** Courses selling "guaranteed POD income".
**Kenya notes:** Local printers also offer on-demand printing; you can run a local version selling via social media.

|---|---|

## 72. Design contests

**What it is:** Platforms where clients post a brief and many designers submit; the client picks and pays one winner.
**How it works:** You submit designs speculatively; only the winner is paid (some platforms also offer direct projects).

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Fast concept creation, presentation |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop, software |
| Where to find work | Design contest platforms |
| Payment | Prize to the winner |
| Realistic earnings | Very low for most; unpredictable |
| Time to first income | Unpredictable |

**How to start:** Use contests sparingly, mainly to practise briefs; focus on direct clients for real income.
**Pros:** Practice with real briefs. **Cons & risks:** Lots of unpaid work; low win rates.
**Scam warning:** Non-platform "contests" that collect designs without paying anyone.
**Kenya notes:** Treat contests as practice; local direct clients usually pay more reliably.

## Summary

- Digital design methods include social media, thumbnails, templates, decks, infographics, ebooks, printables, print-on-demand, stickers, email and banner graphics, UI design, icons, illustration, characters, photo editing, product images, vector work, mockups and contests.
- Recurring work (monthly packages, thumbnails, product photos) and higher-skill work (UI, illustration, decks) pay best over time.
- Respect licences and trademarks; build a portfolio; mix local and international clients.
