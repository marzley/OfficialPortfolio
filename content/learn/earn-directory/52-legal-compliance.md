# Legal, compliance and verification services

Businesses, landlords, NGOs and families need documents served, land checked, risks assessed and rules followed. Many of these roles need a licence or registration, which keeps competition lower and trust higher.

## What these methods share

- **Licences first:** practise only with the registration your role requires.
- **Confidentiality:** you handle sensitive documents and personal data; protect them under the Data Protection Act.
- **Records:** keep written instructions, receipts and reports for every job.

## 817. Process server (licensed)

**What it is:** Delivering court documents (summons, notices) to the people named, and swearing affidavits of service.
**How it works:** Advocates instruct you → locate and serve → file proof of service.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Knowledge of court procedure, persistence, honesty |
| Cost & equipment | Licence from the Judiciary, transport, phone |
| Where to find work | Law firms, debt recovery firms, courts |
| Payment | Per document served, plus mileage |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Months (after licensing) |

**How to start:** Learn the process server rules, get licensed, then introduce yourself to law firms near the courts.
**Pros:** Steady work from busy law firms. **Cons & risks:** Hostile recipients; travel costs.
**Scam warning:** Never fake service: it's perjury and ends your licence.
**Kenya notes:** Process servers in Kenya are licensed through the Judiciary; law firms keep lists of reliable servers.

## 818. Conveyancing clerk

**What it is:** Supporting advocates with property transfers: searches, consents, valuations, stamp duty and registration.
**How it works:** Prepare and track documents through land offices and county offices.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Attention to detail, land registration knowledge |
| Cost & equipment | Low: computer, transport |
| Where to find work | Law firms handling property |
| Payment | Monthly salary or per file |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks to months |

**How to start:** Take a paralegal or conveyancing course; apply to law firms with property practices.
**Pros:** Skill in demand with property sales. **Cons & risks:** Slow government processes.
**Scam warning:** Never accept cash “to speed up” a land office process: it's bribery.
**Kenya notes:** Ardhisasa and county land offices are central to conveyancing; knowing both is valuable.

## 819. Company secretary (certified)

**What it is:** Keeping companies compliant: annual returns, resolutions, registers, beneficial ownership filings and board meeting records.
**How it works:** Retainer with companies → file returns and keep statutory records.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Company law, governance |
| Cost & equipment | Certified secretary qualification and registration |
| Where to find work | SMEs, NGOs, SACCOs, startups |
| Payment | Monthly retainer or per filing |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Qualify as a Certified Public Secretary (CPS(K)) and register with the Institute of Certified Secretaries; start with small companies.
**Pros:** Recurring retainers. **Cons & risks:** Responsibility for missed deadlines.
**Scam warning:** Only certified secretaries may act; check credentials before hiring one.
**Kenya notes:** Every Kenyan company must file annual returns and beneficial ownership information with the Business Registration Service.

## 820. Data protection compliance consultant

**What it is:** Helping organisations comply with Kenya's Data Protection Act: registration with the ODPC, privacy notices, data audits and staff training.
**How it works:** Gap assessment → policies and registration → training → periodic reviews.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Data protection law, policy writing, training |
| Cost & equipment | Low: laptop; certification helps |
| Where to find work | Schools, clinics, SACCOs, SMEs, NGOs |
| Payment | Per project or retainer |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Study the Data Protection Act and ODPC guidance; get a recognised privacy certification; offer audits to SMEs.
**Pros:** Growing demand as enforcement rises. **Cons & risks:** Must keep up with ODPC rulings.
**Scam warning:** Don't promise organisations they can't be fined: give honest advice.
**Kenya notes:** The ODPC has fined organisations for breaches, which pushes demand for compliance help.

## 821. Accredited mediator

**What it is:** Helping people and businesses settle disputes without going to court.
**How it works:** Parties agree to mediation → sessions → written settlement.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Listening, negotiation, neutrality |
| Cost & equipment | Mediation training and accreditation |
| Where to find work | Courts (court-annexed mediation), businesses, families, landlords and tenants |
| Payment | Per session or per case |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Train with a recognised mediation programme and seek accreditation; join court-annexed mediation panels.
**Pros:** Meaningful work. **Cons & risks:** Income irregular at first.
**Scam warning:** Mediators must stay neutral and keep sessions confidential.
**Kenya notes:** Kenya's Judiciary runs court-annexed mediation to reduce case backlogs.

## 822. Land search and title verification agent

**What it is:** Checking land ownership, encumbrances and survey maps for buyers before they pay.
**How it works:** Client gives details → official search → report on findings.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Land records knowledge, care |
| Cost & equipment | Low; official search fees |
| Where to find work | Land buyers, diaspora, SACCOs, advocates |
| Payment | Per search or report |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Learn how official searches work on Ardhisasa and at land registries; partner with advocates and surveyors.
**Pros:** Protects buyers from fraud. **Cons & risks:** Liability if you miss something.
**Scam warning:** Never forge or “arrange” search results; fake title schemes are a crime.
**Kenya notes:** Land fraud is common; buyers increasingly verify before paying.

## 823. Loss adjuster or assessor (licensed)

**What it is:** Investigating insurance claims (vehicles, property, crops) and reporting the loss to insurers.
**How it works:** Insurer appoints → inspect → assess → report.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Technical knowledge (motor, engineering, agriculture), report writing |
| Cost & equipment | IRA licence, transport, camera |
| Where to find work | Insurance companies |
| Payment | Per assessment |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Gain relevant experience and get licensed by the Insurance Regulatory Authority.
**Pros:** Varied fieldwork. **Cons & risks:** Pressure from claimants.
**Scam warning:** Never accept payments from claimants to inflate a loss.
**Kenya notes:** Insurers rely on licensed assessors for motor and property claims.

## 824. Private investigator (licensed)

**What it is:** Lawful investigations: background checks, fraud, asset tracing, missing persons.
**How it works:** Client brief → lawful inquiries → written report.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Investigation, interviewing, report writing, ethics |
| Cost & equipment | Licensing, phone, transport |
| Where to find work | Law firms, insurers, banks, companies |
| Payment | Per case or day rate |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Get the required licence (private security regulation applies) and experience, often from police or audit work.
**Pros:** Interesting work. **Cons & risks:** Legal limits are strict.
**Scam warning:** Never hack phones, track people illegally or break privacy laws.
**Kenya notes:** The Private Security Regulatory Authority regulates private security services in Kenya; check current licensing requirements.

## 825. Background screening services

**What it is:** Verifying job applicants' identity, education certificates, references and work history for employers.
**How it works:** Consent from candidate → checks → report.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Organisation, verification, data protection |
| Cost & equipment | Low: phone, computer |
| Where to find work | Employers, recruiters, schools, domestic worker agencies |
| Payment | Per candidate |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Create a clear process with candidate consent; partner with recruiters.
**Pros:** Repeat business. **Cons & risks:** Slow responses from institutions.
**Scam warning:** Always get written consent; don't sell candidates' data.
**Kenya notes:** Certificate fraud makes verification valuable to Kenyan employers.

## 826. Occupational safety and health (OSH) advisor

**What it is:** Helping workplaces meet safety law: risk assessments, safety audits, training and safety committees.
**How it works:** Audit → report → training → follow-up.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | OSH knowledge, auditing |
| Cost & equipment | Registration with DOSH, training |
| Where to find work | Factories, construction sites, hotels, schools |
| Payment | Per audit or retainer |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Study OSH, get registered with the Directorate of Occupational Safety and Health Services, and offer audits.
**Pros:** Required by law for many workplaces. **Cons & risks:** Responsibility if advice is wrong.
**Scam warning:** Never sign off on a site you didn't inspect.
**Kenya notes:** The Occupational Safety and Health Act requires workplaces to manage risks and audits.

## 827. Environmental impact assessment (EIA) expert

**What it is:** Preparing EIA and environmental audit reports for projects that need NEMA approval.
**How it works:** Project brief → site visits → public participation → report submitted to NEMA.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Environmental science, report writing |
| Cost & equipment | Registration with NEMA as an expert |
| Where to find work | Developers, factories, county projects, farms |
| Payment | Per report |
| Realistic earnings | Medium to high |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Study environmental science and register with NEMA as an associate then lead expert.
**Pros:** Required for many projects. **Cons & risks:** Strict standards; site travel.
**Scam warning:** Never copy or fake reports: NEMA can deregister you.
**Kenya notes:** Many developments in Kenya need an EIA licence from NEMA before they start.

## 828. Records management and archiving

**What it is:** Organising, storing and retrieving physical files for offices, schools and clinics, and setting retention schedules.
**How it works:** Assess records → organise and index → store securely → retrieve on request.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Organisation, records management |
| Cost & equipment | Storage space, shelving, labels |
| Where to find work | Law firms, clinics, schools, SACCOs, NGOs |
| Payment | Monthly storage fees or per project |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks to months |

**How to start:** Start by organising records on-site for clients; add secure storage later.
**Pros:** Recurring storage income. **Cons & risks:** Space and security costs.
**Scam warning:** Protect confidential files: access logs and locks.
**Kenya notes:** Pairs with document scanning and digitisation (method 263).

## Summary

- Legal and compliance services include process serving, conveyancing support, company secretarial work, data protection, mediation, land searches, loss assessing, private investigation, background screening, OSH, EIA and records management.
- Most need a licence or registration, which builds trust.
- Never fake service, searches, reports or inspections.
