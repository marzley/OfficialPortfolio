# More waste and recycling businesses

Adding to waste collection, recycling and composting (methods 385–389), these turn specific waste streams into income.

## What these methods share

- **Licences:** NEMA licences for waste handling and transport.
- **Safety:** sorting waste needs gloves and protective gear.
- **Buyers:** know who buys your recovered material first.

## 955. Used cooking oil collection

**What it is:** Collecting used cooking oil from restaurants and selling it to biodiesel or soap makers.
**How it works:** Collect in containers → filter → sell in bulk.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Logistics |
| Cost & equipment | Containers, transport, NEMA licence |
| Where to find work | Restaurants, hotels, fast-food outlets |
| Payment | Per litre sold |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Sign up restaurants; find a reliable buyer.
**Pros:** Keeps oil out of drains and street food. **Cons & risks:** Messy logistics.
**Scam warning:** Never resell used oil as cooking oil: it's dangerous and illegal.
**Kenya notes:** Some used oil is illegally recycled into food; legitimate collection protects public health.

## 956. Glass recycling

**What it is:** Collecting and processing waste glass for recyclers or into crafts and beads.
**How it works:** Collect → sort by colour → crush or craft.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Sorting, crafts |
| Cost & equipment | Collection points, crusher or tools |
| Where to find work | Bars, hotels, households, glass recyclers |
| Payment | Per kilo or per product |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Partner with bars and hotels for collection.
**Pros:** Abundant waste. **Cons & risks:** Low prices for raw glass.
**Scam warning:** Wear protective gear when handling glass.
**Kenya notes:** Recycled glass beads are a craft product.

## 957. Tyre recycling

**What it is:** Turning old tyres into products: sandals, mats, planters, flooring and furniture.
**How it works:** Collect tyres → cut and process → make products.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Fabrication |
| Cost & equipment | Cutting tools, workspace |
| Where to find work | Tyre shops, garages; buyers of products |
| Payment | Per product |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Start with simple products like planters and furniture.
**Pros:** Free raw material. **Cons & risks:** Labour-intensive.
**Scam warning:** Never burn tyres: it's toxic and illegal.
**Kenya notes:** Akala sandals made from tyres are a long Kenyan tradition.

## 958. Paper recycling and egg trays

**What it is:** Collecting waste paper and producing egg trays or other moulded products.
**How it works:** Collect paper → pulp → mould → dry → sell.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Machine operation |
| Cost & equipment | Egg tray machine, drying space |
| Where to find work | Offices, schools; poultry farmers buy trays |
| Payment | Per tray |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Secure paper supply and poultry farmer buyers.
**Pros:** Steady demand from poultry farms. **Cons & risks:** Machine costs; drying space.
**Scam warning:** Buy machines from reputable suppliers.
**Kenya notes:** Poultry farms need large numbers of egg trays.

## 959. Scrap metal dealing (licensed)

**What it is:** Buying and selling scrap metal to recyclers and steel mills.
**How it works:** Licensed yard → buy with records → sort → sell in bulk.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Metal identification, record keeping |
| Cost & equipment | Yard, scale, Scrap Metal Council licence |
| Where to find work | Garages, construction sites, households |
| Payment | Margin per kilo |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Get licensed under the Scrap Metal Act and keep seller records.
**Pros:** Big market. **Cons & risks:** Heavy regulation due to theft.
**Scam warning:** Never buy vandalised infrastructure (power cables, rail parts): it's a serious crime.
**Kenya notes:** Kenya regulates scrap dealing under the Scrap Metal Act.

## Summary

- Waste businesses include used cooking oil, glass, tyres, paper egg trays and licensed scrap metal.
- Get NEMA and scrap licences and keep records.
- Never resell used oil as food or buy stolen metal.
