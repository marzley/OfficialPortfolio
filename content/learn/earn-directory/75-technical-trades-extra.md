# More technical trades and installations

Skilled trades with fewer competitors, adding to repairs (526–543) and installations (782–805).

## What these methods share

- **Certification:** many of these need inspection approvals or training.
- **Safety:** heights, machinery and pressure systems are dangerous.
- **Maintenance contracts:** recurring work keeps income steady.

## 983. Lift (elevator) maintenance technician

**What it is:** Maintaining and repairing lifts in buildings.
**How it works:** Scheduled maintenance → repairs → safety checks.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Electrical and mechanical skills |
| Cost & equipment | Training, tools; work with an approved company |
| Where to find work | Office blocks, apartments, hospitals, malls |
| Payment | Monthly salary or contracts |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Join a lift company as a trainee.
**Pros:** Growing with tall buildings. **Cons & risks:** Strict safety rules.
**Scam warning:** Lifts must be inspected by approved persons; never skip checks.
**Kenya notes:** Taller buildings in towns need more lift technicians.

## 990. Water bottling plant

**What it is:** Treating and bottling drinking water for sale.
**How it works:** Treat water → bottle → label → distribute.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Water treatment, quality control |
| Cost & equipment | High: treatment equipment, bottling, KEBS certification |
| Where to find work | Shops, offices, events, schools |
| Payment | Per bottle |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Get KEBS certification and county approvals before selling.
**Pros:** Daily consumption. **Cons & risks:** Strict quality standards.
**Scam warning:** Never sell uncertified water.
**Kenya notes:** Differs from a refill station (method 455): you bottle and distribute.

## Summary

- Technical trades include lift maintenance, boilers, scale calibration, mosquito screens, gabions, arborist work, fish ponds and water bottling.
- Certification, permits and statutory inspections apply.
- Safety and honesty come first.
