# Delivery, transport and local gig work through apps

App-based gig work connects people who need a service (a ride, a delivery, a cleaner, a plumber) with workers nearby. It offers flexibility and fast starts, but income depends on demand, commission rates, fuel and vehicle costs. These methods are mostly offline work found and paid through apps.

## What these methods share

- **Know your real earnings:** subtract commissions, fuel, maintenance, data and insurance from what the app pays.
- **Safety first:** road safety, protective gear, and verified customers.
- **Compliance:** licences, insurance and vehicle requirements (NTSA rules for drivers and riders).
- **Platform terms change:** commission rates and incentives vary; read terms carefully.

## 297. Ride-hailing driver (car)

**What it is:** Driving passengers through ride-hailing apps.
**How it works:** Register with an app → vehicle and document checks → accept rides → earn per trip minus commission.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Safe driving, navigation, customer service |
| Cost & equipment | Car (owned or rented), valid driving licence, PSV-related requirements as applicable, insurance, smartphone |
| Where to find work | Ride-hailing apps operating in your city |
| Payment | App payouts and cash/M-Pesa from riders |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium after costs |
| Time to first income | Weeks (registration) |

**How to start:** Calculate costs carefully, especially if renting a car; meet all legal requirements.
**Pros:** Flexible hours. **Cons & risks:** High costs, commissions, safety risks at night.
**Scam warning:** Car "rent-to-own" deals with unfair terms; read contracts carefully.
**Kenya notes:** Driver associations and regulators have debated commission rates; check current rules and NTSA requirements.

**How it works:** Licensed rider → stage or app jobs → per trip payment.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Safe riding, local knowledge |
| Cost & equipment | Motorbike, licence, insurance, helmet and reflective gear |
| Where to find work | Stages, apps, regular customers |
| Payment | Cash/M-Pesa |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |

**Kenya notes:** NTSA rules on licences, helmets and passenger safety apply.
