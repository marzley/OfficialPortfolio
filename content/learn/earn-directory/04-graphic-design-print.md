# Graphic design (part 1): branding, print and event design

Every business, church, school, family event and political campaign in Kenya needs designs: logos, posters, funeral programmes, wedding invitations, menus, banners, labels. Graphic design is one of the most accessible earning skills: you can start with **Canva** on a phone and grow into professional tools and higher-paying brand work.

This lesson covers branding and print design. Part 2 covers [digital products, social media, illustration and specialist design](./?track=earn-directory&lesson=graphic-design-digital-specialist).

## What all design methods share

- **Core skills:** layout, typography, colour, hierarchy, and listening to clients ([design basics](./?track=graphic-design&lesson=design-basics), [colour and typography](./?track=graphic-design&lesson=colour-typography-graphics)).
- **Tools:** Canva (free/Pro), Adobe Illustrator/Photoshop/InDesign (paid), Affinity apps (one-time purchase), free alternatives (Inkscape, GIMP, Photopea, Figma free plan).
- **Portfolio first:** clients buy what they can see ([design portfolio and clients](./?track=graphic-design&lesson=design-portfolio-clients)).
- **Legal use of assets:** only use fonts, photos and icons you have the right to use; never sell designs built from stolen images or copied logos.
- **Payment:** M-Pesa from local clients (take a deposit), PayPal/Payoneer from international platforms.

See also: [graphics and design earning overview](./?track=earn-online&lesson=graphics-design-earning) and [graphic design with Canva](./?track=graphic-design&lesson=canva-essentials).


## 34. Logo design

**What it is:** Creating a business's main symbol or wordmark: simple, memorable, and usable in many sizes and colours.
**How it works:** Brief (business, audience, values, competitors) → research → sketches → 2–3 concepts → revisions → final files (vector, PNG, colour and black/white versions).

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Concept development, simplicity, typography, vector drawing |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop; Illustrator, Affinity Designer or Inkscape (free) for vector files |
| Where to find work | Local businesses, start-ups, churches, schools, freelance marketplaces, design contests |
| Payment | Fixed project fee; deposit for local clients |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium; very competitive at the cheap end, better with strategy and branding packages |
| Time to first income | Weeks (portfolio first) |

**How to start:** Create 5–10 concept logos for realistic businesses ([logos and branding](./?track=graphic-design&lesson=logos-branding)); learn to deliver proper vector files.
**Pros:** High demand. **Cons & risks:** Price undercutting; clients wanting "just a quick logo" for very little.
**Scam warning:** Never copy existing logos or use AI-generated images that imitate brands; legal and reputation risk.
**Kenya notes:** Many SMEs, chamas, SACCOs and churches need first logos; sell logo + basic brand kit packages.

## 35. Brand identity kits

**What it is:** A complete visual identity: logo variations, colour palette, fonts, patterns, and a brand guideline document showing correct use.
**How it works:** Discovery session → moodboard → logo and system → applications (business card, social post, letterhead) → guideline PDF.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Branding strategy, consistency, presentation |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop, design software |
| Where to find work | Growing SMEs, start-ups, NGOs, schools, agencies (subcontracting) |
| Payment | Project fee in milestones |
| Realistic earnings | Medium; more valuable than logos alone |
| Time to first income | Weeks to months |

**How to start:** Build 2–3 full concept brand kits; present them as case studies.
**Pros:** Higher value; leads to ongoing design work. **Cons & risks:** Longer projects; needs good client communication.
**Scam warning:** Clients requesting "free concepts" from many designers before choosing.
**Kenya notes:** Start-ups and established SMEs rebranding are good targets.

## 36. Business cards, letterheads and stationery

**What it is:** Designing business cards, letterheads, envelopes, email signatures, receipt books and invoice templates that match a brand.
**How it works:** Use brand assets; prepare print-ready files with correct size, bleed and resolution ([print production](./?track=graphic-design&lesson=print-production)).

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Layout, typography, print preparation |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop or phone (Canva); software for print files |
| Where to find work | Local businesses, printers, professionals (lawyers, doctors, consultants) |
| Payment | Small fixed fees; often bundled with printing |
| Realistic earnings | Low; good as add-ons |
| Time to first income | Days to weeks |

**How to start:** Partner with a local printer; offer design + print packages.
**Pros:** Quick jobs; steady local demand. **Cons & risks:** Low prices; print errors can be costly (always send proofs).
**Scam warning:** As above.
**Kenya notes:** Cyber cafés and printers in every town need designers for walk-in customers.

## 37. Flyers and posters

**What it is:** Promotional flyers and posters for businesses, events, sales, schools, churches and campaigns.
**How it works:** Get text and photos → design for the medium (print or WhatsApp/social) → proof → deliver print-ready and digital versions.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Hierarchy (what's read first), bold typography, photo editing |
| Cost & equipment | Phone or laptop (Canva works well) |
| Where to find work | Local businesses, churches, schools, event organisers, WhatsApp marketing |
| Payment | Per design, M-Pesa |
| Realistic earnings | Low to low–medium with volume and regular clients |
| Time to first income | Days |

**How to start:** Design 10 sample posters for different industries ([posters and flyers](./?track=graphic-design&lesson=posters-flyers)); post them on WhatsApp status and Facebook.
**Pros:** Fast to learn; very high local demand. **Cons & risks:** Tight deadlines; clients may send poor photos.
**Scam warning:** Clients who disappear after receiving unwatermarked files: send watermarked previews until paid.
**Kenya notes:** Weekend events, church crusades, sales and school functions create constant demand.

## 38. Event branding (banners, roll-ups, backdrops, tickets)

**What it is:** Designing a coordinated set for an event: roll-up banners, stage backdrops, wall branding, tickets, badges, signage and social media graphics.
**How it works:** Requires exact print dimensions from the printer, high-resolution images and consistent branding across items.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Large-format print knowledge, consistency, deadlines |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop with professional or capable design software |
| Where to find work | Event planners, corporates, NGOs, churches, schools, printers |
| Payment | Package fee per event |
| Realistic earnings | Low–medium to medium per event |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Learn large-format specs (resolution, bleed, safe zones); partner with printers and event planners.
**Pros:** Bigger projects; repeat corporate clients. **Cons & risks:** Last-minute changes; print mistakes are expensive.
**Scam warning:** As above.
**Kenya notes:** Conferences, graduations, product launches and corporate events need full branding packages.

## 39. Funeral programmes, eulogies and memorial posters

**What it is:** Designing funeral and memorial programmes (order of service, eulogy, tributes, photos), memorial posters and social media announcements.
**How it works:** Sensitive, fast work: families provide photos and text, often within 1–3 days; you design, proofread carefully, and coordinate printing.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Layout of multi-page booklets, photo restoration and editing, careful proofreading, empathy |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop (or Canva on phone), printer partnerships |
| Where to find work | Families, churches, funeral homes, printers, referrals |
| Payment | Per programme, often with printing |
| Realistic earnings | Low to low–medium |
| Time to first income | Days |

**How to start:** Create respectful templates; partner with printers and churches; offer quick turnaround with clear proofs.
**Pros:** Constant demand in Kenya. **Cons & risks:** Emotional context; very tight deadlines; errors in names or dates are hurtful, so always get written approval.
**Scam warning:** Avoid overcharging grieving families; build trust and referrals.
**Kenya notes:** Funeral programmes are a major local design market across the country.

## 42. Menu design

**What it is:** Printed menus, wall menus, digital menus with QR codes, and promotional cards for restaurants, cafés, hotels and food vendors.
**How it works:** Organise the menu logically, highlight profitable items, make it readable, deliver print and digital versions.

| | |
|---|---|

## 43. Packaging and label design

**What it is:** Designing product labels, boxes, pouches and bottles that attract buyers and meet labelling requirements.
**How it works:** Get exact packaging dimensions (die-lines) from the printer/manufacturer; design front and back; include required information; print proofs.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Branding, print production, understanding labelling information |
| Cost & equipment | Laptop with vector software |
| Where to find work | Small manufacturers, farmers and agro-processors, cosmetics makers, juice and honey producers |
| Payment | Project fees |
| Realistic earnings | Low–medium to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Create concept labels for local products; learn about die-lines and finishes.
**Pros:** Higher value; brands come back for new products. **Cons & risks:** Mistakes on large print runs are costly; clients must confirm regulatory text.
**Scam warning:** As above.
**Kenya notes:** Small producers seeking supermarket listing need professional labels; clients are responsible for regulatory approvals and required details (e.g. standards marks, ingredients); you design what they confirm.

**How it works:** Cover: concept, typography, imagery, exact spine width from the printer. Interior: styles, margins, page numbers, chapter openings.

| | |
|---|---|

| Cost & equipment | Laptop, design and layout software |
| Where to find work | Self-publishing authors, publishers, churches and schools publishing books |
| Payment | Per cover or per page |

**How to start:** Design covers for public-domain classics as practice; learn print-on-demand specifications.
**Pros:** Growing self-publishing market. **Cons & risks:** Image licensing needs care.

**Kenya notes:** Kenyan authors, pastors and schools increasingly self-publish; local printers and online platforms both need files.

## Summary

- Branding and print design methods range from quick flyers and funeral programmes to brand identities, packaging, reports and book design.
- Start with Canva and a portfolio; grow into professional tools, print knowledge and higher-value packages.
- Take deposits, send watermarked previews, proofread carefully, and only use assets you have rights to.
