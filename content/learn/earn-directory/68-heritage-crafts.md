# Heritage crafts and art

Kenya's craft traditions sell to tourists, collectors and exporters. These add to crafts and making (methods 265–280).

## What these methods share

- **Authenticity:** tell the story behind your craft.
- **Sustainable materials:** use legal, sustainably sourced wood and stone.
- **Markets:** tourism, export, online and corporate gifts.

## 951. Soapstone carving

**What it is:** Carving sculptures, bowls and chess sets from soapstone.
**How it works:** Source stone → carve → polish → sell.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Carving |
| Cost & equipment | Tools, stone |
| Where to find work | Tourists, curio shops, exporters, online |
| Payment | Per piece |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Learn from carvers in Kisii; sell online and to curio shops.
**Pros:** Distinct Kenyan craft. **Cons & risks:** Dust; tourist seasonality.
**Scam warning:** Wear masks against dust.
**Kenya notes:** Kisii soapstone is known worldwide.

## 952. Wood carving

**What it is:** Carving animals, masks, utensils and décor from wood.
**How it works:** Source legal timber → carve → finish.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Carving |
| Cost & equipment | Tools, timber |
| Where to find work | Tourists, hotels, exporters |
| Payment | Per piece |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Use sustainable woods; sell to curio markets.
**Pros:** Strong tourist demand. **Cons & risks:** Timber supply rules.
**Scam warning:** Use legally sourced timber; avoid protected species.
**Kenya notes:** Akamba carving traditions are famous.

## 953. Musical instrument making and repair

**What it is:** Making and repairing guitars, drums, nyatiti, keyboards and wind instruments.
**How it works:** Craft or repair → sell or return.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Woodwork, instrument knowledge |
| Cost & equipment | Tools, materials |
| Where to find work | Churches, schools, musicians |
| Payment | Per instrument or repair |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Start with repairs for churches and schools.
**Pros:** Steady church and school demand. **Cons & risks:** Specialist skills.
**Scam warning:** Be honest about instrument quality.
**Kenya notes:** Churches and schools buy and repair instruments regularly.

## 954. Metal art and sculpture

**What it is:** Creating sculptures and décor from scrap metal.
**How it works:** Collect scrap → design → weld → finish.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Welding, design |
| Cost & equipment | Welding equipment, scrap |
| Where to find work | Hotels, homes, galleries, tourists |
| Payment | Per piece |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |

**How to start:** Make signature pieces; display at hotels and galleries.
**Pros:** Uses recycled materials. **Cons & risks:** Heavy items to transport.
**Scam warning:** Buy scrap from licensed dealers only.
**Kenya notes:** Recycled metal art is popular with hotels and tourists.

## Summary

- Heritage crafts include soapstone carving, wood carving, instrument making and metal art.
- Use legal, sustainable materials and tell the story behind each piece.
- Sell to tourists, galleries, hotels and online.
