# Specialist printing and branding

Every business, school, church and event needs branded items: banners, uniforms, stamps, signs, ID cards and packaging. Beyond a general print shop (method 260), each specialist printing skill can be a business of its own.

## What these methods share

- **Quality and deadlines:** clients come back when work is sharp and on time.
- **Deposits:** take a deposit before printing custom orders.
- **Rights:** don't print trademarks, club logos or designs you don't have permission to use.

## 771. Screen printing

**What it is:** Printing designs on T-shirts, bags and fabrics with screens.
**How it works:** Design → screen → print → cure.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Screen printing |
| Cost & equipment | Screens, inks, press, drying |
| Where to find work | Schools, events, companies, churches |
| Payment | Per item |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Learn from a printer; start with simple one-colour designs.
**Pros:** Cheap for bulk orders. **Cons & risks:** Messy; setup per design.
**Scam warning:** Deposits for bulk orders.
**Kenya notes:** Election, school and event seasons bring bulk orders.

## 774. DTF (direct-to-film) garment printing

**What it is:** Printing full-colour designs onto film, then heat-pressing them onto T-shirts, hoodies, caps and bags of almost any fabric.
**How it works:** Design → print on DTF film → powder and cure → heat press onto the garment.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Design, machine operation |
| Cost & equipment | DTF printer and curing unit (or buy printed transfers from a DTF supplier), heat press |
| Where to find work | Schools, events, churches, brands, individuals |
| Payment | Per item |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Start by ordering printed DTF transfers and pressing them yourself; buy a printer when orders grow.
**Pros:** Works on cotton and blends; no minimum order. **Cons & risks:** Printer maintenance and ink costs.
**Scam warning:** Don't print brand logos or copyrighted designs without permission.
**Kenya notes:** DTF suits small custom orders that screen printing can't do cheaply.

## Summary

- Specialist printing includes screen printing, embroidery, large-format, DTF garment printing, sublimation, laser engraving, stamps, signage, ID cards, packaging and corporate gifts.
- Quality, deadlines and deposits matter.
- Respect trademarks, data privacy and rules (county signage, KEBS labels).
