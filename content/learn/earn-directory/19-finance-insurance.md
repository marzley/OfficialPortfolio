# Finance, insurance and money-services work

People and businesses need help with insurance, savings, loans, payments and records. These methods earn commissions or fees by connecting customers with licensed financial products, or by providing financial support services. Many require licences; all require honesty, because you're handling other people's money and futures.

## What these methods share

- **Licensing:** insurance agents are licensed by the Insurance Regulatory Authority (IRA); investment advisers and fund intermediaries by the CMA; SACCOs by SASRA; banks by CBK. Check what your role requires.
- **Ethics:** recommend what suits the customer, disclose commissions, never mis-sell.
- **No guaranteed returns:** anyone promising fixed high returns is likely running a scam.

## 331. Insurance agent

**What it is:** Selling insurance (motor, medical, life, education, business) on behalf of licensed insurers, earning commissions.
**How it works:** Get licensed → contract with insurers → find clients → advise → sell → service policies and claims.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Product knowledge, sales, trust, follow-up |
| Cost & equipment | Licensing exam/fees, phone, transport |
| Where to find work | Families, businesses, boda boda and matatu owners, social media |
| Payment | Commissions from insurers |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium–high (renewals build income) |
| Time to first income | Weeks to months (licensing) |

**How to start:** Meet IRA licensing requirements (agents usually need training and registration through an insurer); start with motor and medical cover.
**Pros:** Renewal commissions grow over time. **Cons & risks:** Rejection-heavy sales; claims disputes.
**Scam warning:** Never collect premiums into a personal account; never issue fake insurance stickers or certificates.
**Kenya notes:** Motor insurance for boda bodas, matatus and private cars is a large market.

## Summary

- Finance methods include insurance agency and claims, bank and SACCO sales, agency banking, loan facilitation, bookkeeping, tax services, financial literacy training, chama advisory, ethical debt collection, payment solutions, pensions, fund agents, accounting software, field officer work and finance content.
- Licensing and ethics are essential; never handle customers' money personally or promise returns.
