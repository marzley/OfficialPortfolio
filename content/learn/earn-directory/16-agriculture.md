# Agriculture and agribusiness with online tools

Agriculture employs a large share of Kenyans, and digital tools are changing how farmers learn, sell and get paid. These methods combine farming or agribusiness with online marketing, information and M-Pesa.

## What these methods share

- **Start small and learn:** test before investing heavily; consult agricultural extension officers.
- **Market first:** find buyers before producing large volumes.
- **Records and quality:** good records and consistent quality bring better prices.
- **Scams:** fake seed, fake buyers and "too-good" livestock deals are common.

## 281. Selling farm produce directly online

**What it is:** Selling vegetables, fruits, eggs and dairy directly to urban customers via WhatsApp and social media.
**How it works:** Weekly orders → harvest → delivery.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Farming, logistics, customer service |
| Cost & equipment | Farm, transport |
| Where to find work | Urban households, offices, restaurants |
| Payment | M-Pesa |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Build a customer list in one estate; deliver on fixed days.
**Pros:** Better prices than middlemen. **Cons & risks:** Perishability.
**Scam warning:** Buyers who never pay after delivery; take payment first or on delivery.
**Kenya notes:** Urban demand for fresh, organic produce is growing.

## Summary

- Agriculture methods include direct produce sales, poultry, fish, mushrooms, honey, value addition, input reselling, consulting, content, aggregation, greenhouses, dairy, machinery rental, nurseries, farm management and pet services.
