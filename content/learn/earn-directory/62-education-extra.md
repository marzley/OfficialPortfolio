# More education services

Schools, parents and adult learners need more than tutoring. These add to teaching and education businesses (methods 204–220 and 557–570).

## What these methods share

- **Child safety:** background checks and safeguarding for anyone working with children.
- **Curriculum fit:** align with CBE (competency-based education) where relevant.
- **Relationships:** head teachers and parents' associations open doors.

## 910. KNEC examiner, marker and supervisor roles

**What it is:** Seasonal work marking national exams or supervising and invigilating exam centres.
**How it works:** Qualified teachers apply or are appointed → training → marking or supervision sessions.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Teaching qualification, subject expertise, integrity |
| Cost & equipment | None beyond being a qualified teacher |
| Where to find work | Kenya National Examinations Council, TSC |
| Payment | Per session or script |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium (seasonal) |
| Time to first income | Months (exam season) |

**How to start:** Register and keep your TSC and KNEC details updated; apply when invitations open.
**Pros:** Extra income for teachers. **Cons & risks:** Seasonal; strict confidentiality.
**Scam warning:** Exam leaks are serious crimes: never share papers or marking schemes.
**Kenya notes:** KNEC recruits examiners and supervisors every exam season.

## 911. Adult literacy and numeracy teacher

**What it is:** Teaching adults to read, write and count, often for business and daily life.
**How it works:** Groups or one-to-one classes → practical lessons.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Patience, teaching skills |
| Cost & equipment | Low: books, venue |
| Where to find work | Churches, chamas, NGOs, county adult education programmes |
| Payment | Per class or monthly |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Partner with community groups and adult education centres.
**Pros:** Life-changing impact. **Cons & risks:** Low pay in community settings.
**Scam warning:** Never charge for “certificates” you can't legitimately issue.
**Kenya notes:** Adult education programmes exist through counties and the national government.

## 912. STEM and robotics clubs for schools

**What it is:** Running after-school clubs where children build robots and science projects.
**How it works:** Bring kits → weekly sessions → competitions.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | STEM knowledge, teaching children |
| Cost & equipment | Robotics kits, laptops |
| Where to find work | Private schools, academies, holiday camps |
| Payment | Per child per term |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Start with one school; enter local science and robotics competitions.
**Pros:** Popular with parents. **Cons & risks:** Kit costs.
**Scam warning:** Background checks and safeguarding are essential.
**Kenya notes:** Fits CBE's focus on practical skills.

## 913. Debate and public speaking coaching

**What it is:** Coaching students and professionals in debate, speeches and presentations.
**How it works:** Workshops or weekly coaching → competitions or events.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Public speaking, coaching |
| Cost & equipment | Low |
| Where to find work | Schools, universities, professionals |
| Payment | Per session or term |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |

**How to start:** Coach a school debate team to a competition; use results as proof.
**Pros:** Valuable skill for life. **Cons & risks:** Competitive market.

## 914. School trips organiser

**What it is:** Planning educational trips for schools: transport, venues, permits, meals and safety.
**How it works:** Package trips → handle logistics → supervise.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Planning, safety management |
| Cost & equipment | Low upfront (pay suppliers from deposits); insurance |
| Where to find work | Schools, colleges |
| Payment | Per student or package |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Partner with transport and venues; get insurance.
**Pros:** Repeat school clients. **Cons & risks:** High responsibility for safety.
**Scam warning:** Use licensed, inspected vehicles only.
**Kenya notes:** Trips to parks, museums and industries are common for schools.

## 915. Science lab equipment and chemical supply

**What it is:** Supplying schools with lab apparatus, chemicals and models.
**How it works:** Take orders → source from licensed suppliers → deliver.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Product knowledge, procurement |
| Cost & equipment | Stock or supplier accounts, transport |
| Where to find work | Secondary schools, colleges |
| Payment | Per order |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |

**How to start:** Register as a supplier with schools; follow chemical handling rules.
**Pros:** Repeat termly orders. **Cons & risks:** Payment delays from schools.
**Scam warning:** Handle chemicals safely and legally.
**Kenya notes:** Schools buy lab supplies every term.

## 916. Early childhood teaching aids production

**What it is:** Making charts, flashcards, counters and learning materials for ECDE centres.
**How it works:** Design → print or craft → sell to schools and parents.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Design, crafts |
| Cost & equipment | Printing access, materials |
| Where to find work | ECDE centres, daycares, parents |
| Payment | Per item or set |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Design materials aligned to the CBE early years curriculum.
**Pros:** Low cost to start. **Cons & risks:** Competition from printed imports.
**Scam warning:** Don't copy publishers' copyrighted content.
**Kenya notes:** Many ECDE centres need affordable materials.

## Summary

- Education services include KNEC roles, adult literacy, STEM clubs, debate coaching, school trips, lab supplies and ECDE materials.
- Safeguarding and background checks matter whenever you work with children.
- Exam integrity and copyright must be respected.
