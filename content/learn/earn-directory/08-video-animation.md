# Video, photography and animation work

Video is now the main way businesses, creators, churches and organisations communicate. Weddings, events and products still need great photos. This category covers filming, editing, animation and photography, from phone-only starts to professional production.

## What these methods share

- **Equipment ladder:** start with a good smartphone, a tripod and a microphone; upgrade to cameras, lights and stronger computers as income grows.
- **Software:** CapCut, DaVinci Resolve (free version), Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut; Canva for simple video; Blender (free) for 3D; Lightroom/Snapseed for photos.
- **Rights:** use licensed music and footage; get consent from people you film, especially children; agree usage rights with clients.
- See [content creation](./?track=earn-online&lesson=content-creation-earning) for building your own channels.

## 116. Short-form video editing (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)

**What it is:** Editing short vertical videos with hooks, captions, cuts, music and effects for social media.
**How it works:** Clients send raw clips or long videos → you cut 15–90 second videos → deliver weekly batches.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Fast-paced editing, captions, trends, storytelling hooks |
| Cost & equipment | Smartphone (CapCut) or laptop |
| Where to find work | Creators, coaches, businesses, agencies, freelance marketplaces |
| Payment | Per video or monthly package |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium with monthly clients |
| Time to first income | Days to weeks |

**How to start:** Edit 10 sample shorts from your own or public-domain footage; post them; offer packages.
**Pros:** Huge demand; phone-friendly start. **Cons & risks:** Fast turnaround; volume work.
**Scam warning:** "Video editing jobs" asking for deposits to receive projects.
**Kenya notes:** Kenyan businesses and creators increasingly post daily short videos and need editors.

## 118. Event videography (weddings, funerals, graduations, conferences)

**What it is:** Filming and editing events into highlight videos and full recordings.
**How it works:** Pre-event planning → filming (often with a team) → edit highlights and full video → deliver via flash drive or online link.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Camera operation, audio capture, editing, client service |
| Cost & equipment | Camera(s), lenses, microphones, lights, tripod/gimbal, editing laptop; significant investment |
| Where to find work | Couples, families, churches, schools, companies, event planners |
| Payment | Package fees with deposits |
| Realistic earnings | Medium to medium–high in peak seasons |
| Time to first income | Weeks to months (portfolio, equipment) |

**How to start:** Assist an established videographer; film events for free or cheap to build a reel.
**Pros:** High demand, especially weekends. **Cons & risks:** Equipment cost and theft risk; weekend work.
**Scam warning:** Clients who don't pay balances; always take deposits.
**Kenya notes:** Weddings, ruracio, funerals and graduations keep event videographers busy; offer livestreams too.

## Summary

- Video, photo and animation methods include shorts and long-form editing, event and corporate video, livestreams, property and drone work, motion graphics, explainers, 3D and architectural visualisation, captions, colour, music videos, stock, photography niches and equipment rental.
- Start with a phone and free software; reinvest income into equipment.
- Respect music and image rights, get consent, and follow drone regulations.
