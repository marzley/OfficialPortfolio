# More property and finance businesses

Adding to property (methods 357–360) and finance (331–346), these businesses need more capital or licences but can build lasting income.

## What these methods share

- **Licences:** valuers, forex bureaus, digital lenders and investment advisers are regulated.
- **Due diligence:** verify land, titles and approvals before investing.
- **Honesty:** transparent pricing and terms protect you and your customers.

## 943. Student hostel

**What it is:** Providing accommodation for college and university students near campuses.
**How it works:** Build or lease → furnish → rent per semester → manage.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Property management |
| Cost & equipment | High: building or lease, furnishing |
| Where to find work | Universities, colleges, TVETs |
| Payment | Per bed per semester |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Start near a growing campus; offer Wi-Fi, security and study space.
**Pros:** Steady demand. **Cons & risks:** Vacancies during holidays.
**Scam warning:** Use written tenancy agreements.
**Kenya notes:** Hostels differ from tourist hostels (method 498): students stay for whole semesters.

## 944. Rental units (being a landlord)

**What it is:** Building or buying bedsitters, one-bedroom units or shops to rent out.
**How it works:** Acquire property → rent → manage or hire a property manager.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Property and tenant management |
| Cost & equipment | Very high: land and construction, or purchase |
| Where to find work | Growing towns, near jobs and transport |
| Payment | Monthly rent |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Years |

**How to start:** Start small; check approvals, title and demand before building.
**Pros:** Long-term income. **Cons & risks:** High capital; vacancies; repairs.
**Scam warning:** Check land titles and county approvals before building.
**Kenya notes:** Rental demand is high in fast-growing towns.

## 945. Land banking and subdivision

**What it is:** Buying land, getting approvals to subdivide, and selling plots.
**How it works:** Buy → survey and approvals → title processing → sell plots.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Land law, negotiation, patience |
| Cost & equipment | Very high capital |
| Where to find work | Growing peri-urban areas |
| Payment | Profit on plot sales |
| Realistic earnings | High (with risk) |
| Time to first income | Years |

**How to start:** Work with an advocate and licensed surveyor; do full due diligence.
**Pros:** Potentially high returns. **Cons & risks:** Legal and title risks; long timelines.
**Scam warning:** Never sell plots without proper titles and approvals: buyers sue and it can be criminal.
**Kenya notes:** Land fraud is a major problem; honest sellers stand out.

## 946. Co-working space

**What it is:** Renting desks, private offices and meeting rooms to freelancers and small teams.
**How it works:** Fit out space → rent by day or month → provide internet and amenities.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Management, marketing |
| Cost & equipment | High: lease, furniture, fast internet, backup power |
| Where to find work | Freelancers, startups, remote workers |
| Payment | Daily or monthly memberships |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Start in a busy town with good internet and backup power.
**Pros:** Recurring memberships. **Cons & risks:** Lease costs.
**Scam warning:** Clear membership terms.
**Kenya notes:** Remote workers want reliable internet and power.

## 947. Property valuer (registered)

**What it is:** Valuing land and buildings for banks, buyers, courts and insurers.
**How it works:** Inspection → analysis → valuation report.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Valuation, real estate economics |
| Cost & equipment | Registration with the Valuers Registration Board |
| Where to find work | Banks, SACCOs, advocates, insurers |
| Payment | Per valuation |
| Realistic earnings | Medium to high |
| Time to first income | Years (qualification) |

**How to start:** Study land economics; register as a valuer.
**Pros:** Professional fees. **Cons & risks:** Long qualification path.
**Scam warning:** Never inflate valuations for loans: it's fraud.
**Kenya notes:** Banks need valuations for mortgages and loans.

## 948. Foreign exchange bureau (licensed)

**What it is:** Buying and selling foreign currencies.
**How it works:** Get a CBK licence → set up premises → trade currencies.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Finance, compliance |
| Cost & equipment | High: capital, CBK licence, security |
| Where to find work | Towns, borders, tourist areas |
| Payment | Margin on exchange |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months to years |

**How to start:** Meet CBK capital and compliance rules, including anti-money laundering checks.
**Pros:** Steady trade in busy areas. **Cons & risks:** Heavy regulation.
**Scam warning:** Follow anti-money laundering rules strictly.
**Kenya notes:** Forex bureaus in Kenya are licensed and supervised by the Central Bank.

## 949. Digital credit provider (licensed)

**What it is:** Offering small loans through apps or USSD.
**How it works:** Get licensed → build credit scoring → lend and collect fairly.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Finance, technology, compliance |
| Cost & equipment | High: capital, technology, licence |
| Where to find work | Individuals, SMEs |
| Payment | Interest and fees |
| Realistic earnings | Medium to high |
| Time to first income | Years |

**How to start:** Apply for a Central Bank of Kenya digital credit provider licence.
**Pros:** Large market. **Cons & risks:** Strict rules on pricing and collection.
**Scam warning:** Debt shaming and harassing borrowers is illegal.
**Kenya notes:** Since 2022, digital lenders must be licensed by the CBK.

## 950. Investment adviser (licensed)

**What it is:** Advising clients on investments such as unit trusts, shares and bonds.
**How it works:** Licensed by the Capital Markets Authority → assess clients → advise → review.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Finance, ethics |
| Cost & equipment | CMA licence, qualifications |
| Where to find work | Professionals, SMEs, diaspora |
| Payment | Fees or commissions |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Years |

**How to start:** Get relevant qualifications and a CMA licence.
**Pros:** Professional income. **Cons & risks:** Regulation and liability.
**Scam warning:** Never promise guaranteed returns.
**Kenya notes:** The CMA licenses investment advisers in Kenya.

## Summary

- Property and finance businesses include student hostels, rental units, land subdivision, co-working, valuation, forex bureaus, digital lending and investment advice.
- Many are licensed by CBK, CMA or the Valuers Registration Board.
- Due diligence, honest terms and fair collection are essential.
