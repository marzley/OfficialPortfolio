# Web, software and tech services

Tech skills are among the best-paid earning methods, locally and internationally. Kenyan businesses need websites, M-Pesa payments, systems and IT support; international clients hire developers, testers and specialists remotely. Most methods here need a laptop and months of learning, but the free hub lessons and [career roadmaps](./?track=career-roadmaps&lesson=choose-a-tech-career) take you through them.

## What these methods share

- **Proof beats certificates:** a portfolio of working projects ([Build real projects](./?track=projects&lesson=how-to-build-projects)).
- **Security and ethics:** protect client data, never leave secrets in code, and only test systems you're authorised to test.
- **Contracts and deposits** for direct clients ([discovery, quotes and contracts](./?track=freelancing&lesson=discovery-quotes-contracts)).

**What it is:** Building websites for businesses, schools, churches and organisations.
**How it works:** Brief → design → build (code or WordPress) → content → launch → training and care plan.

|---|---|
| Skills | HTML, CSS, JavaScript or WordPress; hosting; SEO basics |

| Where to find work | Local businesses, referrals, freelance platforms, agencies |
| Payment | Project fee with deposits; M-Pesa/bank |
| Realistic earnings | Low–medium to medium–high |
| Time to first income | Months (learning) then weeks per project |

**How to start:** Follow the [web developer roadmap](./?track=career-roadmaps&lesson=web-developer-roadmap) and the [business website project](./?track=projects&lesson=business-website).

**Kenya notes:** See [web design business](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=web-design-business).

## Summary

- Tech methods range from websites, WordPress, e-commerce and M-Pesa integration to systems, apps, APIs, maintenance, security, hosting, email setup, IT support, networks, CCTV, QA, data, Excel, automation, AI chatbots, databases, cloud, cybersecurity and teaching.
- Learning takes months, but pay and demand are strong; projects are your proof.
- Work ethically: authorisation for security work, licensed software, clients own their domains and data.
