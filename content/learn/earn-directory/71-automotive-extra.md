# More automotive specialist trades

Adding to automotive trades (methods 431–445), these specialists serve garages and fleet owners who need specific repairs.

## What these methods share

- **Skills:** technical training and experience matter more than equipment.
- **Honesty:** explain faults and show replaced parts.
- **Partnerships:** garages refer specialist work.

## 965. Auto glass replacement

**What it is:** Replacing and repairing windscreens and vehicle windows.
**How it works:** Source glass → fit → seal → test.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Glass fitting |
| Cost & equipment | Tools, adhesives, glass supplier |
| Where to find work | Motorists, insurers, fleets |
| Payment | Per job |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Train under a fitter; partner with insurers and garages.
**Pros:** Insurance work. **Cons & risks:** Breakage risk.
**Scam warning:** Use quality glass and adhesives.
**Kenya notes:** Insurers pay for windscreen claims.

## 966. Vehicle air-conditioning repair

**What it is:** Servicing and repairing car AC systems.
**How it works:** Diagnose → recharge or repair → test.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | AC systems, diagnostics |
| Cost & equipment | Recharge machine, tools |
| Where to find work | Motorists, fleets, matatu owners |
| Payment | Per job |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Train in refrigeration and auto AC.
**Pros:** Hot regions need AC. **Cons & risks:** Equipment costs.
**Scam warning:** Handle refrigerants properly.
**Kenya notes:** Mombasa and other hot towns have strong demand.

## 967. Exhaust and silencer fabrication

**What it is:** Repairing and fabricating exhaust pipes and silencers.
**How it works:** Inspect → cut and weld → fit.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Welding, fabrication |
| Cost & equipment | Welding equipment, pipe bender |
| Where to find work | Motorists, matatus, trucks |
| Payment | Per job |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Learn on the job at an exhaust shop.
**Pros:** Steady demand. **Cons & risks:** Noise and fumes.
**Scam warning:** Don't remove required emissions parts.
**Kenya notes:** NTSA inspections check excessive noise and smoke.

## 968. Diesel injection specialist

**What it is:** Testing and repairing diesel injectors and pumps.
**How it works:** Remove → test on bench → repair or calibrate → refit.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Diesel systems, calibration |
| Cost & equipment | Test bench, tools |
| Where to find work | Trucks, matatus, tractors, generators |
| Payment | Per job |
| Realistic earnings | Medium to high |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Train with an injection specialist; invest in test equipment gradually.
**Pros:** Specialist, well-paid work. **Cons & risks:** Expensive equipment.
**Scam warning:** Use genuine parts where needed and explain options.
**Kenya notes:** Diesel fleets and tractors need injection specialists.

## Summary

- Automotive specialists include auto glass, AC repair, exhaust fabrication and diesel injection.
- Training and honest explanations build trust.
- Follow emissions and safety rules.
