# Community, NGO, public programmes and other legitimate earning routes

Some income comes from community work, public programmes, competitions and sharing what you already own. These methods are varied: some are jobs, some are side incomes, some are one-off opportunities. All are legitimate when done honestly.

## What these methods share

- **Verify programmes** through official websites and offices.
- **Never pay to be "selected"** for government programmes, NGO jobs or competitions.
- **Keep records and stay honest** with communities.

## 414. Community health promoter and outreach work

**What it is:** Supporting community health programmes (education, referrals, data collection).
**How it works:** Recruited through county health departments or NGOs, often with stipends.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Communication, community trust, training provided |
| Cost & equipment | Phone (often provided for data) |
| Where to find work | County health departments, NGOs |
| Payment | Stipends/salaries |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Time to first income | Months (recruitment) |

**How to start:** Follow official county recruitment announcements.
**Pros:** Community impact. **Cons & risks:** Low pay.
**Scam warning:** Fake recruitment demanding fees.
**Kenya notes:** Community health promoters are part of Kenya's primary healthcare system.

**How it works:** Registration → training → mentorship → job linkages.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Willingness to learn |
| Cost & equipment | Access to centres or a device |
| Where to find work | Official programme portals and centres |
| Payment | Programme-dependent; income comes from the skills and opportunities you pursue |

## Summary

- Community and other routes include health promoters, NGO roles, stipend programmes, Ajira and Jitume, official enumeration and election jobs, competitions and grants, renting assets and space, referral programmes, decluttering, start-up feedback, fixing, sign language interpretation, funeral coordination and personal shopping.
- Verify official programmes and never pay to be selected.
