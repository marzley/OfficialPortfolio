# Interior finishing and home specialty services

New buildings, renovations and rentals all need finishing work beyond basic masonry, tiling and painting (methods 348–351). Each specialty below can be a focused business with good margins.

## What these methods share

- **Portfolio:** before-and-after photos win most jobs.
- **Clear quotes:** measure carefully, quote materials and labour separately, take a deposit for materials.
- **Safety:** chemicals, dust and heights need protective gear and training.

## 794. Gypsum ceilings and partitions

**What it is:** Installing gypsum ceilings, bulkheads and partition walls, including lighting cut-outs.
**How it works:** Measure → design → frame → board → finish.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Gypsum work, measuring, finishing |
| Cost & equipment | Tools, scaffolding access |
| Where to find work | New homes, offices, shops, churches |
| Payment | Per square metre or per project |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks to months |

**How to start:** Work under an experienced fundi; post photos of finished ceilings.
**Pros:** High demand in new builds. **Cons & risks:** Dusty; competitive pricing.
**Scam warning:** Take deposits only for materials, with receipts.
**Kenya notes:** Gypsum ceilings are standard in new Kenyan homes and offices.

## 795. Wallpaper and 3D wall panel installation

**What it is:** Supplying and fitting wallpaper, wall panels and feature walls.
**How it works:** Consult → measure → order → install.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Measuring, fitting, finishing |
| Cost & equipment | Tools, sample books |
| Where to find work | Homes, salons, restaurants, offices |
| Payment | Per roll or square metre |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Partner with wallpaper shops as their installer; build a portfolio.
**Pros:** Quick jobs, visible results. **Cons & risks:** Damp walls ruin work.
**Scam warning:** Check walls for damp before installing.
**Kenya notes:** Feature walls are popular in Kenyan living rooms, salons and restaurants.

## 796. Epoxy and resin flooring

**What it is:** Laying seamless epoxy floors for garages, shops, clinics, kitchens and showrooms.
**How it works:** Prepare surface → prime → pour epoxy → cure.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Surface preparation, mixing, application |
| Cost & equipment | Grinder, materials, protective gear |
| Where to find work | Showrooms, clinics, garages, factories, homes |
| Payment | Per square metre |
| Realistic earnings | Medium to high |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Train with a supplier or contractor; start with small floors.
**Pros:** Premium niche. **Cons & risks:** Chemicals need ventilation and gear; mistakes are costly.
**Scam warning:** Use genuine materials; cheap resin peels.
**Kenya notes:** Clinics, food businesses and showrooms value easy-to-clean floors.

## 799. Home staging for sale and rent

**What it is:** Styling empty or cluttered homes with furniture and décor so they rent or sell faster and photograph well.
**How it works:** Assess → style with own or hired items → photos → remove after sale or rent.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Interior styling, photography basics |
| Cost & equipment | Décor and furniture stock or rental partners |
| Where to find work | Landlords, agents, developers, short-stay hosts |
| Payment | Per project or monthly hire |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Stage your own space or a friend's rental; partner with agents.
**Pros:** Low start-up if you hire items. **Cons & risks:** Items get damaged; irregular work.
**Scam warning:** Get signed agreements for items left in homes.
**Kenya notes:** Agents and short-stay hosts need good listing photos.

## 800. Professional home organising and decluttering

**What it is:** Helping people sort, declutter and organise homes, wardrobes, kitchens and offices.
**How it works:** Consult → sort → donate, sell or discard → organise with storage.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Organising, patience, discretion |
| Cost & equipment | Low: labels, bins (client buys storage) |
| Where to find work | Busy professionals, families moving house, offices |
| Payment | Per session or project |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Start with friends; post before-and-after photos (with permission).
**Pros:** Low cost. **Cons & risks:** Physical work; trust is essential.
**Scam warning:** Never take items without clear permission.
**Kenya notes:** Pairs well with moving services (method 302) and house cleaning (303).

| | |

## 802. Water tank cleaning and disinfection

**What it is:** Emptying, scrubbing and disinfecting water tanks for homes, schools, hotels and estates.
**How it works:** Schedule → drain → clean → disinfect → refill and record.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Cleaning, safe chemical use |
| Cost & equipment | Pumps, brushes, protective gear, safe disinfectants |
| Where to find work | Homes, schools, apartments, hotels, hospitals |
| Payment | Per tank or contract |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Learn safe disinfection practice; offer schools and estates regular contracts.
**Pros:** Recurring work. **Cons & risks:** Confined spaces are dangerous: never enter a tank alone.
**Scam warning:** Don't use unsafe chemicals; rinse properly.
**Kenya notes:** Tanks are everywhere in Kenya and regular cleaning protects health.

**How it works:** Survey → rig → clean or repair → inspect.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Certified rope access (e.g. IRATA), cleaning |

| Where to find work | Office towers, hotels, apartments |
| Payment | Daily rate or contract |

**Kenya notes:** Nairobi, Mombasa and other towns have more high-rises needing facade maintenance.

## 805. Waterproofing and damp-proofing

**What it is:** Stopping leaks and damp in roofs, basements, bathrooms, water tanks and walls.
**How it works:** Inspect → find cause → apply membranes or coatings → warranty.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Waterproofing systems, diagnosis |
| Cost & equipment | Tools, materials |
| Where to find work | Homes, apartments, developers, schools |
| Payment | Per square metre or project |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Train with a materials supplier; start with bathrooms and flat roofs.
**Pros:** Problem-solving work with referrals. **Cons & risks:** Callbacks if the cause wasn't fixed.
**Scam warning:** Fix the real cause, not just the stain.
**Kenya notes:** Flat roofs and long rains make waterproofing a common need.

## Summary

- Interior specialties include gypsum ceilings, wallpaper and wall panels, epoxy and wooden floors, fitted kitchens and wardrobes, home staging, organising, window film, tank and solar panel cleaning, rope access and waterproofing.
- Before-and-after photos, careful measuring and clear quotes win work.
- Chemicals, confined spaces and heights need training and protective gear.
