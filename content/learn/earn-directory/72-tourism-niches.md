# Tourism niches

Adding to tourism businesses (methods 495–509), these niche tours attract specific travellers.

## What these methods share

- **Licences:** tour guides and operators need the right licences.
- **Reviews:** online reviews bring most tourists.
- **Safety:** insurance and safety briefings.

## 969. Birding guide

**What it is:** Guiding birdwatchers to see and identify birds.
**How it works:** Plan routes → guide → identify species → share lists.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Bird identification, guiding |
| Cost & equipment | Binoculars, field guide, licences |
| Where to find work | International birders, lodges, tour operators |
| Payment | Per day |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Learn birds through clubs (e.g. Nature Kenya bird walks); get guide licences.
**Pros:** Kenya has over 1,000 bird species. **Cons & risks:** Specialist knowledge needed.
**Scam warning:** Respect wildlife and protected areas.
**Kenya notes:** Kenya is a top birding destination.

## 970. Cycling tours

**What it is:** Leading bike tours in towns, villages, parks and countryside.
**How it works:** Plan routes → provide bikes and helmets → guide.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Cycling, guiding, safety |
| Cost & equipment | Bikes, helmets, repair kit, insurance |
| Where to find work | Tourists, expats, corporate groups |
| Payment | Per person |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Start with a scenic route; list on travel platforms.
**Pros:** Healthy, eco-friendly. **Cons & risks:** Traffic and weather.
**Scam warning:** Always provide helmets and safety briefings.
**Kenya notes:** Places like Hell's Gate and Lake Naivasha suit cycling.

## 971. Food tours

**What it is:** Guiding visitors through local food: markets, street food and traditional dishes.
**How it works:** Plan stops → guide tastings → share stories.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Food knowledge, storytelling |
| Cost & equipment | Low: partnerships with vendors |
| Where to find work | Tourists, expats, business visitors |
| Payment | Per person |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Partner with hygienic vendors; list on tour platforms.
**Pros:** Unique experience. **Cons & risks:** Food safety for visitors.
**Scam warning:** Choose vendors with good hygiene.
**Kenya notes:** Nairobi and Mombasa food scenes attract visitors.

## Summary

- Tourism niches include birding guides, cycling tours and food tours.
- Licences, reviews and safety matter.
- Respect wildlife and choose hygienic food vendors.
