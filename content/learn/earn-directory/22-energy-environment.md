# Energy, recycling and environmental work

Kenya is a leader in renewable energy, and waste management and climate projects are growing. These methods turn environmental needs into income, from collecting recyclables to installing solar and selling clean cookstoves. Some need technical training and licences; others start with very little capital.

## What these methods share

- **Safety:** electrical work, chemicals and waste handling require training and protective equipment.
- **Licences:** solar technicians (EPRA), waste handling (NEMA and county rules) and others.
- **Honest claims:** avoid exaggerated "carbon credit" or savings promises.
