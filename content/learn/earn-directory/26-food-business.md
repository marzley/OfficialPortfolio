# Food and drink businesses with online ordering

Everyone eats every day. Food businesses in Kenya range from roadside smokie vendors to cloud kitchens delivering through apps. Online ordering, WhatsApp menus and delivery riders have opened new models with lower start-up costs.

## What these methods share

- **Food safety and licences:** county public health licences, medical certificates for food handlers, and KEBS standards for packaged products.
- **Consistency:** same taste, same portion, every time.
- **Costing:** know your cost per plate or unit, including packaging and delivery.
- See [food and baked goods online](./?track=earn-directory&lesson=selling-online-ecommerce-methods) and [catering](./?track=earn-directory&lesson=events-hospitality-wellness-methods).

## 446. Cloud kitchen (delivery-only restaurant)

**What it is:** A kitchen that sells only through delivery apps and WhatsApp, without a dining area.
**How it works:** Menu → listings on delivery apps → orders → riders deliver.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Cooking, menu costing, operations |
| Cost & equipment | Kitchen, equipment, licences |
| Where to find work | Delivery apps, social media, offices |
| Payment | App payouts (minus commission), M-Pesa |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Test a small menu; price for app commissions.
**Pros:** Lower rent than restaurants. **Cons & risks:** App commissions; competition.
**Scam warning:** As usual: fake suppliers and fake "app listing agents".
**Kenya notes:** County food licences apply even without a dining area.
