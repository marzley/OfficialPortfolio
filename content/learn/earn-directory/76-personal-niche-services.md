# Personal and niche services

Small but real businesses that meet specific personal needs, completing the directory's first thousand methods.

## What these methods share

- **Low start-up:** many need little capital.
- **Location and reputation:** word of mouth drives most of them.
- **Legality:** follow county permits and consumer rules.

## 991. Shoe shining stand

**What it is:** Cleaning and polishing shoes for commuters and office workers.
**How it works:** Set up at a busy spot → shine shoes → sell polish and laces.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Shoe care |
| Cost & equipment | Polishes, brushes, stand, county permit |
| Where to find work | Office districts, bus stations, malls |
| Payment | Per pair |
| Realistic earnings | Low |
| Time to first income | Days |

**How to start:** Choose a busy, legal spot near offices.
**Pros:** Daily cash. **Cons & risks:** Low price per job.
**Scam warning:** Get a county permit for your spot.
**Kenya notes:** City centres have steady demand from office workers.

- Niche services include shoe shining, photo restoration, media digitisation, sharpening, mobile salons, perfume oils, ethical dog breeding, film fixing, sports equipment and floristry.
- Low start-up, location and reputation drive them.
- Avoid counterfeits and treat animals well.
