# Selling online and e-commerce

Selling online means using the internet to find customers for products, physical or digital. In Kenya, much of this happens on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, plus marketplaces like Jumia and Kilimall, with M-Pesa payments and boda boda or courier delivery.

## What these methods share

- **You need stock or products** (or a supplier relationship), a way to take payments, and reliable delivery.
- **Trust is everything:** real photos, clear prices, honest descriptions, reliable delivery and good customer service.
- **Business compliance:** register when you grow, keep records, follow KRA rules (including eTIMS invoicing requirements where they apply) and product regulations (standards, food and cosmetics rules).
- See [selling online earning](./?track=earn-online&lesson=selling-online-earning), [e-commerce](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=ecommerce) and [e-commerce and dropshipping in Kenya](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=ecommerce-dropshipping-kenya).

## 223. WhatsApp selling (status, catalogue, groups)

**What it is:** Selling products to your contacts and customers through WhatsApp status updates, the Business catalogue and broadcast lists.
**How it works:** Source products → post photos and prices → take orders → M-Pesa payment → delivery.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Photography, persuasive captions, customer service |
| Cost & equipment | Smartphone, stock or supplier access |
| Where to find work | Your contacts, groups (with permission), referrals |
| Payment | M-Pesa (till or paybill preferred for trust) |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium (depends on product margins and volume) |
| Time to first income | Days |

**How to start:** Start with products you understand and can source reliably; set up [WhatsApp Business](./?track=marketing&lesson=whatsapp-business).
**Pros:** Low start-up cost. **Cons & risks:** Limited reach; stock risk.
**Scam warning:** Fake suppliers who take payment and disappear; buy small first.
**Kenya notes:** A Lipa na M-Pesa till builds customer trust compared with a personal number.

## 228. Dropshipping (local)

**What it is:** Selling products that a local supplier stores and delivers, so you don't hold stock.
**How it works:** Agreement with supplier → you market → customer pays → supplier delivers → you keep margin.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Marketing, supplier management, customer service |
| Cost & equipment | Phone, marketing budget |
| Where to find work | Local wholesalers/suppliers willing to dropship |
| Payment | Customer pays you or on delivery; you pay supplier |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Test supplier reliability with your own orders first.
**Pros:** Low capital. **Cons & risks:** You depend on the supplier's quality and speed.
**Scam warning:** "Dropshipping courses" promising easy riches.
**Kenya notes:** Clear delivery timelines matter; many customers prefer pay-on-delivery.

## Summary

- Selling methods include WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram/TikTok and marketplace selling, own stores, local and international dropshipping, thrift fashion, handmade and digital products, print-on-demand, electronics, food, cosmetics, produce, group buying, second-hand goods, tickets, subscriptions, international marketplaces, reviews, licensed pharmacy and seller support.
- Trust (genuine products, reliable delivery, clear payment) is your biggest asset.
- Avoid counterfeits, unregistered products, stolen goods and unlicensed medicines; follow KRA and product rules.
