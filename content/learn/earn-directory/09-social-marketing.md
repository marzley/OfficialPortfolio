# Social media and digital marketing services

Most Kenyan businesses know they "should be online" but don't have the time or skills. Digital marketing services help them get found, attract customers and sell. These methods suit people who understand social media, writing, data and customer psychology.

## What these methods share

- **Results matter:** clients want enquiries and sales, not just likes. Learn to measure ([measuring results](./?track=marketing&lesson=measure-results-analytics)).
- **Honesty:** never promise guaranteed followers, viral posts or #1 rankings; never buy fake followers or reviews for clients.
- **Ad budgets:** client ad spend is separate from your fee; agree who pays platforms.
- See the [Digital marketing & SEO subject](./?track=marketing&lesson=seo-basics) and [marketing services overview](./?track=earn-online&lesson=marketing-services-earning).

## 136. Social media management

**What it is:** Planning, creating, posting and responding on a business's social media accounts.
**How it works:** Monthly content calendar → designs and captions → scheduling → replying to comments/messages → monthly report.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Content planning, basic design, writing, customer communication |
| Cost & equipment | Phone or laptop, Canva, scheduling tools (free tiers) |
| Where to find work | Local SMEs, restaurants, salons, schools, churches, freelance platforms |
| Payment | Monthly retainer, M-Pesa or bank |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium (several retainers) |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Manage one business's pages for a month at a discount; show results in a report ([social media management](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=social-media-management)).
**Pros:** Recurring income. **Cons & risks:** Clients expect quick growth; set realistic goals.
**Scam warning:** Never buy followers or engagement for clients; it damages accounts.
**Kenya notes:** Kenyan SMEs often prefer WhatsApp updates and simple monthly reports.

|---|---|
| Skills | Content creation, SEO or social media, trust-building |

| Where to find work | Affiliate programmes (local and international; check eligibility and payouts) |
| Payment | Commissions |
| Realistic earnings | Very low for most; can grow with traffic and trust |

**Kenya notes:** Promoting betting, forex "investment" or get-rich schemes harms your audience.

## Summary

- Digital marketing methods include social media and community management, Meta/Google/TikTok ads, local SEO and Google Business, email and WhatsApp marketing, influencer work, affiliate marketing, strategy, analytics, reputation, lead generation, funnels, consulting, channel management, activations, training, and specialist niches.
- Results and honest reporting build long-term clients; never buy fake engagement or reviews.
- Respect data protection, advertising and election laws.
