# Specialist crops and agritech services

Beyond general farming (methods 281–296), some high-value crops and agricultural technology services offer strong opportunities, especially for export and modern farming. They usually need good research, quality inputs and reliable markets.

## What these methods share

- **Research and extension support:** consult agricultural officers and research institutions.
- **Certified inputs:** seedlings and seed from certified sources (KEPHIS-regulated).
- **Markets and contracts:** export crops need buyers and quality standards.
- **Scams:** "guaranteed export market" seedling schemes and fake buyers.

## 633. Avocado farming (including export varieties)

**What it is:** Growing avocados (e.g. Hass) for local and export markets.
**How it works:** Certified seedlings → orchard management → harvest → buyers/exporters.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Orchard management |
| Cost & equipment | Land, certified seedlings, water |
| Where to find work | Exporters, local markets |
| Payment | Per kg |
| Realistic earnings | Medium (after trees mature) |
| Time to first income | Years (trees take time) |

**How to start:** Buy certified seedlings; join farmer groups that link to exporters.
**Pros:** Strong export demand. **Cons & risks:** Long wait; export quality rules.
**Scam warning:** Fake seedlings and "buy-back guarantee" schemes.
**Kenya notes:** Export registration and maturity rules apply.
