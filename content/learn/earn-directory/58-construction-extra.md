# Construction equipment and specialist building services

Construction keeps growing in Kenyan towns. Beyond contractors and fundis (methods 347–352), builders need equipment, specialist trades and inspections.

## What these methods share

- **Safety:** sites need protective gear and safe equipment.
- **Contracts:** written terms and deposits protect you.
- **Standards:** follow National Construction Authority (NCA) rules where they apply.

## 876. Scaffolding hire

**What it is:** Renting and erecting scaffolding for building and maintenance work.
**How it works:** Deliver → erect safely → dismantle → collect.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Scaffolding safety |
| Cost & equipment | Scaffold stock, transport |
| Where to find work | Contractors, painters, facility managers |
| Payment | Per day or week |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Start with a small stock; train crews in safe erection.
**Pros:** Repeat contractor clients. **Cons & risks:** Theft and damage.
**Scam warning:** Inspect before every job: unsafe scaffolds kill.
**Kenya notes:** Multi-storey building in towns keeps demand high.

## 877. Formwork and timber hire

**What it is:** Renting formwork, props and timber for concrete work.
**How it works:** Supply → collect after casting.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Construction knowledge |
| Cost & equipment | Formwork stock, transport |
| Where to find work | Contractors, self-builders |
| Payment | Per day or project |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Partner with contractors; keep stock in good condition.
**Pros:** Reusable assets. **Cons & risks:** Wear and loss.
**Scam warning:** Record items out and in.
**Kenya notes:** Common on storey buildings.

## 878. Steel fixing (rebar work)

**What it is:** Cutting, bending and tying reinforcement steel for concrete structures.
**How it works:** Read drawings → cut and bend → fix in place.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Reading structural drawings, steel fixing |
| Cost & equipment | Bending tools |
| Where to find work | Contractors, building sites |
| Payment | Daily rate or per tonne |
| Realistic earnings | Medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Train on site with experienced steel fixers.
**Pros:** Skilled, in-demand trade. **Cons & risks:** Physically demanding.
**Scam warning:** Follow engineers' drawings exactly.
**Kenya notes:** Every storey building needs steel fixers.

## 879. Construction equipment hire

**What it is:** Renting concrete mixers, compactors, vibrators and excavators.
**How it works:** Book → deliver with or without operator → collect.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Equipment maintenance, logistics |
| Cost & equipment | High: machines, transport |
| Where to find work | Contractors, self-builders, counties |
| Payment | Per day |
| Realistic earnings | Medium to high |
| Time to first income | Months |

**How to start:** Start with mixers and compactors; add bigger plant later.
**Pros:** High demand. **Cons & risks:** Maintenance and breakdowns.
**Scam warning:** Use contracts covering damage.
**Kenya notes:** Different from quarry equipment hire (method 730): this serves building sites.

## 880. Ready-mix concrete and pumping

**What it is:** Supplying mixed concrete and pumping it into slabs and columns.
**How it works:** Batch → deliver → pump.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Concrete technology, logistics |
| Cost & equipment | Very high: batching plant, trucks, pumps |
| Where to find work | Contractors, developers |
| Payment | Per cubic metre |
| Realistic earnings | High |
| Time to first income | Years |

**How to start:** Usually starts as a pump-hire service before batching.
**Pros:** Large contracts. **Cons & risks:** Very capital intensive.
**Scam warning:** Concrete must meet specified strengths: test cubes.
**Kenya notes:** Large projects in towns prefer ready-mix.

## 881. Building inspection and snag lists

**What it is:** Inspecting new or renovated buildings and listing defects before handover or purchase.
**How it works:** Inspect → photograph → report defects.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Construction knowledge, report writing |
| Cost & equipment | Low: tools, camera |
| Where to find work | Home buyers, landlords, diaspora |
| Payment | Per inspection |
| Realistic earnings | Low to medium |
| Time to first income | Weeks |

**How to start:** Use a checklist; partner with property agents.
**Pros:** Protects buyers. **Cons & risks:** Liability for missed defects.
**Scam warning:** Disclose any links to the builder.
**Kenya notes:** Buyers of off-plan apartments increasingly hire inspectors.

## 882. Demolition and salvage

**What it is:** Safely demolishing structures and selling salvaged materials.
**How it works:** Survey → permits → demolish → salvage and clear.

| | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Demolition safety |
| Cost & equipment | Tools, transport, permits |
| Where to find work | Developers, homeowners, counties |
| Payment | Per job plus salvage sales |

**Kenya notes:** Redevelopment in older estates creates demolition work.

|---|---|

**How to start:** Partner with pool maintenance businesses (method 763).
**Pros:** Big-ticket projects. **Cons & risks:** Technical risks.
**Scam warning:** Get staged payments in writing.
**Kenya notes:** Hotels and villas at the coast and in towns build pools.

## Summary

- Construction services include scaffolding, formwork, steel fixing, equipment hire, ready-mix concrete, inspections, demolition, prefab units and pools.
- Safety, NCA standards and written contracts protect you.
- Asbestos and unsafe scaffolding are serious dangers.
