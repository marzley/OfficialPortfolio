# General freelance marketplaces: Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer, PeoplePerHour, Guru, Contra and more

Open marketplaces accept almost any skill. They're where most Kenyan freelancers get their first reviews, and competition is high, so a sharp profile and a clear niche matter.

:::warning Check before you rely on it
Fees, accepted countries and payout options change often. Confirm on each platform's official website.
:::

## 1. Upwork

**What it is:** The largest freelance marketplace, with hourly and fixed-price contracts from clients worldwide.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Writing, design, development, virtual assistance, marketing, data, admin and more |
| Fees for you | A service fee on each payment (Upwork has moved to a variable fee: check the current rate) and paid “Connects” to apply for jobs |
| Getting paid | M-Pesa (directly in Kenya), Payoneer, bank or other methods in your account |
| Kenya | Open to Kenyan freelancers; profile approval isn't guaranteed |
| Competition | High |
| Best for | Most skills, especially longer contracts |

**How to start:** Choose one niche, write a focused profile, add 2–3 portfolio pieces, and send short, tailored proposals. Full guide: [Upwork step by step](./?track=freelancing&lesson=upwork-step-by-step).
**Pros:** Biggest client base; payment protection on contracts. **Cons:** Connects cost money; tough first jobs; profile approval can be slow.
**Watch out:** Anyone selling “approved Upwork accounts” or asking you to work under their account is breaking the rules.

## 2. Fiverr

**What it is:** A marketplace where you list fixed-price services (“gigs”) and buyers come to you.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Design, writing, video editing, voice-over, programming, marketing, AI services |
| Fees for you | 20% from each order |
| Getting paid | Payoneer, PayPal or bank (options vary) |
| Kenya | Open to Kenya |
| Competition | High in popular categories |
| Best for | Clear, packaged services (e.g. “a logo in 3 days”) |

**How to start:** Create 2–3 specific gigs with strong thumbnails and clear packages; reply fast. Full guide: [Fiverr step by step](./?track=freelancing&lesson=fiverr-step-by-step).
**Pros:** Buyers find you; good for repeatable services. **Cons:** 20% fee; ranking takes time.
**Watch out:** Never deliver outside Fiverr or accept payment off the platform.

## 3. Freelancer.com

**What it is:** A large bidding marketplace and contest site.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Development, design, writing, data entry, engineering |
| Fees for you | A fee per project (a percentage or a minimum amount) and paid membership options |
| Getting paid | PayPal, Payoneer, bank or other methods |
| Kenya | Open to Kenya |
| Competition | Very high, with many low-budget jobs |
| Best for | People willing to bid a lot and enter contests |

**How to start:** Bid only on jobs you can do well; verify your account; build a few reviews.
**Pros:** Huge job volume. **Cons:** Price competition; some low-quality clients.
**Watch out:** Clients asking you to pay a “registration” or “security deposit” are scammers.

## 4. PeoplePerHour

**What it is:** A UK-based marketplace with projects and fixed-price “offers”.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Design, web development, writing, marketing |
| Fees for you | A service fee on earnings (tiers vary) and proposal credits |
| Getting paid | PayPal, Payoneer or bank |
| Kenya | Open to freelancers worldwide including Kenya |
| Competition | Medium to high |
| Best for | Designers, developers and writers wanting UK and European clients |

**How to start:** Get your profile approved with a strong portfolio; post “offers” as well as bidding.
**Pros:** Good-quality clients. **Cons:** Profile approval can be strict.
**Watch out:** Keep all communication on the platform.

## 5. Guru

**What it is:** A long-running marketplace with flexible payment options and workrooms.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Programming, design, writing, admin, engineering |
| Fees for you | A service fee that depends on your membership level |
| Getting paid | PayPal, Payoneer or bank |
| Kenya | Open to Kenya |
| Competition | Medium |
| Best for | Freelancers who want a less crowded marketplace |

**How to start:** Complete your profile, add portfolio samples, and use the free bids carefully.
**Pros:** Less crowded than the biggest platforms. **Cons:** Fewer jobs.
**Watch out:** Use SafePay (the platform's escrow) for every job.

## 6. Contra

**What it is:** A commission-free platform where freelancers build portfolio-style profiles and sell services.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Design, product, development, writing, marketing |
| Fees for you | Free for freelancers (clients pay fees); paid upgrades optional |
| Getting paid | Payment methods offered in your account |
| Kenya | Open to many countries; check payout options for Kenya |
| Competition | Medium |
| Best for | Designers and creatives with strong portfolios |

**How to start:** Build a beautiful portfolio profile and share it on LinkedIn and social media.
**Pros:** Keep what you earn; great portfolio pages. **Cons:** You bring much of your own traffic.
**Watch out:** Check payout methods work for you before taking a project.

## 7. Truelancer

**What it is:** A freelance marketplace popular in Asia and Africa.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Web development, design, writing, data entry |
| Fees for you | A fee per project and membership plans |
| Getting paid | PayPal or bank |
| Kenya | Open to Kenya |
| Competition | High, budget-focused |
| Best for | Beginners building first reviews |

**How to start:** Bid on small projects you can deliver perfectly.
**Pros:** Easy to start. **Cons:** Low budgets.
**Watch out:** Avoid clients wanting work before funding the milestone.

## 8. Hubstaff Talent

**What it is:** A free directory where remote companies find freelancers.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Development, design, marketing, support, admin |
| Fees for you | Free for freelancers and companies |
| Getting paid | Paid by the client directly (agree how) |
| Kenya | Open to freelancers worldwide |
| Competition | Medium |
| Best for | Freelancers who want direct clients without fees |

**How to start:** Create a detailed profile; apply to listed jobs.
**Pros:** No fees. **Cons:** No payment protection.
**Watch out:** Use written agreements and milestone payments for direct clients.

## Summary

- Upwork and Fiverr are the biggest and work for Kenyans; Freelancer, PeoplePerHour, Guru, Contra, Truelancer and Hubstaff Talent are alternatives.
- Each charges differently: service fees, bid credits or memberships (Contra and Hubstaff Talent are free for freelancers).
- Keep payments on the platform and never pay a client.
