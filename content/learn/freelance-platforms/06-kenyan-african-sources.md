# Kenyan and African sources of work: Ajira Digital, Jitume, BrighterMonday, Fuzu and MyJobMag

Local sources connect you with Kenyan businesses, NGOs and remote roles open to Kenyans, plus free training. Local clients pay in shillings, often via M-Pesa, and are a great place to start.

:::warning Check before you rely on it
Fees, accepted countries and payout options change often. Confirm on each platform's official website.
:::

## 33. Ajira Digital

**What it is:** A government programme that trains young Kenyans for online and digital work and connects them to opportunities.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Training, mentorship, links to online work and Ajira centres |
| Fees for you | Free |
| Getting paid | Not a payment platform: you're paid by clients or platforms |
| Kenya | Kenya-wide, with physical centres |
| Competition | N/A |
| Best for | Beginners who want training and community |

**How to start:** Register on the official site, join a centre near you, and follow the training paths. More: [Ajira Digital and Jitume](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=ajira-jitume-programmes).
**Pros:** Free training and community. **Cons:** You still need to find clients.
**Watch out:** Ajira never charges for registration: fake “Ajira jobs” asking for money are scams.

## 34. Jitume Digital Labs

**What it is:** A government initiative providing digital labs and training in institutions across Kenya.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Digital skills training, access to computers and internet for online work |
| Fees for you | Free access through participating institutions |
| Getting paid | Not a payment platform |
| Kenya | Kenya-wide in participating institutions |
| Competition | N/A |
| Best for | Students and youth needing computers and internet |

**How to start:** Find a Jitume lab near you (often in TVETs and universities).
**Pros:** Free computers and internet. **Cons:** Limited hours and places.
**Watch out:** Use official channels only.

## 35. BrighterMonday

**What it is:** A major Kenyan job site.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Full-time, part-time and contract jobs, including remote roles |
| Fees for you | Free for job seekers |
| Getting paid | Paid by the employer |
| Kenya | Kenya |
| Competition | High |
| Best for | Kenyan job seekers |

**How to start:** Create a strong profile and set alerts for remote or contract roles.
**Pros:** Many Kenyan employers. **Cons:** Mostly full-time office jobs.
**Watch out:** Real employers don't charge interview or medical fees upfront.

## 36. Fuzu

**What it is:** A Kenyan and East African career platform with jobs and learning.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Jobs, internships and courses |
| Fees for you | Free basic; paid extras |
| Getting paid | Paid by the employer |
| Kenya | Kenya and East Africa |
| Competition | High |
| Best for | Graduates and early-career workers |

**How to start:** Complete your profile and skill tests.
**Pros:** Career resources. **Cons:** Competitive.
**Watch out:** Same: no upfront fees to employers.

## 37. MyJobMag Kenya

**What it is:** A job listings site covering Kenya.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Jobs, internships, NGO roles |
| Fees for you | Free |
| Getting paid | Paid by the employer |
| Kenya | Kenya |
| Competition | High |
| Best for | Job seekers |

**How to start:** Subscribe to alerts in your field.
**Pros:** Many listings. **Cons:** Mostly local roles.
**Watch out:** Verify the employer's details before applying.

## Summary

- Ajira Digital and Jitume offer free training, community and access; BrighterMonday, Fuzu and MyJobMag list Kenyan jobs and contracts.
- Local clients pay in shillings, often via M-Pesa: great for first reviews and income.
- Real government programmes and employers never charge registration or interview fees.
