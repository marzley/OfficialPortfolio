# Remote job boards: LinkedIn, We Work Remotely, Remote OK, Remotive, Wellfound and more

Remote job boards list contracts and full-time jobs with companies. There are no platform fees for you, but also no payment protection, so check every company carefully. Filter for “worldwide” or roles open to Africa.

:::warning Check before you rely on it
Fees, accepted countries and payout options change often. Confirm on each platform's official website.
:::

## 25. LinkedIn

**What it is:** The world's professional network, with jobs, freelance services and direct client contact.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Every professional field |
| Fees for you | Free; Premium optional |
| Getting paid | Paid by employers or clients directly |
| Kenya | Fully available in Kenya |
| Competition | High |
| Best for | Everyone: it's also your public CV |

**How to start:** Complete your profile, post about your work weekly, and use “Open to work” and service pages.
**Pros:** Direct access to decision-makers. **Cons:** Takes consistent effort.
**Watch out:** Fake recruiters ask for fees or move you to Telegram: real ones don't.

## 26. We Work Remotely

**What it is:** One of the largest remote job boards.

| | |
|---|---|
| Work | Programming, design, support, marketing, sales |
| Fees for you | Free for job seekers |
| Getting paid | Paid by the employer |
| Kenya | Filter for roles open worldwide |
| Competition | High |
| Best for | Experienced remote workers |

**How to start:** Apply to roles marked anywhere in the world; tailor each application.
**Pros:** Quality companies. **Cons:** Many roles limited to certain countries.
**Watch out:** Check the company's website and LinkedIn before sharing documents.

## Summary

- LinkedIn is essential for everyone; We Work Remotely, Remote OK, Remotive, Working Nomads, Wellfound, Himalayas and JustRemote list remote roles.
- Filter for roles open worldwide or to Africa.
- No payment protection: verify companies, and never pay to apply or buy equipment upfront.
