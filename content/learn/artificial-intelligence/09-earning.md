# Level 3: Earning with AI skills

AI has created new kinds of paid work, and it makes existing freelance work faster. Here are realistic ways to earn with AI skills from Kenya, from beginner to advanced.

## 1. AI training and data annotation (beginner to expert)

Companies pay people to rate, correct and write examples for AI models, and to label data. Specialists (coders, maths graduates, doctors, lawyers, speakers of African languages) earn more. Full details and platforms: [AI training & data annotation](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=ai-training-data-annotation).

## 2. AI-assisted freelance services

Use AI to deliver normal services **faster and better**, not to deliver AI junk:

| Service | How AI helps | Where to sell |
|---|---|---|
| Content writing | Research, outlines, first drafts (you edit and fact-check) | Upwork, Fiverr, local businesses |
| Social media management | Content calendars, captions, ideas | Local businesses (see Make Money Online) |
| Graphic design | Canva AI, background removal, image generation for concepts | Fiverr, local clients |
| Video editing | Auto-captions, cutting silences, B-roll ideas | Creators, churches, businesses |
| Transcription | AI first pass, human correction | GoTranscript, direct clients |
| Translation (English ↔ Kiswahili) | Draft translation, human polish | NGOs, businesses |
| Virtual assistance | Email drafts, scheduling, research | Upwork, LinkedIn |

Many clients now **ask whether you use AI**. Be honest: "I use AI tools to work faster, and I check and edit everything myself."

## 3. AI services for local businesses

Most Kenyan small businesses know AI exists but not how to use it. You can sell:

- **AI setup and training**: teach a shop owner and staff to use ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini for marketing and customer replies (half-day workshop).
- **Content packages**: monthly social media posts produced with AI and edited by you.
- **Prompt and template libraries**: a business-specific set of prompts (for example, for a real-estate agency or a school).
- **Simple automations**: forms → spreadsheet → AI summary → email (see the next lesson).
- **WhatsApp catalogue and auto-replies**, upgraded later to an AI assistant.

## 4. Prompt and automation specialist (intermediate)

Businesses need people who can design reliable AI workflows: writing system prompts, testing them, connecting tools with Zapier/Make/n8n, and documenting how they work. Learn the automation lesson, then build 2 to 3 example workflows for your portfolio.

## 5. Building AI-powered apps (advanced)

Developers who can connect AI models to websites, apps and business systems are in high demand: chatbots trained on a company's documents, AI document processing, AI features in school or hospital systems. Levels 4 and 5 of this course start you on that path; combine with the **Python**, **JavaScript**, **PHP** and **Flutter** subjects.

## 6. Teaching AI

Run AI literacy classes for teachers, SACCO members, church groups and youth programmes (such as Jitume and Ajira centres). Prepare simple, practical sessions using Levels 1 and 2 of this course.

## Pricing ideas (Kenya)

| Offer | Starting idea |
|---|---|
| Half-day AI workshop for a small team | Price per session, plus a printed prompt guide |
| Monthly AI-assisted social media package | Per month, by number of posts and platforms |
| Simple automation setup | Fixed price per workflow + monthly support |
| Custom AI chatbot on a website | Project price + monthly running costs (AI usage, hosting) |

Prices depend on your skill and the client's size: start modestly, collect results and testimonials, then raise prices.

## Ethics that protect your income

- **Don't sell AI output as purely human work** when a client asked for that.
- **Check facts** before delivering: one hallucinated statistic can lose a client.
- **Respect copyright**: don't feed clients' confidential documents into tools without permission; don't copy other creators' work.
- **Avoid contract cheating** (writing students' assignments with AI). It's unethical and platforms ban it.

## Earning with AI skills: realistic expectations

AI has created new kinds of work (data annotation, AI training, prompt and automation services) and changed old ones (writing, design, customer support, transcription). Many people earn by using AI to deliver services faster and better, not by expecting AI to "make money automatically". Be wary of courses and schemes promising easy passive income from AI; sustainable income comes from real skills, honest work and satisfied clients.

## Building a portfolio quickly (honestly)

1. Create sample work for imaginary or real local businesses (with permission), clearly labelled as samples.
2. Offer a few discounted projects to get testimonials.
3. Document your process: brief → AI-assisted draft → your editing → final result.
4. Publish samples on LinkedIn, a simple website, Behance or a Google Drive portfolio.

## Freelance platforms and AI rules

- Read each platform's rules on AI use; some clients forbid AI-generated content, others welcome AI-assisted work.
- Never misrepresent AI output as purely human work when the client asks for human-only work.
- Data annotation and AI training platforms often require tests and have strict quality guidelines; follow them carefully.
- Protect accounts: never share your login, never "rent" accounts, and beware of agents who demand fees to get you onto platforms.

## Pricing your AI-assisted services

- Price by **value and outcome**, not by how long AI took. Your skill in prompting, editing, checking and understanding the client's business is what they pay for.
- Include revision rounds and clear deliverables.
- Charge separately for tool subscriptions the project needs, if significant.
- Raise prices as your portfolio and results grow.

## Quality control: what separates professionals

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| Fact-check every claim | Clients lose trust after one false statistic |
| Edit for voice and local relevance | Generic AI text doesn't convert |
| Check originality | Avoid content too similar to existing work |
| Proofread names, prices and contacts | Errors cost clients money |
| Keep client data private | Required by clients and the law |

## Avoiding scams in the "AI money" space

- "Earn KSh 10,000 a day with AI, pay for the secret course" → be very sceptical.
- "Pay a registration fee to access AI training jobs" → genuine platforms don't charge to apply.
- "AI trading bot with guaranteed profits" → a classic scam pattern.
- Requests to buy or rent verified freelancer accounts → violates platform rules and risks bans and fraud.

:::think A client hired you to write 10 blog posts and later discovers they were copied straight from an AI tool, with two invented statistics. What went wrong, and how should AI-assisted writing be done?
The writer delivered unedited AI output without fact-checking or adding value, which damages the client's credibility and the writer's reputation. Professional AI-assisted writing means researching, using AI for outlines or drafts, verifying every fact, adding expertise and local relevance, editing for the brand voice, and being honest with the client about how AI is used.
:::
