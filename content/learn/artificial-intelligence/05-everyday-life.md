# Level 2: AI for everyday life: study, jobs, money and health questions

AI becomes really useful when you use it for **your own problems**. This lesson gives ready-to-use prompts for everyday situations in Kenya, with the safety notes that matter.

## Studying and learning

| Need | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Understand a topic | *"Explain [topic] simply, then give a real-life Kenyan example, then 3 questions to test me."* |
| Revise for exams | *"Quiz me on [topic], one question at a time. Wait for my answer, mark it, explain mistakes."* |
| Make notes | *"Turn these notes into a one-page summary with headings and key terms: [paste]."* |
| Learn a language | *"Let's practise Kiswahili/French conversation about shopping at a market. Correct my mistakes gently."* |
| Plan your time | *"I have exams in 3 weeks: Maths, Biology, English. I can study 3 hours a day. Make a revision timetable."* |

**Honest use:** use AI to *learn*, not to hand in work that isn't yours. Most schools and colleges treat AI-written assignments as cheating. Ask it to explain, quiz and give feedback instead. (See the AI tools subject's lesson on studying the honest way.)

## Finding a job

- *"Here's a job advert and my CV. List the skills the employer wants that my CV doesn't show yet, and suggest honest ways to show them."*
- *"Write a cover letter for this job using my real experience: [paste]. Under 250 words."*
- *"Ask me 8 interview questions for a [role] one by one, and score my answers."*
- *"Rewrite my LinkedIn 'About' section to sound professional but warm."*

Never let AI invent qualifications or experience. Employers check, and it can cost you the job.

## Money and small business

- *"Make a monthly budget for KSh 25,000 income: rent 8,000, food, transport, school fees for 1 child, savings. Show it as a table."*
- *"Explain the difference between a SACCO loan, a bank loan and a mobile loan, with the risks of each."*
- *"I sell second-hand clothes (mitumba) in Gikomba. Give me 10 ideas to get more customers on WhatsApp and TikTok."*
- *"Calculate my profit: I buy a bale for KSh 18,000, it has about 200 pieces, I sell at KSh 200 each, and transport is KSh 1,500."*

**Check the maths** with a calculator for important decisions, and confirm rules (taxes, loans) with official sources.

## Farming

- *"My maize leaves have yellow streaks and holes. What are the possible causes and what should I check?"* (Send a photo for better help.)
- *"Make a planting and spraying calendar for tomatoes in a greenhouse in Kiambu."*
- *"Write a short WhatsApp message to sell 20 trays of eggs to local hotels."*

Always confirm diagnoses and chemical use with an **agricultural extension officer** or agrovet. Wrong chemicals waste money and can be dangerous.

## Health questions (carefully)

AI can help you **understand** health information and **prepare questions** for a doctor:

- *"Explain in simple words what 'high blood pressure' means and what questions I should ask my doctor."*
- *"What do these terms on my lab results mean? [paste]"*

But **never use AI instead of a doctor**, especially for emergencies, children, pregnancy, medicines and doses. If it's urgent, go to a hospital or call emergency services.

## Letters, forms and government services

- *"Write a polite letter to my county office asking about the status of my bursary application."*
- *"Explain step by step how to apply for a KRA PIN on iTax."* (Then follow the official website, which may have changed.)
- *"Help me fill in this form: what should I write in 'purpose of the loan'?"*

## Your weekly AI habit

Pick **three** areas from this lesson and use AI in each this week. Notice where it saves time and where you had to correct it. That judgement is exactly what makes you good at AI.
