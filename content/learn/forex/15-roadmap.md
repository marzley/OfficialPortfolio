# From zero to advanced: the complete forex learning checklist and milestones

This final lesson pulls the whole subject together into a **point-by-point checklist**. Use it to track progress, decide what to study next, and judge honestly whether you're ready for each step. It also explains what "pro" really means and what to do if forex turns out not to be for you.

## What "pro" really means

A professional trader isn't someone with a big win or a fancy lifestyle. It's someone who, over **years**:
- follows a written, tested plan consistently;
- controls risk on every trade;
- keeps detailed records and reviews them;
- survives drawdowns without blowing up;
- produces results that justify the risk compared with simpler alternatives (like index funds, T-bills or money market funds).

Many people study seriously and still find they can't beat the market consistently after costs. That's a valid outcome; the knowledge still helps with personal finance, business and avoiding scams.

## Level 1: Foundations checklist

Tick each when you can do it **without notes**:

1. Explain base and quote currency, long and short, bid, ask and spread.
2. Name the seven major pairs and when the London and New York sessions open in Kenyan time.
3. Calculate pips on normal and JPY pairs.
4. Calculate profit/loss for any lot size on XXX/USD pairs.
5. Explain leverage, margin, equity, free margin, margin call and stop out.
6. Check a broker's licence on the CMA website and explain what segregated funds and negative balance protection are.
7. Place, modify and close market, limit and stop orders on a demo platform, with stop loss and take profit.

## Level 2: Analysis checklist

1. Read an economic calendar in Nairobi time and identify high-impact events for your pairs.
2. Explain hawkish vs dovish and why surprises vs forecasts move markets.
3. Read candlesticks and explain what wicks and bodies show.
4. Draw key support and resistance zones on the daily chart.
5. Identify trend structure (HH/HL, LH/LL) and ranges.
6. Use multiple timeframes: direction, setup, entry.
7. Explain what moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands and ATR calculate, and when they mislead.

Lessons: [fundamentals](./?track=forex&lesson=fundamental-analysis), [charts and candlesticks](./?track=forex&lesson=charts-candlesticks), [support, resistance and trends](./?track=forex&lesson=support-resistance-trends), [indicators](./?track=forex&lesson=forex-indicators).

## Level 3: Risk and planning checklist (the gate to real money)

1. Calculate position size from account, risk % and stop distance in under a minute.
2. Explain why 0.5–1% risk per trade, the recovery maths and correlation risk.
3. Calculate expectancy and break-even win rates for different reward-to-risk ratios.
4. Have a **written trading plan** with specific rules and no-trade rules.
5. Complete **100+ backtested trades** with positive expectancy after costs.
6. Complete **50+ demo trades** over 2+ months, following the plan in at least 90% of them.
7. Keep a complete journal and know your win rate, average win/loss, profit factor, maximum drawdown and longest losing streak.

Lessons: [risk management](./?track=forex&lesson=risk-management-position-sizing), [trading plan](./?track=forex&lesson=trading-plan-strategies), [backtesting and journaling](./?track=forex&lesson=backtesting-journaling).

## Level 4: Mind and safety checklist

1. Name your three most common rule-breaks and the emotion behind each.
2. Have hard limits (daily loss, max trades, cooling-off after losses) and follow them.
3. Recognise the warning signs of gambling and know what you'd do.
4. Identify the main scam types instantly (account managers, fake brokers, guaranteed returns, recovery scams).
5. Understand the real risks of copy trading, robots and prop-firm challenges.

Lessons: [psychology](./?track=forex&lesson=trading-psychology), [scams and prop firms](./?track=forex&lesson=forex-scams-prop-firms).

## Level 5: Small live trading (only if Levels 1–4 are complete)

Conditions before your first live trade:
- An emergency fund covering 3–6 months of expenses, and no high-interest debt.
- Live capital is money you could lose completely without affecting your life.
- Smallest possible position sizes (micro or cent account).

Milestones:
1. **First 50 live trades:** goal is plan adherence, not profit. Compare live statistics with demo.
2. **Next 100 trades:** if adherence ≥ 90% and expectancy is still positive, consider a small increase in size.
3. **Every quarter:** full review. If results are consistently negative after costs, return to demo and re-examine the plan, or decide to stop.

## Level 6: Advanced topics (for later)

Once the foundations are solid, deeper areas you may study:
- Market structure and order flow concepts (liquidity, sessions, institutional behaviour), studied critically.
- Statistics for trading: sample size, confidence, Monte Carlo simulation of drawdowns.
- Algorithmic trading and automated backtesting with Python ([Python course](./?track=python&lesson=introduction)), being careful about overfitting.
- Portfolio thinking: correlation, diversification across strategies, risk budgeting.
- Macro economics in depth: central bank policy, bond markets, capital flows.
- Other markets: see [trading and investing options explained](./?track=trading-markets&lesson=trading-markets-start-here).

| Month | Focus | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lessons 1–5, demo platform practice | Foundations checklist done |
| 2–3 | Lessons 6–9; daily chart markup practice | Analysis checklist done |
| 4 | Lessons 10–11; write the plan | Written plan |

| 7–8 | Demo forward test 50+ trades; lessons 13–14 | Mind and safety checklist done |

## If you decide forex isn't for you

That's a smart, honest decision for many people. Alternatives that use what you've learned:
- **Long-term investing:** money market funds, T-bills and bonds, SACCO savings, diversified funds ([lower-risk options](./?track=trading-markets&lesson=bonds-tbills-mmfs)).
- **Skills-based income:** [freelancing](./?track=freelancing&lesson=freelancing-roadmap), [ways to earn](./?track=earn-online&lesson=start-here), or a career in finance, data or tech ([career roadmaps](./?track=career-roadmaps&lesson=choose-a-tech-career)).
- **Finance careers:** knowledge of markets, Excel and data analysis is useful in banks, SACCOs, insurance and fintech ([data analyst roadmap](./?track=career-roadmaps&lesson=data-analyst-roadmap)).

## Summary

- "Pro" means consistent process, risk control and records over years, not big wins.
- Work through the checklists level by level; Level 3 is the gate to real money.
- Start live only with money you can lose, tiny sizes, and written milestones.
- Deciding not to trade is a valid, often wise, outcome; the knowledge remains valuable.
