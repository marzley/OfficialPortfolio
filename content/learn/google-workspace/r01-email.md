# Professional email with Gmail and Outlook: setup, writing, organising, security and etiquette

Despite WhatsApp and social media, email remains the official channel for job applications, client work, institutions, banks, government notifications, invoices and contracts. People judge your professionalism by your emails: the address, subject line, tone and attachments. This unit teaches professional email from setup to advanced organisation in **Gmail** (with Outlook equivalents), plus security and etiquette.

:::note What you will learn
- Choosing a professional address (personal vs business domain)
- Gmail setup: signature, profile photo, 2-Step Verification, recovery options
- Writing effective emails: structure, subject lines, tone, examples
- Replying, forwarding, CC/BCC, attachments and Drive links
- Organising: labels, filters, search, archive, stars, snooze
- Templates, scheduling, undo send and vacation replies
- Email on your phone
- Security: phishing, attachments, spam
- Etiquette and common mistakes
:::

## Choosing your address

| Type | Example | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Free personal | `wanjiku.kamau@gmail.com`, `@outlook.com` | Job seekers, students, freelancers starting out |
| Business domain | `info@mamanjerisalon.co.ke`, `wanjiku@kamaudesigns.co.ke` | Businesses: looks established and trustworthy |

Business email options: email included with web hosting (cPanel), **Google Workspace** (Gmail with your domain, paid), **Microsoft 365**, **Zoho Mail** (has a free tier for small teams). Use role addresses (`info@`, `sales@`, `support@`) plus personal ones for staff.

Avoid nicknames, numbers that look like birth years, and anything you wouldn't put on a CV.

## Setting up Gmail properly

1. **Profile photo:** a clear, professional photo or business logo (Google Account → Personal info).
2. **Signature:** Settings (gear icon) → See all settings → General → **Signature** → create:

```
Wanjiku Kamau
Graphic Designer | Kamau Designs
0712 345 678 · kamaudesigns.co.ke
```

Keep it short: name, role, phone, website. Avoid long quotes and big images.

3. **Security:** myaccount.google.com → Security → **2-Step Verification** on; add a recovery phone and email; review devices signed in.
4. **Display name:** your real name (Settings → Accounts → Send mail as → edit info).
5. **Inbox type:** Default (tabs: Primary, Promotions, Social) or Priority Inbox.

**Outlook:** File/Settings → Signatures; Account security via account.microsoft.com.

## Writing effective emails

### Structure

1. **Subject:** specific and scannable.
2. **Greeting:** "Dear Mr. Otieno," (formal), "Hi Achieng," (friendly professional).
3. **Opening line:** the purpose.
4. **Body:** short paragraphs; key details (dates, amounts, references); bullet points for several items.
5. **Action/request:** what you need and by when.
6. **Closing:** "Kind regards," / "Best regards," / "Thank you," + name and signature.

### Subject lines

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Hello" | "Quotation request: 5-page website for Juma Hardware" |
| "Documents" | "Signed contract and ID copy for Plot 45 sale" |
| "Urgent!!!" | "Action needed by Friday: confirm delivery address for Order #1042" |
| (empty) | "Application: Accounts Assistant (Ref AA/2026/04)" |

### Example: client follow-up

```
Subject: Website draft ready for review: Mama Njeri Salon

Hi Njeri,

The first draft of your website is ready for review:
https://preview.example.co.ke/mamanjeri

Please check:
- Service list and prices (Prices page)
- Opening hours and location (Contact page)
- The photos you'd like to replace

Kindly send your feedback by Thursday 9 October so we can launch on
Monday 13 October as planned.

Best regards,
Kelvin
```

### Tone

- **Polite and direct:** "Could you please send..." rather than "Send...".
- **Positive framing:** "We can deliver by Friday if we receive the logo by Tuesday" instead of "We can't deliver because you haven't sent the logo".
- **Avoid** ALL CAPS, too many exclamation marks, slang and excessive emojis in formal emails.
- **Read it aloud** before sending; check names and attachments.

## Replying, forwarding, CC and BCC

| Action | Use |
|---|---|
| **Reply** | Respond to the sender only |
| **Reply all** | Respond to everyone on the thread (only when all need your reply) |
| **Forward** | Send the email to someone new (add a note explaining why) |
| **CC** | Keep someone informed (visible to all) |
| **BCC** | Hidden recipients: use for sending to many people who don't know each other (protects their addresses) |

Keep the subject line when replying (it keeps the thread together); change it only if the topic changes.

## Attachments and links

- Name files clearly: `Invoice-1042-Mama-Njeri.pdf`, not `scan0001.pdf`.
- Prefer **PDF** for final documents.
- Gmail attachments are limited to about **25 MB**; bigger files are shared as **Google Drive links** automatically. Set link permissions correctly (Viewer/Commenter/Editor; restricted to named people for private documents).
- Mention attachments in the email ("Please find attached...") and check they're actually attached before sending (Gmail warns if you mention one and forget).
- Scan documents with your phone (Google Drive app → Scan, or other scanner apps) for clean PDFs.

## Organising your inbox

| Tool | How (Gmail) | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Archive** | Archive icon or `E` (keyboard shortcuts on) | Removes from inbox, keeps it searchable: "inbox zero" approach |
| **Labels** | Label icon → create labels (Jobs, Clients/Juma, Invoices) | Like folders, but an email can have several |
| **Filters** | Search options (the sliders icon) → Create filter | Automatically label, archive, star or forward emails from certain senders/keywords |
| **Star / Important** | Star icon | Emails needing action |
| **Snooze** | Clock icon | Hide an email until a chosen time |
| **Search** | Operators: `from:`, `to:`, `subject:`, `has:attachment`, `filename:pdf`, `older_than:1y`, `larger:10M` | Find anything quickly |
| **Unsubscribe** | Link near the sender name | Cut newsletter clutter |

Example filter: emails from `@kra.go.ke` → label "Tax" and star them.

**Outlook:** folders, rules (like filters), Focused Inbox, flags and categories.

## Productivity features

- **Templates** (Gmail: Settings → Advanced → Templates → enable; then save frequent replies): pricing replies, application acknowledgements.
- **Schedule send** (arrow next to Send): send at 8:00 a.m. instead of midnight.
- **Undo send** (Settings → General → Undo Send period up to 30 seconds).
- **Vacation responder** (Settings → General): automatic replies when you're away.
- **Confidential mode** (lock icon): expiry dates and no forwarding (recipients can still take screenshots).
- **Keyboard shortcuts** (enable in Settings): `C` compose, `R` reply, `A` reply all, `F` forward, `E` archive, `/` search.

## Email on your phone

- Use the official **Gmail** or **Outlook** apps; secure the phone with a lock screen.
- Turn notifications on only for important mail (Primary tab, or VIP senders) to avoid distraction.
- Be extra careful with links on small screens: press and hold to preview the real address.

## Security

### Phishing

Fake emails imitate banks, KRA, eCitizen, employers, Google or delivery companies to steal passwords or money. Signs: urgency, threats, unexpected attachments, requests for passwords/PINs/OTPs, mismatched sender addresses and suspicious links (see the scams and phishing lessons).

Gmail helps: warnings in yellow/red banners, spam filtering, and "Report phishing" (three dots menu). Never ignore warnings.

### Attachments

- Don't open unexpected `.zip`, `.exe`, `.iso`, `.js` or Office files asking to "enable macros".
- Open documents in preview (Gmail's built-in viewer) when unsure.
- Keep antivirus and Windows updated.

### Business email compromise

Criminals hack or spoof a supplier's email and send "updated bank details" for invoices. **Always confirm payment detail changes by phone** using a known number, not one in the email.

:::think Your client receives an email that appears to be from you saying "Our M-Pesa paybill has changed, please pay the KSh 85,000 invoice to this new paybill." You didn't send it. What happened, and what should both of you do?
Your email account may be compromised, or someone spoofed your address. Tell the client immediately not to pay and to confirm any payment changes by phone. Secure your account: change the password, check 2-Step Verification, review recent logins and forwarding rules, and warn other clients. Establish a rule that payment details are only changed after a phone confirmation.
:::

## Etiquette and common mistakes

| Mistake | Better practice |
|---|---|
| Empty or vague subject | Specific subject with reference numbers |
| Long walls of text | Short paragraphs, bullets |
| Forgetting attachments | Attach first, then write |
| Reply-all storms | Reply only to who needs it |
| Emotional replies | Wait, reread, respond calmly |
| Ignoring emails for days | Acknowledge within 24–48 hours ("Received, I'll reply by Friday") |
| Exposing everyone's addresses in mass emails | Use BCC or a mailing tool |
| Forwarding long threads without context | Add a short summary at the top |

## Practice tasks

1. Create a professional signature and enable 2-Step Verification.
2. Create three labels and a filter that labels emails from a specific sender.
3. Write a follow-up email to a client with a clear subject, bullets and a deadline.
4. Save a template for a frequently asked question (e.g. your price list).
5. Use search operators to find all emails with PDF attachments from the last year.

## Summary

- Use a professional address; a business domain builds trust.
- Set up a signature, profile photo, 2-Step Verification and recovery options.
- Write specific subjects, clear purpose, short paragraphs and a clear request; keep a polite, professional tone.
- Use Reply/Reply all/Forward and CC/BCC correctly; name attachments clearly; share big files via Drive links with correct permissions.
- Organise with archive, labels, filters, stars, snooze and search operators; use templates, scheduling and undo send.
- Watch for phishing, risky attachments and fake payment-change requests; confirm payment changes by phone.
