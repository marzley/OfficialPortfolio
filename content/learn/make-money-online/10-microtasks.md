# Micro-tasks, surveys and testing websites

**Micro-tasks** are small online jobs that take seconds or minutes: tagging an image, checking a search result, answering a survey, testing a website and saying what confused you. They need almost no skill, which is exactly why they **pay little**. They're useful for learning how online work and payments work, earning small amounts in spare time, and moving towards better-paid work like testing or annotation.

## Honest expectations

- Most people earn **a few hundred to a few thousand shillings a month** from micro-tasks and surveys.
- You may be screened out of many surveys (they want specific age groups or countries).
- **Website and app testing** pays the best in this category, but tests are limited.
- Use it as a **side income or a stepping stone**, not a main income.

## Platforms

> Availability depends on country and changes often. Check the official site, and never pay to join.

| Platform | What you do | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Clickworker](https://www.clickworker.com) | Short text, categorisation and data tasks | Assessments unlock more tasks; paid via PayPal. |
| [Toloka](https://toloka.ai) | Label images, rate search results, simple AI tasks | Many short tasks; good practice for annotation work. |
| [Prolific](https://www.prolific.com) | Academic research surveys and studies | Fair pay per study when available; eligibility varies by country. |
| [Microworkers](https://www.microworkers.com) | Varied small tasks from many employers | Choose tasks carefully. |
| [ySense](https://www.ysense.com) | Surveys, tasks and offers | Long-running; payouts via PayPal and others. |
| [UserTesting](https://www.usertesting.com) | Record yourself using websites and apps and say what you think | Better pay per test; needs clear spoken English and a microphone. |
| [Userlytics](https://www.userlytics.com) | Website and app usability tests | Paid per completed test. |
| [Test IO](https://test.io) | Find bugs in apps and websites (crowdtesting) | Learn basic software testing; paid per accepted bug. |
| [uTest](https://www.utest.com) | Crowdtesting community | Free training academy for testers. |

## Turning micro-tasks into a skill

Website and app **testing** can grow into a real career:

1. Join **uTest** and finish their free academy courses.
2. Learn how to write a **good bug report**: steps to reproduce, expected result, actual result, device and screenshots.
3. Practise on websites you use. Write bug reports in a Google Doc as a portfolio.
4. Learn the basics of **QA (quality assurance)**: test cases, regression testing, and later automation with tools like Selenium or Playwright (learn JavaScript or Python first in this hub).

A good bug report looks like this:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Title | Checkout button does nothing after entering M-Pesa number |
| Steps | 1. Add any product to cart 2. Go to checkout 3. Enter 0712345678 4. Tap "Pay" |
| Expected | M-Pesa PIN prompt is sent to the phone |
| Actual | Nothing happens; no error shown |
| Device | Tecno Spark 10, Android 13, Chrome 129 |
| Evidence | Screenshot and screen recording attached |

## Scams that pretend to be micro-tasks

These are **not** real jobs:

- **"Like videos / follow accounts and earn"** apps where you must **deposit money** to "upgrade" or "unlock withdrawals". These are pyramid and task scams. Many Kenyans have lost savings to them.
- **"Watch ads and earn KSh 1,000 a day"** apps: the money comes from new members' "registration fees", not ads.
- **Telegram "tasks"** that start by paying small amounts, then ask you to "invest" to continue.
- Anyone asking for your **M-Pesa PIN**, OTP code or to "send a small fee to receive your payment".

**Rule:** if you must pay money to earn money from simple tasks, it's a scam. See [Avoiding online money scams](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=avoid-scams).
