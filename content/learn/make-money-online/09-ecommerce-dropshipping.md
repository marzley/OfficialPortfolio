# E-commerce and dropshipping in Kenya

Selling online in Kenya has never been easier: customers already pay with **M-Pesa**, order on **WhatsApp** and **Instagram**, and couriers deliver countrywide. This lesson lists the platforms, explains dropshipping honestly, and walks you through delivery, payment and the numbers.

## Where to sell

| Channel | What it is | Good for | Costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| [WhatsApp Business](https://business.whatsapp.com) | Free app with a product catalogue, quick replies and labels | Every seller, especially to start | Free |
| [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com) shops | Product posts, reels and shop tabs | Fashion, beauty, food, décor | Free (ads optional) |
| [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com) | Short videos that sell products | Trendy, visual products | Free (ads optional) |
| [Jumia Kenya](https://www.jumia.co.ke) | Kenya's largest online marketplace: list products, Jumia handles checkout and delivery | Electronics, fashion, home goods | Commission per sale + fees |
| [Kilimall](https://www.kilimall.co.ke) | Online marketplace in Kenya | Similar to Jumia | Commission per sale |
| [Jiji Kenya](https://jiji.co.ke) | Classified ads (like an online market) | Second-hand, cars, phones, property, furniture | Free listings; paid boosts |
| Your own website | Your brand and your rules: WooCommerce, Shopify or custom with M-Pesa | Growing brands | Hosting + domain (+ app fees) |
| [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com) | Hosted online shop builder | Brands that want a quick professional shop | Monthly subscription |
| [WooCommerce](https://woocommerce.com) | Free shop plugin for WordPress | Full control, low monthly cost | Hosting + plugins |

**Start where your customers already are** (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok), then add a website when you have steady sales and want to look bigger and take payments automatically.

## Payments

| Method | How |
|---|---|
| **M-Pesa Till (Buy Goods)** | Apply through Safaricom (Lipa na M-Pesa). Customers pay your till number. |
| **M-Pesa Paybill** | Account numbers per order; good for businesses and websites. |
| **STK push on your website** | The customer gets a PIN prompt on their phone; needs the Daraja API. See [How to add M-Pesa payments to your website](../how-to-add-mpesa-payments-to-your-website). |
| [Paystack](https://paystack.com/ke) | Cards and M-Pesa through one checkout for websites |
| Cash on delivery | Builds trust with new customers, but risk of fake orders; ask for a small deposit for far deliveries |

Never send goods before confirming the money **in your own M-Pesa messages** (fake "payment confirmation" SMS are a common scam).

## Delivery options

| Option | Use |
|---|---|
| **Boda riders** you trust | Same-day delivery in town |
| [Pickup Mtaani](https://pickupmtaani.com) | Cheap pickup points and agents around Kenya |
| [G4S Courier](https://www.g4s.com) / Fargo Courier | Countrywide parcels |
| Bus and shuttle parcels (Easy Coach, Modern Coast, matatu saccos) | Cheap upcountry delivery |
| [Posta Kenya](https://www.posta.co.ke) | Countrywide postal and EMS services |
| Marketplace logistics | Jumia and Kilimall deliver for their sellers |

## Dropshipping, explained honestly

**Dropshipping** means you sell products you don't keep in stock: when a customer orders, your supplier sends it directly to them. You earn the difference between your price and the supplier's price.

| Type | How it works in Kenya | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **International** (AliExpress, Alibaba, CJ Dropshipping) | Supplier in China ships to the customer | Long delivery (weeks), customs duties and delays, returns are hard. Kenyan customers rarely accept waiting. |
| **Local dropshipping** | Partner with a wholesaler in Nairobi (Luthuli Avenue, Eastleigh, Gikomba, Industrial Area) who packs and sends orders you bring | You need a reliable supplier and clear agreement on stock, prices and who pays for returns. |
| **Import and hold small stock** | Buy small quantities (from local wholesalers or import via consolidators) and deliver yourself | Needs capital, but delivery is fast and you control quality. |

Most successful Kenyan "dropshippers" actually do **local dropshipping or small-stock selling**: they market on TikTok/Instagram, collect orders and payment, and a nearby supplier or rider delivers the same or next day.

## Know your numbers (example)

| Item | KSh |
|---|---|
| Selling price of a phone holder | 1,200 |
| Supplier cost | − 450 |
| Delivery you pay (free delivery offer) | − 250 |
| Ads spent per sale (TikTok/Facebook) | − 200 |
| M-Pesa and packaging | − 50 |
| **Profit per sale** | **250** |

If ads cost more than KSh 450 per sale, you lose money even though "sales are coming in". Track **cost per sale** every week. (Practise these sums in the **Excel** subject.)

## Legal and trust basics

- **Register a business name** on eCitizen (Business Registration Service) when you grow; get a **KRA PIN** and understand Turnover Tax.
- **Clear return policy** (for example, 7 days for faulty items) builds trust.
- **Real photos and videos** of your actual products beat stock images.
- **Don't sell counterfeits** or restricted goods: marketplaces ban sellers, and it's illegal.
- **Respond fast**: WhatsApp quick replies, opening hours, and delivery updates.

## Your first 30 days

1. Pick **one product category** you understand and can source reliably.
2. Set up **WhatsApp Business** (catalogue, greeting message, labels) and one social account.
3. Post **daily**: product videos, customer reviews, how-to-use clips.
4. Get your first 10 sales from friends, family and local groups; ask each for a review.
5. Track every sale, cost and profit in a spreadsheet. Only then try paid ads.
