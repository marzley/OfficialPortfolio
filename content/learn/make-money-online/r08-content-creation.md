# Content creation step by step: plan, film, edit, publish and grow on YouTube, TikTok and Facebook

The **Content creation** lesson in "130+ ways to earn online" explains how each platform pays. This unit is the hands-on production guide: how to plan content people want, film good videos with just a phone, edit them, publish with titles and thumbnails that get clicks, analyse results and stay consistent without burning out.

:::note What you will learn
- Finding your niche and content pillars
- Researching ideas people actually search for
- Scripting hooks and structure
- Filming with a phone: light, sound, framing
- Editing with CapCut and DaVinci Resolve
- Titles, thumbnails, captions and hashtags
- Posting schedules and repurposing one video into many
- Reading analytics and improving
- Copyright, ethics and safety
:::

## Step 1: Niche and content pillars

Choose a niche at the intersection of **what you know/love**, **what people want**, and **what brands or customers pay for** (see the niche table in 130+ ways). Then define 3–4 **content pillars** (recurring themes):

Example niche: "Tech for Kenyan students and small businesses"
- Pillar 1: Budget phone and laptop reviews
- Pillar 2: How-to tutorials (Excel, Canva, KRA iTax)
- Pillar 3: Side hustles and digital skills
- Pillar 4: Tech news explained simply

Pillars keep you consistent and help viewers know what to expect.

## Step 2: Research ideas

- **YouTube/TikTok search suggestions:** type a topic and note autocomplete phrases.
- **Comments:** questions on your and competitors' videos are free ideas.
- **Google Trends:** compare interest over time (set to Kenya).
- **Competitor analysis:** which videos in your niche got unusually high views? Why (topic, title, thumbnail)?
- **Seasonal calendar:** KCSE results, back to school, KRA deadline (June), Black Friday, Christmas, budget day.

Keep an **ideas list** (Google Sheets/Notion) with columns: idea, pillar, format, priority.

## Step 3: Script and structure

### The hook (first 3 seconds on TikTok, first 15 seconds on YouTube)
- Ask a question: "Why is your phone battery dying so fast?"
- Bold statement: "This free tool replaced my KSh 3,000 monthly subscription."
- Show the result first: the finished design, the before/after.

### Structure
- **Short videos (15–60 s):** hook → 1 clear point/steps → payoff → call to action (follow, comment).
- **Long videos (6–15 min):** hook → promise ("by the end you'll know...") → content in clear sections → recap → call to action.

Write bullet-point scripts; read them a few times; speak naturally rather than reading word for word.

## Step 4: Filming with a phone

| Area | Tips |
|---|---|
| **Light** | Face a window (natural light); avoid bright light behind you; a cheap ring light helps at night |
| **Sound** | The most important quality factor: record in a quiet room with soft furnishings; use a clip-on (lavalier) mic; avoid echoey rooms and wind |
| **Framing** | Vertical (9:16) for TikTok/Reels/Shorts; horizontal (16:9) for YouTube long videos; eyes about one-third from the top; clean background |
| **Stability** | Phone tripod or stack of books; avoid shaky footage |
| **Camera** | Use the rear camera for better quality when possible; clean the lens; lock focus/exposure |
| **Settings** | 1080p at 30 fps is plenty; check storage space |
| **B-roll** | Extra footage (hands typing, product close-ups, location shots) to cut into talking segments |

## Step 5: Editing

### Tools
- **CapCut** (free, phone and PC): templates, auto-captions, effects; great for short videos.
- **DaVinci Resolve** (free, PC): professional editing for long videos.
- **Canva** video editor: simple edits and templates.

### Editing workflow
1. Import clips; **cut** mistakes, pauses and filler ("eh", "umm").
2. Add **B-roll**, screenshots, zooms to keep visual interest.
3. Add **captions** (auto-generate, then correct spelling).
4. **Music:** royalty-free/licensed only, at low volume under speech.
5. **Colour and audio:** basic brightness/contrast; normalise and denoise audio.
6. **Export** at the correct aspect ratio and resolution.

Pacing tip: change something on screen every few seconds in short videos (cut, zoom, text, B-roll).

## Step 6: Titles, thumbnails, captions and hashtags

- **Titles:** clear benefit + keyword: "How to File KRA Nil Returns in 2026 (Step by Step on Phone)". Avoid clickbait that doesn't deliver.
- **Thumbnails (YouTube):** a face with emotion or the result, 3–4 big words, high contrast, readable on a phone (1280×720).
- **Descriptions:** summarise, add timestamps (chapters), links and keywords.
- **TikTok/Reels captions:** short, a question to prompt comments; 3–5 relevant hashtags (not 30 random ones).
- **Accessibility:** captions on all videos.

## Step 7: Posting schedule and repurposing

- Choose a **realistic schedule** you can sustain (e.g. 1 YouTube video + 3 shorts per week).
- **Batch** work: script on Monday, film 3 videos on Tuesday, edit Wednesday–Thursday.
- **Repurpose:** one long video → 3–5 short clips (Shorts, TikTok, Reels, Facebook) + a blog post + a carousel + a WhatsApp Channel update.
- **Engage:** reply to comments in the first hour; pin a good comment; reply with video replies on TikTok.

## Step 8: Analytics and improvement

| Metric | Meaning | If low, try |
|---|---|---|
| **Impressions / reach** | How many saw your thumbnail/video in feeds | Better topics, consistency |
| **Click-through rate (CTR)** | % who clicked after seeing the thumbnail | Better thumbnails and titles |
| **Average view duration / retention** | How long people watched | Stronger hook, tighter editing, deliver the promise faster |
| **Watch time** | Total minutes watched | Longer valuable videos, playlists |
| **Engagement** | Likes, comments, shares, saves | Ask questions, create share-worthy content |
| **Followers gained** | Growth | Consistent pillars, clear niche |

Review monthly: double down on topics and formats that perform; drop what doesn't.

## Step 9: Copyright, ethics and safety

- **Copyright:** don't upload others' videos, movies, TV clips or popular music without rights; you'll get claims, strikes or demonetisation. Use your own footage and licensed/royalty-free music (YouTube Audio Library, CapCut commercial library).
- **Disclose sponsorships** (#ad, paid partnership labels).
- **Fact-check** health, money and news claims; avoid promoting scams, unlicensed investments or betting to minors.
- **Privacy:** get permission before filming people; don't reveal your home location or children's details carelessly.
- **Mental health:** handle negative comments calmly; use filters, block abusers; take breaks.

:::think A creator's videos get many impressions but very few clicks (low CTR), and those who click watch most of the video. What should they improve?
The content is good (people who click keep watching), but the **packaging** isn't attracting clicks. Improve thumbnails (clear face/result, few bold words, contrast) and titles (clear benefit, curiosity, keywords). Test variations and compare CTR.
:::

## Summary

- Choose a niche with 3–4 content pillars; research ideas from search suggestions, comments, trends and competitors.
- Script strong hooks and clear structures for short and long videos.
- Film with good light, clean sound (clip-on mic), stable framing and B-roll.
- Edit with CapCut or DaVinci Resolve: cut, B-roll, captions, licensed music, good audio.
- Package with clear titles, strong thumbnails, captions and a few relevant hashtags.
- Post consistently, batch and repurpose, read analytics (CTR, retention) and improve.
- Respect copyright, disclose sponsorships, fact-check and protect privacy.
