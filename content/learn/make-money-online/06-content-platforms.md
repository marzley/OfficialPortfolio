# Content creation platforms: how each one pays

Content creators earn in five main ways: **platform payouts** (ad revenue, creator funds), **brand deals**, **selling their own products**, **fan support** (tips, memberships) and **affiliate links**. Each platform pays differently, and some programmes aren't open in every country. This lesson explains each one so you choose where to put your effort.

> Requirements and country lists change. Check each platform's official help page before planning around a programme.

## The platforms

| Platform | How creators earn | Typical entry requirement | Notes for Kenya |
|---|---|---|---|
| [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/creators/) | Ad revenue share, channel memberships, Super Thanks, Shopping | YouTube Partner Program: 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in a year, or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days (lower tier for fan funding) | Available in Kenya. Paid through Google AdSense to your bank. The most reliable long-term video income. |
| [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/business/creators) | Ads on videos and reels, Stars (fan tips), subscriptions | Followers, watch-minutes and compliance with monetisation policies | Monetisation tools are rolled out by country; check Professional Dashboard → Monetisation. |
| [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/creators/) | Creator Rewards Program, LIVE gifts, Subscriptions, TikTok Shop | Follower and view thresholds; country restrictions | Many payout programmes are limited by country; brand deals and LIVE gifts are common for Kenyan creators. Verify current eligibility. |
| [Instagram](https://creators.instagram.com) | Brand partnerships, subscriptions, gifts | Professional account; feature availability by country | Mostly brand deals and selling your own products/services. |
| [X (Twitter)](https://x.com) | Ads revenue sharing, subscriptions | Premium subscription and impression thresholds | Check eligibility; popular for influencer campaigns in Kenya. |
| [Spotify for Creators](https://creators.spotify.com) | Podcast hosting; partner programme and ads in some markets | Depends on market | Free hosting to grow a podcast; earn via sponsors. |

## Brand deals: where most Kenyan creators earn

Brands pay creators to mention or show their product. In Kenya, banks, telcos, betting firms (think carefully about promoting gambling), beauty brands, restaurants and tech companies all run influencer campaigns.

How to get them:

1. **Pick a clear niche** (tech reviews, Kenyan food, farming, fashion, comedy, personal finance). Brands buy audiences, not just numbers.
2. **Make a media kit** (one page, Canva): who you are, your audience (age, location from your analytics), average views, past work, contact.
3. **Pitch** local businesses and agencies by email or DM with a specific idea.
4. **Price**: start with what a business would pay for a small ad; raise prices as results prove your value. Ask for **50% upfront**.
5. **Disclose** sponsored posts (#ad, "paid partnership"). It's honest and required by platform rules.

## Sell your own things (the biggest earners do this)

- **Services**: photography, video editing, consulting, coaching, training.
- **Digital products**: e-books, Lightroom presets, Canva templates, courses (see [Sell digital products](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=digital-products)).
- **Physical products**: merchandise, beauty products, books.
- Use your content as free marketing and send people to a WhatsApp Business catalogue or your website with M-Pesa checkout.

## Affiliate links

Earn a commission when people buy through your link. Examples: [Amazon Associates](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com) (for international audiences), [Jumia affiliate (KOL) programme](https://www.jumia.co.ke) (Kenya), hosting and software affiliate programmes. See [Affiliate marketing](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=affiliate-marketing).

## A simple content plan that works

| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| Monday | Plan 3 videos/posts: one teaching, one story, one trending format |
| Tuesday to Thursday | Film and edit (CapCut is free and enough to start) |
| Friday | Post, reply to every comment in the first hour |
| Weekend | Study analytics: which video kept people watching longest? Make more like it. |

**Consistency beats perfection**: 3 good posts a week for a year beats 30 posts in one week then nothing.

## What platforms reward

- **Watch time and retention**: people watching to the end.
- **Engagement**: comments, shares and saves (more than likes).
- **Originality**: re-uploading other people's content gets no payouts and can get your account banned.
- **Following the rules**: no copyrighted music in monetised videos without a licence, no misleading thumbnails, no hate or dangerous content.
