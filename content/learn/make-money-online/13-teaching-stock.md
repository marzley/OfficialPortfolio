# Online tutoring, teaching and selling courses

If you can explain something well (a language, maths, coding, music, Excel, exam preparation), people will pay you to teach them. You can tutor **live, one to one**, teach **classes**, or record a **course once and sell it many times**.

## Live tutoring and language platforms

| Platform | What you teach | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Preply](https://preply.com) | Languages and school subjects, one to one | You set your price; the platform takes a commission (higher on trial lessons). Kiswahili tutors are in demand. |
| [italki](https://www.italki.com) | Languages (community tutor or professional teacher) | Teach English, Kiswahili or other languages you speak well. |
| [Cambly](https://www.cambly.com) | English conversation practice | Requirements for tutors change; check the tutor page. |
| [Wyzant](https://www.wyzant.com) | School and university subjects | Mainly US students; check eligibility. |
| [Superprof](https://www.superprof.com) | Any subject, online or in person | Students contact you through your profile. |
| [Outschool](https://outschool.com) | Small live classes for children | Check teacher requirements for your country. |
| Local (WhatsApp, Google Meet) | KCSE/CBC revision, computer classes, coding for kids | Often the fastest start: parents pay by M-Pesa. |

**Teach Kiswahili to the world**: diplomats, NGO staff, missionaries, tourists and the diaspora's children all want lessons. A good Kiswahili tutor profile with a friendly video can fill a schedule.

## Sell recorded courses

| Platform | How it works |
|---|---|
| [Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/teaching/) | Upload video courses; Udemy markets them and shares revenue. Big audience, but prices are often discounted. |
| [Teachable](https://teachable.com) | Your own course website; you keep more of the price and do your own marketing. |
| [Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com) | Similar to Teachable, with a free plan. |
| [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com) | Sell courses, e-books and downloads simply. |
| [Skillshare](https://www.skillshare.com/teach) | Paid by minutes watched by members. |
| [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com) | Free lessons build an audience; earn from ads and sell a full course. |
| Your own website with M-Pesa | Best for Kenyan buyers who prefer M-Pesa over cards. |

## What makes a good online teacher

1. **Clear audio** matters more than video quality (use a cheap clip-on microphone).
2. **Short lessons** (5 to 15 minutes per video) with one goal each.
3. **Practice**: exercises, quizzes and real examples (like this hub).
4. **A friendly intro video** on your profile: who you are, who you help, how lessons work.
5. **Reviews**: ask your first students to leave one.

## Course idea checklist

- Is it a skill people pay for? (Search the topic on Udemy and YouTube: lots of content = demand.)
- Can you teach it better for a specific audience? ("Excel for Kenyan SACCO accountants", "Kiswahili for medical volunteers")
- Can you show a result? ("Build your first website in a weekend")



## Selling photos, videos and music (stock media)

If you take good photos or videos, or make music, you can license them to businesses, bloggers and video makers worldwide through **stock agencies**. Every download earns you a royalty. Authentic **African** photos and videos (everyday life, business, farming, cities, nature) are in demand because there are too few of them.

| Platform | Sell | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Shutterstock Contributor](https://submit.shutterstock.com) | Photos, vectors, videos, music | Huge buyer base; review for quality. |
| [Adobe Stock Contributor](https://contributor.stock.adobe.com) | Photos, vectors, videos, 3D | Built into Adobe apps, so many designers buy here. |
| [Alamy](https://www.alamy.com/contributor/) | Photos | Good share of each sale. |
| [Getty Images / iStock](https://esp.gettyimages.com) | Photos and videos | Contributor app for submissions. |
| [Pond5](https://www.pond5.com) | Stock video, music, sound effects | Video footage of Africa sells well. |
| [Envato (AudioJungle, VideoHive)](https://author.envato.com) | Music, video templates | Large creator marketplace. |

Rules to know:

- **Model releases**: if a person can be recognised, you need their signed permission for commercial use.
- **No logos or brands** in commercial images (blur or avoid them).
- **Keywords matter**: describe what's in the image (who, what, where, mood) so buyers find it.
- Upload consistently: portfolios with hundreds of good images earn steadily; a few images earn little.
