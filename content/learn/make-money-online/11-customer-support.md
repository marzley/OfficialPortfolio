# Virtual assistance, customer support and remote jobs

Businesses everywhere need people to **answer customers, manage inboxes and calendars, update spreadsheets and handle admin**. This work can be done from home in Kenya, and our good English, time zone (close to Europe and the Middle East) and growing BPO industry make Kenyans popular hires.

This lesson complements [Working as a virtual assistant](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=virtual-assistant-work) with **where to find the jobs**.

## Types of roles

| Role | What you do | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| **Virtual assistant (VA)** | Email, calendar, bookings, research, documents | Google Workspace, organisation, clear writing |
| **Customer support (chat/email)** | Answer customer questions, solve problems | Typing speed, patience, product knowledge |
| **Call centre agent** | Phone support or sales | Clear speaking, calm under pressure |
| **Social media support** | Reply to comments and DMs for brands | Tone, speed |
| **Data entry and admin** | Updating CRMs, spreadsheets, catalogues | Accuracy, Excel/Sheets |
| **Online bookkeeping assistant** | Recording invoices and receipts | Excel, QuickBooks/Xero basics |
| **Executive / real-estate / e-commerce VA** | Specialist support for one industry | Industry tools |

**BPOs (business process outsourcing companies) in Kenya** hire agents for international clients, often with training, a salary and benefits. Look up the careers pages of large contact-centre companies operating in Nairobi, Mombasa and other towns, and check the **Ajira Digital** and **Jitume** programmes for partner opportunities.

## Getting hired

1. **Set up properly**: a laptop, a headset with a microphone, and backup internet (a Safaricom/Airtel hotspot besides home Wi-Fi). Many jobs test your speed.
2. **Learn the tools**: Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets), Microsoft 365, Zoom, Slack, Trello/Asana, a help-desk tool (Zendesk, Freshdesk, HubSpot: most have free trials or courses). This hub covers Google Workspace, Word and Excel.
3. **Record a 1-minute introduction video** (clear voice, good light, quiet background). Clients love it.
4. **Create a one-page CV for remote work**: typing speed, tools, availability in **their** time zone, and results ("handled 60 customer chats a day with 95% satisfaction").
5. **Apply consistently**: 5 tailored applications a day.

## Interview questions to prepare

- "Tell me about a time you dealt with an angry customer."
- "How do you prioritise when everything is urgent?"
- "What would you do if you didn't know the answer to a customer's question?"
- "Show us your typing speed" (aim for 50+ words per minute, accurately).

Answer with the **STAR method**: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

## Customer support writing: practise

Good support replies **thank, solve and confirm**:

> Hi Amina, thank you for reaching out, and sorry your order is late. I've checked: it left our Nairobi warehouse this morning and the rider will deliver by 4 pm today. I've also added free delivery to your next order for the trouble. Is there anything else I can help with?
