# Transcription and writing work

If you **type fast and accurately** and have **good English (or Kiswahili)**, transcription and writing are two of the quickest ways to start earning online with only a laptop and headphones.

## Transcription: turning audio into text

Clients include podcasters, researchers, lawyers, journalists, YouTubers and companies recording meetings. You listen and type exactly what's said, following a **style guide** (for example "clean verbatim": remove "umm" and repeated words; or "full verbatim": keep everything).

### Transcription platforms

| Platform | What you do | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [GoTranscript](https://gotranscript.com/transcription-jobs) | Transcribe short audio clips; also captioning and translation | Accepts transcribers from most countries. Pass a grammar and transcription test. |
| [TranscribeMe](https://www.transcribeme.com) | Short clips (often under a minute), with chances to move to specialist teams | Entry test; pay per audio hour. |
| [Rev](https://www.rev.com/freelancers) | Transcription and captions | Applications open by country and demand; check the freelancer page. |
| [Scribie](https://scribie.com) | Short files, review and editing roles | Simple test; good for practising. |
| [Happy Scribe](https://www.happyscribe.com) | Proofread AI transcripts and subtitles | Freelancer applications open at times. |
| [Verbit](https://verbit.ai) | Transcription and captioning | Recruitment by project. |
| [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com) / [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com) | Direct clients (often better pay) | Build a profile: "Swahili and English transcription". |

**Kiswahili and local languages are an advantage**: researchers and NGOs regularly need interviews in Kiswahili, Sheng, Kikuyu, Dholuo or Somali transcribed and translated.

### Tools and setup

- **Good headphones** (wired over-ear headphones beat cheap earphones).
- **Express Scribe** (free) or **oTranscribe** (free, in the browser) to slow down audio and use keyboard shortcuts to pause and rewind.
- A **foot pedal** is optional later.
- Practise typing to **60+ words per minute** with near-perfect accuracy (try monkeytype.com or keybr.com daily).

### Transcription rules that tests check

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Spell numbers as the guide says | "five hundred" vs "500" |
| Mark unclear audio | `[inaudible 00:02:15]` |
| Label speakers consistently | `Interviewer:` / `Respondent:` |
| Don't "fix" what was said in full verbatim | Keep "I seen it" if that's what they said |
| Remove fillers in clean verbatim | "um, so, like, we went" → "So we went" |

## Writing: articles, copy, descriptions and more

Businesses need words all the time: blog articles, website pages, product descriptions, emails, social captions, scripts, CVs.

| Writing type | Who pays | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| **Blog and SEO articles** | Websites, agencies | Research, clear structure, SEO basics (see Digital marketing) |
| **Copywriting** (sales pages, ads, emails) | Businesses, marketers | Persuasion, understanding customers |
| **Product descriptions** | Online shops, Jumia sellers | Short, accurate, benefit-focused writing |
| **Social media captions** | Brands, social media managers | Short, catchy, on-brand |
| **CV and LinkedIn profile writing** | Job seekers | Knowing what recruiters look for |
| **Technical writing** (user guides, documentation) | Software companies | Explaining complex things simply; pays well |
| **Grant and proposal writing** | NGOs, SACCOs, startups | Structure, research, deadlines |

### Using AI tools honestly

AI writing tools are now normal, but clients pay for **accuracy, local knowledge, a human voice and responsibility**. Use AI to brainstorm and outline; write and fact-check yourself. Many clients forbid AI-generated text: follow the brief. Never hand in AI text as fully your own where it's banned.

## A warning about "academic writing"

Many Kenyans have been drawn into writing essays and exams for foreign students ("academic writing", "account renting"). It's **contract cheating**: it breaks universities' rules, platforms ban it, and renting accounts in someone else's name means you can lose all your money with no protection. Build a writing career you can put your real name to.

## Your first 2 weeks

1. **Pick a niche**: transcription in English and Kiswahili, or writing about one area (tech, agriculture, travel in Kenya, personal finance).
2. **Make 3 samples**: a 1-page transcript (record yourself or a friend), a 600-word article, 5 product descriptions. Put them in a Google Drive folder with view links.
3. **Apply** to 2 transcription platforms and set up an Upwork or Fiverr profile.
4. **Send 5 proposals a day** for 14 days, each one written for that job (see [A winning profile and proposals](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=profile-and-proposals)).
