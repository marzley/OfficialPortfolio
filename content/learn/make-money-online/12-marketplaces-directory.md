# Freelance marketplaces directory (with links)

A **freelance marketplace** connects you with clients who need work done, holds the client's money safely and pays you when the work is approved. This is a directory of the main ones: what each is for, how it charges, and who it suits. Use it with [Freelancing platforms compared](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=freelancing-platforms) and [A winning profile and proposals](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=profile-and-proposals).

> Fees and rules change: check each site's current fee page before you join.

## General marketplaces (all skills)

| Marketplace | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com) | Clients post jobs; you send proposals using "Connects" (some free, more can be bought). Hourly or fixed-price contracts with payment protection. | Long-term clients in writing, design, coding, VA, marketing |
| [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com) | You create "gigs" (packaged services with prices); clients buy them. | Clear, packaged services (logo, video edit, website fix) |
| [Freelancer.com](https://www.freelancer.com) | Bid on projects and contests. | Many small projects; competitive on price |
| [PeoplePerHour](https://www.peopleperhour.com) | Proposals and fixed-price "offers"; UK-based clients. | Design, writing, web |
| [Guru](https://www.guru.com) | Profiles, quotes and workrooms. | Programming, design, admin |
| [Contra](https://contra.com) | Commission-free: a portfolio profile you share; clients pay you through it. | Freelancers who bring their own clients |
| [LinkedIn Services](https://www.linkedin.com/services/) | Add services to your LinkedIn profile; clients request proposals. | Professionals with a network |
| [Workana](https://www.workana.com) | Marketplace focused on Latin America. | Spanish/Portuguese speakers |
| [Malt](https://www.malt.com) | European freelance marketplace. | Developers and consultants serving Europe |

## Getting paid from marketplaces in Kenya

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **PayPal → M-Pesa** | Link PayPal to M-Pesa and withdraw directly to your phone (search "PayPal M-Pesa" on Safaricom's site). |
| [Payoneer](https://www.payoneer.com) | Receive from Upwork, Fiverr, Amazon and others; withdraw to a Kenyan bank. |
| **Direct bank transfer** | Many platforms pay straight to Kenyan bank accounts. |
| [Wise](https://wise.com) | Useful for international transfers; check what's supported for Kenyan residents. |

## Choosing your first marketplace

| If you... | Start on |
|---|---|
| Have a clear service you can package | Fiverr |
| Want long-term clients and are willing to write proposals | Upwork |
| Are an experienced developer | Toptal, Arc, Turing, Andela (alongside Upwork) |
| Teach a language or subject | Preply or italki |
| Already have clients from your network | Contra or LinkedIn Services |

**Focus on one or two** at first. A complete profile, 3 portfolio samples and daily proposals on one platform beat a half-empty profile on ten.

## Staying safe on marketplaces

- **Keep communication and payment on the platform** until you trust the client. Moving off-platform early loses your payment protection (and breaks most platforms' rules).
- **Never pay a "client" to receive work**, buy "training materials" from them, or deposit cheques for them.
- **Don't rent or buy accounts.** Use your own identity: verified accounts in your name protect your earnings.
- **Watch for fake job posts** asking you to install apps, share passwords or contact them on Telegram immediately.
