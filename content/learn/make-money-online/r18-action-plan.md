# Your 30-day action plan: from zero to your first online income, week by week

You've learned about many ways to earn online. Knowledge alone doesn't pay; **action** does. This final unit turns everything into a practical 30-day plan with daily tasks. It won't make you rich in a month (nobody honest can promise that), but if you follow it, by day 30 you'll have a skill direction, a portfolio, profiles, a pipeline of applications or content, and very possibly your first paid work.

:::note What you will build in 30 days
- A clear choice of one main earning path
- A basic skill level and 3 portfolio samples
- Professional profiles (LinkedIn + one platform)
- A payment setup (M-Pesa, PayPal/Payoneer or bank)
- 30+ applications, pitches or published pieces of content
- A simple tracker and weekly review habit
:::

## Before you start: your setup

| Need | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Device | A smartphone (a laptop is better for most skills; a cyber café or shared laptop can work at first) |
| Internet | A data bundle or Wi-Fi you can rely on daily |
| Time | 2–3 focused hours a day (adjust if you're working or studying) |
| Accounts | Gmail, LinkedIn, M-Pesa; later PayPal/Payoneer |
| Tracker | A Google Sheet with tabs: Learning, Portfolio, Applications/Content, Income |

## Choose one path

Pick **one** main path for these 30 days. Switching every week is the number one reason people never earn.

| If you... | Consider |
|---|---|
| Write well | Content writing, copywriting, transcription, blogging |
| Like design | Graphic design, social media content, printables |
| Like tech/logic | Web design, coding, IT support, data entry, Excel |
| Are good with people | Virtual assistant, customer support, sales, tutoring |
| Like being on camera | Content creation (YouTube/TikTok), UGC, tutoring |
| Have products or a trade | E-commerce, social media selling |
| Are detail-oriented | Data annotation, QA testing, bookkeeping |

## Week 1: Choose, learn and set up (days 1–7)

| Day | Tasks |
|---|---|
| 1 | Write down your goal (e.g. "earn my first KSh 5,000 online by day 30"). Choose your path. Create your tracker. |
| 2 | Find one free course for your skill (Ajira Digital, Google, freeCodeCamp, HubSpot, YouTube or this learning hub). Study 2 hours. |
| 3 | Study 2 hours. List 10 people/companies who pay for this skill and how much (search job boards and Fiverr gigs). |
| 4 | Study 2 hours. Practise: create your first rough sample. |
| 5 | Set up LinkedIn properly: photo, headline, about, skills. Connect with 20 people in your field. |
| 6 | Set up payments: confirm M-Pesa details; open PayPal and/or Payoneer if you'll work internationally (use your real details). |
| 7 | **Review**: what did you learn? Adjust your study plan. Rest a bit. |

## Week 2: Build your portfolio (days 8–14)

| Day | Tasks |
|---|---|
| 8 | Study the work of top people in your field. Note what makes it good. |
| 9 | Create portfolio sample 1 (a real-looking project: a blog post, a logo, a landing page, a spreadsheet, a short video). |
| 10 | Get feedback from someone experienced (a community, a friend, a mentor). Improve it. |
| 11 | Create sample 2. |
| 12 | Create sample 3. |
| 13 | Put your samples in one place: Google Drive folder, Behance, GitHub, Canva PDF or a simple website. |
| 14 | **Review**: are your samples good enough that you'd pay for them? Fix the weakest one. |

:::tip Real projects beat imaginary ones
Offer to do one small project for a local business, church, chama or friend at a low price or free in exchange for a testimonial. A real client name on your portfolio builds trust fast.
:::

## Week 3: Go to market (days 15–21)

| Day | Tasks |
|---|---|
| 15 | Create a profile on one platform (Upwork, Fiverr, a tutoring site or a local job board) or set up your content channel. Use the profile lesson's templates. |
| 16 | Send 5 tailored applications/pitches or publish 1 piece of content. |
| 17 | Send 5 more / publish 1 more. Tell your network what you offer (WhatsApp status, LinkedIn post). |
| 18 | Approach 5 local businesses directly with a specific offer. |
| 19 | Send 5 more / publish 1 more. Follow up on earlier messages politely. |
| 20 | Improve your profile based on what you've learned (headline, gig images, pricing). |
| 21 | **Review**: how many replies? What's working? Adjust your pitch. |

A simple pitch template:

```
Hi [Name], I saw [specific thing about their business/job post].
I [do X] for [type of client] – here's a similar sample: [link].
For you, I'd [specific idea: e.g. redesign your menu / write 4 blog posts a month].
Would you like me to send a quick quote? – [Name]
```

## Week 4: Deliver, follow up and grow (days 22–30)

| Day | Tasks |
|---|---|
| 22 | Keep sending 5 applications or publishing daily. Reply to every enquiry within hours. |
| 23 | If you get a job: confirm scope, price, deadline and deposit in writing. Deliver early and well. |
| 24 | Ask satisfied clients for a review/testimonial and referrals. |
| 25 | Learn one advanced topic in your skill. |
| 26 | Create sample 4 based on the type of work clients ask for most. |
| 27 | Record all income and expenses in your tracker; set aside a share for tax and savings. |
| 28 | Follow up with every warm lead. |
| 29 | Plan your next 30 days: income goal, skills to learn, number of pitches. |
| 30 | **Big review**: total applications, replies, clients, income. Celebrate progress. |

## Tracking your progress

Your numbers tell you what to fix:

| Metric | If it's low... |
|---|---|
| Applications sent | Send more: aim for 5+ a day |
| Reply rate | Improve targeting and personalise your pitch |
| Interviews/calls → jobs | Improve portfolio and how you explain your process |
| Repeat clients | Improve quality, communication and deadlines |

```try-python
applications = 60
replies = 9
jobs = 3
income = 14500
print("Reply rate: %.0f%%" % (replies / applications * 100))
print("Job rate from replies: %.0f%%" % (jobs / replies * 100))
print("Average per job: KSh %.0f" % (income / jobs))
```

## Common reasons people quit (and how to push through)

- **"No one is replying."** Normal at the start. Improve one thing at a time and keep volume up.
- **"I'm not good enough yet."** Start with simpler jobs; you improve by doing paid work.
- **"Everyone is doing it."** Be specific: a niche, a local market or a better service beats competing on everything.
- **"I spent money on a course and got nothing."** Use free learning first; avoid guaranteed-income programmes.
- **"I earned, then nothing."** Build a pipeline: keep pitching and creating even when busy.

:::think After 30 days, Wanjiru has sent 70 applications, got 6 replies, 1 small job worth KSh 3,000 and a good review. Should she switch to a new skill?
No. One job and a review is real progress. Her reply rate (about 9%) suggests her pitch is working somewhat; she should keep the same skill, improve her portfolio with the job, specialise further, and keep the volume up. Switching resets her progress to zero.
:::

## Summary

- Choose one path for 30 days and track everything in a simple sheet.
- Week 1: learn and set up profiles and payments. Week 2: build 3 portfolio samples. Week 3: go to market with 5 pitches or content pieces daily. Week 4: deliver, ask for reviews, follow up and plan the next month.
- Use metrics (applications, reply rate, job rate, average per job) to know what to fix.
- Expect slow starts; consistency and specialisation beat switching skills.
