# The 10 categories of online work (a map)

"Making money online" is not one thing. It is at least **ten different kinds of work**, each with its own skills, platforms, start-up time and income. This map (the same ten categories used in Kenya's **Jitume** and **Ajira Digital** trainings) helps you choose **one** to start with, instead of jumping between everything and earning nothing.

## How to choose your first category

Answer honestly:

1. **What can I already do well?** Typing fast, writing, design, speaking clearly, selling, coding? Start there: the fastest money comes from skills you already have.
2. **How much time do I have per day?** Micro-tasks fit 30 minutes. Building a design or coding skill needs 2 to 3 hours a day for months.
3. **Do I need money this month, or am I building for next year?** Short term: transcription, annotation, VA work, local social media clients. Long term: coding, design, content creation, e-commerce brands.
4. **What equipment do I have?** A smartphone is enough for social media management, content creation and some micro-tasks. Writing, transcription, design and coding need a laptop (even an old one).

## Income ladder (realistic)

| Level | Typical monthly income | How people get there |
|---|---|---|
| Starting out | KSh 0 to 10,000 | Micro-tasks, first small gigs, testing what you like |
| Steady side income | KSh 10,000 to 50,000 | One skill, a few repeat clients or a steady platform |
| Full-time | KSh 50,000 to 200,000+ | A strong skill (design, coding, VA, marketing), portfolio, reviews, direct clients |

These are typical ranges, not promises. **Anyone promising "KSh 5,000 a day from your phone, no skills" is lying**: see [Avoiding online money scams](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=avoid-scams).

## The rule that matters most

> **Pick one category. Learn it properly. Do it for 90 days.**

People who earn online consistently are not lucky: they chose one thing, built a skill and a portfolio, and kept applying when the first 20 proposals got no reply.

## Before you sign up anywhere

- **Never pay to get a job or "activate" an account.** Real platforms pay you; they don't charge registration fees.
- **Use your real name and ID.** Buying or renting someone else's account breaks the platform's rules, and you can lose all your earnings.
- **Check the platform works in Kenya.** Rules change; the official website's FAQ is the only reliable source.
- **Set up a way to get paid first**: M-Pesa, a bank account, PayPal linked to M-Pesa, or Payoneer. See [Getting paid in Kenya](./?track=make-money-online&lesson=getting-paid-kenya).
