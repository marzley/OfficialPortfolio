# Social media management as a service: skills, packages, content calendars, clients and reports

Almost every business in Kenya knows it should be on social media: the salon, the school, the hardware shop, the restaurant, the law firm, the church. Most owners don't have time to post consistently, design graphics, reply to comments or understand what's working. A **social media manager** does this for them for a monthly fee. It's one of the most accessible online services to start: you need a smartphone, a laptop helps, and the skills can be learned free.

:::note What you will learn
- What a social media manager actually does each week
- Skills and free tools you need

- Finding clients locally and online
- Onboarding a client and planning a content calendar

- Running basic paid ads
- Monthly reports that keep clients paying
:::

## What the job involves

| Task | Example |
|---|---|
| **Strategy** | Decide goals (more bookings, more WhatsApp enquiries), audience, platforms and content themes |
| **Content creation** | Graphics in Canva, short videos, captions, carousels, stories |
| **Scheduling** | Plan a month of posts and schedule them in Meta Business Suite or other tools |
| **Community management** | Reply to comments and DMs, hide spam, pass leads to the client |
| **Paid ads** | Run boosts or Ads Manager campaigns with the client's budget |
| **Reporting** | Monthly results: reach, followers, engagement, messages, sales leads |

## Skills and tools

- **Writing** short, clear captions in English, Swahili or Sheng depending on the audience.
- **Basic design**: Canva (templates, brand kit, resizing), CapCut for video editing.
- **Platform knowledge**: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, YouTube Shorts; each has different formats.
- **Planning**: Google Sheets or Notion for a content calendar.
- **Scheduling**: Meta Business Suite (free for Facebook and Instagram), TikTok's built-in scheduler, other tools with free tiers.
- **Analytics**: platform insights, Google Analytics if the client has a website.
- **Free learning**: Meta Blueprint, Google Digital Garage, HubSpot Academy social media courses, Ajira Digital training.

## Step 1: Build a portfolio before clients

Clients want proof. Create it:
1. Run your own page about a topic and grow it for 30 days.
2. Create **3 sample content packs** for imaginary or real local businesses (a café, a salon, a school): 9 posts each with captions.
3. Offer one or two businesses a **2-week free or discounted trial** in return for a testimonial and permission to show results.
4. Put everything in a simple portfolio: a Canva PDF, a Behance page or a page on your website.

## Step 2: Packages and pricing

Sell monthly **packages**, not "per post", so income is predictable.

| Package | Includes | Example price (Kenyan SMEs) |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 1 platform, 12 posts/month, captions, scheduling | KSh 8,000–15,000 |
| **Growth** | 2 platforms, 16–20 posts, 4 short videos, comment replies | KSh 15,000–35,000 |
| **Premium** | 3 platforms, daily posting, videos, DM handling, ads management, report | KSh 35,000–80,000+ |

Prices vary hugely by client size and city; international clients often pay more. **Ad spend is separate** from your fee: the client pays Meta/TikTok directly or gives you a budget.

:::tip Always write a scope
List exactly what's included (number of posts, platforms, revisions, response hours). Extra requests ("can you also design our menu?") are charged separately.
:::

## Step 3: Find clients

- **Walk in locally**: businesses near you with inactive or messy pages. Show them a mock-up of 3 posts for their brand.
- **Your network**: tell friends, family, church and chama members what you do.
- **LinkedIn and Facebook groups** for business owners.
- **Freelance platforms**: Upwork, Fiverr ("social media manager", "Instagram content"), and local job boards.
- **Partner** with web designers, photographers and branding agencies who need someone for social media.

A cold message template:

```
Hi [Name], I came across [Business] on Instagram – your [product/service] looks great.
I noticed the page hasn't posted since [month]. I help local businesses post consistently
and turn followers into WhatsApp enquiries. I made 3 sample posts for you (attached).
Would you be open to a 10-minute call this week to see if a monthly plan would help?
– [Your name], [portfolio link]
```

## Step 4: Onboard a new client

1. **Contract and deposit**: sign a simple agreement; take the first month's fee upfront.
2. **Access**: ask to be added as a partner/admin in Meta Business Suite rather than getting passwords. Never ask for or store their personal passwords if a role-based option exists.
3. **Brand kit**: logo, colours, fonts, tone of voice, photos, products and prices, FAQs.
4. **Goals**: what does success look like? More calls? More visits? Choose 2–3 measurable goals.
5. **Audit**: current followers, best and worst posts, competitors, what's missing.

## Step 5: Plan the content calendar

Use content **pillars** (repeating themes) so you never run out of ideas. For a salon:

| Pillar | Example posts |
|---|---|
| Showcase | Before/after hairstyles, new braids styles |
| Education | How to care for locs, best products for natural hair |
| Social proof | Client reviews, stylist of the month |
| Offers | Tuesday discount, back-to-school package |
| Behind the scenes | Team, setup, day in the salon |

A monthly calendar in a sheet: Date | Platform | Pillar | Format (reel, carousel, image, story) | Caption | Visual link | Status (draft, approved, scheduled, posted).

Send the month's plan for **approval** a week before it starts.

## Step 6: Create, schedule and engage

- Batch-create content: shoot videos on one day, design graphics on another.
- Write captions with a hook, value, and a call to action: "Book on WhatsApp: 07xx…".
- Schedule in Meta Business Suite; post TikToks natively.
- Check comments and messages daily (agree response hours in the contract). Pass enquiries to the client quickly; leads are what they pay for.

## Step 7: Basic paid ads

- Start with a clear goal: messages, website visits or calls.
- Target by location (e.g. 5 km around the shop), age and interests.
- Test 2–3 creatives with a small daily budget, keep the best performer.
- Track cost per message or cost per lead, not just likes.

## Step 8: Report monthly

A one-page report keeps clients renewing:
- Posts published vs planned
- Reach and impressions, follower growth
- Engagement (likes, comments, shares, saves)
- Messages/enquiries and, if known, sales
- Top 3 posts and why they worked
- Plan for next month

:::think A client says "we're paying you but sales haven't increased" after two months. What should you have done from the start, and what do you do now?
Agree measurable goals and how leads are tracked (e.g. a WhatsApp link, "mention this post" offers). Now: review the report together, show enquiries generated, check whether the client is responding to leads quickly, and adjust content and ads toward offers and calls to action.
:::

## Summary

- Social media managers plan, create, schedule, engage, run ads and report for businesses.
- Learn Canva, CapCut, Meta Business Suite and platform insights; free courses exist.
- Build a portfolio with your own page, sample packs and trial clients.
- Sell monthly packages with a written scope; ad spend is separate.
- Onboard with a contract, deposit and role-based access; plan with content pillars and a calendar; report results monthly.
