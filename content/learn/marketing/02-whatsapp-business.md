# Selling with WhatsApp Business

In Kenya, customers already live on WhatsApp. The free **WhatsApp Business** app turns it into a shop window, customer service desk and marketing tool. Used well, it's often the highest-converting channel a small business has.

## WhatsApp vs WhatsApp Business

| Feature | Personal | Business |
|---|---|---|
| Business profile (hours, address, website, category) | No | Yes |
| Catalogue of products with prices | No | Yes |
| Automatic greeting and away messages | No | Yes |
| Quick replies (saved answers) | No | Yes |
| Labels to organise chats | No | Yes |
| Short link and QR code | No | Yes |

Use a separate phone number for the business if you can, so work and personal chats don't mix.

## Set up in 20 minutes

1. Install **WhatsApp Business** and register your business number.
2. **Business profile**: logo as the profile photo, business name, category, a clear description ("Fresh cakes baked daily in Thika. Order 24 hours ahead. Delivery in Thika and Juja."), address with map pin, hours, email and website.
3. **Catalogue**: add products with a good photo, price, short description and a link. Group them into collections (Birthday, Wedding, Cupcakes).
4. **Greeting message**: "Karibu Njeri's Bakery! 🎂 See our menu in the catalogue. Tell us the cake, size and date and we'll confirm the price."
5. **Away message** for outside business hours.
6. **Quick replies**: type `/` to insert saved answers, e.g. `/price`, `/delivery`, `/mpesa`.

```
/mpesa  →  Pay to Till Number 123456 (Njeri's Bakery). Send us the M-Pesa message and we'll confirm your order. We never ask for your PIN.
```

7. **Labels**: New customer, Pending payment, Paid, Delivered, Follow-up.

## Get people to message you

- Your **wa.me link**: `https://wa.me/2547XXXXXXXX?text=Hi%2C%20I%27d%20like%20to%20order` opens a chat with a pre-filled message. Put it on your website, Instagram bio, Facebook page and Google Business Profile.
- Print the **QR code** (Settings → Business tools → Short link) on flyers, packaging and the shop counter.
- "Click to WhatsApp" ads on Facebook and Instagram.

## Status: your free daily advert

Your contacts see your Status every day. Post:

- New products and today's specials, with the price
- Behind-the-scenes (baking, packing orders)
- Customer reviews and photos (with permission)
- Limited offers: "Only 5 cakes available for Saturday"

2–4 good posts a day beats 20 blurry ones. Customers who save your number see them, so ask happy customers to save it.

## Broadcast lists (not groups)

A **broadcast list** sends one message to many people individually (they reply privately). People only receive it if **they have saved your number**. Use it for announcements to customers who want updates. Don't spam: weekly at most, and let people opt out. (See the data protection lesson.)

## Great customer service on WhatsApp

- Reply fast: set expectations in your away message.
- Confirm orders clearly: item, price, delivery date, delivery fee, total, how to pay.
- Send a receipt or confirmation after payment.
- Follow up after delivery: "Did you enjoy the cake? A quick review would help us a lot."
- Stay polite even with difficult customers: chats can be screenshotted.

## Staying safe

- Turn on **two-step verification** (Settings → Account).
- Never share the 6-digit registration code.
- Confirm payments in your actual M-Pesa statement, not from screenshots (fake screenshots are common).

## Measure it

WhatsApp Business shows message statistics (sent, delivered, read). Also track: how many chats became orders each week, and which Status posts brought messages.
