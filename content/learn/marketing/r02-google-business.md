# Google Business Profile: set up, verify, optimise, get reviews and appear in Google Maps

When someone searches "pharmacy near me", "car wash Thika Road" or "best nyama choma in Kitengela", Google shows a **map with three businesses** (the "local pack") above the normal results. Those listings come from **Google Business Profile** (formerly Google My Business). It's **free**, and for many local businesses (salons, clinics, restaurants, hardware shops, schools, garages, hotels) it brings more calls, directions requests and WhatsApp messages than their website. This unit shows how to create, verify and optimise a profile, collect reviews, and track results, step by step.

## Who should use it

Any business that serves customers **in person** at a location (shop, office, clinic) or **in an area** (plumbers, cleaners, delivery services, mobile mechanics). Online-only businesses without a customer-facing location aren't eligible for a map listing.

## Step 1: Create the profile

1. Go to Google Business Profile (business.google.com) and sign in with a Google account the **business owns** (not a staff member's personal account that may leave).
2. Enter the **business name exactly** as on your signboard and documents. Don't add keywords ("Mama Mboga Fresh Vegetables Best Cheap Nairobi" breaks the rules).
3. Choose the **primary category**: the most specific that fits ("Dental clinic", not just "Clinic"). Add secondary categories for other services.
4. Add your **location** (pin it precisely on the map) or, for service-area businesses, hide the address and list the **areas you serve**.
5. Add **phone number** and **website**.

## Step 2: Verify

Google must confirm the business is real before it appears fully. Depending on the business, Google offers methods such as:
- **Video verification**: record a continuous video showing the signage, location, equipment and proof of management (e.g. opening the till or showing business documents).
- **Phone/SMS or email codes** (for some businesses).
- **Postcard** by mail (less common in Kenya).
- **Live video call** with Google support.

Verification can take days. Make sure your signage is visible and your details match your documents.

## Step 3: Complete every section

| Section | Tips |
|---|---|
| **Hours** | Accurate regular hours, plus special hours for holidays (Madaraka Day, Christmas) |
| **Description** | 750 characters: what you do, who you serve, what makes you different; natural keywords, no links or promotions |
| **Services / products** | List each with descriptions and prices if possible |
| **Attributes** | Wheelchair accessible, Wi-Fi, accepts M-Pesa/cards, women-led, etc. |
| **Photos** | Logo, cover, exterior (helps people find you), interior, team, products, work samples; real and recent; add regularly |
| **Booking/appointment links** | Your booking page or WhatsApp link |
| **Opening date, social profiles** | Where available |

Profiles with complete information and many real photos tend to get more engagement.

## How local rankings work

Google says local results are based on:
1. **Relevance**: how well your profile matches the search (categories, services, description, reviews mentioning the service).
2. **Distance**: how far the searcher is from your location (you can't change this).
3. **Prominence**: how well-known the business is: reviews (number and rating), links and mentions across the web, your website's SEO, and activity.

Fake addresses or keyword-stuffed names may work briefly but risk suspension.

## Reviews

Reviews influence both rankings and customers' decisions.

### Getting more reviews (legitimately)

- Copy your **review link** from the profile ("Ask for reviews") and share it by WhatsApp/SMS after a good service.
- Print a **QR code** at the counter or on receipts.
- Ask at the right moment: when the customer is happy (on delivery, after a successful repair).
- Make it easy: a short message with the direct link.

Not allowed: paying for reviews, offering discounts in exchange for reviews, posting fake reviews, or reviewing competitors negatively. Google removes them and can restrict or suspend profiles.

### Replying to reviews

Reply to **all** reviews, politely and promptly:

```
Positive: "Thank you, Achieng! We're glad the birthday cake made the day special. See you next time! – Team Bake House"

Negative: "Sorry about the delay with your order, Kamau. That's not the experience we want for you. Please WhatsApp us on 07xx xxx xxx so we can make it right. – Joyce, Manager"
```

For negative reviews: stay calm, apologise for the experience, take the conversation offline, fix the problem, and never reveal private customer information. Prospective customers read your replies.

## Posts, Q&A and messaging

- **Posts**: share offers, events, new products and updates with a photo and button ("Call", "Order online"). Post regularly to show the business is active.
- **Questions & answers**: add common questions yourself and answer them (parking? delivery areas? payment methods?). Monitor what others post.
- **Messaging and calls**: respond quickly; set up call history if available. Response speed affects customer experience.
- **Products**: showcase items with prices.

## Insights (Performance)

The Performance section shows:
- Searches that found you (search terms)
- Profile views on Search and Maps
- **Calls, website clicks, direction requests, messages, bookings**

Track these monthly, alongside enquiries you actually receive. Add **UTM tags** to the website link (e.g. `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`) so Analytics shows profile visits separately.

## Managing profiles for clients

- The business should be the **Primary owner**; you get **Manager** access (Profile → ⋮ → Business Profile settings → People and access).
- Never create profiles under your own Google account and keep them hostage.
- Agencies with many locations can use a Business Profile Manager organisation account.
- Offer GBP setup and monthly management (posts, photos, review replies, reports) as a service.

## Mistakes and violations to avoid

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Keywords or locations stuffed into the business name | Suspension risk; edits reverted |
| Virtual offices, PO boxes or fake addresses | Suspension |
| Multiple profiles for the same business at one location | Duplicates removed or suspended |
| Wrong hours (especially holidays) | Angry customers, bad reviews |
| Paying for or faking reviews | Reviews removed; restrictions |
| Ignoring the profile after setup | Outdated info, missed messages |

If a profile is suspended, fix the issue and submit an appeal with evidence (business registration, utility bills, signage photos).

:::think A barber in Kisumu has a profile with 3 photos, no hours, 4 reviews, and the name "Best Barber Kisumu Cheap Haircut Fade Kids". What would you change to improve results without breaking rules?
Change the name to the real business name; set accurate hours and special hours; add services with prices, attributes (M-Pesa accepted), and a strong description; upload many real photos (exterior, interior, haircut styles); share the review link/QR code with satisfied customers and reply to every review; post weekly updates; and track calls and directions in Performance.
:::

## Summary

- Google Business Profile is free and powers the local pack and Maps for businesses serving customers in person or in an area.
- Create it under a business-owned account, use the real name and most specific category, then verify (often by video).
- Complete hours, description, services, attributes and many real photos; rankings depend on relevance, distance and prominence.
- Ask for reviews legitimately with links/QR codes, and reply to all reviews professionally.
- Use posts, Q&A, messaging and Performance insights; avoid keyword-stuffed names, fake addresses and fake reviews.
