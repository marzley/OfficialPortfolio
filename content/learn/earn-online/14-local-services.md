# M. Local services powered by online tools (ways 127–132): cyber services, photography, repairs, delivery apps and hotspots

Some of the most reliable income combines a **local, physical service** with **online marketing, booking and payments**. Customers find you on Google Maps, WhatsApp or TikTok, book and pay with M-Pesa, and you deliver the service in person or digitally. These businesses suit people who want steady income in their own town.

---

### 127. Cyber and e-services (KRA, eCitizen, KUCCPS, HELB and more)

**What it is.** Helping people with online government and institutional services for a fee: KRA PIN registration and annual returns (nil and simple employment returns), eCitizen services (birth certificates, passports applications, business name registration, good conduct certificate applications), KUCCPS course applications, HELB applications, NTSA services, SHA registration, job applications, printing, scanning and CV typing.

**How to start, step by step:**
1. Learn the processes thoroughly (the **E-services in Kenya** subject on this hub explains many step by step), and keep up with changes on official sites.
2. Set up: a laptop, printer/scanner, reliable internet, a small space (or start mobile, visiting clients).
3. Display clear prices for each service (official government fees separate from your service fee).
4. Market: Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Status, posters near schools and offices; peak seasons are KRA returns (before 30 June), KUCCPS application windows, HELB and school admissions.
5. Offer remote service: clients send details via WhatsApp and pay by M-Pesa.

**Ethics and data protection.** You handle IDs, passwords and personal data:
- Get the client's consent; let clients type their own passwords where possible, or help them change passwords after.
- Don't keep copies of documents longer than needed; never reuse data.
- **Never** falsify documents or information (e.g. income or results): it's illegal.

### 128. Photography and videography (booked and delivered online)

**What it is.** Event photography (weddings, graduations, birthdays, church events, corporate functions), portraits, product photography for online sellers, real estate photos and videos, and content creation for businesses, marketed and delivered online.

**How to start:**
1. Learn camera basics (exposure, light, composition) on a phone or entry-level camera; learn editing (Lightroom, CapCut/DaVinci).
2. Build a portfolio: free or low-cost shoots for friends, a local business and an event.
3. Post your best work on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Google Business Profile.
4. Offer clear packages (hours, number of edited photos, delivery time); take deposits.
5. Deliver via online galleries or Google Drive links.

**Product photography** for Jumia/Instagram sellers is a fast-growing niche: clean white-background images and lifestyle shots.

### 129. Phone and computer repair (marketed online)

**What it is.** Repairing phones (screens, batteries, charging ports, software), laptops (upgrades to SSD/RAM, OS installation, cleaning, virus removal) and offering pickup/drop-off booked via WhatsApp.

**How to start:** train with an experienced technician or a course (the **Computer maintenance** subject covers PC basics), get basic tools, source genuine parts, offer warranties, and market with before/after videos on TikTok and a Google Business Profile. Popular online add-ons: data backup and recovery, software installation, remote support (method 87).

---

### 130. Delivery and ride-hailing apps

**What it is.** Earning through apps such as **Uber**, **Bolt**, **Little** (rides) and **Glovo** or other delivery apps (food and parcels), using a car, motorbike or bicycle.

**How to start:** meet each app's requirements (valid licence, PSV/insurance requirements for drivers, good conduct certificate, vehicle inspection), understand commission rates and fuel costs, and calculate real profit per day after fuel, maintenance, insurance and loan repayments.

**Honest view.** Income depends on hours, location, fuel prices and app commissions; vehicle financing can make profits thin. Do the maths before taking a vehicle loan.

---

### 131. Home tutoring booked online

**What it is.** In-person tutoring (method 92) marketed online: parents find you through WhatsApp groups, Facebook, Google and referrals; you teach at their home or a centre.

**How to start:** create a clear profile (subjects, levels, experience, results), ask for reviews, price per session or monthly, and use WhatsApp for scheduling and M-Pesa for prepayment.

---

### 132. Wi-Fi hotspot business

**What it is.** Selling internet access in estates, hostels, markets, matatu stages or rural centres: customers buy time or data packages (e.g. hourly, daily, weekly) and pay with M-Pesa, using a **hotspot billing system** with captive portal login.

**How to start, step by step:**
1. Get a reliable internet source (fibre or business internet with good speeds).
2. Buy routers/access points that support hotspot management (MikroTik is common).
3. Use a **hotspot billing system** with M-Pesa integration, vouchers and user management (Marzley Tech builds Wi-Fi hotspot billing systems for Kenyan ISPs and hotspot owners: see our [Wi-Fi hotspot billing system](../wifi-hotspot-billing-system-kenya.html) page).
4. Price packages for your customers (students and estates prefer small hourly or daily bundles).
5. Check legal requirements: reselling internet in Kenya may require licensing from the **Communications Authority of Kenya**, depending on your setup; ask your upstream provider and check CA guidance.
6. Maintain good service: uptime and support decide whether customers return.

## Summary

- Local services + online marketing and payments: cyber/e-services, photography and video, phone and computer repair, delivery/ride apps, home tutoring and Wi-Fi hotspots.
- Use Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business, short videos and M-Pesa to win and serve customers.
- Handle personal data responsibly, never falsify documents, check licensing (e.g. Communications Authority for internet resale), and calculate real profit.
