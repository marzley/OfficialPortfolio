# Best AI for business and marketing: social media, ads, SEO, customer service and WhatsApp

For a small business, AI is like having a junior marketer, designer and assistant who works for a small monthly fee. Used well, it saves hours every week. Used carelessly, it makes your brand sound generic or gives customers wrong answers.

## The best tools by job

| Job | Best tools | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Social media posts and captions** | Chat assistants, **Canva** (Magic Write + designs), Meta's AI tools in Business Suite | Plan a month of posts in one sitting |
| **Graphics and product photos** | Canva, Photoroom, Adobe Express | Clean product photos sell better |
| **Ads (Facebook, Instagram, Google)** | Meta Advantage+ and Google Ads' built-in AI, chat assistants for ad copy | Start with small budgets and measure |
| **SEO and website content** | Chat assistants + Google Search Console | Write helpful content for real questions; check facts |
| **Customer service chat on your website** | **Tidio**, **Intercom (Fin)**, other chatbot builders | Answers FAQs; hands over to a human |
| **WhatsApp customer service** | WhatsApp Business (quick replies, catalogue, away messages); WhatsApp Business API providers for AI bots | Most Kenyan customers prefer WhatsApp |
| **Emails and newsletters** | Chat assistants, Mailchimp/Brevo AI helpers | Personal, short and useful |
| **Business plans and proposals** | Chat assistants | Draft structure; add your real numbers |

## A one-hour monthly content plan

```text
I run a hardware shop in Kenol, Murang'a. Customers: builders, farmers and homeowners.
Create a 4-week social media plan (3 posts a week) for Facebook and WhatsApp status:
mix tips, products, offers and customer stories. For each post: idea, caption (under 60 words), image idea.
```

Then:
1. Edit captions so they sound like you; add real prices and offers.
2. Design images in Canva using your colours and logo.
3. Schedule posts in Meta Business Suite.
4. After a month, check what got the most messages, not just likes.

## Customer replies that sound human

```text
A customer on WhatsApp says: "Your delivery was 2 days late and the cement bag was torn."
Write a short, warm apology in simple English, offer a replacement bag and a discount on the next order.
Under 60 words. Don't make promises I haven't approved.
```

Save your best replies as **quick replies** in WhatsApp Business.

## Chatbots: do's and don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Train it on your real FAQs, prices and policies | Let it invent prices, stock or delivery times |
| Always offer "talk to a person" | Hide that it's a bot |
| Review conversations weekly and improve answers | Use it for refunds or complaints without a human |

## SEO with AI (the honest way)

- Use AI to find the **questions** your customers ask and to outline helpful articles.
- Add **your own experience**, local details, real photos and prices. Google rewards helpful, original content.
- Don't publish hundreds of thin AI articles; that can hurt your site.
- See [what is SEO](../what-is-seo) and [how to get your business on Google Maps](../how-to-get-your-business-on-google-maps).

:::warning Honesty in marketing
Don't use AI to write fake reviews, fake testimonials or fake "before and after" images. It misleads customers, breaks platform rules and can break consumer protection law. Ask real customers for reviews instead.
:::

:::kenya Kenyan examples
- A salon uses Canva and a chat assistant to post daily styles and offers, and WhatsApp quick replies for bookings.
- A farm input shop uses AI to write planting-season tips in Kiswahili and English, building trust and sales.
- A tutor uses AI to make practice questions and promotional posters for holiday tuition.
:::

:::think A restaurant wants an AI chatbot on WhatsApp to take orders. What must be in place before launch?
Accurate, up-to-date menu and prices; delivery areas and times; payment instructions (M-Pesa Till/Paybill) and confirmation process; a clear handover to a human for problems; testing with real staff; and someone checking conversations daily at first. The bot must never invent menu items, prices or delivery promises.
:::

## Summary

- Chat assistants + Canva handle most marketing content; Meta and Google ads have built-in AI.
- WhatsApp Business is essential in Kenya; chatbots must hand over to humans.
- Add real experience to SEO content; never fake reviews.
- Plan monthly, measure what brings messages and sales.
