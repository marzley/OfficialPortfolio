## The prompt habit that matters most

Give **context, task, examples and format**:

```text
Context: I'm a [role] helping [audience] with [goal].
Task: [exactly what you want].
Details: [facts, notes, numbers I provide].
Format: [length, structure, tone, language].
Rules: Don't invent facts; ask me if anything is missing.
```

## The five golden rules

1. **One chat assistant first**; add specialists only when you hit a limit.
2. **Give your own facts**; AI writes best from your material.
3. **Verify** facts, numbers, references and code.
4. **Protect privacy**: no passwords, PINs or others' personal data.
5. **Be honest**: follow school and work rules; no deepfakes or fake reviews.
