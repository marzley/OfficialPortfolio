# Best AI for images and graphic design: posters, logos, photos and social media

AI can now create images from a description, remove backgrounds, extend photos and design whole posters. For graphic designers it's a powerful assistant; for small businesses it's a quick way to get decent visuals. But good design still needs a human eye, and **copyright and trademarks still matter**.

## The best tools by job

| Job | Best tools | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Posters, flyers, social posts** | **Canva** (with Magic Studio) | Templates + AI images + easy text editing in one place; huge Kenyan user base |
| **Generate images from text** | ChatGPT image generation, Gemini, **Midjourney** | ChatGPT/Gemini: easy and good at following instructions; Midjourney: very artistic, high-quality styles |
| **Images with readable text** | **Ideogram**, ChatGPT images | Better at spelling words inside images (posters, signs) |
| **Commercially safer images** | **Adobe Firefly** (also inside Photoshop and Express) | Trained with licensed content and designed for commercial use |
| **Logos and vector graphics** | **Recraft**, Adobe Illustrator's AI features | Can produce editable vector (SVG) output |
| **Edit photos** | Photoshop (Generative Fill), Canva, **Photoroom** | Remove objects, extend backgrounds, product photos |
| **Remove backgrounds** | remove.bg, Canva, Photoroom | One click for product and profile photos |
| **Upscale / improve old photos** | Photoshop, Canva, dedicated upscalers | Sharpen low-quality images (check results carefully) |

**Best overall for most people in Kenya:** Canva for finished designs, plus one image generator (ChatGPT or Gemini) for custom pictures.

## How to write a good image prompt

Describe it in this order: **subject, setting, style, lighting, composition, colours, format.**

```text
A smiling Kenyan woman selling fresh mangoes at a busy market stall in Nyeri, morning sunlight,
realistic photo style, shallow depth of field, warm colours, square 1:1 format, space at the top for text.
```

```text
Flat vector illustration of a young man coding on a laptop at a desk with a plant,
blue and yellow brand colours, clean minimal style, white background.
```

Tips:
- Generate several options, then refine: "Same image but the stall is green and there are bananas too."
- **Add text later in Canva**, not in the AI image; AI often misspells words.
- Say what you *don't* want: "no extra fingers, no text, no watermark".

## A poster workflow for a business (15 minutes)

1. Write the message first: offer, date, place, contact.
2. Generate one or two background images (or use a real photo of the shop).
3. Open a Canva template in the right size (Instagram post, WhatsApp status, A4 poster).
4. Add the image, the text, the logo and the phone number.
5. Check spelling, contrast and that the phone number is correct.
6. Export as PNG for social media, PDF for printing.

## Logos: be careful

- AI can give **ideas and directions** for a logo quickly.
- But a good logo needs to be simple, unique and work in one colour and at small sizes; AI often produces busy images.
- AI images may look similar to existing brands. **Check it doesn't copy a known logo**, and consider registering your trademark with KIPI (Kenya Industrial Property Institute) if the brand matters.
- Many professionals use AI for ideas, then **redraw the final logo as clean vectors**.

:::warning Rights, people and fakes
- Don't create images of real people (especially politicians or celebrities) doing things they didn't do. Deepfakes can be illegal and harmful.
- Don't use other artists' names to copy their exact style for commercial work.
- Check each tool's terms for commercial use, especially for client work.
- Don't present AI images as real photos in news, adverts or evidence.
:::

Politely refuse to copy it: it would infringe Safaricom's trademark and could get the client sued or forced to rebrand. Offer an original logo instead, use AI for fresh ideas, and suggest registering the final logo with KIPI.

## Summary

- Canva for finished designs; ChatGPT, Gemini or Midjourney to generate images; Ideogram for text in images; Adobe Firefly for commercially safer images; Recraft for vectors.
- Prompt with subject, setting, style, lighting, composition, colours and format.
- Add text in Canva, not in the AI image.
- Respect trademarks, people's rights and commercial-use terms.
