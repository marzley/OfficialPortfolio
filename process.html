Process

How we’ll work together

A simple, transparent process so you always know what’s happening and what comes next.

  1. Step 1

    Discuss

    We talk through your goals, audience and budget on a call or WhatsApp, and agree on scope.

  2. Step 2

    Design

    We plan the pages and design the look, and you review it before any code is written.

  3. Step 3

    Build

    We develop the site or system, including payments, forms and admin tools, and share progress.

  4. Step 4

    Launch & support

    We go live on your domain and hosting, show you how to manage it and stay available for support.
