About

Developer, designer, trainer

Marzley Tech Solutions was founded by Kelvin Wanyoike, known in the tech space as Marzley: a web developer, designer and IT professional based in Kenya.

We build systems and websites across industries: hospital and pharmacy systems, e-commerce, college portals, sales systems and portfolios. Alongside client work, we train and mentor students in IT and programming, with a focus on real, project-based skills.

  • Front-end & UI/UXHTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design
  • Back-end & APIsPHP, MySQL, M-Pesa, Paystack, PayPal
  • PlatformsWordPress, hosting & deployment
  • SystemsSecurity, backups, data management
