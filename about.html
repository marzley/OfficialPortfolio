About Me

I’m Kelvin Wanyoike — Marzley

Also known in the tech space as Marzley — a passionate web developer, designer, and IT professional based in Murang'a County, Kenya. I’m the founder of Marzley Tech Solutions, a digital brand focused on turning ideas into reality through innovative web and app solutions.

Expert on Information Technology, I build systems and websites across industries — hospital & pharmacy systems, e-commerce platforms, college portals, sales systems and portfolios. I’m also a trainer and mentor, empowering students in IT and programming.

Coding isn’t just work — it’s how I create, relax and push boundaries. When not building or teaching, I explore frameworks, watch tech documentaries, and design interfaces.
