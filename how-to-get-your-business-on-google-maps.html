How to get your business on Google Maps in Kenya

By Kelvin Wanyoike · 27 Sep 2026 · 2 min read

When people search for a service near them, Google shows a map with nearby businesses before any website. Getting your business onto that map is free, and it’s one of the fastest ways to get new customers calling. Here’s how.

1. Create your Google Business Profile

  1. Go to business.google.com and sign in with a Google account you control long term.
  2. Enter your exact business name, the way it appears on your signboard and receipts.
  3. Choose the category that best describes what you do, for example “Website designer” or “Pharmacy”.
  4. Add your location, or choose to hide your address if you serve customers at their place.
  5. Add your phone number and website.

2. Verify it

Google needs to confirm your business is real. Depending on your business, this may be by phone, email, video or a postcard. Your profile only appears on Maps after verification, so complete this step.

3. Complete every section

  • Opening hours, including if you’re open 24 hours.
  • Photos of your premises, products, team and work. Profiles with photos get far more clicks.
  • Services and prices, so customers know what you offer before they call.
  • A description in plain words: what you do, where, and for whom.

4. Get reviews

Reviews are one of the biggest factors in which businesses Google shows first. After every happy job, send the client your review link and ask for an honest review. Always reply to reviews, good or bad, politely.

5. Keep it active

Post updates and offers, add new photos and keep your hours correct. An active profile signals to Google that your business is open and trustworthy.

Connect it to your website

Your Google profile and website work best together: the profile gets you found, and your website convinces people to choose you. Make sure your business name, phone and location match exactly on both. Run a free website check to see if your site is ready for Google.
